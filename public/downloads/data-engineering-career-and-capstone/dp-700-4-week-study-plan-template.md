            Lakehouse, Warehouse, capacities)

Domain 3 -> Fabric Chapter 3 (Monitoring Hub, Direct Lake, CI/CD,
            incident response, governance)

## Exam-day time budget (Lesson 39)

```
Total time: ~110-120 minutes for ~50-60 questions
Average:    a little under 2 minutes per question
Short, factual questions:  aim for well under a minute
Long scenario questions:   spend the extra time you banked
```

- After the first 15-20 questions, do a pace check. Behind? Speed up on the
  easy ones — don't panic on the hard ones.
- Flag and return on anything not resolving after a genuine attempt
  (full read + at least one eliminated option). Don't fight a single
  question for five minutes.
- If the real exam interface warns that a case-study section can't be
  revisited once you leave it, budget extra time for that section
  specifically — there's no second pass.
- Use process of elimination first, every time — Lesson 37's trap patterns
  (lookalike item types, overcomplicated external-service options, absolute
  wording, over-broad controls) remove wrong answers before you hunt for
  the right one.

# Lesson 62 — Gathering Business Requirements · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Each segment is one TTS call so slide timing follows
the audio. Target: ~2 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

Seven chapters assumed you already knew what to build. This one backs
up a step: before you drag a single visual, know who's looking, and
why.

## S2 · STEPS: How do they use it? -> What metrics matter? -> What assumptions? -> What do they need?

Four questions, straight from Microsoft's own design guidance. How does
your audience actually use this. What metrics change what they do
next. What cultural or learned assumptions might shape how they read
it. And what do they need to succeed — not what's merely interesting.

## S3 · CODE: Dashboard = overview. Report = detail. Readers drill from one to the other.

A dashboard sits on top of reports that already hold the detail.
Readers can always drill deeper — so the dashboard itself should show
only what someone needs to monitor, nothing more.

## S4 · OUTRO CARD (SVG: next lesson, LTV seal)

Write the answers down, even briefly — that becomes your reference
point for every design choice the rest of this chapter covers. Next:
choosing which KPIs actually earn a spot on the dashboard.

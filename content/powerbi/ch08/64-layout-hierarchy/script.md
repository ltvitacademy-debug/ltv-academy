# Lesson 64 — Dashboard Layout & Visual Hierarchy · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Each segment is one TTS call so slide timing follows

## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

You've chosen your KPIs. Now: where do they actually go on the page?

## S2 · IMAGE: pbi_card.png (GDP card, large number)

Most people read top to bottom, left to right — so the headline fact
goes top-left, with detail increasing as the eye moves further. A card
visual is the simplest way to make one number impossible to miss. Size
alone signals importance, before anyone's processed what it means.

## S3 · IMAGE: power-bi-marketing-sample-dashboard.png (Sales and Marketing sample dashboard)

Here's that principle applied for real. Total Volume, top-left — the
single highest-level number on the page. Moving right and down: a
trend, a segment breakdown, progressively more specific detail.
Nothing here is accidental.

## S4 · OUTRO CARD (SVG: next lesson, LTV seal)

And it all has to fit on one screen, no scrollbars — consistent scales
and colors throughout, so nobody has to relearn a convention tile by
tile. Next: storytelling with data — choosing visuals that actually
support the point you're making.

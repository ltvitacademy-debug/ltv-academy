# Lesson 63 — Choosing KPIs · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Each segment is one TTS call so slide timing follows
the audio. Target: ~2 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

Not every number you can compute deserves a spot on a dashboard. Here's
how to tell the difference.

## S2 · CODE: If this number moved significantly, would anyone do anything differently?

One test, applied metric by metric. If the honest answer is no, it's
interesting, not actionable — it belongs in a report someone can drill
into, not the handful of numbers checked every day.

## S3 · CODE: 15 tiles = nothing prioritized. 3 tiles, well chosen = everything prioritized.

A dashboard crowded with everything available doesn't communicate more
— it communicates that nothing was worth prioritizing. Fewer,
well-chosen KPIs beat a longer list that just gets skimmed past.

## S4 · CODE: Value alone: "$2.3M in sales." A real KPI: value + target.

And a genuine KPI needs a target to compare against, not just a raw
value — that's exactly why the KPI visual exists as something distinct
from a plain card.

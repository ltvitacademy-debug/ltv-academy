## S2 · CODE: Total Sales = SUM(Sales[SalesAmount])

DAX — Data Analysis Expressions — is the formula language behind every
calculation in Power BI. Here's the simplest one possible: a measure that
sums a column. On its own, that's nothing a dragged field couldn't do.
The payoff comes once this measure has a name — you can reuse it inside
other formulas instead of repeating the same SUM everywhere.

## S3 · IMAGE: qsdax_3_chart.png (Previous Quarter Sales chart)

Here's what a real DAX formula unlocks: comparing this quarter's sales
against last quarter's. That's not something a dragged field can produce
— it took one formula, filtering the same column down to a different
period. That's the gap DAX fills, and it's what the rest of this chapter
is about.

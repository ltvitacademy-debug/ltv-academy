# Lesson 54 — Bar, Column, Line & Pie Charts · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Each segment is one TTS call so slide timing follows
the audio. Target: ~2.5 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

The visuals you'll use constantly. Let's start with the most common one
of all: the column chart.

## S2 · IMAGE: column-drill.png (four drill icons)

Give it a real date hierarchy, and four drill icons appear. Drill up
moves to a higher level. Drill down lets you select one column to see
its detail. Next level expands every column at once — no clicking
through one at a time.

## S3 · IMAGE: column-cross.png (cross-filter/highlight, interaction icons)

Select a data point, and it can affect other visuals two ways.
Cross-filter hides unrelated data entirely. Cross-highlight dims it
instead, keeping the full picture visible while emphasizing your
selection.

## S4 · IMAGE: column-formatting.png (gradient conditional formatting dialog)

And just like tables, chart colors can be data-driven — a gradient
here, shading lower values red and higher values blue, performance
visible at a glance.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD (SVG: next lesson, LTV seal)

Line charts for trend, pie charts for a small part-to-whole breakdown
— same principles, different shapes. Next: cards, KPIs, and slicers —
the visuals for single numbers and on-canvas filtering.

## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

Sometimes one number matters more than any comparison. Here's how to
show it well — and let viewers filter it themselves.

## S2 · IMAGE: build-card.png (card visual with Units/Revenue)

A card puts one measure front and center. Add more than one value, and
more cards appear automatically.

## S3 · IMAGE: walkthrough-add-reference-labels.png (six card sections with reference labels)

A bare number rarely tells the whole story, though. Reference labels
pair the headline with supporting detail — Revenue variance and its
percentage, right alongside the main figure. A KPI visual goes one step
further still: instead of just a value, it shows progress toward an
actual target.

## S4 · IMAGE: select-all-is-not-filtering.png (Segment slicers, inverted selection)

Slicers put filtering right on the canvas — always visible, unlike the
Filters pane. And here's a subtlety worth knowing: select all, then
clear one value, and the slicer applies an "is not" filter for just
that one value — not an "is" filter listing everything else. New data
added later gets included automatically, since nothing explicitly
excluded it.

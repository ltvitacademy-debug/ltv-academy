## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

When exact numbers matter more than shape, you reach for one of two
visuals. Here's the more powerful one.

## S2 · IMAGE: power-bi-matrix-create.png (matrix with Year/Quarter rows, Subcategory/Product columns)

A matrix works like an Excel PivotTable — fields on rows and columns
both, automatically aggregated at every intersection. Year and Quarter
down the side, Subcategory and Product across the top, Units Sold
filling every cell.

## S3 · IMAGE: matrix-visual-expand-and-collapse-column-headers.png (expand/collapse icons)

Plus and minus icons on the headers let viewers drill into more detail
or collapse back to a summary — or turn on Auto expand to show every
level the moment the visual loads.

## S4 · IMAGE: matrix-visual-frozen-row-headers.png (frozen row headers)

And by default, row headers stay frozen as the matrix scrolls
horizontally — category labels never disappear off screen, no matter
how many columns you're comparing.

# Lesson 60 — Buttons, Bookmarks & Navigation · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Each segment is one TTS call so slide timing follows
the audio. Target: ~2 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

A bookmark saves the exact state of a report page — filters,
selections, even which visuals are showing. Here's how to put that to
work.

## S2 · IMAGE: bookmarks-pane.png (Bookmarks pane, Add/View)

Turn on the Bookmarks pane from View, get the page looking exactly how
you want it captured, and select Add.

## S3 · IMAGE: bookmarks-add.png (bookmark added, guidance text)

Power BI names it automatically — rename it right away to something
meaningful. And under More options, you control exactly what it
restores: filters and slicers, visual visibility, even whether it
navigates to a specific page at all.

## S4 · IMAGE: bookmarks-button.png (Format button, Action, Bookmark type)

The real payoff: attach a bookmark to a button. Toggle Action on, set
Type to Bookmark, pick the one you want — now selecting that button
resets filters or switches views in one click.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD (SVG: next lesson, LTV seal)

Group several bookmarks together, and View plays just that group as a
slideshow — a guided walkthrough, no clicking required. Last lesson of
the chapter: analytics, forecasting, and advanced visuals.

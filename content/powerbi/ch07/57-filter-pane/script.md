# Lesson 57 — Filters & the Filter Pane · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Each segment is one TTS call so slide timing follows
the audio. Target: ~2 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

Every filter you build lives in one pane, and applies at one of three
levels. Let's see all of it.

## S2 · IMAGE: power-bi-filter-visual.png (filter icon popup)

Visual, page, or report — that's the reach a filter can have. And any
viewer can check exactly what's affecting one visual just by hovering
and selecting the filter icon — no editing, just visibility.

## S3 · IMAGE: power-bi-desktop-format-filter-pane.png (format filter pane colors)

Lock a filter, and viewers can see it but not touch it. Hide it, and
they can't see it at all — useful for cleanup filters nobody needs to
know about. You can even format the pane itself to match your report's
look.

## S4 · IMAGE: apply-filter-button.png (Apply button)

And on larger reports, turn on a single Apply button — viewers make
several filter changes first, and nothing re-queries until they select
Apply. One setting, real performance difference.

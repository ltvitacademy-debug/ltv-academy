# Lesson 52 — Choosing the Right Visual · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Each segment is one TTS call so slide timing follows
the audio. Target: ~2 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

Dozens of visual icons in that pane, and it's tempting to pick whichever
looks most impressive. Here's the better approach.

## S2 · STEPS: Comparison -> Trend -> Part-to-whole -> Exact values -> Single number

Start from the question, not the icon. Comparing categories — bar or
column. Showing change over time — line. Parts of a whole — pie, donut,
or treemap. Exact values — table or matrix. One number that matters
most — a card.

## S3 · STEPS: What's the question? -> How many categories? -> Exact or visual? -> Will it filter others?

Four questions settle almost every choice: what are you asking, how
many categories are involved, does the audience need exact numbers or
just a visual impression, and will this visual need to filter or be
filtered by others on the page.

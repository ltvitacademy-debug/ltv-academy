## S1 · TITLE CARD (SVG: lesson title, LTV brand)

Color and style, driven by the data itself instead of set by hand. Three
ways to do it, starting with the most common.

## S2 · IMAGE: table-formatting-1-color-by-rules-table.png (color by rules)

Rules: green above a target, yellow near it, red below. Set each range
as a percent of the overall spread, or as a literal number — mixing
those two up is the single most common mistake here.

## S3 · IMAGE: table-formatting-3-default-table-bars.png (data bars)

Data bars turn each cell into a small bar chart — longer bar, higher
value. Toggle "Show bar only" to let the bar speak for itself, no
number needed.

## S4 · IMAGE: table-formatting-1-default-dialog.png (icons)

Icons attach a status symbol to each value — a diamond, a triangle, a
circle — readable at a glance, no legend required. And this same
Gradient, Rules, and Field value logic works well beyond tables — charts,
cards, and slicers all support it too.

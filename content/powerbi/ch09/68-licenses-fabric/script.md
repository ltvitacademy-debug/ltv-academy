# Lesson 68 — Understanding Power BI Licenses & Fabric · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Every person in the Power BI service holds one of three licenses —
and what you can actually do depends on more than just which one.

## S2 · CODE: Free -> Pro -> Premium Per User

Free lets you build for yourself, nothing more. Pro adds sharing and
collaboration with other Pro users. Premium Per User adds most Premium
features, but only shareable with other PPU users.

## S3 · STEPS: Shared workspace -> No Free access | Capacity workspace -> Free can view

Here's the part people miss: a Free user can view shared content, but
only if that content lives in a workspace assigned to a Premium or
Fabric capacity. The license alone never decides this — where the
workspace lives decides it too.

## S4 · CODE: Fabric = Power BI + data engineering + data science + real-time analytics

Microsoft Fabric is the umbrella platform now housing Power BI
alongside these other tools, all sharing one capacity. A Fabric F64 or
larger capacity does everything a Power BI Premium capacity did.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Once you know what your license allows, Lesson 69 puts you inside your
first real workspace.

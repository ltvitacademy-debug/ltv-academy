# Lesson 72 — Sharing Reports & Managing Access · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

One rule matters more than any button in this lesson: sharing a report
also shares its underlying semantic model.

## S2 · IMAGE: power-bi-share-links.png

Three link types, three outcomes. People in your organization, for
anyone internal. Specific people, for named individuals or guests.
People with existing access, which grants nothing new at all.

## S3 · CODE: Reshare (default ON) -> Build (default OFF)

Every link also carries two optional permissions. Reshare lets the
recipient pass it along further. Build is the bigger one — it lets
them create entirely new reports from your semantic model.

## S4 · IMAGE: power-bi-share-manage-settings.png

Manage permissions shows everyone with direct access in one place —
grant more, review pending requests, or remove someone entirely.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Hiding a table doesn't stop someone from seeing its data — only row-
and object-level security actually restrict it. Lesson 73 packages
all of this into something bigger: an app.

## S1 · TITLE CARD

One page, telling a story at a glance — that's a Power BI dashboard,
and it's the last piece of this chapter.

## S2 · IMAGE: power-bi-dashboard2.png

Chapter Eight's whole discipline — headline number first, everything
else supporting it — was preparing you for exactly this. A dashboard
is that discipline, built as an actual Power BI object.

## S3 · IMAGE: power-bi-diagram.png

Tiles get pinned from reports, and reports sit on semantic models. A
dashboard can pull tiles from more than one report, and more than one
semantic model, into a single consolidated view.

## S4 · CODE: One page -> no filtering -> not in Desktop -> alerts on tiles

Dashboards and reports differ in real ways: one page instead of many,
no filtering at all, never available in Desktop, but dashboards do
support alerts on individual tiles.

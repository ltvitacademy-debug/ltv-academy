# Lesson 73 — Power BI Apps · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Sharing works for one or two people. For a whole department, you
package content into an app instead.

## S2 · IMAGE: power-bi-apps.png

Apps show up in their own list, distinct from the workspace they came
from. Users can filter and drill into them, but never modify what's
underneath.

## S3 · IMAGE: create-app-button.png

Every app starts from Create app, right there in the workspace list
view — one level up from the workspace itself.

## S4 · IMAGE: setup-page.png

Setup captures the name, description, and theme — the first thing app
users see before they even open it.

## S5 · IMAGE: audience-tab.png

Audiences are the real power here. A Sales team audience and a
Product team audience can see completely different content, from the
exact same app.

## S6 · OUTRO CARD

One published app, many possible views. Lesson 74 covers the last
piece — dashboards themselves, back in the Power BI service.

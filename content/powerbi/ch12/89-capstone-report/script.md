# Lesson 89 — Build the Report · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~3.5 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Three report pages, each answering one group of requirements from
Lesson 84 — not one page trying to do everything.

## S2 · STEPS: Regional Performance -> Product Profitability -> Fulfillment & Trend

Page one covers territory sales versus target. Page two covers
category and subcategory margin. Page three covers reseller
fulfillment and year-over-year trend.

## S3 · CODE: Headline card top-left -> sorted bar, not alphabetical -> consistent color and sort everywhere

Every page follows the same rules: headline card top-left and
largest, bars sorted by the metric itself rather than alphabetically,
and identical color conventions across all three pages.

## S4 · OUTRO CARD

Three pages, each with a five-second headline number. Lesson 90 adds
the interactivity that lets a viewer actually explore past that
headline.

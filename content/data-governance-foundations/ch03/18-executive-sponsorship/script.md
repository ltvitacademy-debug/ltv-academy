# Lesson 18 — Executive Sponsorship · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Chapter 3 has given you every individual and group role a governance program needs. This
closing lesson covers the one role that determines whether any of the others actually stick:
executive sponsorship.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (why sponsorship matters)

Industry research on governance program failure returns to the same finding again and
again: a lack of sustained executive sponsorship is one of the most common reasons programs
fail. Owners and stewards only carry real authority when someone senior enough to matter is
visibly backing their role.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (what a sponsor actually does)

A real sponsor isn't a figurehead who signs off once. The job has four ongoing parts:
aligning governance with business strategy so it solves problems people actually care about,
securing real budget and time, modeling the behavior in the rooms where priorities get set,
and breaking ties at the top when the governance committee itself can't resolve something.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (signs of sponsorship in name only)

Sponsorship usually doesn't disappear in one dramatic moment — it erodes quietly. Watch for
the signs: a name on a slide who's never attended a meeting, governance work that always
loses to other priorities without pushback, or nobody on the team being able to say who the
sponsor actually is.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Roles, committees, RACI, and sponsorship — that's the complete structure Chapter 3 set out
to build. Next chapter covers what that structure actually produces: policies and standards.
See you there.

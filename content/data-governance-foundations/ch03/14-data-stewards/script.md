# Lesson 14 — Data Stewards · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Lesson 13 gave you the owner — the person Accountable for a data domain. This lesson covers
who actually does the recurring work the owner is accountable for: the data steward.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what a steward does)

DAMA-DMBOK defines a steward as a business leader or subject matter expert accountable for
tasks related to data management. Concretely: maintaining the domain's agreed definitions,
monitoring quality against the owner's thresholds, answering what-does-this-mean questions
from other teams, and escalating anything they can't resolve themselves.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (three flavors of steward)

Stewardship isn't one job — DAMA-DMBOK and the field that followed it generally split it
into three flavors. The business steward understands what the data means. The technical
steward understands how it's structured and stored. The data management steward owns the
glossary, the quality rules, and the documentation. One person can hold all three on a small
domain; a regulated, high-risk domain often needs a dedicated person for each.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (owner, steward, custodian)

Put the chain together and you get the backbone of this chapter: the owner is Accountable,
the steward is Responsible for daily work, and — as the next lesson covers — the custodian
handles the technical implementation underneath both of them.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Owner decides, steward maintains, and next lesson adds the two roles that round out the full
picture: custodians and consumers. See you there.

# Lesson 15 — Data Custodians and Data Consumers · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Owner decides, steward maintains — and this lesson closes out the role roster with the two
that round out the full chain: the custodian who implements it all technically, and the
consumer who relies on the result.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what a custodian holds)

A data custodian is responsible for the safe custody, transport, and storage of data, and
for implementing the business rules the owner and steward have already defined. That's the
database structure, access controls, backups, encryption, and the pipelines moving data
between systems — almost always an IT, platform, or DBA role.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (owner, steward, custodian — three questions)

A simple way to keep all three straight: the owner answers who decides what the rules are.
The steward answers what the data means and whether it's good enough. And the custodian
answers where it physically lives and who implements the controls that enforce it.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (the consumer's role)

The fourth role is the data consumer — anyone using data without owner, steward, or
custodian authority: an analyst, a manager, a sales rep. Consumers still carry real
responsibilities: follow access rules, report problems instead of quietly working around
them, respect classification, and give feedback — consumers are often the first to notice a
definition doesn't match reality.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Four roles, one chain: owner decides, steward maintains, custodian implements, consumer
relies on the result. Next lesson: where these roles actually meet face to face — governance
councils and committees. See you there.

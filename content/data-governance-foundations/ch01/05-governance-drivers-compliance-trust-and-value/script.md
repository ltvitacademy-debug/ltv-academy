# Lesson 5 — Governance Drivers: Compliance, Trust and Value · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Nobody funds "governance" in the abstract. Every real program gets approved for one of three concrete reasons.

## S2 · STEPS — Three drivers

Compliance — avoiding legal and regulatory consequences. Trust — making data something people actually believe and use. Value — making data generate more business value, faster. Knowing which one is actually paying for your program changes how you pitch it and measure it.

## S3 · STEPS — Compliance, in real terms

This is the easiest driver to fund because the cost of ignoring it is explicit. GDPR — fines up to 20 million euros or 4 percent of global revenue. CCPA and CPRA in California — enforceable consumer rights. HIPAA for health data in the US. It's narrow in scope, but it has real teeth.

## S4 · STEPS — Trust, the harder sell

Trust is harder to put a dollar figure on — but it's often what sustains a program after the deadline passes. It means someone trusts a report number without double-checking it. The visible symptom of low trust: people quietly rebuilding their own version of a report because they don't trust the official one.

## S5 · STEPS — Value, the enabler pitch

Value-driven governance isn't "stop bad things" — it's "good data lets the business move faster." An AI initiative that actually works because its training data is trustworthy. A faster executive decision because the numbers aren't in dispute. This is the hardest driver to pitch upfront, but the one that turns governance into something people want more of.

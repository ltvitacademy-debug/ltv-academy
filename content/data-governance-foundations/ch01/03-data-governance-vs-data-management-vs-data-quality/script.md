# Lesson 3 — Data Governance vs. Data Management vs. Data Quality · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Three terms, used interchangeably in almost every meeting — and that's exactly where organizational dysfunction starts.

## S2 · STEPS — Three different jobs

Governance sets the rules — who decides, who's accountable. Management is the doing — acquiring, storing, moving, processing data day to day. Quality is the scorecard — how accurate and complete the data actually is, right now. The rulebook, the operation, and the scorecard. Not the same job.

## S3 · STEPS — How DAMA-DMBOK frames it

DAMA International's DMBOK puts governance at the literal center of its wheel diagram, with the other ten knowledge areas — including data quality management — arranged around it. That's deliberate: governance gives the others their authority, it isn't competing with them.

## S4 · STEPS — Why the confusion is expensive

Three real failure patterns. A "governance program" that's actually just a tooling purchase — a catalog gets bought, conflicting definitions persist, because nobody answered who decides. A quality fix with no teeth to stick organization-wide. And IT getting blamed for bad data when nobody ever defined what correct was supposed to look like.

## S5 · STEPS — The quick test

When a data problem shows up, ask: is this a disagreement about rules? A breakdown in day-to-day handling? Or a factual defect in the data itself? The answer tells you exactly who needs to be in the room.

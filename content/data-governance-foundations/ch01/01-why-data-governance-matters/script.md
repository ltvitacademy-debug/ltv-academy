# Lesson 1 — Why Data Governance Matters · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Welcome to Data Governance Foundations. This first lesson answers the question everyone skips past: why does any of this actually matter?

## S2 · STEPS — What governance actually is

Data governance is the system of decision rights, accountability, and rules over an organization's data — who can create it, change it, access it, and retire it, and what "good" is supposed to look like. It's not a tool, and it's not one team. It's the agreements that make sure two departments looking at "revenue" are looking at the same thing.

## S3 · STEPS — Three forces making it unavoidable

Three forces have made this a board-level topic. Data volume and sprawl — information scattered across SaaS tools, warehouses, and spreadsheets nobody fully inventories. Regulation — GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, with real financial penalties attached. And AI — models trained on ungoverned data inherit every inconsistency and repeat it at scale, automatically.

## S4 · STEPS — What it looks like without governance

Without governance, it rarely looks dramatic. It looks like three departments presenting three different customer counts in the same meeting. A shadow spreadsheet of "the real numbers" one analyst quietly maintains. A breach investigation that takes weeks because nobody can say where the data actually lives.

## S5 · STEPS — Where this course goes

This chapter stays conceptual — what governance is, what it costs to skip. Chapter two covers named frameworks and operating models. Chapter three covers roles: owners, stewards, councils. Chapter four covers policies and standards. Chapter five is about actually starting a program.

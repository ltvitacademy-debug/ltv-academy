# Lesson 19 — Data Policies · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Chapter 3 built the people and the structure. Chapter 4 covers what that structure actually
produces, starting with the document everything else in this chapter builds on: the data
policy.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what a policy answers)

A data policy provides high-level vision and direction — it answers what is required and
why. It deliberately does not answer how to implement that requirement. That second
question belongs to a standard, which the next lesson covers — policy and standard are a
deliberate pair, not two words for the same thing.

## S3 · CODE CARD (real policy statement)

Here's what that looks like in practice, for a bank's customer data: all customer financial
information is highly confidential and may only be accessed on a need-to-know basis for
legitimate business purposes. Notice what it doesn't say — no encryption algorithm, no
access request process. That's intentional.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (who writes, who approves)

Chapter 3's roles do the real work here. The owner typically proposes policy for their
domain, the steward drafts the detailed language, the governance committee formally approves
it — especially anything crossing domains — and the executive sponsor backs anything that
needs organization-wide or regulatory weight.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

A clear rule, a clear reason, and no implementation detail baked in. Next lesson covers
exactly where that detail goes: data standards. See you there.

# Lesson 7 — DAMA-DMBOK Overview · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Chapter Two starts with the field's most widely cited reference framework — DAMA International's DMBOK.

## S2 · STEPS — What DAMA and the DMBOK actually are

DAMA International is the global, vendor-neutral professional association for data management practitioners. The DMBOK — the Data Management Body of Knowledge — is its flagship publication, organizing the entire discipline into eleven knowledge areas, with governance deliberately at the center.

## S3 · SCREENSHOT — The DAMA Wheel

This is DAMA's own published wheel diagram. Data governance sits at the hub. The other ten knowledge areas are arranged as spokes around it. The placement isn't decorative — it's the framework's central argument: none of the other ten can be done well without governance supplying the decision rights and accountability that make them consistent.

## S4 · STEPS — The ten knowledge areas, briefly

Architecture, modeling and design, storage and operations, security, integration — moving data across systems. Document and content management, reference and master data — the single source of truth for things like customers. Warehousing and BI, metadata management, and data quality.

## S5 · STEPS — Why this matters even unadopted

Most organizations never implement DMBOK2 cover to cover — it's a reference encyclopedia, not a quick-start guide. What matters is its vocabulary became the field's shared language. When a job posting says "master data management," it's using DAMA's framing, whether the company's ever read the book or not.

# Script — Capstone: Wrap-Up & Portfolio Presentation

## Segment 1 (title)

Most people demoing a RAG project show the happy path only. What makes this capstone worth presenting is everything added on top — an abstention path firing correctly, a citation that actually traces to a real source, evaluation scores before and after a measured change.

## Segment 2 (steps: the portfolio sentence)

One sentence for a resume or an interview: a retrieval-augmented assistant with tuned chunking and re-ranking, grounded and cited prompt assembly, an honest abstention path, and a Ragas-based evaluation used to diagnose and fix a specific weakness — not just a demo that happened to work. Every clause in that sentence maps to a lesson you can speak to in detail.

## Segment 3 (code: six chapters, one assistant)

Nothing in this course was theoretical by the end. Chapter 1 became this assistant's architecture. Chapters 2 and 3 became the embedding model and where chunks live. Chapter 4 became the build itself. Chapter 5 became the evaluation metrics behind the tuning pass. Chapter 6 was all of it, built, measured, and presented together.

## Segment 4 (steps: what's next)

This course closes the RAG stage of the AI Engineer path. Next up: AI Agents — what happens when a model gets to decide for itself when to call a tool, including the retrieval tool from this course's agentic RAG lesson, hold for human approval, and operate inside real safety guardrails.

## Segment 5 (outro)

That's RAG and vector databases, start to finish — from why RAG exists, through embeddings and vector databases, to a measured, tuned, production-shaped assistant. Next course: AI Agents.

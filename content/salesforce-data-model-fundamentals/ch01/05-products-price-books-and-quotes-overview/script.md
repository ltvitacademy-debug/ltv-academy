# Script — Products, Price Books, and Quotes Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

Opportunities track deals, but a deal is usually about something specific being sold. This lesson covers the three objects behind that: Product, Price Book, and Quote.

## Segment 2 (steps: Product / Price Book / Quote)

Three layers. A Product is the thing being sold — a service, a physical item, a subscription tier. A Price Book is a price list a Product belongs to, because the same product can sell for different prices to different segments. A Quote is a formal, printable snapshot of a proposed sale for a specific customer.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Products list)

Products live on their own object, independent of any one deal. This is a real Product list — solar panels, installation services, support tiers — each one reusable across as many Opportunities as it gets added to.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Quote page layout selection)

A Quote isn't just a report — it's a real record with its own object, its own fields, and its own page layouts, the same layout-assignment mechanism you'll see on every standard object in this course.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: Quote Create PDF)

And a Quote is built to leave Salesforce. Create PDF turns its line items — product, quantity, price — into an actual document a rep can email straight to a customer.

## Segment 6 (outro)

Product for what's being sold, Price Book for what it costs, Quote for the paper trail. Next up: Activities — the Tasks and Events that record what reps are actually doing.

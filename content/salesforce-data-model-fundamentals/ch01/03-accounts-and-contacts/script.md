# Script — Accounts and Contacts

## Segment 1 (title)

Account and Contact are the first two standard objects in Salesforce Sales Cloud, and almost everything else in this course eventually points back to one or the other.

## Segment 2 (steps: Account vs Contact)

The split is simple. An Account is the company or organization — the business you have a relationship with. A Contact is a person tied to that Account — someone who works there, or represents it.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: New Account form)

Here's the New Account form. Dozens of fields are available — Type, Industry, Rating, Billing and Shipping Address — but Account Name is really the only one that's required to save.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Contact record)

And here's a Contact record: Alan Johnson, tied to the Get Cloudy Account. Look at the Related tab — Opportunities and Cases both show up directly on the Contact, not just the Account. A Contact sits at a real intersection of a company's people and its business.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: related contacts list)

One more wrinkle worth knowing: a Contact can relate to more than one Account. This list shows Alan's primary Account, Get Cloudy, alongside an indirect relationship — he's also a contact at Consulting Partners Southwest. That feature is called Contacts to Multiple Accounts, and it's off by default.

## Segment 6 (outro)

Account is the company, Contact is the person. Next up: Lead, Opportunity, and Case — the three objects that actually track what's happening with them.

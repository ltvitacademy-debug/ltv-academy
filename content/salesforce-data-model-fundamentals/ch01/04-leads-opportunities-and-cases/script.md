# Script — Leads, Opportunities, and Cases

## Segment 1 (title)

Lead, Opportunity, and Case are the three objects that actually track the sales and service process. Lesson 3 covered who the data is about. This lesson covers what's happening to them.

## Segment 2 (steps: Lead / Opportunity / Case)

Three jobs, three objects. A Lead is an unqualified prospect — not yet a customer. An Opportunity is a deal in progress, tracked toward a close. A Case is a customer service issue or request, usually tied to an existing Account.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Lead record)

Here's a Lead record: Aparna Bosu, status Working-Contacted. Notice there's no Account or Contact here yet — a Lead is deliberately separate from those objects until it's qualified. The Convert button, top right, is how that separation ends.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Convert Lead dialog)

Convert is the moment a Lead becomes real Sales Cloud data. One click creates an Account, a Contact, and — unless you opt out — an Opportunity, all pre-filled from whatever information the Lead already had.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: Opportunity closed stage)

An Opportunity doesn't just sit there — it moves through stages, shown as a path across the top of the record. This is the Closed stage being selected at the end of a deal, win or lose.

## Segment 6 (screenshot: Case change owner)

A Case works similarly on the service side. Here's a Case list view — Change Owner reassigns a case to a different support rep or a queue, which is how case volume actually gets distributed across a team.

## Segment 7 (outro)

Lead for prospects, Opportunity for deals, Case for service issues. Next up: what an Opportunity is actually selling — Products, Price Books, and Quotes.

# Script — Full Procure-to-Pay Walkthrough

## Segment 1 (title)

This lesson replays LTV Manufacturing's entire bearing transaction in one sitting, start to finish. Treat it as the reference you come back to any time you need the whole cycle in one place.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Dana submits a requisition for fifty bearings; it routes to her supervisor and gets approved. Marcus processes the approved line into a standard purchase order with Meridian, confirming payment terms and a three-way match level, and it's approved and sent through the Supplier Portal.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A truck arrives. Priya receives all fifty units against the purchase order, they're routed for inspection, and accepted in full. That instant, Receipt Accounting books a journal debiting maintenance expense and crediting an uninvoiced accrual.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Meridian invoices LTV. Chen matches it three ways against the purchase order and the receipt - clean, no hold - validates it, and accounts it, clearing the accrual into accounts payable. A payment run later pays Meridian, debiting payable and crediting cash.

## Segment 5 (outro)

With receiving and invoicing both complete, the purchase order closes out to Finally Closed. One requester, one buyer, one receiving clerk, one AP processor, five documents, three journals, all chained by reference.

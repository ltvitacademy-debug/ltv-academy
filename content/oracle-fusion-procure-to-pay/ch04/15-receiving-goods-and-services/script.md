# Script — Receiving Goods and Services

## Segment 1 (title)

A truck from Meridian arrives at LTV's dock. This is where Priya, the receiving clerk, takes over, and where paper purchasing turns into a physical, real-world event.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Receiving records that goods arrived, or a service was performed, against an open purchase order. It's a distinct event from the invoice - the receipt confirms physical reality, the invoice confirms a financial claim. Keeping them separate is exactly what makes three-way matching possible later.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Receiving goods is usually straightforward - a quantity shows up and gets recorded. Receiving a service works differently, since there's often nothing to count; instead, someone certifies the work was performed before invoicing can proceed. LTV's bearing order is a goods receipt, so that's the path this course follows.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Priya searches for the open purchase order and confirms the schedule, the quantity received - fifty, matching the packing slip - the receiving location at the dock, and the date. Saving this creates a brand new receipt number, referencing the purchase order it came from.

## Segment 5 (outro)

For simple, complete shipments, express receipt handles it in one action. For anything needing more scrutiny - like an inspection requirement - the full receiving flow is used instead, which is exactly LTV's situation here. Up next, lesson sixteen: receipt routing and inspection.

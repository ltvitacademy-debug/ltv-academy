# Script — Receipt Accounting and Accruals

## Segment 1 (title)

The bearings are received and accepted. Before Meridian's invoice ever shows up, the receipt itself already triggers accounting. Let's cover Receipt Accounting and what an accrual actually is.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A business that received goods owes money for them, whether or not the paperwork caught up. Receipt Accounting recognizes that obligation the moment of receipt, instead of waiting for an invoice that might arrive weeks later - otherwise a company would understate its liabilities at period end.

## Segment 3 (steps)

There are two approaches. Perpetual, or on-receipt, accrual creates the journal automatically the moment the receipt happens - required for every inventory item, no option to defer it. Period-end accrual only applies to expense items, and only waits until period end if no invoice has shown up yet.

## Segment 4 (steps)

LTV's bearing is an expense item, but it's configured to accrue on receipt anyway. So the moment Priya's receipt is accepted, the accrual journal is created automatically, just like an inventory item would behave.

## Segment 5 (outro)

That journal debits the maintenance expense account and credits an uninvoiced receipts accrual account - a real liability on LTV's books before any invoice exists. When Chen matches Meridian's invoice later, that accrual account gets cleared and replaced with accounts payable.

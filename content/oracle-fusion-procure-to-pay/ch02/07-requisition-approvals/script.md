# Script — Requisition Approvals

## Segment 1 (title)

Dana's requisition is now pending approval. Let's cover what that actually means - who approves it, why, and what happens if nobody does.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Oracle Fusion routes approvals through a BPM-based approval engine, built from configurable rules rather than one hard-coded approver. Rules can consider the requested amount, the item category, the business unit, and the requester's position in the supervisory hierarchy.

## Segment 3 (steps)

For Dana's requisition, the amount is modest and the category is routine MRO maintenance parts, so it routes to just her direct supervisor. A larger purchase, or an unusual category, could add a second approver, like a plant finance manager, before it's allowed to proceed.

## Segment 4 (steps)

While it's pending, Dana can see the status and whose queue it's sitting in, but she can't push it forward herself. This is exactly the scenario from lesson two - a requisition that looks stuck is almost always just waiting on someone else's approval.

## Segment 5 (outro)

If approved, it moves on to become a purchase order. If rejected, it goes back to Dana with a reason, and resubmitting restarts the routing. Up next, lesson eight: how the approved requisition's accounting distribution is actually determined.

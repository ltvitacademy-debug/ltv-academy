# Script — Purchase Order Types and Purchase Agreements

## Segment 1 (title)

Marcus has a draft document referencing Dana's requisition line. Before building it out, we need to know which of Oracle Fusion's purchasing document types it actually is, and why that choice matters.

## Segment 2 (steps)

There are three main types. A standard purchase order is for a one-time, fully defined need - known item, quantity, price, delivery date. A blanket purchase agreement locks in pricing with a supplier over time, without a known schedule, fulfilled later by releases. A contract purchase agreement covers longer-term legal terms, with or without specific items.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Dana's order is a known item, a known quantity, a known price, and a known delivery date - that's exactly the profile of a standard purchase order. If LTV instead ran high recurring volumes against a negotiated agreement, Marcus might issue a release against a blanket agreement instead.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Companies often use all of these together. A contract agreement sets master legal terms. A blanket agreement locks in pricing for a part number over the year. Releases or standard purchase orders fulfill actual delivery needs as they come up.

# Script — Purchase Order Approvals and Communication

## Segment 1 (title)

Marcus's purchase order is drafted. Two things still have to happen before Meridian knows anything about it: internal approval, and then actually communicating the order to the supplier.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Purchase orders route through the same approval engine as requisitions, but they're not redundant. The requisition approval confirmed Dana's department should spend the money. The purchase order approval confirms the specific supplier, price, and terms Marcus negotiated are acceptable - often approved by a procurement manager, not Marcus himself.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Once approved, the order still has to reach Meridian. Oracle Fusion supports email, fax, the Supplier Portal, or structured electronic exchange like cXML. Meridian is set up on the Supplier Portal, so once approved, their staff can see the order directly online.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Many companies also use supplier acknowledgement - the supplier confirms inside the portal that they received the order and accept its terms. It's not required for validity, but it's documented proof the supplier agreed to the quantity, price, and dates.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Up to now, nothing outside LTV's own system has been affected. Once this order is approved and sent, Meridian has a binding order to fulfill - and the next event is a truck arriving at LTV's dock, which is where chapter four picks up. Up next, lesson thirteen: what happens when this purchase order needs to change.

# Script — Procurement Setup Review: Business Units and Agents

## Segment 1 (title)

You configured business units back in Enterprise Structures. This is a quick review, not new setup - just the pieces that make procurement possible, so you understand why things default the way they will later in this course.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Two BU roles matter here. A requisitioning BU is the business unit the requester belongs to - the plant that needs the bearings. A procurement BU is the one that actually owns purchasing: negotiating with suppliers and issuing purchase orders. One procurement BU can serve several requisitioning BUs when a company centralizes buying.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A business unit doesn't get to issue purchase orders just by existing. It has to be assigned the Procurement business function - one of several standard business functions in Oracle Fusion. That assignment is what turns an ordinary BU into one capable of owning purchasing documents.

## Segment 4 (steps)

A procurement agent is a specific person authorized to act as a buyer for a given procurement BU. That assignment is what actually lets Marcus create and manage purchase orders - not just his job role. Agent assignments also carry document access levels, which is how a procurement manager supervises a team of buyers.

# Script — Following One Transaction: The Plan

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the last lesson before the transaction actually starts moving. Here's the complete map of where LTV Manufacturing's bearing purchase goes, lesson by lesson, so you always know where you are in the cycle.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Here's the transaction in full. Dana submits a requisition for fifty pump bearings. Marcus turns the approved requisition into a purchase order with Meridian Bearing Supply Co. Priya receives the shipment at the dock. Meridian invoices LTV, Chen matches and validates it, and the accounting flows into the General Ledger.

## Segment 3 (steps)

That maps directly onto the chapters ahead. Chapter two is requisitions. Chapter three is purchase orders, including change orders and closing. Chapter four is receiving, including returns and accruals. Chapter five is invoice to ledger, including reconciliation. Chapter six replays the whole thing and adds exception scenarios.

## Segment 4 (steps)

A handful of numbers connect every document: the requisition number, the purchase order number, the receipt number, and the invoice number. Each one references the one before it. Keep an eye on the quantity too - fifty units at one agreed price - because exception scenarios later will deliberately change it.

## Segment 5 (outro)

At each stage there's a clean expected outcome, and chapter six will deliberately break several of them so you can practice spotting it. Up next, lesson five: Dana's requisition actually begins, starting with Self Service Procurement.

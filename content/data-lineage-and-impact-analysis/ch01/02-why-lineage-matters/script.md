Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Lineage isn't bookkeeping for its own sake. This lesson covers four
concrete business reasons organizations invest in it, and what actually
breaks, in practical terms, when it doesn't exist.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (four reasons)

Trust — when a number looks wrong, lineage answers where it came from
instead of "nobody fully knows." Impact analysis — before changing a
column, you know exactly what downstream reports depend on it. Root
cause analysis — you walk backward to the exact hop where things
diverged. Audit requirements — regulated industries need to prove how a
figure was calculated, from source to final number.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (what breaks without it)

Picture a revenue number that drops unexpectedly. Without lineage, three
people independently check three systems, duplicating effort, because
nobody has a map of what feeds that number. With lineage, the team opens
the record, sees every hop, and checks each one in order. The
investigation becomes mechanical instead of exploratory.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (connection to prior course)

Metadata Management closed on giving every concept one definition and
one home. Lineage extends that from identity to movement — proving every
report pulling that concept is pulling it correctly, through every hop,
rather than silently drifting onto a stale copy somewhere else.

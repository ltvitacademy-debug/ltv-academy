# Lesson 11 — Match Review and Stewardship · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

The review band from Lesson 7 needs a human because getting it wrong in
either direction carries a real cost — this lesson is what a steward
actually does about that.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (two ways to get it wrong)

A false positive merges two different people — one customer's data gets
contaminated with a stranger's history. A false negative leaves a true
duplicate unmerged — quieter, but it defeats the whole point of MDM for
that record. A human steward, with context an algorithm doesn't have, is
the right tool for the uncertain middle band.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the steward's job)

Confirm or reject the candidate pair based on the evidence. Fix the
source data when the real problem turns out to be a typo, not the match
logic. Tune the rules — a pattern in how reviews land is feedback that
improves the automated matching over time.

## S4 · CODE CARD (the review workflow)

A pair lands in the queue because its score fell between the two
thresholds. The steward decides: merge, reject, or needs more data. That
decision resolves the case and becomes a labeled example for future rule
tuning.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

That closes Chapter 2 — matching and consolidation. Chapter 3 moves from
mechanics to domains: customer master, product master, vendor master,
and more, starting next.

# Lesson 9 — Golden Records · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

A golden record is the single, trusted, best-available version of a
master data entity — the one answer everything else gets measured
against.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what it's not)

It's not just picking your most trusted system's record and ignoring the
rest — that's privileging one incomplete source over others. It's not a
copy of any single original. And it's not static — it updates as source
systems change.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (how it's built)

Once matching has found the records that refer to the same entity,
building the golden record means deciding, attribute by attribute, which
source's value is most trustworthy. Phone from the CRM, billing address
from the ERP, email from whichever system confirmed it most recently.

## S4 · CODE CARD (assembled, field by field)

The result is often more complete than any single source. Phone:
sourced from the CRM. Address: sourced from the ERP. Email: sourced from
whichever system is freshest. One merged row, built from three partial
ones.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Next: survivorship rules — the explicit, documented logic that decides
which source wins for each attribute, instead of leaving it to
guesswork.

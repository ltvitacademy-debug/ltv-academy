# Lesson 13 — Product Master · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Product master: the authoritative record of everything an organization
sells or stocks — and a domain with its own tricky distinction between a
product and a specific sellable variant.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what a record holds)

A usable product record holds a unique ID, unit of measure, dimensions
and weight, a global trade identifier like a GTIN, lifecycle status, and
a classification. None of it changes transaction by transaction — that's
what makes it master data.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (product vs SKU)

A product is the conceptual item — "Men's Crew Socks, Size M." A SKU is a
specific sellable variant — one color, one pack size — with its own
barcode and inventory count. Roll everything up to the product level only,
and a warehouse can't tell which barcode to pick off the shelf.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (classification hierarchy)

Products sit in a hierarchy: brand, category, subcategory, then the
product and its SKUs. That hierarchy drives e-commerce navigation,
reporting roll-ups, and even tax rules that apply at the category level —
which is why reorganizing it is a governed change.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (where it bites)

Inconsistent product data shows up as different descriptions across the
website, the store catalog, and a marketplace feed — which can trigger
compliance penalties. A mismatched unit of measure between systems can
turn an order for ten cases into ten units.

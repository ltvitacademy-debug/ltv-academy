# Lesson 14 — Vendor Master · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Vendor master: the record of every external party an organization pays —
and the one master domain that sits closest to financial fraud controls.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what a record holds)

A vendor record carries a unique ID, legal name and tax ID, remittance
versus physical address, banking details, payment terms, and status.
Banking and payment data is why vendor master sits closer to financial
controls than other master domains.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (fraud prevention)

Duplicate vendor fraud: someone creates a second record for a real
supplier with different banking details, then redirects a payment there.
Strict duplicate checking and change control on bank details is the
primary defense — and sanctions screening against watch lists happens
right at record creation.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (onboarding as a gate)

Vendor onboarding collects and verifies a new supplier's information —
tax ID, banking, sanctions screening — before the record goes live for
purchasing. Treating that as a gate, not something any employee can
bypass ad hoc, is what makes the fraud controls actually work.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (segmentation)

Not every vendor needs the same scrutiny. A strategic supplier gets a
dedicated owner and performance scorecards; a one-time office-supply
vendor gets a lighter record. The segment itself should live in the
master data so downstream workflows apply different rules automatically.

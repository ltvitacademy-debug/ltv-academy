# Lesson 4 — The MDM Business Case · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

MDM programs get funded because bad master data is already costing the
business in ways people can point to — not because the topic is
interesting on its own.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (cost of doing nothing)

Duplicate outreach, mailing the same customer three times. Mismatched
records that block automation, forcing someone to resolve them by hand
forever. Inconsistent reporting, where two departments can't even agree
on revenue per customer, so leadership stops trusting either number.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (drivers that get it funded)

A single trusted view of the customer is the pitch that lands broadest.
Mergers and acquisitions almost always force two separate customer and
product sets together. Regulatory pressure often requires a
de-duplicated view just to demonstrate compliance.

## S4 · CODE CARD (before and after)

Before: three customer records, three different phone numbers, nobody
sure which is current. After: one golden record — the subject of
Chapter 2 — built by matching and merging those three down to one
trusted answer.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Next: MDM governance — the roles and policies that keep a program like
this from drifting back into chaos once it's funded and running.

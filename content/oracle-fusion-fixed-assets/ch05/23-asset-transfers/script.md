# Lesson 23 — Asset Transfers · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Welcome to Chapter 5. This lesson covers transfers — moving an asset's where, without touching its what.

## S2 · STEPS — What stays the same

A transfer moves an asset physically, organizationally, or both, without changing its cost or category. The asset, its method, and its life stay exactly as they were.

## S3 · STEPS — Three kinds of transfer

Location transfer moves it physically and updates the location flexfield. Assignment transfer reassigns it to a different employee. Distribution transfer changes which cost center bears the depreciation expense.

## S4 · STEPS — Transfers and distributions

A transfer can apply to a whole asset or to just one distribution. If only part of a shared machine's expense needs to move to another department, that's a partial distribution transfer, not a full asset transfer.

## S5 · STEPS — Why accuracy matters

Location data drives property tax and physical audits. A transfer that's never recorded means the books no longer match reality — exactly the gap a physical inventory is designed to catch.

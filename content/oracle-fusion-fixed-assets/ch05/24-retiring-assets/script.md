# Lesson 24 — Retiring Assets · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This is the last lifecycle stage. This lesson covers retirement — when an asset permanently leaves service.

## S2 · STEPS — Retiring by cost or by units

When an asset has multiple units, a retirement can target a specific dollar amount, or a specific count of units — letting Oracle Fusion Assets calculate the proportional cost automatically.

## S3 · CODE — A worked example

Meridian has twenty identical shelving units at five hundred dollars each, ten thousand total. Five are damaged and scrapped. Retiring by units — five of twenty — calculates twenty-five hundred dollars of cost retired automatically.

## S4 · STEPS — Full versus partial

A full retirement takes the entire asset out of service, with no further depreciation. A partial retirement takes only a portion out — the remaining units keep depreciating on their reduced cost basis.

## S5 · STEPS — What the transaction captures

Retirement date, proceeds of sale, cost of removal, and a retirement type or reason. None of this is just reference data — proceeds and cost of removal feed directly into the gain and loss calculation ahead.

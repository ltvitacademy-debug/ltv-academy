# Lesson 27 — Mass Transactions · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Real events rarely touch just one asset. This lesson covers applying transfers, retirements, and reclassifications across many assets at once.

## S2 · STEPS — Why one at a time doesn't scale

A warehouse closes, a product line discontinues, a company restructures — these affect dozens or hundreds of assets simultaneously. Doing each individually is slow, and risks inconsistency between assets that should be treated identically.

## S3 · STEPS — The main mass transaction types

Mass Transfers move a group to a new location or distribution. Mass Retirements retire a group together. Mass Reclassifications move a group between categories. Mass Changes update category defaults and optionally apply them retroactively.

## S4 · STEPS — How assets get selected

A mass transaction works from selection criteria — category, location, cost center, date range — producing a candidate list for review before anything commits.

## S5 · STEPS — Review matters more at scale

A mistake in the selection criteria is a mistake multiplied by every asset that matched it. That's exactly why the full candidate list gets reviewed before final submission — the same principle as the mass additions queue, just at bulk scale.

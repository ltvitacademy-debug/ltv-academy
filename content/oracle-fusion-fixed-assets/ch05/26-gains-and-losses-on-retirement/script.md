# Lesson 26 — Gains and Losses on Retirement · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Every retirement runs through the same formula. This lesson covers gains and losses — the real financial consequence of how an asset was depreciated.

## S2 · CODE — The formula

Gain or loss equals proceeds of sale, minus cost of removal, minus net book value. A positive result is a gain. A negative result is a loss.

## S3 · CODE — Working an example

Meridian sells a delivery van: forty-five thousand cost, thirty-eight thousand accumulated depreciation, seven thousand net book value. Proceeds of nine thousand five hundred, minus three hundred removal cost, minus seven thousand net book value — a twenty-two-hundred-dollar gain.

## S4 · STEPS — When net book value is already zero

A fully depreciated asset scrapped for nothing produces zero gain or loss. But sold for even a small amount, that entire amount becomes a gain, since there's no remaining book value left to offset it.

## S5 · STEPS — Why this number matters

Every depreciation method, every cost adjustment, every reclassification along the way determined the net book value at retirement — the single biggest input to whether the result is a gain or a loss.

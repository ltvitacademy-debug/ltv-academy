# Lesson 6 — Depreciation Methods · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Welcome to Chapter 2: Depreciation Setup. This lesson covers the depreciation methods Oracle Fusion Assets can calculate, and the math behind the two you'll see most often.

## S2 · STEPS — Four method types

Oracle Fusion Assets groups methods into four types. Calculated, or straight-line, spreads cost evenly. Table-based looks up a published rate, the way many tax schedules work. Flat-rate applies a percentage to cost or net book value. Units of production ties depreciation to actual usage instead of time.

## S3 · CODE — The straight-line formula

Here's the formula worked through an example. Meridian Fabrication Co.'s CNC machine cost one hundred twenty thousand dollars, with a twenty-thousand-dollar salvage value and a ten-year life. Subtract salvage from cost, divide by life, and you get ten thousand dollars a year. All figures are illustrative.

## S4 · STEPS — Flat-rate produces a different shape

Apply a flat twenty percent rate to net book value instead, and the pattern changes completely. Year one takes twenty percent of one hundred twenty thousand — twenty-four thousand. Year two takes twenty percent of the now-lower balance — nineteen thousand two hundred. The charge shrinks every year instead of staying level.

## S5 · STEPS — Match the method to reality

An office building loses value steadily — straight-line fits. A vehicle under an accelerated tax schedule needs a table-based or flat-rate method. A mining excavator whose wear tracks tonnage, not time, needs units of production.

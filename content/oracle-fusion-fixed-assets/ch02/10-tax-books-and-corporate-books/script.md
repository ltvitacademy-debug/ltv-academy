# Lesson 10 — Tax Books and Corporate Books · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

One real asset, two depreciation answers. This lesson covers how corporate and tax books stay in sync without double data entry.

## S2 · STEPS — Two books, one asset

A corporate book depreciates for financial statements. A tax book depreciates the same assets for tax filing, often with accelerated rules like MACRS. Neither is wrong — they answer different questions.

## S3 · STEPS — Mass copy populates the tax book

Instead of manual re-entry, mass copy runs periodically and copies new additions, and often later transactions, from the corporate book into the tax book — using the tax book's own category book defaults, not the corporate book's.

## S4 · STEPS — What can differ, what can't

Method, life, convention, and bonus depreciation are expected to differ between the two books. What stays the same is the underlying fact pattern — the asset's existence, its cost, and its date placed in service. How fast it depreciates is where the books diverge.

## S5 · STEPS — Staying in sync over time

Mass copy isn't a one-time event. Every period, new additions and later adjustments, transfers, and retirements in the corporate book get propagated into the tax book so its asset population never drifts.

# Lesson 14 — Preparing Mass Additions · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

A mass addition line isn't an asset yet — it's a candidate waiting for a decision. This lesson covers how that decision actually gets made.

## S2 · STEPS — Queue statuses

Every line carries a queue status. New, just arrived. On hold, flagged for review. Post, ready to become an asset. Merged, combined into an existing asset. Split, divided into multiple lines. Delete, rejected outright.

## S3 · STEPS — The real questions being answered

Is this a new asset or additional cost on an existing one? Does the category need correcting? Does one invoice line actually cover several physical assets? Is the date placed in service actually right?

## S4 · CODE — A worked example

Meridian gets a ninety-thousand-dollar line described as three forklifts. That's one line covering three physical assets — it needs to be split into three thirty-thousand-dollar lines before each forklift can post as its own numbered asset.

## S5 · STEPS — Why this review matters

Create Mass Additions is mechanical — it just follows account coding. Prepare Mass Additions is where a person applies judgment: catching a miscoded expense, splitting a bundled invoice, setting the category correctly before depreciation ever calculates.

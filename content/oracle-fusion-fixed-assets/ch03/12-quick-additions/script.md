# Lesson 12 — Quick Additions · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Most assets are completely routine. This lesson covers Quick Additions — the fast path built for exactly that case.

## S2 · STEPS — A deliberately smaller form

Quick Additions asks for only what can't be defaulted: description, category, cost, units, date placed in service, and book. Everything else — method, life, convention, GL accounts — comes straight from the category book with no per-asset review screen.

## S3 · STEPS — It leans on category design

Quick Additions is only as good as the categories behind it. Well-designed categories mean it handles routine additions correctly every time. Poorly designed categories mean wrong defaults slip through silently, because those fields are never shown.

## S4 · STEPS — When it's the wrong tool

Quick Additions is wrong whenever the asset needs a non-default method, life, or account; needs multiple distributions at addition; or needs a location or key flexfield value Quick Additions doesn't expose. Go back to manual addition instead.

## S5 · STEPS — A worked scenario

Twelve identical laptops, same cost, same day, same well-established category — a textbook Quick Additions case. But if three of them need a different cost center than the other nine, that's a distribution the Quick Additions screen can't set up, so it's back to manual addition.

# Lesson 29 — Fixed Assets to General Ledger Transfer · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Create Accounting isn't the last step. This lesson covers what actually gets journal entries from Assets into the General Ledger.

## S2 · STEPS — One more step after Create Accounting

Journal entries exist inside Assets until the Fixed Assets to GL transfer moves them into GL's journal batches — sometimes automatic with final accounting, sometimes its own scheduled process.

## S3 · STEPS — Transfer versus post

Transfer moves the data into GL as an unposted batch — it exists there, but hasn't updated balances yet. Post is the separate GL action that actually updates account balances. This two-step design gives GL its own review checkpoint.

## S4 · STEPS — Why frequency matters

Transfer only at period end, and GL's fixed-asset balances lag behind Assets all month. Transfer more frequently, and GL stays closer to real time, at the cost of more processing. It's a deliberate tradeoff, not a universal right answer.

## S5 · STEPS — When something looks out of balance

Before assuming an error, check timing first: is there a batch transferred but not posted in GL, or accounted but not yet transferred from Assets? That's very often the whole explanation.

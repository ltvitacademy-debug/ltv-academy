# Lesson 30 — Fixed Assets Reports · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Almost every lesson in this course raised a question. This lesson covers the reports that actually answer them.

## S2 · STEPS — Reports tied to earlier chapters

Asset additions confirms nothing fell through the cracks. Cost detail shows every adjustment and reclass. The reserve ledger ties to depreciation runs. The CIP report shows what's still uncapitalized. Fully reserved and retirements reports close the loop on Chapter 5.

## S3 · STEPS — The forward-looking exception

Every report so far describes what already happened. Depreciation projection forecasts future depreciation for budgeting, based on current setup, without running or committing anything.

## S4 · STEPS — Picking the right report

Did everything post? Additions report. Why did cost change? Cost detail. How much reserve does this category carry? Reserve ledger. What's next year's expense? Projection.

## S5 · STEPS — Why reports drive review

Nobody reviews thousands of individual asset records one at a time. Reports are structured output built for a specific question — the same idea behind exception reports and mass addition queues from earlier chapters.

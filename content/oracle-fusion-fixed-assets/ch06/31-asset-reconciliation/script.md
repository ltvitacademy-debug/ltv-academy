# Lesson 31 — Asset Reconciliation · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Does the detail in Assets actually agree with the summary in GL? This lesson covers reconciliation — confirming exactly that.

## S2 · STEPS — The standard approach

Compare two things for the same account and period: the Assets subledger balance from its reports, and the posted GL account balance. If they don't match, something in the chain needs investigating.

## S3 · STEPS — The most common causes

Timing — a batch not yet transferred or posted. Manual GL entries bypassing Assets entirely. A failed Create Accounting run that never turned a transaction into a journal entry. Incorrect account mapping sending entries to the wrong place.

## S4 · STEPS — Check timing first

Timing is usually the fastest path to an answer, because it resolves itself — a batch catches up on its own. The other three causes need an actual fix, not just patience.

## S5 · STEPS — A recurring discipline

Reconciliation happens every period, as part of closing, because new transactions happen every period too. Catch small discrepancies early, and they're easy to trace. Let them accumulate, and they become genuinely hard to unwind.

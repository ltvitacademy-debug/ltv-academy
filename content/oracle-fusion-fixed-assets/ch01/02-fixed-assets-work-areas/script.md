# Lesson 2 — Fixed Assets Work Areas · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Before we touch a single asset, let's get oriented. This lesson covers the work areas you'll actually navigate in Oracle Fusion Assets, and why your role decides what you see.

## S2 · STEPS — Work areas, not one big menu

Oracle Fusion organizes tasks into work areas: role-based landing pages with just the tasks a job needs, not one flat menu of everything the system can do. Two matter most here: Fixed Assets, for day-to-day transactions, and Setup and Maintenance, for configuration.

## S3 · STEPS — Inside the Fixed Assets work area

The Fixed Assets work area groups tasks around the lifecycle. Mass additions tasks bridge Payables and Assets. Transaction tasks cover adding, adjusting, transferring, and retiring. Periodic processing covers depreciation and period close. And reporting ties it all together.

## S4 · STEPS — Scheduled processes

Most of these tasks have a scheduled-process twin — the same job run automatically on a recurring schedule instead of a person clicking a button. Depreciation is the classic example: often scheduled to run at period end rather than triggered manually every time.

## S5 · STEPS — Roles decide what you see

Job roles like Asset Accountant and Asset Accounting Manager each carry a different set of privileges. This isn't just convenience — it's segregation of duties. The person adding an asset isn't necessarily the person who can close the period that locks it in.

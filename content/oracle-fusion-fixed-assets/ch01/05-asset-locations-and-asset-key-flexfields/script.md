# Lesson 5 — Asset Locations and Asset Key Flexfields · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Category tells you what an asset is. This lesson covers the two structures that tell you where it is, and how else you might need to group it.

## S2 · STEPS — Location: where it physically sits

Every asset carries a location, built from a hierarchical flexfield — country, state, city, building, floor. A delivery van's location might be a city depot; a server's might drill down to a specific data center rack.

## S3 · STEPS — Why location matters

Location drives two real business needs. Property tax reporting, since many jurisdictions assess tax based on where equipment physically sits. And physical inventory audits, where someone has to confirm the assets on the books are actually where the books say.

## S4 · STEPS — Asset key flexfields

A key flexfield is a separate, optional structure identifying assets by something other than category or location — a project, a grant, an internal tag scheme. Not every company needs one; it exists for whatever question category and location can't answer.

## S5 · STEPS — A one-time decision

Both flexfields are configured with a fixed segment structure before assets start referencing them. Changing that structure after thousands of assets are already tagged is disruptive, so this is worth getting right from real requirements upfront.

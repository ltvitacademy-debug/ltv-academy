# Lesson 1 — Fixed Assets Concepts and the Asset Lifecycle · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Welcome to Oracle Fusion Fixed Assets. This lesson answers the question everything else builds on: what is a fixed asset, and what happens to it from the day it's bought to the day it's gone?

## S2 · STEPS — What makes something a fixed asset

A fixed asset is property a company uses in its own operations for more than one period, so its cost isn't expensed all at once — it's capitalized, then recognized gradually through depreciation. Every company sets a capitalization threshold: below it, a purchase is just an expense, no matter how long it lasts.

## S3 · STEPS — The five-stage lifecycle

Every asset moves through the same five stages. Addition, when it's first recorded. Capitalization, when it's placed in service and depreciation starts. Depreciation itself, period after period. Adjustments and transfers while it's in use. And retirement, when it leaves service for good.

## S4 · STEPS — Key vocabulary

Three terms you'll see constantly. Cost is what you paid, capitalized. Accumulated depreciation is the running total expensed so far. Net book value is cost minus accumulated depreciation — what's left.

## S5 · STEPS — Where Assets fits in Fusion Financials

A supplier invoice gets entered in Payables. If it's coded to an asset clearing account, it becomes a candidate for an asset addition. Assets depreciates it every period, then Create Accounting sends summarized journal entries to the General Ledger. Assets is the subledger that holds all the detail GL never sees.

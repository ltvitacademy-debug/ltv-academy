# Script — Full Order-to-Cash Walkthrough

## Segment 1 (title)

This lesson is a deliberate full recap of SO-48217, start to finish, every number in one place. If any stage felt fuzzy earlier, use this one as your reference.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Chapter one set up LTV Manufacturing Corporation and Harborview Industrial Supply. Chapter two: four hundred units at one forty-five list, a five percent volume discount down to fifty-five thousand, one hundred dollars net, a credit hold triggered by size, released after review. Chapter three: picked, packed, shipped, confirmed - then twenty units came back damaged and got an RMA.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Chapter four: the shipment staged into Receivables, got rejected once for a missing remit-to address, got corrected, and created a fifty-five thousand, one hundred dollar invoice. The RMA generated a two thousand, seven hundred fifty-five dollar credit memo, dropping the balance to fifty-two thousand, three hundred forty-five dollars.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Chapter five: Harborview paid that exact amount through lockbox, applied automatically. Three journal entries came out of this - the invoice, the credit memo, and the receipt - and all three transferred and posted to the General Ledger. Every checkpoint reconciled.

## Segment 5 (outro)

The specific numbers are this fictional example's. The shape is what to carry forward: price and validate, fulfill and sometimes unwind, bill and catch your own exceptions, collect and prove it all ties out. Up next, lesson twenty-four: new exception scenarios to test that shape on situations you haven't seen before.

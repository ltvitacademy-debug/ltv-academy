# Script — Order-to-Cash Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

Welcome to Oracle Fusion Order-to-Cash, part of the End-to-End Business Processes stage of the Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant path. You've already followed a purchase through Procure-to-Pay. Now we follow the opposite side: a sale, from the order to the cash in the bank.

## Segment 2 (steps)

One sales order can touch five different areas before it's finished. Order Management captures the order and decides if it's allowed to proceed. Inventory and Shipping get the goods out the door. Receivables turns the shipment into an invoice. Cash Management matches the customer's payment. And the General Ledger, through Subledger Accounting, records the revenue and the cash.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Why call this a cycle instead of a transaction? Because what happens at the end of one order shapes the next one. A customer's payment history affects whether their next order clears credit check. An unresolved dispute can delay the next invoice. Order-to-Cash loops back on itself; it doesn't just run in a straight line.

## Segment 4 (steps)

By now you know General Ledger, Payables and Receivables, and you've already walked a purchase end to end in Procure-to-Pay. This course assumes that background. What's new here is Order Management — the piece that decides what gets invoiced in the first place.

# Script — The Customer Lifecycle and Roles

## Segment 1 (title)

A sales order doesn't move itself through the system. At every stage, a specific role is responsible for an action, in a specific screen. Let's lay out the whole customer lifecycle before we zoom into any one transaction.

## Segment 2 (steps)

There are six stages. Order capture, where a request becomes a sales order. Order validation, where credit checks and approvals decide if it can proceed. Fulfillment, where the warehouse ships the goods. Billing, where the shipment becomes an invoice. Collection, where the customer's payment is matched to that invoice. And reconciliation, where the accounting is verified against the General Ledger.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Each stage has an owner. Order administration or inside sales handles capture. Credit and collections analysts, sometimes with a manager's approval, handle validation. Warehouse and shipping staff handle fulfillment. A billing specialist handles billing, almost always through an automated process rather than typing invoices by hand. And an accountant handles the final reconciliation.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Here's why that matters to you. Most real Order-to-Cash problems happen at the handoffs between people, not inside one screen. A warehouse worker ships a quantity the order didn't authorize. A billing specialist runs the invoice process too early. A collections analyst applies a payment to the wrong invoice. Your first troubleshooting question should be "whose stage was this in," not "which table holds this field."

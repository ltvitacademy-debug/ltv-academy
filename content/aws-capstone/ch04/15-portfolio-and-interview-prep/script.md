# Script — Portfolio & Interview Prep

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the last lesson of AWS Capstone, and the last lesson of the entire AWS Data Engineer career path. Everything converges here: writing this project up to get you the interview, and prepping for the questions it's going to draw once you're in the room.

## Segment 2 (code: the resume bullet)

A strong project bullet names the real architecture and a real before-and-after outcome, plus proof of production maturity — CI/CD, least-privilege IAM, a documented disaster recovery plan — not just a list of service names.

## Segment 3 (steps: interview question bank)

Every likely question traces back to a real decision you made in this course: why Redshift Serverless over a cluster, how pipeline failures get caught, what the disaster recovery plan actually is — each with a specific, defensible answer, not a guess.

## Segment 4 (code: both stages, complete)

This closes out both stages of the AWS Data Engineer path — the Job Ready stage that taught every individual AWS service, and the Advanced stage, this capstone, where every one of those services got assembled into one monitored, secured, cost-optimized project.

## Segment 5 (outro)

You now have both halves of what an interview tests: fluency with the individual AWS services, and a finished, defensible project built with them. That's the entire AWS Data Engineer career path, both stages, done.

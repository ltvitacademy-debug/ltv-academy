# Script — Welcome to Snowflake: Course Overview & What You'll Build

## Segment 1 (title)

This is a focused specialization, not a mega-course. It assumes you already know SQL — joins, CTEs, indexes, fact and dimension tables — from T-SQL Development. Every lesson answers one question: how does the thing you already know how to do get done in Snowflake specifically?

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Snowsight worksheet)

Nearly everything happens inside Snowsight, Snowflake's web interface — a SQL worksheet, a tree of databases and schemas, and a role and warehouse you actively choose for every query. Coming from SSMS, the role you're running as and the warehouse powering the query are both explicit and switchable right here, not buried in a settings menu.

## Segment 3 (steps: the four groups)

This course moves through four groups: foundations and SQL differences, getting data in and shaping it, running it like production with security and performance, then connecting it to Power BI and the rest of the modern data stack.

## Segment 4 (steps: the capstone)

Chapter 15 isn't a quiz — it's a real pipeline: raw data loaded into Snowflake, transformed through staging, warehouse, and reporting layers, modeled dimensionally, secured with RBAC, and connected to Power BI. Everything before it exists to make that capstone possible.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Next lesson: Snowflake's architecture — storage, compute, and cloud services, and why that separation is the single most important thing to understand before writing a line of SQL.

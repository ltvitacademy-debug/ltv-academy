# Lesson 30 — Presenting Governance Architecture · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This is the course's final lesson. Building a sound architecture is one skill. Getting people to understand and fund it is a different one — and it's where good designs most often fail.

## S2 · STEPS — Know your audience

The architecture doesn't change; what you lead with does. Executives want outcome, risk, and cost in the first thirty seconds, and the decision you need from them. Technical peers want the actual design reasoning and the ADRs. Operational teams and stewards want to know what changes for them specifically, day to day.

## S3 · STEPS — A structure that works

Whatever the audience, one order rarely fails: problem, stated concretely like Castellan's month of manual reconciliation. Impact, in terms the audience already cares about. Recommendation, the architecture decision, plainly stated. And an ask — the specific thing you need from this room right now.

## S4 · STEPS — Common mistakes

Leading with the diagram instead of the problem. One presentation for every audience instead of reordering for each. Ending with no specific ask. And jargon like "federated operating model" used without ever translating it into plain language first.

# Lesson 29 — Governance Architecture Practice Lab · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson is entirely hands-on. You'll run the same design sequence Castellan and Northbridge went through — on a scenario of your own.

## S2 · STEPS — Choose your scenario

Pick one before starting: your own employer, scoped to one real domain, or a plausible fictional organization, clearly labeled fictional, in a different industry than either case study. Write down a few concrete facts first — how many systems, what's driving the need, and who would plausibly sponsor it.

## S3 · STEPS — The seven-step checklist

Work through seven steps in order, each tied to an earlier lesson: a capabilities snapshot, an operating model choice with a reason, a metadata and catalog shape, a security and platform decision worth an ADR, a one-sentence strategy anchor, a Now-slash-Next roadmap sketch, and a review board charter.

## S4 · STEPS — What a complete package looks like

A finished exercise reads like a shorter Castellan or Northbridge: a named scenario, a chosen model with a reason, a real ADR, a roadmap with at least one quick win, and a review board charter — not course definitions restated with no scenario attached.

## S5 · OUTRO

Final lesson of the course: taking whatever you just built and actually presenting it to stakeholders — Presenting Governance Architecture.

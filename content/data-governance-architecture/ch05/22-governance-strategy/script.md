# Lesson 22 — Governance Strategy · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Welcome to Chapter Five, Governance Strategy. The first four chapters designed the architecture. This chapter is about the plan to actually build it.

## S2 · STEPS — Architecture is the design, strategy is the plan

A strategy is a different document than the architecture. The architecture describes the target state and its principles. The strategy describes the path: what gets built first, who pays, who's accountable, and what success looks like a year from now.

## S3 · STEPS — Four questions a real strategy answers

Every real strategy answers four questions. Where are we now, honestly? Where are we going, and why, tied to a business outcome? What do we build first, in what order? And how is it funded and sponsored, with a named executive attached?

## S4 · STEPS — Three ways strategies fail

Strategies fail three common ways. They become a slideshow presented once and never referenced again. They have no sponsor and no budget, which makes them a proposal, not a strategy. Or they try to govern everything at once instead of sequencing priorities.

# Lesson 23 — Governance and Architecture Roadmaps · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson turns last lesson's strategy into an actual roadmap — a time-ordered sequence people can plan and budget against.

## S2 · STEPS — Three honest horizons

Most governance roadmaps work best with three horizons rather than precise dates far out. Now: funded and starting immediately. Next: planned and sequenced, a few quarters out. Later: directionally agreed, deliberately left undetailed until circumstances are clearer.

## S3 · STEPS — Quick wins and foundational investments

A roadmap needs both. Quick wins are visible and fast — one catalog entry published, one conflicting number resolved — and they build trust early. Foundational investments are slower and less visible, but everything else depends on them: operating model, metadata architecture, access control.

## S4 · STEPS — The dependency step most roadmaps skip

Before sequencing on a timeline, map what each initiative actually depends on. A catalog rollout depends on the metadata architecture being settled first. Skip this step and initiative three starts on schedule, then stalls, because it silently depended on initiative one finishing first.

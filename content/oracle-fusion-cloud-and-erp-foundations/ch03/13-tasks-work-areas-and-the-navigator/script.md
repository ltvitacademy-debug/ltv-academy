# Script — Tasks, Work Areas and the Navigator

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 12 introduced the Navigator as the directory of work areas. Now let's look at the layer underneath each one — the tasks inside it — and put the whole hierarchy together.

## Segment 2 (steps: the hierarchy)

Three nested levels. The Navigator is the top-level directory of everything you can reach. A work area is a landing page for one function, like General Accounting. And a task is a specific activity inside that work area, like Create Journal or Review Journal Batches.

## Segment 3 (code: the tasks panel)

Inside most work areas, a Tasks panel lists the specific activities available there, often grouped by category — Journals, Period Close, Allocations, each expandable underneath. Clicking a task opens the exact page you need.

## Segment 4 (steps: quick actions)

Some tasks are frequent enough that Oracle surfaces them as Quick Actions right on the home page — skipping the Navigator, the work area, and the Tasks panel entirely for something like Create Journal.

## Segment 5 (outro)

When you later work with Oracle Fusion Security, access is often granted at exactly this level — a work area, specific tasks, or both. Knowing this hierarchy now means you'll already know where to look. Next up, Lesson 14: searching, favorites, and saved searches.

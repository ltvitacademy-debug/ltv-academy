# Script — Searching, Favorites and Saved Searches

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 12 briefly introduced Favorites. Let's cover it properly now, alongside two closely related tools every daily Fusion user relies on: global search and saved searches.

## Segment 2 (steps: global search)

A search icon sits in the global header on every page, giving access to global search — one box that looks across both transactions, like a specific invoice or journal, and navigation, like work areas and reports. Type a supplier's name and it can take you straight to the record, no digging through menus.

## Segment 3 (code: favorites vs. saved searches)

Favorites are personal, one-click shortcuts to a page, task, or report. A saved search is different — it's stored filter criteria on a specific list, so you don't rebuild the same filter every time, like all invoices over five thousand dollars still pending approval.

## Segment 4 (steps: three different problems)

Global search answers "find it anywhere." Favorites answer "take me there in one click." Saved searches answer "remember how I always filter this list."

## Segment 5 (outro)

A proficient user combines all three instead of relying on the full Navigator every time, and teaching that to end users is real, practical consulting work. Next up, Lesson 15: scheduled processes and job monitoring.

# Script — Signing In and the Home Page

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 2 covered what Oracle Fusion Cloud is. Chapter 3 shifts to how you actually work inside it. We're learning the layout and vocabulary now, conceptually, so the real screens feel familiar the moment you get hands-on access.

## Segment 2 (steps: signing in)

Fusion Cloud is accessed entirely through a browser, at a URL specific to each environment — production and non-production each have their own address. A user signs in with an account carrying whatever security roles were assigned to them, and two different people in the same environment can see noticeably different menus based entirely on those roles.

## Segment 3 (steps: the home page pieces)

The home page is built from consistent pieces: a global header with search and notifications, the Navigator menu for every work area you have access to, a springboard of shortcut icons for your most frequent starting points, and infolets — small widgets surfacing a glanceable summary relevant to your role.

## Segment 4 (code: glanceable by design)

Oracle built this around one idea: within seconds of logging in, you should know what needs your attention, without digging through menus first.

## Segment 5 (outro)

That tell-me-what-matters-right-away philosophy carries through the rest of Fusion's interface. Next up, Lesson 12: navigating Oracle Fusion Cloud in more depth.

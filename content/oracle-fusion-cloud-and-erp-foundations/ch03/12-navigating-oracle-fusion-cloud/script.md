# Script — Navigating Oracle Fusion Cloud

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 11 named the pieces of the home page. Now let's go one level deeper into the most important one for getting things done: the Navigator, and the design philosophy behind it.

## Segment 2 (steps: the Navigator)

The Navigator is the master menu for the whole application, opened from an icon next to the Oracle logo. It lists every work area a signed-in user has access to, organized by product family — Financials, Procurement, and more, depending on the company's setup and that user's security roles.

## Segment 3 (code: simplified UI)

Older enterprise software, including Oracle's own E-Business Suite, was built around dense forms and keyboard-driven navigation. Fusion Cloud was built instead around a simplified UI — fewer fields at once, click-driven, closer to a modern consumer app than a 1990s terminal screen. That's a deliberate bet on less training and more productivity.

## Segment 4 (steps: favorites and recent items)

Nobody wants to dig through the full Navigator every day for the same three work areas. Favorites give quick links to the pages you use constantly, and Recent Items automatically tracks what you just visited, so you're never retracing your steps from scratch.

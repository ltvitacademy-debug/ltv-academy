# Script — The Finance Department and Who Uses the System

## Segment 1 (title)

This course prepares you for the Financials side of Oracle Fusion Cloud. Before touching a screen, let's meet the people you're actually building this for — who works in a finance department, and who outside finance touches the ERP too.

## Segment 2 (steps: roles inside finance)

An Accounts Payable specialist processes supplier invoices and payments. An Accounts Receivable specialist manages customer invoices and incoming payments. A General Ledger accountant posts and reconciles journal entries. A Controller owns the accuracy of the financial statements, and an FP&A analyst builds budgets and forecasts on top of all of it.

## Segment 3 (steps: people outside finance)

But finance isn't the only department in the system. Procurement buyers create the purchase orders that become AP invoices. Warehouse staff record the shipments that feed AR invoicing. Sales reps start the order-to-cash cycle. And in most companies, every single employee submits their own expense reports directly into the system.

## Segment 4 (code: where you fit)

As a functional consultant, you won't be entering those invoices yourself — your job is to configure the system so every one of those roles can do their job correctly, and to troubleshoot it when something breaks.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Every role you just met is a future stakeholder you'll interview, listen to, and design for. That closes Chapter 1. Next up, Chapter 2: Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications, starting with what they actually are.

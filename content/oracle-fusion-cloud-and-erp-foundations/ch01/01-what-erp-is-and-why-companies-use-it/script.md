# Script — What ERP Is and Why Companies Use It

## Segment 1 (title)

Welcome to Oracle Fusion Cloud and ERP Foundations, the first course on the Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant path. No accounting or ERP background assumed — we start with one question: what is ERP, and why does a company bother with one?

## Segment 2 (steps: the problem before ERP)

Picture a fictional company, Blue Harbor Manufacturing. Sales tracks orders in one spreadsheet, the warehouse tracks inventory in another, and accounting retypes everything into a bookkeeping program at month end. That barely works at ten employees. At two hundred, it falls apart — the warehouse sells inventory that's already promised elsewhere, and the books close three weeks late.

## Segment 3 (code: the definition)

ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning. Instead of separate tools for sales, inventory, accounting, and HR, a company runs all of it inside one connected system — a single source of truth called the system of record. A sales order placed in the morning is instantly visible to the warehouse, and when it ships, accounting already has what it needs to invoice, with no retyping.

## Segment 4 (steps: why companies adopt one)

Companies adopt ERP for real business reasons: eliminating duplicate data entry, getting one accurate picture of sales, inventory, and cash, standardizing how every department works, supporting audits, and scaling to new locations without rebuilding spreadsheets from scratch every time.

## Segment 5 (outro)

An ERP isn't one program — it's a family of connected modules: Financials, Procurement, Supply Chain, HR, and more, all sharing data automatically. Next up, Lesson 2: how a business process actually flows through those modules from end to end.

# Script — Implementation Terminology You Will Hear

## Segment 1 (title)

Walk into your first Oracle Fusion implementation meeting and you'll hear a wall of acronyms, often without anyone pausing to explain them. This lesson is your glossary, built in advance.

## Segment 2 (steps: lifecycle terms)

An implementation is the whole project, from kickoff to go-live. Go-live is the date the company starts running its real business in the new system. Cutover is the specific window right around go-live where the old system gets switched off. And hypercare is the extra support staffing right after, because new issues surface fastest in the first weeks.

## Segment 3 (steps: requirements and testing)

Fit-gap analysis compares what the company needs against what the standard system does out of the box. A CRP, or Conference Room Pilot, is a structured demo of the configured system against real scenarios. UAT is User Acceptance Testing — actual end users signing off before go-live. And data migration moves historical data from the old system into the new one.

## Segment 4 (code: people and roles)

An SI, or Systems Integrator, is the consulting firm leading the implementation. Functional and technical consultants split the configuration and the technical extension work, which Lesson 19 covers in depth. And a project sponsor is the executive accountable for the whole thing.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A candidate who uses this vocabulary correctly in an interview signals real project exposure, not just screen familiarity. Next up, Lesson 18: offerings, functional areas, and Setup and Maintenance.

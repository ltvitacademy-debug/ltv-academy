## What the practice environment is

Oracle Fusion Cloud is enterprise software, licensed to real companies, not something you can simply sign up for and click around in on your own like a free consumer app. To get genuine hands-on practice — the actual screens, the actual Setup and Maintenance work area, real transactions — this career path requires a **practice-instance subscription**, purchased separately from a third-party provider called **OracleERPGuide (OEG)**. As of this course's last verification, that access runs approximately **$85 for three months**. This is **not included** in your LTV Academy tuition, and LTV Academy does not own, operate, resell, or receive any benefit from that provider — it is simply the option currently available for students who need real, hands-on Oracle Fusion access. Pricing, availability, and terms are controlled entirely by OracleERPGuide and can change, so verify current details with them directly before purchasing.

## Why timing matters so much

That access is sold for a fixed window — about three months. If you activate it today, before you're ready to actually use it for configuration exercises, you are burning down a clock on access you paid for, while you're still reading conceptual material that doesn't require logging into anything. The entire point of this lesson is: **don't do that**.

## When NOT to activate it

Do not purchase or activate your Oracle Fusion practice environment because you just enrolled in LTV Academy, finished this course, or feel eager to get started. This course, Oracle Fusion Cloud and ERP Foundations, is entirely conceptual — everything in it (ERP fundamentals, what Fusion Cloud is, how to navigate it, implementation vocabulary) was designed to be learned without touching a real environment. The next course on this path, **Accounting Fundamentals for Oracle Professionals**, is also conceptual: debits and credits, journal entries, financial statements — real accounting knowledge you need before Oracle-specific configuration will make any sense, and none of it requires Oracle access either.

## When TO activate it

Activate your practice environment when you reach the course after that: **Enterprise Structures & Chart of Accounts**, the first course in this path's "Financials Configuration" stage. That is where hands-on exercises genuinely begin — legal entities, business units, ledgers, and the Chart of Accounts, configured by you, inside a real environment. Activating right before that course starts means your three-month window covers the courses that actually need it: Enterprise Structures & Chart of Accounts, General Ledger, Accounts Payable, and Accounts Receivable, with room to revisit earlier exercises if needed.

## What's next

You've completed Oracle Fusion Cloud and ERP Foundations — all of ERP fundamentals, what Oracle Fusion Cloud is, how to work inside it conceptually, and the implementation vocabulary and career landscape around it. The next course on the Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant path is **Accounting Fundamentals for Oracle Professionals**, covering debits and credits, assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, expenses, journal entries, the trial balance, financial statements, accounting periods, and ledgers and subledgers — the accounting foundation every Oracle Financials configuration decision ultimately rests on.

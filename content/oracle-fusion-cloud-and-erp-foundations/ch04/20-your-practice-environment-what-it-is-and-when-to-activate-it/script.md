# Script — Your Practice Environment: What It Is and When to Activate It

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the most important practical lesson in this entire course, even though there's no Oracle screen to show you. Read it carefully before you spend a dollar on anything related to this career path.

## Segment 2 (steps: what it is, and why timing matters)

Oracle Fusion Cloud is licensed enterprise software, not something you sign up for on your own. Real hands-on practice requires a separate practice-instance subscription from a third-party provider, OracleERPGuide, currently around eighty-five dollars for three months — not included in your LTV Academy tuition, and sold for a fixed window. Activate it too early, and you burn down that clock while you're still reading conceptual material that needs no login at all.

## Segment 3 (code: when NOT to activate)

Don't activate your practice environment just because you enrolled or just finished this course. This course was entirely conceptual. The next course, Accounting Fundamentals for Oracle Professionals, is conceptual too — real accounting knowledge you need before Oracle configuration makes any sense, and it requires no Oracle access either.

## Segment 4 (steps: when TO activate)

Activate it when you reach Enterprise Structures and Chart of Accounts — the first course in this path's Financials Configuration stage. That's where hands-on exercises genuinely begin, and your three-month window will cover exactly the courses that need it.

## Segment 5 (outro)

You've completed Oracle Fusion Cloud and ERP Foundations. Next up on the Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant path: Accounting Fundamentals for Oracle Professionals — debits and credits, journal entries, and financial statements, the accounting foundation every configuration decision ahead rests on.

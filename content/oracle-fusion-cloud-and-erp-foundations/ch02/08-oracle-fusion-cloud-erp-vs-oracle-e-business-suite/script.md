# Script — Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP vs. Oracle E-Business Suite

## Segment 1 (title)

Many experienced Oracle consultants started on E-Business Suite, not Fusion Cloud. EBS is still widely used today, so understanding how it differs from Fusion Cloud will help you make sense of job postings and conversations with senior colleagues.

## Segment 2 (code: what EBS is)

Oracle E-Business Suite is Oracle's mature, on-premises ERP suite, first released in the late nineteen-nineties and still actively used by a large number of enterprises. Oracle keeps extending its Premier Support commitment year after year, but that's maintenance, not new innovation — Oracle's new feature investment, including built-in AI, goes into Fusion Cloud.

## Segment 3 (steps: concrete differences)

EBS is on-premises with deep code-level customization and customer-scheduled upgrades. Fusion Cloud is multi-tenant SaaS with configuration-first changes and Oracle-scheduled quarterly updates for everyone, on a modern browser-based interface.

## Segment 4 (steps: why EBS hasn't disappeared)

Large organizations rarely rip out a deeply customized, working ERP overnight. Many run EBS for years to come, sometimes alongside Fusion Cloud in what's called a coexistence strategy — which is exactly why EBS experience still shows up in job postings.

## Segment 5 (outro)

This career path is called Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant for a reason: new implementations are overwhelmingly choosing Fusion Cloud, and that's where Oracle's roadmap is headed. Next up, Lesson 9: a closer look at Fusion Cloud's own architecture.

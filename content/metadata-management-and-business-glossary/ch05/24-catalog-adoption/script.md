# Lesson 24 — Catalog Adoption · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

By this point you know how to build a technically sound catalog — scan
for technical metadata, curate business context, link to the glossary
and dictionary, make it searchable. None of that guarantees anyone
actually uses it.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (a separate, harder problem)

Adoption is a separate, harder problem — and it's the one that
determines whether all the work in this course pays off or sits
unused.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (three barriers)

Three barriers sink well-built catalogs. Habit — people already have a
working method that's faster for them right now. Trust — one stale or
wrong search result, and they conclude the catalog isn't reliable,
often permanently. Visibility — a catalog nobody knows exists gets zero
adoption regardless of quality.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (a tactic for each)

For habit: integrate the catalog into existing workflows, a search
widget embedded in the BI tool people already open, not a separate
destination. For trust: launch with a smaller, genuinely high-quality
scope — a hundred reliable entries beat a thousand of mixed quality.
For visibility: actively announce it where people already look, not
passive publishing.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Most failed metadata programs aren't failed because the technology was
wrong — they're failed because nobody treated adoption as its own
deliberate workstream. Next lesson: a metadata case study, pulling all
five chapters together, end to end.

# Script — Slicers & Timelines

## Segment 1 (title)

A PivotTable's own dropdown filter hides its current state behind an arrow icon. Slicers and Timelines make that state visible at a glance, with a single click to change it.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Insert Slicers dialog)

Insert > Slicer opens a dialog listing every available field as a checkbox — check several and Excel creates a slicer for each one in a single step.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: slicer anatomy)

Highlighted buttons are the values currently selected; everything else is filtered out. A clear-filter icon in the corner resets it back to everything — far more discoverable than a dropdown's buried clear option.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Insert Timelines and Timeline control)

A regular slicer on a date field would list every individual date — unusable across years of data. A Timeline solves this with a drag-to-select range and a zoom level that switches between years, quarters, months, and days.

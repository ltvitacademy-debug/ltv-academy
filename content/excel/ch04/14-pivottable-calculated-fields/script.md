# Script — PivotTable Calculated Fields

## Segment 1 (title)

Two different ways to add a computed number to a PivotTable, and a real tradeoff about whether that calculation belongs inside the PivotTable at all.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Fields, Items & Sets)

Calculated Field lives on the Analyze tab, in the Calculations group's Fields, Items & Sets menu. Its formula references other fields already in the PivotTable by name, and operates on their summed totals — a ratio of sums, not an average of row-level percentages.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Value Field Settings)

Not every computed result needs a custom formula. Right-clicking a field already in Values and choosing Value Field Settings opens Show Values As — built-in options like percent of grand total, with nothing to write.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: percent of grand total)

The same numeric field, redisplayed as a percentage of the report's total, computed automatically. The real decision isn't which button to click — it's whether the calculation belongs inside this one PivotTable, or as a column on the source data that every other formula and chart can use.

# Script — Interface & Shortcuts for Speed

## Segment 1 (title)

This isn't a 100-video "Excel from zero" course — it assumes you already know the basics. What it teaches is the specific slice of Excel that shows up constantly in analyst work: lookups, PivotTables, dynamic arrays, and Power Query.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: the grid)

Every workbook starts here — the grid is the whole product, and everything else in Excel exists to manipulate what's in it. The ribbon groups commands by tab, and the Name Box, next to the formula bar, lets you jump instantly to any cell by typing its address.

## Segment 3 (code: shortcuts)

A handful of shortcuts separate a fast analyst from a slow one. Ctrl+Arrow jumps to the edge of a data region instead of scrolling through it. F4 toggles a reference between absolute and relative. Alt equals AutoSums a selection in one keystroke.

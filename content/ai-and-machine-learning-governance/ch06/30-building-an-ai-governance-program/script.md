# Lesson 30 — Building an AI Governance Program · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This is it — the closing lesson. This course didn't teach eight separate subjects. It taught one program, one chapter at a time. Let's assemble it.

## S2 · STEPS — Start with who and what

Step one: name an accountable owner for every system — not "the data team," a person. Step two: inventory every AI system you actually have, including the shadow ones nobody approved. Nothing else in this sequence works without both.

## S3 · STEPS — Tier it, then govern the data and the model

Step three: risk-tier everything, so you spend effort where it matters, not everywhere equally. Step four: govern the training data behind your highest-tier systems first — provenance, quality, bias. Step five: require model documentation before anything above your lowest tier goes live.

## S4 · STEPS — Operate it and know when outside expertise is needed

Step six: access controls, monitoring, and approval gates around anything live. Step seven: incident response and an audit schedule, defined before you need them. Step eight: use the frameworks and regulations you've learned to recognize as your vocabulary — then bring in real legal and compliance expertise for the actual determination.

## S5 · OUTRO

Congratulations — that's all thirty lessons and six chapters of AI and Machine Learning Governance. The Data Governance career path continues next with Data Governance Program Management, applying this same sequencing to your entire data estate.

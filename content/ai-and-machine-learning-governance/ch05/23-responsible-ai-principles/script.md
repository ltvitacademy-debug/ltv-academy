# Lesson 23 — Responsible AI Principles · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Welcome to Chapter Five, Responsible AI and Risk. We're starting with the principles nearly every responsible-AI framework shares, no matter who wrote it.

## S2 · STEPS — Three of the six recurring principles

Fairness means the system doesn't produce systematically worse outcomes for some groups without a legitimate reason. Transparency and explainability mean people can understand why it reached a decision. Accountability means a specific person or role owns that system's behavior — "the model did it" is never an acceptable answer.

## S3 · STEPS — The other three

Privacy and security protect the personal data the system touches. Safety and robustness mean the system behaves predictably and fails safely. And human oversight means a person can meaningfully review, challenge, or shut the system down — not just rubber-stamp it.

## S4 · STEPS — These aren't new — they're the "why"

These six principles aren't an add-on to this course. Fairness runs through the bias controls in Chapter Two. Transparency runs through model cards in Chapter Three. Accountability is the governance roles from Lesson Four. They're the reasoning behind everything you've already learned.

## S5 · OUTRO

Principles are voluntary — until a regulation or contract makes them mandatory. Next lesson: turning these principles into an actual AI risk management process.

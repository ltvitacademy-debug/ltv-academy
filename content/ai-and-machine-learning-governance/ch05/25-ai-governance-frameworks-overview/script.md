# Lesson 25 — AI Governance Frameworks Overview · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson is a map of the major AI governance frameworks you'll hear referenced in the real world — orientation, not legal advice.

## S2 · STEPS — Two voluntary frameworks

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework is voluntary U.S. guidance built around four functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. ISO slash IEC 42001 is an international standard for an AI management system — the same pattern as ISO's quality and security standards — and organizations can actually get certified against it.

## S3 · STEPS — One binding law

The EU AI Act is different — it's binding law, not voluntary guidance. It classifies AI systems into risk tiers, from prohibited uses down to minimal-risk systems with light obligations. The exact details are specific and time-sensitive, which is exactly why this lesson won't try to state them precisely.

## S4 · STEPS — How to actually use this

Treat this as a map, not a manual. Sector regulators, national strategies, and vendor frameworks exist too — the goal here is recognizing the names, not mastering any one of them. When a real framework applies to your work, go to its primary source and bring in legal or compliance expertise.

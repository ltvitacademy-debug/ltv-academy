# Lesson 26 — AI Regulation Overview · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson is orientation, not legal advice — the single most important sentence in it. Let's look at what makes a regulation different from a voluntary framework.

## S2 · STEPS — Regulation versus framework

A framework is something an organization chooses to follow because it's useful. A regulation is something a government body requires, with real enforcement behind it. The EU AI Act's risk-tiered approach is the pattern most other jurisdictions are watching — though the specific obligations are detailed enough that we won't restate them here as settled fact.

## S3 · STEPS — AI is already regulated, just not by name

A common myth is that AI is a legal vacuum until an "AI law" passes. Existing data-protection law already governs personal data used to train models. Employment law already applies to a hiring algorithm exactly as it applies to a human recruiter. "No AI law yet" was never the same as "no legal exposure."

## S4 · STEPS — Recurring patterns to recognize

A few shapes keep recurring: risk tiering, transparency obligations telling people they're interacting with AI, documentation requirements, and human oversight requirements. Recognize these patterns — your organization's actual obligations depend on where it operates and current law, which only a legal review can determine.

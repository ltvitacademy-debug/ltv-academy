# Lesson 27 — Auditing AI Systems · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson closes Chapter Five by asking the question that actually matters: is governance real, or just written down somewhere?

## S2 · STEPS — What an AI audit checks

An AI audit asks whether a system is actually operating the way the organization says, and whether the controls that were supposed to exist actually exist. It's where principles, risk tiers, and whatever framework applies get checked against reality, not assumed.

## S3 · STEPS — The evidence a real audit looks for

Model documentation, a justified risk tier, evidence bias testing actually happened, an access log that matches the approved list, monitoring and drift records, and a signed-off approval record. If any of those is missing on a high-risk system, that's a finding — the gap itself is the problem.

## S4 · STEPS — A simple audit structure

Scope which systems and risk tiers are in play. Request the evidence by name before you start. Walk through a real decision end to end with the system's owner. Compare promise against evidence. Document any gap with an owner and a deadline — the same discipline a financial audit finding gets.

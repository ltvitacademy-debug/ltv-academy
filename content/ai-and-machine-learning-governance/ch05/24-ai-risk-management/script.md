# Lesson 24 — AI Risk Management · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson turns the principles from last time into an actual risk management process — one you can apply without a law degree.

## S2 · STEPS — Why AI needs its own risk lens

A traditional outage shows up on a dashboard. A model that's working exactly as designed but quietly produces unfair outcomes doesn't trigger any alert at all. That's the risk this lesson is really about — risk that hides inside normal operation.

## S3 · STEPS — Three kinds of AI risk

Risk to the organization — penalties, reputation, litigation. Risk to the people affected — someone wrongly denied a loan or flagged as fraud. And risk to the model itself — performance drifting as the world changes. All three count, not just the first one.

## S4 · STEPS — A simple risk-tiering approach

Score any system on two questions: likelihood of a bad outcome, and impact if it happens. High on both gets your most expensive controls — human review, bias testing, approval gates. Low on both gets a lighter touch. It's the same logic Chapter Four used for critical data elements.

## S5 · OUTRO

Risk tier is the dial; every governance control in this course is what that dial turns. Next lesson: the named frameworks that formalize this kind of thinking.

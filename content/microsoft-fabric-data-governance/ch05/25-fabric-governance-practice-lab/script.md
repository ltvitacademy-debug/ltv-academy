# Lesson 25 — Fabric Governance Practice Lab · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

This one's yours to actually do — in a free Fabric trial or your
own sandbox tenant. Five tasks, one per chapter, start to finish.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (tasks 1-3)

Structure first: a workspace, assigned to a domain, with roles you
actually understand. Then land real data — a Lakehouse, load to
tables, and a look at where OneLake security would restrict a
folder. Then protect it: a sensitivity label, and a read-through of
a real row-level security role if your tenant has one.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (tasks 4-5)

Make it discoverable — find it in the OneLake catalog, set it
Promoted, and see what happens when you try Certified without
authorization. Then close the loop: open Manage permissions, see
who can Build right now, and write out what a real request-access
flow should say.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (check your own work)

Five questions, about the one item you built: which domain and who
has which role, where the data actually lives, what label it
carries, whether it's endorsed, and who can build against it right
now. Answer all five without hunting, and you've internalized the
course.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Congratulations — Chapter 1's tenant structure, all the way through
a certified, permissioned, cataloged semantic model feeding an
enterprise Purview catalog. The Data Governance path continues next
with Databricks Unity Catalog Governance — the same instincts,
applied to Databricks' own catalog and lineage tooling.

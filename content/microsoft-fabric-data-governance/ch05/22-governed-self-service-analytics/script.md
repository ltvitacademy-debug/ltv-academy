# Lesson 22 — Governed Self-Service Analytics · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Self-service analytics means lots of people building lots of
content — and gates alone can't keep that organized. Endorsement is
Fabric's other lever: not a gate, a signal.

## S2 · SCREENSHOT CARD (endorsement pane)

Every Fabric item carries this same pane: None, Promoted, Certified,
Master Data. Promoted is low-bar by design — any owner can flag
their own item as worth using. Certified and Master Data are a
different story entirely.

## S3 · SCREENSHOT CARD (certification setting)

Because Certified means something, not everyone can grant it. An
admin enables certification tenant-wide and decides exactly who's
authorized to apply it — off by default, on purpose.

## S4 · SCREENSHOT CARD (endorsement badges across a catalog)

Scaled up across a real catalog, that badge is the scanning
advantage — Master data or Certified tells a report author which
copy to build on, without asking around.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Signals that travel with the item, visible the moment someone's
scanning the catalog. Next lesson: Purview integration — what
happens when that catalog needs to span more than just Fabric.

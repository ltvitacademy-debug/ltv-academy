# Lesson 17 — The OneLake Catalog · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Chapter Four turns to one of Fabric's most central features — the OneLake catalog.

## S2 · STEPS — Three tabs, one catalog

The OneLake catalog is Fabric's single home for finding data and understanding governance across your whole tenant. It opens from the OneLake catalog icon in the left nav, with three tabs: Explore, Govern, and Secure.

## S3 · SCREENSHOT — The Explore tab's layout

This is Microsoft's own annotated view of Explore, the default tab. On the left, a filterable item list; select anything, and its detail pane opens right alongside it — so you can browse several items without losing your place.

## S4 · STEPS — Filters, selectors, and a catch

Predefined filters get you to My items or Endorsed items fast. Type, tag, and keyword selectors narrow further. And here's the catch: items you don't yet own can still appear — specifically discoverable semantic models — precisely so you know they exist and can request access.

## S5 · SCREENSHOT — The domain selector

If your organization defines domains — business groupings like Finance or Sales — this selector scopes the entire catalog to just one. Your choice persists across sessions, and it scopes the Govern tab's insights the same way.

## S6 · SCREENSHOT — The Govern tab's insights

Govern is where you check governance health instead of browsing items. This panel gives a tenant-wide snapshot — domains, capacities, workspaces, item counts — refreshed daily, backed by Recommended Actions cards for fixing what needs attention.

## S7 · SCREENSHOT — The item detail pane

Selecting any item opens this four-tab pane: Overview for description and ownership, Lineage for upstream and downstream relationships, Monitor for refresh history, and Permissions for who has access — and where you'd request it yourself.

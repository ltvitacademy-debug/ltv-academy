# Lesson 3 — The Fabric Admin Portal · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Chapter One's governance surfaces all live in one place: the Fabric admin portal.

## S2 · STEPS — Getting there, and what's on it

You reach it from the gear icon in the top right of Fabric, then Admin portal. It's a separate surface from your everyday workspace view, built for the people governing the tenant. On it: tenant settings and capacity settings, domains, workspaces, and users — plus audit logs and information protection, previewed here and covered fully later in Chapter 3.

## S3 · SCREENSHOT — A tenant setting's controls

Every tenant setting has the same two-part shape. This is a real one, Git integration, chosen as a representative example. First, scope: apply it to the entire organization or specific security groups, with an option to exclude groups even from a broad grant. Second, delegation: these checkboxes let capacity admins and workspace admins enable or disable this one setting themselves, without a tenant admin in the loop every time.

## S4 · SCREENSHOT — Grouped into categories

Tenant settings aren't one giant list. They're grouped under named categories — here, "Git integration," with four related settings underneath, each showing its current scope at a glance before you even open it.

## S5 · SCREENSHOT — Domains, previewed

One of the admin portal's other pages is Domains: a table of every domain the tenant has defined, its admins, and its subdomains. This lesson only previews it — Lesson 4 walks through it end to end.

## S6 · STEPS — Why delegation matters

By default, a tenant admin can manage every setting alone — but that doesn't scale forever. Delegating the settings that make sense to capacity or workspace admins means one central team isn't the bottleneck for every decision as the organization grows. That's the pattern this course keeps coming back to.

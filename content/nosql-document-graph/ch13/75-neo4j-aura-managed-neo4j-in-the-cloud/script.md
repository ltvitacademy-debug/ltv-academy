# Script — Neo4j Aura: Managed Neo4j in the Cloud

## Segment 1 (title)

This course has already covered two managed-service stories: Cosmos DB by design, and MongoDB Atlas. Neo4j Aura is the same idea applied to Neo4j itself — the official, Neo4j-run managed cloud service, built on Enterprise Edition.

## Segment 2 (steps: Aura tiers)

Aura comes in real product tiers: AuraDB Free, a small permanently free instance for learning and prototypes; AuraDB Professional and Business Critical, production tiers with SLAs and support; and AuraDS for graph data science workloads.

## Segment 3 (steps: what's managed)

Everything this chapter just taught you to configure by hand, Aura runs for you — automatic backups with point-in-time restore, managed causal clustering and failover, console-driven scaling, and patching and monitoring handled by Neo4j.

## Segment 4 (code: same Cypher, same driver API)

Aura doesn't give you a lesser version of Neo4j — the query language and driver APIs are identical to self-hosted Enterprise Edition. What you give up is direct infrastructure control, not database capability.

## Segment 5 (outro)

All three platforms in this course now have the same managed-cloud option next to their self-hosted story — MongoDB has Atlas, Cosmos DB is managed by definition, and Neo4j has Aura. Next up: monitoring and operating Neo4j in production.

# Script — Users & Licenses

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 5 was about the shape of the data. Chapter 6 shifts to who can actually see and do things with it — starting with the most basic unit of "who" in Salesforce: the User.

## Segment 2 (code: a real object)

Every person who logs into an org has a corresponding record on the standard User object — Name, Email, Profile, Role, and more. Users aren't an invisible layer; other objects, like Opportunity, relate to them exactly the way they relate to anything else.

## Segment 3 (steps: not every user is equal)

Creating a User record doesn't automatically grant full access. Every User is assigned a license, which sets a hard ceiling before profiles even enter the picture. A full Salesforce license gets standard CRM access. A Platform license is cheaper but skips standard CRM objects entirely. Chatter Free is collaboration only.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Licenses are step one of understanding why access varies user to user. Next up: how a user's role in the hierarchy affects what records they can see.

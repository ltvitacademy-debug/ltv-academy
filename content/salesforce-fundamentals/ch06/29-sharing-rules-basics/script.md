# Script — Sharing Rules, Basics

## Segment 1 (title)

The default is visibility flowing up the Role Hierarchy, and peers not automatically seeing each other's records. Real orgs frequently need to extend visibility beyond that default — that's what Sharing Rules are for.

## Segment 2 (code: beyond ownership and hierarchy)

By default a user sees records they own, plus records owned by people below them in the hierarchy. A Sharing Rule extends visibility to a named group of users without touching ownership or restructuring the hierarchy at all.

## Segment 3 (steps: two ways to trigger)

There are two types. An ownership-based rule shares records based on who owns them — every record owned by East Region Sales, shared with Support. A criteria-based rule shares based on field values on the record itself — every Closed Won deal over $100,000, shared with Executives, no matter who owns it.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Sharing Rules are why ownership and visibility aren't the same question in Salesforce. Next up: Field-Level Security — when a report silently omits a field's value, and why that's not a data problem.

# Script — Service Cloud Reporting Context

## Segment 1 (title)

Lessons 13 and 14 covered what a Case is and how it moves through its lifecycle. This lesson closes the chapter by connecting that structure to the actual numbers an analyst gets asked to report.

## Segment 2 (code: Average Handle Time)

Average Handle Time is the average active time a rep spends working a case — talking to the customer, researching, writing notes. It's a rep and team efficiency metric, and it depends on activity-level timestamps tied to the case, not just the Status field.

## Segment 3 (steps: three core metrics)

Case Resolution Time is the total elapsed time from creation to Closed — the clock a customer actually feels, and usually the first number leadership asks for. First Contact Resolution is the percentage of cases resolved on the very first interaction, with no reopen — the best signal of real service quality, not just speed.

## Segment 4 (code: why FCR is hard to calculate)

First Contact Resolution isn't a single field Salesforce hands you by default. It typically has to be inferred from whether a case was ever reopened, or from custom fields an org built specifically to track it — which is exactly why understanding standard versus custom fields matters next.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A team can have a fast resolution time while still frustrating customers with cases closed too soon. Next up: Chapter 4 opens with standard versus custom objects — what ships with Salesforce, and what's been built for one specific org.

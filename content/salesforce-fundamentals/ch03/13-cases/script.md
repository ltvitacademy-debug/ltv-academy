# Script — Cases

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 2 covered the Sales Cloud objects that track a deal before it closes. Now we shift to what happens after the sale: a customer has a problem, and the core object for tracking it is the Case.

## Segment 2 (code: a Case record)

A Case is a single customer support request — a support ticket, in plain language. A billing question by email, a broken feature reported by phone, a how-to question in chat: each becomes one Case record, tied to a Contact and the Account that Contact belongs to.

## Segment 3 (steps: the fields that make it reportable)

Case Number is a system-generated, read-only identifier. Status tracks where the case is in its lifecycle — New, Working, Closed. Priority reflects urgency. Origin records which channel the case came in through: email, phone, web, or chat.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Those four fields — Case Number, Status, Priority, Origin — are what makes Case data reportable at all. Next up: the case lifecycle, and how a case actually moves from open to closed.

# Script — Campaigns

## Segment 1 (title)

Every Lead and Opportunity so far has just "appeared." In a real org, most trace back to something: a specific marketing effort. That's the Campaign object.

## Segment 2 (code: tracked marketing initiative)

A Campaign represents a specific marketing initiative — an email blast, a webinar, a trade show booth. It's a tracked, named effort with its own dates and budget, and a defined set of people it targeted — much more granular than a broad Lead Source value.

## Segment 3 (code: Campaign Members)

A Campaign Member is the join record connecting a Campaign to a specific Lead or Contact, tracking their status — Sent, Responded, Attended. One person can be a member of multiple Campaigns, which is how repeated touches become real, answerable data.

## Segment 4 (steps: path to ROI)

Because Campaign Members link to Leads and Contacts, and Leads convert into Opportunities, there's a real traceable path from a Campaign to the revenue it influenced. Opportunity even has a Primary Campaign Source field that records which Campaign gets primary credit.

## Segment 5 (outro)

That path is what makes "which Campaign actually drove revenue" answerable instead of a guess. Next up, Chapter Two's finale: Activities.

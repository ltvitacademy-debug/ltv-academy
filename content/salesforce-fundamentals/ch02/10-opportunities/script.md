# Script — Opportunities

## Segment 1 (title)

Leads, Accounts, and Contacts all set up for this: the Opportunity, the object representing an actual real deal, in progress or already decided. This is the object underneath every pipeline report.

## Segment 2 (code: real deal)

An Opportunity is a genuine sales pursuit, not a raw unqualified inquiry — that's a Lead. Every Opportunity ties to an Account and usually to Contacts through Contact Roles, and it tracks that deal's progress from open to closed, won or lost.

## Segment 3 (steps: four fields)

Four fields carry most of the analytical weight. Stage defines the pipeline itself. Amount is the deal's dollar value. Close Date drives monthly and quarterly forecasting. Probability estimates likelihood of closing, usually tied to Stage.

## Segment 4 (code: pipeline demystified)

Here's the thing worth internalizing: the sales pipeline isn't a separate object or report type. It's just open Opportunities — Stage not yet Closed Won or Closed Lost — grouped by Stage. Pipeline data is Opportunity data.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Once you see it that way, pipeline reporting stops being mysterious. Next up: Campaigns.

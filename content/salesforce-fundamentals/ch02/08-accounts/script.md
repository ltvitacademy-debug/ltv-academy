# Script — Accounts

## Segment 1 (title)

Once a Lead converts, it produces an Account and a Contact. This lesson covers the Account — the object representing the company or organization at the center of almost every other Sales Cloud object.

## Segment 2 (code: company at the center)

An Account represents a company Salesforce is tracking a relationship with. Contacts belong to an Account, Opportunities are tied to it, and Cases are too. When someone asks what our relationship with a company is, the Account is where that question gets answered.

## Segment 3 (steps: business vs person)

Most Accounts are Business Accounts — the standard case, a company with related Contacts. But Salesforce also supports Person Accounts, a real feature an admin has to turn on, used when the customer is an individual consumer rather than a company, common in retail and other B2C industries.

## Segment 4 (code: why it matters)

This distinction matters directly. If Person Accounts is enabled, some Account records represent individual people, not companies — and averaging something like Contacts per Account across both types will skew the number.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Because so many objects point back to Account, it's often the natural anchor for company-level analysis. Next up: Contacts.

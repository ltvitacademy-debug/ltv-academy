# Script — Leads

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter Two starts where the sales pipeline starts: the Lead. It's the object every deal begins its life as, before it's proven to be a real opportunity at all.

## Segment 2 (code: unqualified prospect)

A Lead represents a person or company that might become a customer, but hasn't been qualified yet — a trade show badge scan, a website form-fill, a cold-call contact. Salesforce keeps Leads separate so unproven prospects don't inflate the real pipeline data sales reports on.

## Segment 3 (steps: three key fields)

Three fields do most of the analytical work. Status shows where the Lead sits in qualification. Lead Source shows where it came from, and drives most marketing-attribution analysis. Rating is a quick quality signal — Hot, Warm, or Cold.

## Segment 4 (code: Lead Conversion)

Lead Conversion happens once a Lead is qualified as real: it creates an Account, a Contact, and usually an Opportunity. The original Lead record isn't deleted — it's marked Converted and stays linked to what it became.

## Segment 5 (outro)

That link matters: a Lead's original source lives on the converted Lead, while its ongoing sales activity moves to the new Opportunity. Next up: Accounts.

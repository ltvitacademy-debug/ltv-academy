# Script — Contacts

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 8 covered the company. This lesson covers the person: the Contact — an individual, typically related to an Account for the company they work at.

## Segment 2 (code: person tied to company)

A Contact represents one specific person, linked to an Account through its Account Name field. A single company commonly has many related Contacts: a champion, a procurement contact, a day-to-day user. Reporting on everyone you know at a company means pulling every related Contact.

## Segment 3 (steps: Lead vs Contact)

It's easy to lump Lead and Contact together since both represent people, but they're different stages of the same relationship. A Lead is a person before they're qualified and connected to a real Account. A Contact is a person after that connection exists. Lead Conversion is the process that bridges the two.

## Segment 4 (code: Contact Roles)

Contacts connect to Opportunities too, through Contact Roles — recording not just that someone is associated with a deal, but what role they play: Decision Maker, Influencer, Economic Buyer.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Finding decision makers across your pipeline means traversing Opportunity through Contact Role to Contact — three objects, not one link. Next up: Opportunities.

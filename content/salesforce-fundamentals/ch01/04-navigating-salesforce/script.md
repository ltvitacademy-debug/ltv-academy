# Script — Navigating Salesforce

## Segment 1 (title)

Before any of this course's data-model theory is useful, you need to find your way around the actual interface. This is the real Lightning Experience UI — the one you'll actually click through as an analyst.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: navigation bar)

This is the navigation bar you'll see on every screen: a row of tabs across the top, a global search bar in the middle, and the App Launcher grid icon in the top-left corner, next to your profile menu on the right.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: App Launcher)

Click that grid icon and you land here — the App Launcher. It's a full screen of every app installed in the org, split into All Apps and All Items, with its own search box for jumping straight to an app or object by name.

## Segment 4 (steps: three things)

Three things to get comfortable with: the App Launcher for switching apps, tabs that open list views — filtered tables of many records at once — and global search for jumping straight to one specific record.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Getting comfortable with these now will make everything later second nature. Next up: the core Salesforce vocabulary an analyst needs.

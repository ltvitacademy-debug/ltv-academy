# Script — What Is Salesforce & CRM?

## Segment 1 (title)

This course isn't a Salesforce Administrator mega-course. It teaches exactly what a data analyst needs: what Salesforce data actually means, before you ever try to analyze it.

## Segment 2 (code: CRM as a system of record)

CRM software exists because a growing company's customer and deal data stops fitting in one person's head or one spreadsheet. Before CRM, a salesperson's pipeline lived in their own notes. A CRM makes that data a shared, structured, queryable asset that survives any one person leaving.

## Segment 3 (steps: Salesforce's real position)

Salesforce is the dominant CRM platform by market share — the default choice for mid-size and large companies. That means understanding Salesforce data isn't a niche skill, it's the platform underneath an enormous share of real analyst job postings.

## Segment 4 (outro)

It's tempting to skip straight to the query language, but a query is only as good as your understanding of what you're actually querying. This course builds platform understanding first, on purpose. Next up: Sales Cloud vs. Service Cloud.

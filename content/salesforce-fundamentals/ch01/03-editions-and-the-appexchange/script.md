# Script — Editions & the AppExchange

## Segment 1 (title)

Not every Salesforce org is the same size. A ten-person startup and a ten-thousand-employee enterprise are both "running Salesforce" — but they're very likely on different editions, and that shapes what actually exists in the org you're analyzing.

## Segment 2 (steps: four editions)

There are four real edition tiers. Essentials is built for the smallest teams with the fewest options. Professional gives the full CRM feature set with real automation limits. Enterprise is the most common tier for mid-size and larger companies, with deep customization and API access. Unlimited pushes limits and support even further.

## Segment 3 (code: why it matters)

Here's why this matters to you: a field, automation, or API access you expect to exist might not, depending on the org's edition. That's not a data quality problem — it's a licensing reality you need to recognize before assuming something is broken.

## Segment 4 (code: AppExchange)

The AppExchange is Salesforce's official marketplace for third-party apps that install directly into an org. Those installed apps often bring their own custom objects and custom fields — data that won't show up in any generic Salesforce documentation you look up.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Part of reading a real org is telling native Salesforce data apart from AppExchange-sourced data. Next up: actually navigating Salesforce.

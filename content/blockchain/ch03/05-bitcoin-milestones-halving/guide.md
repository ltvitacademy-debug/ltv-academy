## Real milestones, in order

Bitcoin's own real history, from experiment to mainstream asset:

| Year | What actually happened |
|---|---|
| 2010 | The first real-world Bitcoin transaction: 10,000 BTC for two pizzas — now celebrated annually as **Bitcoin Pizza Day** |
| 2011 | Bitcoin reaches price parity with the US dollar |
| 2013 | Price surges to nearly $250 |
| 2017 | All-time high near $20,000 |
| 2021 | Price surpasses $64,000; **El Salvador** becomes the first country to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender |
| 2024 | The SEC approves spot Bitcoin ETFs; price surpasses $108,000 |

## Halving: the real deflationary mechanism

**Bitcoin halving** (also called "havening") is a real, scheduled event
that occurs roughly every four years: the reward for mining a new block is
literally cut in half. This controls Bitcoin's inflation and enforces a
hard cap of **21 million coins** that will ever exist.

The math is concrete: the very first block rewarded its miner **50
Bitcoin**. After the fourth halving, in April 2024, the reward dropped to
**3.125 Bitcoin** — a quarter of what it started at, more than a decade
earlier.

## Real technical upgrades worth knowing by name

- **SegWit (Segregated Witness, 2017)** — addressed transaction
  malleability and increased how much data fits in a block.
- **Taproot (2021)** — enhanced privacy and smart contract capabilities on
  Bitcoin itself.

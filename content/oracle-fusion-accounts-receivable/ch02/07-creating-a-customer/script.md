# Script — Creating a Customer

## Segment 1 (title)

Now that you know the party, account and site structure, let's walk through what actually happens, step by step, when someone creates a brand new customer in Oracle Fusion Receivables.

## Segment 2 (steps)

First, search the shared party registry so you don't create a duplicate. Then create or select the party itself, name and tax registration. Then add a party site, an address. Then create the customer account, where the party formally becomes a customer with a profile class and an owner. Then create account sites and assign site uses, bill-to and typically ship-to. Contacts and bank accounts can follow after that.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Here's the part that matters most: what's actually required before you can bill anyone. Just a party isn't enough. You need a customer account, at least one account site, and that site carrying a bill-to use. Everything else, contacts, extra addresses, bank accounts, can be added later without disrupting anything already recorded.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Picture Cascade Outdoor Supply, a brand new prospect signing its first order with Northwind Fixtures Co. The AR specialist searches first, finds nothing, creates the party, adds its Spokane warehouse as a party site, creates the account with a standard profile class, and creates one account site carrying both bill-to and ship-to. That's enough to enter the first invoice. A billing contact gets added afterward. Up next, lesson eight: customer accounts, sites, and contacts in more depth.

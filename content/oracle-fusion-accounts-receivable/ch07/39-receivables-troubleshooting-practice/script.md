# Script — Receivables Troubleshooting Practice

## Segment 1 (title)

This final lesson pulls the whole course together with three realistic troubleshooting scenarios. Identify the symptom, trace it to a cause, and name the specific fix - the way a real consultant would.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A customer insists they paid, but the invoice still shows open. Before assuming anyone is wrong, search for a receipt that might be sitting unapplied, on-account, or stuck in a lockbox exceptions queue. Apply it, and the problem disappears.

## Segment 3 (steps)

GL and Receivables don't reconcile at close. Run the Differences report, check whether a transaction was accounted but never transferred, and watch for a manual journal entry made directly in GL that bypassed Receivables entirely.

## Segment 4 (steps)

A long-overdue balance includes a real dispute and a genuine shortfall. Fix the disputed piece with an adjustment, because the number is wrong. Chase the undisputed piece through collections, and only write it off once collection has truly failed.

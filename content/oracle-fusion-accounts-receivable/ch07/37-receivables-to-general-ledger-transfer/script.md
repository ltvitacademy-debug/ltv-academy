# Script — Receivables to General Ledger Transfer

## Segment 1 (title)

Final journal entries sit in the Receivables subledger. This lesson covers the last leg: getting those entries into the General Ledger, where they combine with every other subledger.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Transfer and posting are two different steps. Transfer moves summarized entries into GL's own journal tables. Posting is the separate GL-side action that makes an entry final and updates account balances.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Companies choose continuous transfer, moving and posting entries throughout the month for near-real-time visibility, or period-end transfer, batching everything up once for a clean, controlled cutoff.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Once transferred and posted, GL's Accounts Receivable balance should match Receivables' own records. A mismatch usually means an unaccounted transaction, a skipped transfer run, or a manual journal entry made directly in GL.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Transfer and posting close the loop between subledger and GL. Up next, lesson 38: Receivables period close and reconciliation, where this becomes a formal close checklist.

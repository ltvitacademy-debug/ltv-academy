# Script — Receivables Period Close and Reconciliation

## Segment 1 (title)

Everything in this course comes together once a month into one disciplined event: closing the Receivables period. This lesson walks through the close checklist and the formal reconciliation that proves AR and GL agree.

## Segment 2 (steps)

The close sequence runs in order: finish month-end activity, run final accounting for the period, set the status to Close Pending, run reconciliation, then resolve any differences before closing for good.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Close Pending is a deliberate middle state. It signals close is underway without fully locking the period, so if reconciliation surfaces a problem, it can still be investigated and fixed before the final, hard close.

## Segment 4 (steps)

The Prepare Receivables to General Ledger Reconciliation process compares the Receivables-side balance against the GL-side balance by account. When they match, AR activity is proven consistent with the General Ledger, not just assumed.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A Differences report pinpoints the cause when they don't match - an unaccounted transaction, a skipped transfer, or an out-of-process manual entry. Up next, lesson 39: Receivables troubleshooting practice, the final lesson of this course.

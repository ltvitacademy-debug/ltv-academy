# Script — Miscellaneous Receipts

## Segment 1 (title)

Not every dollar that lands in the bank came from a customer paying an invoice. Interest income, a vendor rebate, proceeds from selling old equipment — none of that touches a customer balance. That's what a miscellaneous receipt is for.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A standard receipt always reduces a customer's open balance. A miscellaneous receipt does the opposite: it records cash coming in and distributes it straight to General Ledger accounts, with no subledger balance affected at all.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Every miscellaneous receipt needs a Receivables Activity of type Miscellaneous Cash. That activity determines the GL account the credit side hits - interest income, a gain account, a rebate clearing account - while the debit side is the cash account for the remittance bank.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Distributions can be one line or several. A simple receipt, like interest income, distributes one hundred percent to a single account. A more complex event can be split across accounts manually, or using a predefined distribution set for a recurring type of receipt.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A miscellaneous receipt records cash that has nothing to do with a customer balance, distributed straight to the General Ledger. Up next, lesson 25: applying receipts to transactions in more depth.

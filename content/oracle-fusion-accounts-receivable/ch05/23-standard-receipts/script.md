# Script — Standard Receipts

## Segment 1 (title)

Welcome to Chapter 5, Receipts. A customer owes money, and now money actually arrives. A standard receipt is how Oracle Fusion Receivables records a payment applied against specific transactions.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Every standard receipt carries a few key fields. The receipt method is the most important — it points to the accounting rules and the remittance bank account. You also need the bank account the money lands in, the receipt number and date, and the customer and amount.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A standard receipt moves through a predictable lifecycle. It's entered as unapplied, then applied against one or more open transactions, then remitted to the bank, and finally cleared once the bank confirms the funds actually settled.

## Segment 4 (steps)

In practice you enter and apply in the same pass. Search for the customer's open transactions, select the ones the payment covers, and apply the full balance or a partial amount. Receivables recalculates the open balance immediately.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A standard receipt is money received and matched to what a customer owes. Up next, lesson 24: miscellaneous receipts, for money that has nothing to do with a customer balance.

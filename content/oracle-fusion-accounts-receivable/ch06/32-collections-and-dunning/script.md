# Script — Collections and Dunning

## Segment 1 (title)

Before a balance is ever written off, someone has to try to collect it. Oracle Fusion's collections tools track past-due customers and automate escalating reminder notices known as dunning.

## Segment 2 (steps)

The Collections Workbench is where a collector works. It surfaces open balances by aging bucket, dunning history, notes from prior contact, and the ability to print a statement or letter on the spot.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Dunning runs on one of two methods. Aged dunning triggers letters based on how old the customer's single oldest past-due transaction is. Staged dunning instead triggers the next letter based on how long it's been since the last letter was sent.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Dunning letters escalate in tone - a friendly first reminder, a firmer second notice, a formal final warning of credit hold or collections referral. The system tracks exactly which letter went out, at which level, and when.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Collections pairs a consolidated workbench with escalating dunning, scoped to the customer, account, or site as needed. Up next, lesson 33: customer statements.

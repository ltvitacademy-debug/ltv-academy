# Script — Aging Reports

## Segment 1 (title)

Collections, dunning, write-offs - every tool in this chapter depends on knowing exactly how overdue a balance is. The aging report buckets every open transaction by days past due, giving shape to the whole AR portfolio.

## Segment 2 (steps)

An aging bucket is a range of days past due - current, 1 to 30 days, 31 to 60, and so on. Every open transaction sorts into exactly one bucket based on days since its due date, totaled up by customer or across the company.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A 4-bucket report gives a quick, high-level view of portfolio health. A 7-bucket report slices the same balances more finely, letting a collector tell apart a transaction barely 31 days overdue from one that's 89 days overdue.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Two more variants matter: aging by common currency converts everything into one reporting currency, and aging by collector organizes the data by who's assigned to each customer, so a manager can see each person's workload.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Aging data directly drives dunning escalation and supports every write-off decision. That wraps up Chapter 6. Up next, Chapter 7: Accounting, Reconciliation and Close, starting with lesson 35.

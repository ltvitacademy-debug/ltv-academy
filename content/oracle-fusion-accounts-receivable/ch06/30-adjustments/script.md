# Script — Adjustments

## Segment 1 (title)

Balances don't always resolve through cash. A small goodwill credit, a rounding difference - Receivables has a dedicated tool for changing a transaction's balance directly: the adjustment.

## Segment 2 (steps)

An adjustment changes the balance due on a specific transaction without a receipt or a credit memo. A negative adjustment reduces the balance - the common case. A positive adjustment increases it, for less common corrections.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Every adjustment needs a Receivables Activity of type Adjustment, which determines the GL account that absorbs it automatically, the same way a miscellaneous receipt needs its own activity type.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Adjustments aren't a backdoor around controls. Approval limits cap how large an adjustment a given user can create without escalation - a clerk might be capped at five hundred dollars, with anything larger routing to a supervisor.

## Segment 5 (outro)

An adjustment is a lightweight correction tool, not a replacement for a formal credit memo. Up next, lesson 31: write-offs and approval limits, where the balance isn't corrected - it's abandoned.

# Lesson 16 — Access Reviews and Certification · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

RBAC, least privilege, and segregation of duties are preventive — they try
to stop a bad grant before it happens. Access reviews are detective: the
recurring check that catches whatever slipped through anyway.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (attestation)

The defining feature isn't that someone runs a report — it's who signs off
on it. IT can list everyone with access in minutes. What takes a real
process is getting the data owner to attest, person by person: still
needed, or remove it. Only the business owner actually knows which.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (cadence and triggers)

Most organizations review quarterly for sensitive systems, annually for
lower-risk ones. But certain events trigger an unscheduled review too: a
role change, a new audit finding, a security incident, a reorg or system
integration.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (remediation)

Finding a problem is half the process. Remediation is actually fixing it —
removing the flagged access, with an owner, a deadline, and confirmation
it happened. Ten flagged grants that never get closed isn't a control,
it's a to-do list.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Next up: privileged access — the smallest, most powerful set of accounts,
and why they get a review process stricter than everything else in this

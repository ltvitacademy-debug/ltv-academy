# Lesson 15 — Segregation of Duties · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Least privilege asked if one account has too much access. Segregation of
duties asks a different question: can one person, alone, complete an
entire sensitive process end to end?

## S2 · STEPS CARD (finance example)

The classic case is accounts payable. Who creates a vendor record. Who
approves it. Who issues payment. If the same person does all three, there's
no independent check — which is exactly how a lot of payment fraud
actually happens. Split it across three people, and fraud now requires
collusion.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (mapping onto access roles)

This maps directly onto the four access-governance roles from Lesson 12.
If the same person requests their own access and approves it, there's no
independent check on that grant — the identical flaw, just applied to
access instead of money.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (toxic combinations)

An SoD program names toxic combinations — permission pairs that should
never sit with one person. Can grant access, plus can approve your own
request. Can write the audit log, plus no independent review of it. A
conflict matrix lists these pairs so reviews can catch them.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Next up: access reviews and certification — the recurring process that
catches SoD violations and permission creep after they've already
accumulated.

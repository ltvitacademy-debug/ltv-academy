# Lesson 27 — Breach Response Concepts · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE CARD

A data breach is unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or
destruction of protected data. Organizations that handle this well
follow roughly the same lifecycle — this lesson walks through it.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (the lifecycle)

Detect — a monitoring alert or an unusual pattern in the audit log.
Contain — stop the ongoing exposure before you fully understand it:
revoke a credential, isolate a system. Assess — what data, how many
people, how serious — which depends entirely on the audit trail and
classification labels already being in place. Then notify, and
remediate the actual cause.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (who's in the room)

A real response pulls in roles that rarely work together day to day:
security for investigation and containment, legal and a privacy
officer for notification obligations, communications for what gets
said, and leadership for decisions that carry risk beyond any one
team. The plan that was never rehearsed gets discovered incomplete in
the middle of an actual incident.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (GDPR notification)

GDPR's breach notification framework sets out two separate
obligations. Notify the supervisory authority without undue delay,
and within seventy-two hours where feasible — unless the breach is
unlikely to pose a risk. Separately, if the breach is likely high-risk
to individuals, you also notify those individuals directly. Not every
breach triggers both.

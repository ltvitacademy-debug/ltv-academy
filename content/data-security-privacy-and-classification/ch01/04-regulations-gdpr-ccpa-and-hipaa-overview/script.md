# Lesson 4 — Regulations: GDPR, CCPA and HIPAA Overview · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

This is an orientation overview, not legal advice. Three regulations
that shape almost every data classification scheme you'll touch: GDPR,
CCPA, and HIPAA. What each one protects, and the one contrast that
makes them click.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (GDPR)

GDPR protects the personal data of people in the EU, no matter where
the processing company is based. It requires a documented legal basis
for processing, grants rights like access, erasure, and portability,
demands breach notice within 72 hours, and backs it all with fines up
to 20 million euros or 4% of global revenue.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (CCPA/CPRA + the contrast)

CCPA, expanded by CPRA, gives California consumers the right to know,
delete, and opt out of the sale of their data. Here's the distinction
worth remembering: GDPR is opt-in — processing needs permission first.
CCPA is opt-out — collection is allowed by default until the consumer
says stop.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (HIPAA)

HIPAA protects Protected Health Information held by covered entities —
health plans, clearinghouses, providers — and their business
associates. Its Privacy Rule governs use and disclosure, its Security
Rule covers electronic PHI specifically, and its Breach Notification
Rule requires telling affected people when something goes wrong.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

One takeaway to carry forward: GDPR opt-in, CCPA opt-out, HIPAA scoped
to health data specifically. Next lesson: SOX and the industry-specific
rules — PCI DSS, GLBA, FERPA — that create their own data obligations
outside privacy law entirely.

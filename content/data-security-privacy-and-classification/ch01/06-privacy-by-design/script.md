# Lesson 6 — Privacy by Design · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Privacy by Design isn't a law — it's a design philosophy. Seven
principles, developed by Ann Cavoukian, for building privacy into a
system's architecture from day one, instead of bolting it on after the
fact.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (principles 1-3)

Principle one: proactive, not reactive — prevent privacy problems
before they happen. Principle two: privacy as the default setting —
protection without the user having to ask for it. Principle three:
privacy embedded into design — a core part of the architecture, not an
add-on feature.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (principles 4-6)

Principle four: full functionality — positive-sum, not zero-sum. You
shouldn't have to trade away features or security to get privacy.
Principle five: end-to-end security, across the entire data lifecycle.
Principle six: visibility and transparency — practices that are
verifiable, not hidden in fine print.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (principle 7 + Article 25)

Principle seven: respect for user privacy — stay user-centric. And
here's where this becomes law: GDPR Article 25 requires "data
protection by design and by default" — this exact philosophy, written
directly into binding EU regulation.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Retrofitting privacy into a system never built for it is expensive and
incomplete. Building these seven principles in from day one makes
privacy a property of the architecture itself. That closes out this
chapter — Chapter 2 moves into how sensitive data actually gets
classified.

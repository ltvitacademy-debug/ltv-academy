# Lesson 10 — Verifying Current Certification Requirements · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson closes the chapter with the one habit that matters more than anything else in it: checking, instead of trusting memory.

## S2 · STEPS — A real example

While building this very course, the credential most people call Salesforce Certified Administrator turned out to be listed on Trailhead's live site today as Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator. Neither name is wrong -- it's that the live catalog moves, and course material can lag behind it.

## S3 · STEPS — Preparation, not certification

LTV Academy prepares you for the skills and objectives behind these credentials. It does not issue them. Salesforce issues its own certifications, through its own proctored exam, registered on Trailhead.

## S4 · STEPS — The checklist

Before you register for anything: go straight to Trailhead's live credentials page. Find your exact role. Read the current exam guide in full. Check pricing and the retake policy live. And re-check again shortly before you actually schedule.

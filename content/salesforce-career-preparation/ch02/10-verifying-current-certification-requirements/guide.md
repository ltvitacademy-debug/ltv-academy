## A real example of drift

While building this chapter, the credential most students know informally as "Salesforce Certified Administrator" — including how it's referred to elsewhere in this course's own career-path roadmap — turned out to be listed on Trailhead's live site today as **Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator**. Neither name is wrong, exactly; it's that Salesforce renames, restructures, and updates its certification catalog on its own schedule, and course material, blog posts, and even official-sounding roadmaps can lag behind the live source without anyone intending to mislead you.

This isn't a one-time fact to memorize. It's a demonstration of the actual habit this lesson teaches: the names, prerequisites, exam structure, and pricing you need are only ever reliable at the moment you check them directly.

## LTV Academy does not issue Salesforce certifications

This course, and this entire path, prepares you for the skills and objectives behind these credentials — it does not issue them, and completing it does not automatically earn you one. Salesforce certifications are earned directly through Salesforce's own proctored exams, registered and scheduled through Trailhead. Treat everything in Chapter 2 as preparation and strategy, and treat Trailhead itself as the only authoritative source for whether you've actually earned a credential.

## A verification checklist, before you register for anything

1. **Go to trailhead.salesforce.com/credentials directly.** Not a search result, not a third-party blog, not this course — the live, current catalog.
2. **Find the exact credential for your target role**, using the role-based browser covered in Lesson 8, and open its own "About the Exam" page.
3. **Read the current exam guide in full** — objectives, prerequisites, question format, time limit, and passing score. This page is maintained by Salesforce and updates when Salesforce updates it; nothing you read in this course should override it.
4. **Check current registration pricing and retake policy directly on Trailhead** (or with Salesforce support) before you pay for anything — pricing and retake terms are exactly the kind of detail this course won't quote, because it changes.
5. **Re-check shortly before you actually schedule**, even if you checked a few months ago. Salesforce ships multiple releases a year, and exam content can shift with them.

## Why this is the last lesson in this chapter, not an afterthought

It would be easy to end a certification chapter on a confident, specific checklist — "here's exactly what's on the exam." That confidence would be fake the moment Salesforce updates the page this chapter is based on. Ending here, with the habit of checking instead of a memorized fact, is the version of this chapter that's still accurate a year from now.

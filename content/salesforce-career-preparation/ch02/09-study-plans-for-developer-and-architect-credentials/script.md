# Lesson 9 — Study Plans for Developer and Architect Credentials · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

The same study discipline from last lesson, now applied to the Developer and Architect tracks -- where the credentials themselves change.

## S2 · STEPS — The Developer track

Platform Developer assesses Apex and Visualforce -- extending the platform with real code. Platform Developer II goes further into advanced programmatic work and data modeling, and Trailhead lists Platform Developer as its required prerequisite.

## S3 · CODE — Study the way you'll work

Study this track the way you'll actually work. Create a scratch org with the sf CLI, push your source, then write Apex and real unit tests, push, review, and repeat.

## S4 · STEPS — Architect domains

Above that sit the Architect domain credentials, each covering one focused area: Data Architect, Sharing and Visibility Architect, Integration Architect, and Identity and Access Management Architect.

## S5 · STEPS — Experience matters more here

A trail can teach you what a sharing rule does. It can't replace having designed one under real constraints -- contradictory requirements, a legacy system you can't change, a deadline that won't move. Treat this tier as a years-long target, not a post-course checklist.

## S6 · OUTRO

Next lesson: the habit that keeps everything in this chapter accurate -- verifying current certification requirements yourself.

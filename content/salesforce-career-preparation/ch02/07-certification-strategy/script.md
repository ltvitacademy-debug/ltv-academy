# Lesson 7 — Certification Strategy · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Certifications aren't meant to be taken in any order you like. This lesson covers how to sequence them sensibly.

## S2 · STEPS — Order matters

Each Salesforce credential assumes the skills of the ones before it. Skip the foundation and jump to an advanced exam, and you either fail it, or pass it without actually being able to do the job -- which catches up with you fast.

## S3 · STEPS — A realistic sequence

The foundation is shared by both destinations: Administrator, then Platform App Builder. From there, the Technical Architect track adds a Developer credential, then the Architect domain credentials. The Data Analyst track shifts toward SQL, SOQL, and analytics instead of stacking more platform credentials.

## S4 · STEPS — Are you ready?

Before you schedule an exam, check three things. Can you rebuild the core objectives hands-on, not just recognize the right answer? Did your capstone work actually cover those objectives? And are you using practice exams as a readiness check, not as the study plan itself?

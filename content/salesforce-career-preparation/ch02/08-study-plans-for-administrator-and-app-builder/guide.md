## The foundation credentials, as they exist today

Trailhead's live certification catalog currently lists three credentials directly relevant to this stage of your path: **Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator**, **Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator II** (a follow-on credential for seasoned admins), and **Salesforce Certified Platform App Builder**. Each has its own "About the Exam" page on Trailhead, with a curated Trailmix or Trail built specifically to prepare for it.

## A study plan for Platform Administrator

1. **Start from what you've already built.** Your LTV Customer Management System capstone already covers a meaningful share of this exam's real territory: objects and fields, security (profiles, roles, sharing), data management, and reports and dashboards.
2. **Follow Trailhead's own curated preparation path.** Trailhead's current catalog links a Trailmix built specifically to prepare for the Platform Administrator credential — treat it as a checklist of topics to confirm you can do hands-on, not just read about.
3. **Rebuild weak areas from scratch in a Trailhead Playground.** For any topic that still feels unfamiliar, don't just re-read it — configure it yourself, with no guide open.
4. **Use a practice assessment close to your target date**, as a readiness check rather than your primary study method.

## A study plan for Platform App Builder

1. **Build on, don't repeat, your Administrator prep.** Platform App Builder assumes the same security and data-model fluency as Platform Administrator, then adds declarative application design: custom objects purpose-built for an app, process automation choices, and UI considerations (page layouts, Lightning App Builder, mobile).
2. **Follow Trailhead's own curated Trail for this credential** the same way you did for Platform Administrator — Trailhead's catalog currently links one built specifically for App Builder preparation.
3. **Treat your capstone's custom objects as your case study.** Walk through why you chose each object, field type, and relationship the way you did — that reasoning is exactly what this exam tests.

## Why this lesson doesn't quote exam numbers

A certification's exact question count, time limit, passing score, and domain-weighting percentages live on that credential's own "About the Exam" page on Trailhead — and that page is a dynamic, JavaScript-rendered view that updates when Salesforce updates it. Quoting a specific number here risks teaching you something that's already gone stale by the time you read it. The discipline that actually matters is knowing exactly where to look — which is Lesson 10, immediately after Lesson 9 covers the Developer and Architect tracks.

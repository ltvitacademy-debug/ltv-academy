# Lesson 13 — Consultant Interviews · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Consultant interviews test something different from either Administrator or Developer interviews: can you run a client conversation and turn it into a real solution.

## S2 · STEPS — A different kind of interview

Administrator interviews test configuration judgment. Developer interviews test code. Consultant interviews test communication and judgment first -- technical depth still matters, but it's rarely the main event.

## S3 · STEPS — How do you gather requirements?

A strong answer walks a repeatable process: identify stakeholders across every affected department, run discovery workshops before proposing anything, document functional and non-functional requirements in detail, prioritize by business impact and timeline, and validate against scope before build work starts.

## S4 · STEPS — Ambiguous and non-technical stakeholders

Two classic tests: a stakeholder who isn't sure what they need -- the move is asking good clarifying questions, not guessing. And a non-technical stakeholder -- the move is understanding their business process first, then translating it into Salesforce concepts yourself.

## S5 · STEPS — When a request breaks best practice

Acknowledge the request, explain the specific downside in terms the client cares about -- cost, risk, maintainability -- and offer an alternative that gets them most of what they actually need. Just saying "that's against best practice" reads as rigid, not expert.

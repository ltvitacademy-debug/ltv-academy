# Lesson 19 — Long-Term Path Toward CTA · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This is the final lesson of the entire Salesforce Administrator path. It covers the long road toward Certified Technical Architect, and where things go from here.

## S2 · STEPS — The full journey

From the Administrator and Platform App Builder foundation you already have, the Technical Architect direction adds a Developer credential, then the Architect domain credentials -- data, sharing and visibility, integration, identity and access management, and development lifecycle and deployment -- then the Application Architect and System Architect milestones, and finally the CTA review board itself.

## S3 · STEPS — A destination, not a fast track

You don't finish a curriculum and instantly become a Technical Architect. These credentials describe a body of knowledge; CTA additionally requires judgment built from real production experience, over real years on the job. That's not meant to discourage you -- it's meant to keep your expectations accurate.

## S4 · STEPS — You just finished the whole path

This is the last lesson of the entire Salesforce Administrator path -- Foundations, Administration, Declarative Development, your capstone, and this career-prep course. That's a complete, real body of work, worth recognizing before looking at what's next.

## S5 · STEPS — Where the path forks

One destination opens up from here: Salesforce Architect. It forks into two converging ladders, both built on what you just finished -- a Data & Analytics Architect ladder, which goes deeper into SQL, reporting, and analytics tooling; and a Technical Architect ladder, which continues the certification journey just covered toward CTA.

## S6 · STEPS — How to decide, not which one wins

If you loved reports, dashboards, and asking what the data tells you, the Data & Analytics Architect ladder plays to that. If you loved the security-model and automation decisions and the scenario thinking from Chapter 3, the Technical Architect ladder plays to that. Either is a real ladder toward the same destination -- check the catalog's current Salesforce Architect page for the specific course sequence before you commit.

## S7 · OUTRO

You've completed the Salesforce Administrator path. Where you go next is your call.

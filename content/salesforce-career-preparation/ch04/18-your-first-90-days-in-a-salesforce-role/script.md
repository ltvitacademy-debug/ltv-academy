# Lesson 18 — Your First 90 Days in a Salesforce Role · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

The first 90 days in a new Salesforce role follow a fairly predictable arc. This lesson covers it month by month, and the mistakes that cost new hires trust early.

## S2 · STEPS — Days 1-30: learn before you touch

Meet the people who actually touch Salesforce across departments. Run the setup audit trail and save it. Study the real data model and the security model -- who can see what -- before changing any of it. Shadow existing tickets if you can.

## S3 · STEPS — Days 30-60: small, well-scoped wins

Start taking small, real tasks -- a report, a minor field, a simple automation fix. The goal isn't proving you can rebuild the org. It's building trust by listening to real pain points and fixing something real without breaking anything else.

## S4 · STEPS — Days 60-90: own something, propose carefully

Own a small project end to end. Start proposing improvements, carefully, grounded in what you actually learned -- not a generic best practice you read somewhere. Document as you go; it pays off for the rest of your time there.

## S5 · STEPS — Common early mistakes

Changing production directly because something felt small. Stacking new automation on top of logic nobody explained to you first. Trying to re-architect everything in week one. And going quiet instead of asking why something works the way it does.

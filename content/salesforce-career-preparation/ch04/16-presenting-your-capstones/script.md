# Lesson 16 — Presenting Your Capstones · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

You'll present the same capstone to very different audiences. This lesson covers a structure that scales, using the LTV Customer Management System as the worked example.

## S2 · STEPS — One project, three audiences

A recruiter wants the business problem and outcome in plain language, fast. A hiring manager wants the decisions and tradeoffs behind it. A technical panel wants the actual detail -- the real security model, the real automation, and the reasoning behind both. Have all three versions ready.

## S3 · STEPS — A structure that scales

Start with the business problem Cascade Foodservice Equipment Co. was actually facing. Walk the solution design -- the objects and why each exists. Cover the decisions that mattered -- your security model and automation choices, with reasoning. Show the result -- the reports and dashboards. And close with what you'd do differently at scale.

## S4 · STEPS — Demo tips

Narrate while you click, so the reviewer follows your reasoning instead of your mouse. Keep a backup plan -- screenshots or a short recording -- in case the live org has a bad moment. And know your own project cold, so a "why didn't you use a Flow here" question gets a real answer, not a guess.

## S5 · STEPS — Connecting back to your portfolio

This is the live, spoken version of the portfolio README and case study from Lesson 4. Consistent framing across your portfolio page, your resume bullet, and what you actually say in the room makes the whole story easier to follow and remember.

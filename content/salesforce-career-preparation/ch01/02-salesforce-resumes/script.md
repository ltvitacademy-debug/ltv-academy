# Lesson 2 — Salesforce Resumes · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson turns your LTV Customer Management System capstone into resume bullets that actually get a callback.

## S2 · STEPS — The pattern

Every strong bullet names three things: the action you took, the artifact you actually built, and the Salesforce tool or feature you used. "Worked on Salesforce" tells a reviewer nothing they can follow up on.

## S3 · CODE — Before and after

Here's the same work, described two ways. "Worked on automating our sales process" says nothing. "Built a 5-step Record-Triggered Flow that auto-assigns Leads by territory and SLA" gives a reviewer something real to ask about in an interview.

## S4 · STEPS — One-page structure

Header with a linked Trailhead profile. Your capstone project, broken into named deliverables. Skills grouped by category. Then experience and education -- listing only certifications you've actually earned.

## S5 · STEPS — Common mistakes

Vague verbs that say nothing. Listing a certification before you've passed it. Blurring who designed a decision versus who implemented it. And numbers that don't match between your resume and your portfolio.

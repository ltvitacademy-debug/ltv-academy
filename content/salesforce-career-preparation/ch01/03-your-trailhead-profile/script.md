# Lesson 3 — Your Trailhead Profile · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Your Trailhead profile works as a second resume -- one a hiring manager can check directly instead of just trusting.

## S2 · SCREENSHOT — A public profile

This is a real Trailhead profile page: rank, badge count, points, trails completed, and community activity, all public. Setting your own profile to Public makes this same page visible to anyone with the link.

## S3 · SCREENSHOT — The rank ladder

Every badge earns points toward a rank, from Scout at zero up through Double Star Ranger at two hundred badges and beyond. But rank is a volume signal, not a relevance signal -- don't chase it for its own sake.

## S4 · SCREENSHOT — Superbadges

A Superbadge is different from a regular badge. It's a longer, hands-on, scenario-based challenge graded against a real Salesforce org -- which makes it the hardest credential on this page to fake.

## S5 · STEPS — What a reviewer scans for

A reviewer isn't scanning for your total badge count. They're looking for badges and trails relevant to the role, any Superbadges you've completed, your earned certifications, and whether your profile is even set to Public in the first place.

# Lesson 1 — Salesforce Career Paths and Roles · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

You've built the foundation. This lesson maps the real Salesforce ecosystem roles, and shows you exactly where this course fits in your path.

## S2 · STEPS — The ecosystem

Five roles make up this ecosystem. Administrator configures the platform declaratively. App Builder extends that into full custom applications. Developer extends it further with Apex and Lightning Web Components. Consultant turns business needs into a solution. Architect designs how it all holds together at scale.

## S3 · STEPS — Where this course sits

You're reading this at the end of the Administrator path. You've covered Salesforce fundamentals, full administration, declarative development, and built a real capstone -- the LTV Customer Management System.

## S4 · STEPS — Two destinations

From here, this path branches two ways. Salesforce Data Analyst adds SQL, SOQL, and the enterprise analytics stack. Salesforce Technical Architect adds Apex, Lightning Web Components, and the architecture credentials toward CTA.

# Lesson 4 — Portfolio Development · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Your Salesforce org isn't public the way a GitHub repo is. This lesson turns your capstone into a portfolio piece that shows the work anyway.

## S2 · STEPS — The next best thing to a demo

A portfolio is the next best thing to a live demo: the business problem you solved, screenshots of the real configuration, and the reasoning behind it -- the why a resume bullet never has room for.

## S3 · STEPS — Walking through the capstone

Structure it around the same deliverables your capstone produced: the data model and why you chose each relationship type, the security model and the access boundaries it enforces, the automation and the manual process it replaced, and the reports and dashboards, with the decision each one supports.

## S4 · STEPS — What to redact

Treat your portfolio like a real client org. Your capstone already uses fictional LTV Global data, not a real company's. And never publish live login credentials -- screenshots and a read-only walkthrough are enough.

Next lesson: GitHub for Salesforce developers -- where the actual metadata belongs, version-controlled, alongside this narrative portfolio page.

# Script — Project 1 Kickoff: The Business Questions

## Segment 1 (title)

Project One is a guided sales pipeline analysis. I'll show the approach; you'll reproduce it in your own org. Nothing new is taught. The test is applying what you already know, together.

## Segment 2 (steps: the scenario)

The company is Alder and Vale Systems, a fictional mid-market software company running Sales Cloud and Service Cloud. Six reps: Maya Ortiz, Dev Patel, Lena Fischer, Marcus Reid, Priya Nair, and Tomas Silva. The team quota is nine million dollars a year, or two-point-two-five million a quarter. You're the new Salesforce Data Analyst. Every number here is illustrative.

## Segment 3 (steps: five questions)

The VP of Sales wants five answers. Which lead sources actually convert? Does open pipeline cover the gap to quota? How is each rep pacing? What's our typical deal size and sales cycle? And which open deals have gone quiet? One question per report keeps the dashboard honest.

## Segment 4 (code: definitions)

Define before you build. Open pipeline means Closed is false. Win rate is won divided by won plus lost, by count, over four completed quarters. Coverage is open pipeline closing this quarter divided by the remaining gap. Attainment is won this quarter divided by quota. Cycle is created date to close date, on won deals.

## Segment 5 (code: baseline)

Here's the baseline to check yourself against. This quarter, one-point-seven-one million won against a two-point-two-five million quota. That's a five-hundred-forty-thousand-dollar gap, with one-point-three-five million of open pipeline closing this quarter. Thirty-five percent win rate, a forty-five thousand dollar average deal, and a fifty-seven day cycle. Your org's values will differ, and that's fine. The method is what's being checked.

## Segment 6 (steps: the plan)

The plan. Lesson five connects the objects. Six covers pipeline and conversion. Seven, rep performance. Eight, deal size and cycle. Nine assembles the dashboard and the presentation.

## Segment 7 (outro)

First, connecting Leads, Accounts, Contacts, and Opportunities. Keep your own notes as you go; they become your write-up.

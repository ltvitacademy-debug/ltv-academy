# Script — Next Steps Toward Analytics Engineering

## Segment 1 (title)

You've reached the end of the Job-Ready stage of the Salesforce Data Analyst path. You can query Salesforce with SOQL, build reports and dashboards, work in Tableau and Excel, and present three projects for Alder and Vale. This lesson looks ahead.

## Segment 2 (steps: ladder)

A typical ladder looks like this. Salesforce data analyst. Senior analyst. CRM Analytics developer. Then analytics engineer, and beyond. Titles differ between employers, so read job descriptions for the skills, not just the name.

## Segment 3 (steps: Advanced stage)

The Advanced stage has three courses. Salesforce CRM Analytics and Tableau Next picks up where native reporting's limits begin. Snowflake is a cloud data warehouse where CRM data joins other business data at scale. And dbt turns raw data into tested, documented, reusable models, using SQL you already know.

## Segment 4 (code: why analytics engineering)

Why analytics engineering? Look at Project 3. Blending three objects, cleaning at scale, keeping quota current, and storing history are data-modeling problems. An analytics engineer solves them once, upstream, so every dashboard downstream agrees. Roles combining this stack can reach roughly 150 to 200 thousand or more in some markets. A signal, not a guarantee.

## Segment 5 (steps: action plan)

Here's your action plan. This week, finish your portfolio write-ups using sample data only. Over the next two weeks, apply and rehearse your project stories and interview answers. Meanwhile, start the Advanced stage. And consider the Salesforce Certified Tableau Data Analyst credential as a supporting signal.

## Segment 6 (code: foundation)

A last word. A portfolio of real work usually counts for more than any single credential. You've built three projects, practiced presenting them, and prepared for the interview. That's a real foundation.

## Segment 7 (outro)

Congratulations. You've completed Salesforce Analytics Career and Capstone, and with it the Job-Ready stage of the Salesforce Data Analyst path. Next up: the Advanced stage, beginning with Salesforce CRM Analytics and Tableau Next.

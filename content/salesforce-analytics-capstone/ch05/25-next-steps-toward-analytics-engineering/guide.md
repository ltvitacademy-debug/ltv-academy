## What you have built

By now you can turn a business question into a Salesforce report or dashboard, write SOQL, clean and combine leads, opportunities and cases, and explain the result to an executive. You also learned where native tools stop: each dashboard component draws on one report, history needs snapshots, and blending sources is hard. Those limits are exactly the pointers to what comes next.

## The ladder

A typical progression looks like this:

1. **Salesforce Data Analyst:** reports, dashboards and analysis inside the CRM.
2. **Senior Salesforce Data Analyst:** larger scope, more independence, mentoring.
3. **CRM Analytics Developer:** building advanced analytics inside Salesforce's analytics platform.
4. **Salesforce Analytics Engineer:** engineering the data platform behind the analytics.
5. **Senior Analytics Engineer and beyond:** ownership of models, quality and architecture.

Titles differ between employers, and the same job may carry different names. Read job descriptions for the skills rather than the title.

## The Advanced stage

The Advanced stage of this path contains three courses:

- **Salesforce CRM Analytics & Tableau Next:** picks up exactly where native reporting's limits begin, with more powerful dashboards and analytics on Salesforce data.
- **Snowflake:** a cloud data warehouse, where CRM data is combined with other business data at scale.
- **dbt / Analytics Engineering:** transforming raw data into tested, documented, reusable models using SQL you already know.

Together they move you from consuming data to shaping it.

## Why analytics engineering?

Consider the problems in Project 3. Blending leads, opportunities and cases, cleaning data at scale, keeping a quota value current, and storing history are all data-modeling problems. An analytics engineer solves them once, upstream, so every dashboard downstream is consistent. Employers often pay more for the ability to do that, and this path's career overview notes that roles combining CRM Analytics, Data Cloud, Tableau Next, Snowflake and dbt can reach roughly $150K to $200K or more in some markets. Treat that as a broad market signal, not a guarantee.

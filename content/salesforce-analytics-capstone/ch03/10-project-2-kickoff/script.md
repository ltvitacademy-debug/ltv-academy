# Script — Project 2 Kickoff: The Business Questions

## Segment 1 (title)

Welcome to Project 2. Same portfolio shape as Project 1, but with less hand-holding. This time you get the goal and one worked example, and you make more of the decisions yourself.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Meet the client. Alder and Vale Systems has four support agents: Ines Costa, Ravi Menon, Hannah Berg, and Omar Aziz. The Head of Support has three worries. The queue feels like it's growing. Customers say answers take too long. And she can't tell whether the team needs a hire or a better process. All numbers here are illustrative.

## Segment 3 (code)

Turn each worry into a question with a measure, a population, and a time window. Growing queue becomes: how many cases are open at quarter end versus quarter start? Slow answers becomes: how long to first reply, and to closure, by priority? Happy customers becomes: average score, and how many customers actually responded.

## Segment 4 (code)

Now define every measure before you build. Backlog means cases not yet closed at period end. Resolution time is closed date minus created date, and you must say calendar or business hours. First response time may not exist in a standard field, so check whether the org tracks it. Satisfaction is usually a custom field or survey, so always show the response count beside the score.

## Segment 5 (steps)

Here's what changes from Project 1. Lessons describe the goal and show one example. You choose the filters, groupings, and charts, and you justify them. Expect to get stuck and to look things up. That's the point.

## Segment 6 (code)

Your finish line is a folder of reports, one service dashboard, a one-page definitions sheet, and a five-minute readout. Every dashboard number must reconcile to a source report. If it doesn't, you're not done.

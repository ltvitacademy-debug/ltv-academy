# Script — Project 3 Kickoff: Minimal Hand-Holding

## Segment 1 (title)

Project 3. This is the one that looks most like real work, so the training wheels come off. You get a brief and a few hints. The rest is your judgment.

## Segment 2 (code)

Here's the brief from the VP of Sales at Alder and Vale. One CRM dashboard to open every Monday. Are we generating enough leads? Will the pipeline get us to quota? Are service problems putting deals at risk? One page, and the numbers must be right. That's the whole specification.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Notice what's missing. No report list, no field names, no chart types. Turn the three questions into requirements. Leads: enough compared to what? Pipeline: that's coverage, pipeline against the remaining gap. Service risk: that crosses objects, connecting cases to opportunities through the account.

## Segment 4 (code)

Sanity-check facts, all illustrative. Annual quota is nine million across six reps, so an even quarter is 2.25 million. This quarter: 1.5 million won, 2.1 million open pipeline, 3,600 leads, 540 converted, 210 open cases, 88 percent SLA compliance. If your pipeline report says twenty-one million, something is wrong.

## Segment 5 (steps)

Hints, not steps. Sketch the page on paper first: three headline numbers, one supporting chart each. Ask which objects you need and how they connect. Decide what would change the VP's mind, and drop numbers nobody can act on. Expect messy data. Reconcile everything.

## Segment 6 (code)

You're finished when one dashboard answers all three questions on a single screen, every number reconciles to a report or query, a definitions sheet states each measure and limitation, and you can explain each decision in one sentence. If you're stuck twenty minutes, search the docs, revisit earlier courses, then ask. Log what you tried.

# Lesson 57 — Incident Response for Data Pipelines · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Incident response for data pipelines — the five stages every real
incident actually moves through.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (five stages, in order)

Detect, triage, mitigate, resolve, review. Naming these explicitly
is what keeps a stressed team from skipping one — most commonly,
mitigation.

## S3 · CODE CARD (mitigation vs. resolution)

Mitigation stops the bleeding right now, even imperfectly —
pausing a spamming alert, rerouting around a broken piece.
Resolution is the real fix, which takes much longer to find and
verify safely.

## S4 · OUTRO CARD (communication + what "over" means)

Someone also needs to be actively communicating what's happening —
separate work from the technical fix, often assigned to a
different person entirely so neither task starves the other.

An incident isn't over until resolution actually lands and
verification confirms it. Next up: root cause analysis, step by
step — what resolution actually requires finding.

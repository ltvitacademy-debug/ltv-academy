# Lesson 64 — Capacity Planning · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Capacity planning — looking ahead, not just reacting to today's
measured load.

## S2 · CODE CARD (two shapes of "ahead of time")

Gradual growth, like a dashboard rolling out to three times as
many dispatchers, gets projected forward. A known peak night, like
New Year's Eve, gets temporary, deliberate overprovisioning
instead.

## S3 · CODE CARD (forecasting from a trend)

A single day's utilization number says nothing about direction. A
ninety-day rolling view shows whether consumption is flat,
growing, or accelerating — the real signal to plan ahead.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (closing the loop back to cost)

Without planning, a SKU bump is reactive, discovered as a surprise
bill. With planning, the cost of growth is a deliberate, budgeted
decision made in advance.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

The future stops being a surprise. Next up: blue-green and canary
deployments — rolling out a change safely, at scale.

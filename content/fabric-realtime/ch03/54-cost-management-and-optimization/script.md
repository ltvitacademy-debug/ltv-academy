# Lesson 54 — Cost Management and Optimization · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Cost management and optimization — turning capacity units into an
actual conversation about spend.

## S2 · CODE CARD (what actually drives cost)

A hopping window recomputes overlapping aggregates, touching each
event several times — that's real cost, not just an abstract
tradeoff. Long retention on a KQL database costs storage
indefinitely, whether or not anyone ever queries it.

## S3 · CODE CARD (retention policies)

A retention policy ages out data past a defined point
automatically. Thirty days might be generous for a dashboard, or
necessary for compliance — the right number depends on how the
data actually gets used.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (cost-aware means knowing why)

Knowing the bill was four thousand two hundred dollars tells you
almost nothing. Knowing sixty percent of it is one hopping-window
tile, and switching it would cut the bill in half — that's
actually being cost-aware.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Know why, not just how much. Next up: right-sizing compute —
matching capacity to actual need.

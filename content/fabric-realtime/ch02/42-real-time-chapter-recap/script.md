# Lesson 42 — Real-Time Chapter Recap · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Real-time chapter recap — twenty-five lessons, one continuous idea:
how do you compute correct, timely answers over data that never
stops arriving.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (ingestion and querying)

Eventstreams moved data from a source through transformations to a
destination. Eventhouse and KQL databases gave that data somewhere
built for streaming to land. And KQL itself let you actually ask
questions of it — filtering, aggregating, joining, and two real
ways to get data in, streaming or batch.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (windows, watermarks, shaping the stream)

Real-time dashboards put live tiles in front of people. Three
window shapes — tumbling, hopping, and session — each answered a
different question. Watermarks and out-of-order handling made
lateness a deliberate tradeoff instead of an accident. And
eventstream transformations shaped data before it ever landed.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (reacting, syncing, comparing)

Activator turned a rule into a real action. Streamed CDC and data
quality checks kept things in sync and caught bad data early.
Comparing to databricks showed the same computer science under a
different label. And lesson forty-one chained every piece into one
working pipeline.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

None of this complexity was arbitrary — it's what always-on
actually requires. Next up: chapter three, production data
engineering — running what you built, reliably, at scale.

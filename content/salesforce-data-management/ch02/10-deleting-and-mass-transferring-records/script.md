# Script — Deleting and Mass Transferring Records

## Segment 1 (title)

Not every bulk operation is about getting data in or out. Sometimes the job is cleaning up or reorganizing what's already there — Mass Delete Records and Mass Transfer Records are Setup's built-in tools for exactly that.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Mass Delete Records setup)

From Setup, search Mass Delete Records and pick a record type. The page walks you through a plain-language explanation of what gets deleted, a recommendation to export your data first, and a criteria builder to filter down to exactly the records you mean to delete.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: criteria and results, permanently delete)

Run your search, review the matches, and select the ones you actually want gone. The checkbox that changes everything is Permanently delete the selected records — leave it unchecked and deleted records land in the Recycle Bin; check it, and they skip the Recycle Bin entirely, unrecoverable, immediately.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Mass Transfer Records list)

Mass Transfer Records works differently. From Setup, search for it, and you'll find a much longer list — dozens of record types, each with its own dedicated transfer screen.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: Mass Transfer Accounts form)

Pick the current owner and the new owner, then use a criteria builder to find records to move. The checkboxes above it matter more than they look — transferring an Account doesn't automatically bring its open or closed opportunities and cases along. You decide, per transfer, what goes with it.

## Segment 6 (steps: narrow, review, act)

Both tools share a philosophy: narrow with criteria, review the actual matches, then act. The safer default — Recycle Bin for deletes, related records staying put for transfers — should never be something you override by accident.

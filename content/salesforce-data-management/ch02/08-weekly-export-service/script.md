# Script — Weekly Export Service

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 7 covered running an export manually. This lesson covers scheduling exports to run automatically, so backups happen on a reliable cadence without anyone remembering to click a button.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Data Export page, Schedule Export)

From the same Data Export page, click Schedule Export instead of Export Now. The configuration screen is nearly identical to the manual export's options, with one addition: a frequency and start-date setting for when the export should run going forward.

## Segment 3 (steps: weekly vs monthly by edition)

The frequency you get depends on edition. Enterprise, Performance, and Unlimited Edition orgs can schedule a weekly export. Other editions are limited to monthly. Either way, once scheduled, it runs on its own, without anyone needing to come back to this page.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: scheduled export in progress)

Once a scheduled export kicks off, this page updates to show it's in progress, along with who scheduled it and when. You'll also get an email with a download link — and the same 48-hour download window from Lesson 7 applies here too.

## Segment 5 (steps: why automate it)

The entire point of automating this is removing the human step. A manual export depends on someone remembering to run it, usually right when they're focused on something else. A scheduled export exists whether or not anyone thinks about it that week.

# Lesson 1 — Program Overview & Portfolio Strategy · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Welcome to the capstone chapter that closes out the Blockchain Engineer
path. Everything you've learned so far — Solidity, DApp architecture,
DeFi mechanics, testing, and tooling — comes together here, in real
projects you build yourself.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (three flagship projects)

You'll build three flagship projects: a full DeFi protocol with pool
contracts and a frontend, an NFT marketplace with listings and indexing,
and a DAO governance system with voting and a treasury. A final career
chapter covers resumes, interviews, and negotiation.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (why project-based, now)

There's no fictional company running through this course. You build each
project yourself, under your own name, in your own repository — because
the whole point of a capstone is producing something that's genuinely
yours to show an employer.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (portfolio strategy)

Here's the strategy: two or three deep, complete projects beat a long
list of shallow ones. An interviewer skimming your GitHub is looking for
real depth — tests, documented tradeoffs, a working deployment — not a
pile of unfinished scripts.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (what "complete" means)

Complete means: commented contracts that flag their own teaching
simplifications, tests that include fuzz and invariant checks beyond the
happy path, a frontend that reads real on-chain state, a verified testnet
deployment, and a README that explains your actual design decisions.

## S6 · OUTRO CARD

Next: choosing which project to emphasize, based on the kind of
blockchain role you're actually aiming for.

# Lesson 2 — Choosing Your Project Emphasis · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

The three flagship projects share a lot of groundwork, but each one
leans on a different skill set. This lesson gives you a simple way to
decide which one to polish deepest.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what each project emphasizes)

The DeFi protocol leans on AMM math and economic security. The NFT
marketplace leans on escrow patterns, indexing, and marketplace UX. The
DAO leans on governance design — voting weight, quorum, and treasury
timelocks.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the framework)

Ask what role you're targeting. A protocol or smart-contract engineering
role points you toward the DeFi protocol. A full-stack Web3 role points
toward the NFT marketplace. A security or governance-leaning role points
toward the DAO.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (still build all three)

Choosing an emphasis doesn't mean skipping the other two. This course
builds the DeFi protocol first either way, because its testing and
deployment patterns get reused — emphasis just decides which project
gets the extra polish pass before you show it to an employer.

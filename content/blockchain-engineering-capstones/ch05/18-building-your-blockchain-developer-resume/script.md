# Lesson 18 — Building Your Blockchain Developer Resume · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson turns your three capstone projects into a resume built around blockchain's biggest advantage: anyone can verify your work for free.

## S2 · STEPS — Why verifiable matters

A deployed, verified smart contract is public and tamper-evident -- a stranger can check it in thirty seconds. A resume that doesn't point straight at that evidence is wasting its strongest asset.

## S3 · STEPS — The pattern

Every strong bullet names three things: the action, what you actually built, and where a reviewer can verify it. "Worked on a DeFi protocol" says nothing. "Built and deployed a lending pool, fuzz-tested, verified on Sepolia Etherscan" gives a reviewer something real to click.

## S4 · STEPS — One-page structure

Header with a GitHub link. An optional two-line summary. Your three capstone projects, each with proof-backed bullets and a verification link. Skills grouped by category. Then experience and education.

## S5 · STEPS — Common mistakes

A buzzword list with nothing linked. A verified contract link buried where no one finds it. Calling a local-only project "deployed." And numbers that don't match between your resume and your README.

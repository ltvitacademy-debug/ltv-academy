# Lesson 22 — Salary Negotiation & Next Steps · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This is it -- the final lesson of the entire Blockchain Engineer path. Let's close it out with salary research and a real look back at everything you've built.

## S2 · STEPS — Why blockchain pay varies so much

Blockchain titles aren't standardized at all -- Blockchain Developer, Smart Contract Engineer, Web3 Engineer can mean nearly the same job or very different ones. Company stage swings pay enormously. And token-based compensation adds a real variable cash pay simply doesn't have.

## S3 · STEPS — Building your range

Use the same toolkit this catalog always teaches: aggregated salary sites, government labor data, and live postings -- searched under several blockchain-role titles, noting whether a listing includes a token component. Build a low-to-high band from at least two sources.

## S4 · STEPS — Evaluating a real offer

Once you have an offer, look at the whole package -- base, token grant and its vesting schedule, signing bonus, flexibility, and the timeline to your first review. A respectful counter thanks them, states your researched range, and stays collaborative.

## S5 · STEPS — What you've built, part one

Look at everything you've built. JavaScript and TypeScript, the foundation. Blockchain Development -- Ethereum and Solidity. APIs and Backend -- the off-chain half of a dApp.

## S6 · STEPS — What you've built, part two

Then DeFi and Token Engineering -- AMMs, lending, governance mechanics. Testing, DevOps, and Deployment -- a real launch runbook. And this course -- three flagship projects and the job-search skills to present them.

## S7 · OUTRO

That's the whole path, complete. From your first line of JavaScript to a deployed, tested, verified DAO governance system with a portfolio built to prove it. Congratulations -- you're ready.

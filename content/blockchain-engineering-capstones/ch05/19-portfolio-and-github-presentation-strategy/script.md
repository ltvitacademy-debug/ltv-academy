# Lesson 19 — Portfolio & GitHub Presentation Strategy · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson turns your GitHub profile and your project READMEs into something a recruiter can actually evaluate in minutes, not hours.

## S2 · STEPS — Pin three, not thirty

Your six pinned repos are your portfolio's front page. Pin your three capstones from this course -- the DeFi protocol, the NFT marketplace, the DAO -- not a scatter of tutorial follow-alongs. Three complete, documented projects beat fifteen abandoned ones every time.

## S3 · STEPS — The README structure

A strong README front-loads five things: a one-sentence description, an architecture summary, a deployed and verified contract link, how to actually run the tests, and your key design decisions -- the one section a reviewer can't get anywhere else.

## S4 · STEPS — Etherscan as proof

A verified contract on a block explorer is the single highest-value link in a blockchain portfolio. It's public, it's permanent, and it's the one piece of evidence a reviewer can check without trusting your word for it at all.

## S5 · STEPS — Why a short demo video matters

Almost no reviewer will clone your repo and run it themselves. A two to three minute screen recording walking through your real, deployed UI gets you most of the benefit of a live demo, linked right at the top of your README.

# Script — Salary Negotiation Basics

## Segment 1 (title)

Salary negotiation starts long before an offer letter arrives. This lesson covers the fundamentals — researching a real range, avoiding giving a number first, and countering an offer respectfully for a first BI developer role.

## Segment 2 (steps: before the offer stage)

Research a realistic range for your role and market using at least two independent sources, know your own walk-away number, and avoid naming a figure first whenever you can — whoever names a number first anchors the rest of the conversation to it.

## Segment 3 (steps: answering the expectations question)

When asked what your salary expectations are, redirect to the budgeted range if you can, or answer with your researched range instead of a single number. Anchor your answer to real research, not a guess made on the spot.

## Segment 4 (code: a counter-offer script)

Here's what a respectful counter looks like in writing: thank them for the offer, state your researched range, ask specifically whether there's room to move on base or the sign-on bonus, and close by confirming you're ready to move forward once you're aligned. No ultimatums, no invented competing offers — just a clear, specific request.

# Script — Where to Go Next: An Overview of Advanced Tracks

## Segment 1 (title)

You finished a real, complete thing — a full pipeline from raw SQL Server data to a finished Power BI dashboard, built the way a real BI developer job would ask for it. This lesson is an honest look at what can come next, whenever you're ready for it.

## Segment 2 (steps: three honest directions)

Most BI developers who keep growing pick one of three directions, and they're not mutually exclusive: go deeper into the Microsoft BI stack you already know, branch into an adjacent data role, or pursue a relevant certification. None of this needs to be decided today.

## Segment 3 (steps: adjacent data roles)

If branching out appeals to you, a data engineer role builds on your ETL experience from SSIS, plus cloud pipeline tools and larger-scale processing. A data analyst role builds on your SSRS and Power BI reporting experience, plus deeper statistics and forecasting. A database administrator role builds on your SQL Server fundamentals, plus performance tuning and uptime.

## Segment 4 (steps: realistic certifications)

If a credential appeals to you, Microsoft certifications tied to Power BI and Azure data platform work build directly on what you already have hands-on experience in. Vendor-neutral data certifications and cloud platform certifications are other realistic options, depending on which direction you're leaning.

Next lesson, presenting your capstone — turning the finished pipeline you built into the story you tell in an interview or a portfolio review.

# Script — Behavioral Interview Basics

## Segment 1 (title)

Behavioral questions don't test whether you know SQL Server or Power BI. They test how you behave when something goes wrong on a real team — and this capstone just handed you real material to answer them with.

## Segment 2 (steps: the STAR method)

Structure every answer with STAR: Situation, the context; Task, what you were responsible for; Action, what you actually did; and Result, what happened. Result is the beat candidates skip most — say the outcome directly, with a number if you have one, instead of letting the story trail off.

## Segment 3 (steps: three real stories from this capstone)

You don't need invented workplace drama. Use a bug you had to debug in the pipeline, a design decision you had to defend — like why you modeled work orders the way you did — and a deadline or scope change you had to manage when the plan shifted mid-build. Write each one out in full STAR form before your first interview.

## Segment 4 (steps: delivering the answer)

Listen for the specific question being asked, lead with just enough Situation to orient the interviewer, and keep the whole answer under about two minutes. If you're still talking at three minutes, you've likely drifted into a second story — land the Result and stop.

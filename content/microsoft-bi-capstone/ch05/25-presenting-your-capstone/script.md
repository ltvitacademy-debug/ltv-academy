# Script — Presenting Your Capstone

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 4 built the materials a job search runs on. This lesson is where those materials meet the actual project you built — because the ability to walk someone through it clearly, in the right order, is a skill in its own right.

## Segment 2 (steps: the five-part structure)

Structure every walkthrough of this capstone the same way: the business problem, the architecture, the design decisions behind it, the finished result, and what you'd improve next. That last part isn't optional — interviewers ask it whether you volunteer it or not.

## Segment 3 (steps: what to screen-share)

Don't scroll through every package or script live. Show the architecture diagram to orient the viewer, one control flow view of WorkOrderETL, the rendered SSRS report, and the Power BI dashboard's trend and on-time visuals. Describe everything else in a sentence.

## Segment 4 (steps: scope as a strength)

Production.Location is the best example in this whole capstone of a decision worth explaining out loud. Don't say you didn't get to it — say it lives at a finer grain than the fact table you chose, and modeling it would have broken that grain. That's judgment, not a gap.

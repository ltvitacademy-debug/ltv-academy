# Script — Final Wrap-Up & Next Steps

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the last lesson of the Microsoft Data & BI Developer program. Every lesson before this one built toward one moment: a finished, working pipeline, and the ability to explain it to someone else.

## Segment 2 (steps: six courses, one pipeline)

T-SQL Development taught you to query and design the database. SSIS Development taught you to move data. Data Warehousing taught you the dimensional model underneath it all. SSRS and Power BI taught you to report and visualize it. This capstone is the first time all five had to agree with each other, on one business process none of them had already solved for you.

## Segment 3 (steps: what you walk away with)

You walk away with a real pipeline built against real AdventureWorks2012 data, a set of design decisions you made yourself — the fact table, its dimensions, its scope — and a three-minute walkthrough you can deliver confidently to anyone reviewing your work.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Congratulations on finishing the Microsoft Data & BI Developer program. This is a foundation, not a ceiling — LTV Academy's catalog has other advanced tracks and specializations waiting whenever you're ready to keep going.

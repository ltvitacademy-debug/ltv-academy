# Script — Roles on an Implementation Team

## Segment 1 (title)

Before going further into the lifecycle, you need to know who is actually in the room. An Oracle Fusion Financials implementation blends client-side business people with partner-side consultants, and the Functional Consultant, your seat, works alongside all of them.

## Segment 2 (steps)

On the client side, the Executive Sponsor owns the business case and resolves escalations, often the CFO on a Financials project. Business Process Owners and subject matter experts validate requirements and sign off on design. Super users support testing and become the first line of help after go-live. An Organizational Change Management lead handles communication and training readiness.

## Segment 3 (steps)

On the partner side, a Solution Architect owns the overall solution shape across modules. The Functional Consultant, the role this path trains you for, leads requirements, fit-gap, configuration, and testing for one module. A Technical Consultant handles integrations and extensions. A Data Migration Lead and a Testing Lead coordinate those cross-module activities.

## Segment 4 (steps)

The Functional Consultant isn't an island. They take requirements from business process owners, hand anything needing code to the technical consultant, and coordinate with the data migration and testing leads so their module's work lands in the same go-live date as everyone else's.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Brightfield Industrial Group staffs its team with a CFO sponsor, business process owners per module, four functional consultants, two technical consultants, a data migration lead, a shared testing lead, and its own OCM lead. Up next, lesson three: how this gets planned into phases and a schedule.

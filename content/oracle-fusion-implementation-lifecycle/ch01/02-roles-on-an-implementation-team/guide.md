# Roles on an Implementation Team

Lesson 1 introduced Oracle True Cloud Method and Oracle Cloud Success Navigator. Before going further into the lifecycle itself, you need to know who is actually in the room running it. An Oracle Fusion Financials implementation blends client-side business people with partner-side (or Oracle-side) consultants, and a functional consultant — your seat — works alongside all of them.

## What you'll learn

- The client-side roles who own the business and sign off on decisions
- The partner/consultant-side roles who design and build the solution
- Where the Functional Consultant role sits relative to everyone else
- How Brightfield Industrial Group staffed its implementation team

## Client-side roles

- **Executive Sponsor** — a senior leader (often the CFO on a Financials project) who owns the business case, resolves escalations, and makes final go/no-go calls at major milestones.
- **Project Manager (client side)** — co-manages the plan, budget, and resourcing from the customer's side, paired with a partner-side project manager.
- **Business Process Owners / Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)** — the people who actually run AP, AR, GL, or Cash Management today. They validate requirements, attend fit-gap workshops, and sign off on design documents.
- **Super Users / Power Users** — end users selected to go deeper than average, support User Acceptance Testing, and become the first line of help for colleagues after go-live.
- **Organizational Change Management (OCM) Lead** — manages communication, training readiness, and the human side of adopting a new system — separate from technical readiness.

## Partner/consultant-side roles

- **Project Manager (partner side)** — owns the delivery plan, status reporting, and risk/issue log on the implementation side.
- **Solution Architect** — owns the overall technical and functional solution shape across modules, makes sure module-level designs don't conflict with each other.
- **Functional Consultant** — the role this entire career path trains you for. Leads requirements gathering and fit-gap for a specific module (e.g., Cash Management), builds the configuration workbook, performs the Oracle setup, writes test scripts, and supports go-live and hypercare for that area.
- **Technical Consultant** — handles integrations (often built in Oracle Integration Cloud), extensions, reports, and data conversion scripts that go beyond standard configuration.
- **Data Migration Lead** — owns the end-to-end strategy for moving legacy data into Oracle Fusion, covered in Chapter 3.
- **Testing Lead / QA Lead** — coordinates test planning, SIT, and UAT execution across all modules, covered in Chapter 4.
- **Security Lead** — designs the role-based security model (duty roles, data roles, job roles) that controls who can do what in the new system.

## Where the Functional Consultant fits

A Functional Consultant isn't an island. They take requirements from Business Process Owners, hand fit-gap decisions that need code to the Technical Consultant, coordinate with the Data Migration Lead on what legacy data their module needs, and work with the Testing Lead to get their module's scripts into SIT and UAT. The Solution Architect and Project Manager keep all of those module-level threads moving together toward the same go-live date.

## Brightfield Industrial Group: the team

Brightfield's implementation pairs its own CFO (Executive Sponsor) and AP/AR/GL managers (Business Process Owners) with an implementation partner's team: one Project Manager, one Solution Architect, four Functional Consultants (one each for GL, AP, AR, and Cash Management), two Technical Consultants, a Data Migration Lead, and a shared Testing Lead. Brightfield also names an internal OCM Lead to manage training and communications on the client side.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Senior client leader who owns the business case and resolves escalations |
| Business Process Owner / SME | Client staff who validate requirements and sign off on design |
| Solution Architect | Owns the overall cross-module solution shape |
| Functional Consultant | Leads requirements, fit-gap, configuration, and testing for a specific module |
| OCM Lead | Manages change management, communication, and training readiness |

## Recap

An implementation team mixes client-side roles who own the business decisions with partner-side roles who design and build the solution. The Functional Consultant sits at the center of a module's work, coordinating with the Solution Architect, Technical Consultant, Data Migration Lead, and Testing Lead.

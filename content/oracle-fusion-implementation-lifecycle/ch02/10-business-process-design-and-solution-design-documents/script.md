# Script — Business Process Design and Solution Design Documents

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter two has moved from fit-gap through gap documentation, configuration workbooks, and enterprise structure decisions. This lesson pulls those threads together into the deliverables that close out TCM's Design phase: the Business Process Design and the Solution Design Document.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A Business Process Design narrates the future-state process end to end, in business terms, with flow diagrams showing each step, who performs it, and which Oracle screen it corresponds to. Where fit-gap found a gap, the BPD shows the resolved future-state step, so a process owner can confirm this is actually how they'll work.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A Solution Design Document goes one level more technical. For each gap resolved as configuration or extension, it specifies exactly how: which Oracle object is configured and how, or, for an extension, the technical approach with inputs, outputs, and any interface to another system. This is what a technical consultant builds from.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Neither document is new information — they're the formal, sign-off-ready synthesis of fit-gap results, the gap log, the configuration workbook, and the enterprise structure decisions. Signing off the BPD and SDD is the exit criterion for Design and the entry criterion for Configure, the phase gate introduced back in lesson three.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Brightfield's Cash Management BPD walks through statement import, automatic matching, and the custom Treasury report; the SDD specifies the matching rule configuration and the technical build for that report. Both get signed, closing Design and opening Configure. Up next, chapter three: Setup and Maintenance and the environments where configuration actually happens.

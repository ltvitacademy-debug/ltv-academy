# Lesson 3 — Identifying Critical Data Elements · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE CARD

With the landscape mapped, the next question is which specific
elements inside it actually need elevated governance. This lesson
scores LTV Global's candidates.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (the four criteria)

A critical data element earns extra attention by scoring high on at
least one of four criteria: regulatory impact, financial impact,
decision impact, and operational impact.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the scoring)

Four elements make the cut: Customers.Email, high on regulatory impact
and exactly what the Lesson 1 access request was about. Orders.OrderTotal,
feeding the board's revenue number. Products.SKU, keyed on by nearly
every fulfillment process. And Payments.CardToken, sitting inside PCI
scope regardless of query volume.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (why not all five)

Customers.MarketingOptIn stays on the ordinary data dictionary — it
matters, but getting it wrong risks an unwanted email, not a
regulatory finding or a board number. The discipline is naming the
few elements that justify extra rigor, not governing every column
equally.

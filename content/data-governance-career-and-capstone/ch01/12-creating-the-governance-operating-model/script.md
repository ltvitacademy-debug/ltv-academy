# Lesson 12 — Creating the Governance Operating Model · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Eleven lessons of deliverables need a permanent home. This lesson
chooses the operating model that houses all of it going forward.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (choosing a shape)

Fully centralized would make the small program team a bottleneck
across three regions. Fully decentralized is what already existed
before this program started. LTV Global lands on a federated hybrid
model instead: a small central office for standards, domain owners
keeping day-to-day authority.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the structure)

Four layers: Dana Whitfield as executive sponsor, Marcus Ibe's
governance office running the KPIs and glossary, the four domain
owners keeping Lesson 4's accountability, and their stewards doing
the daily work. A monthly Data Governance Council is where escalations
land.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (where everything lives now)

Nothing from this chapter was a one-time document. The landscape
inventory and glossary are maintained, not frozen. CDE scoring and
classification get revisited, as DateOfBirth already proved. Quality
rules, lineage, and access policy stay with their owners, reviewed
monthly.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

This closes the foundational half of the capstone. Next: Lesson 13
builds an AI governance strategy on top of the structure this lesson
just made permanent.

# Lesson 4 — Assigning Data Owners and Data Stewards · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Four critical data elements now have names attached to them: who owns
each one at the business level, and who does the daily work of
stewarding it.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (owner vs steward)

A data owner is accountable at the business level — usually a
director or VP who can settle a dispute or fund a fix. A data steward
does the daily work: fixing records, answering what a field actually
means, and flagging problems upward.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (the RACI)

For customer email specifically: the steward is Responsible for
flagging bad records, the owner is Accountable for conflict decisions
— but the moment a regulatory access request is involved, Chief Data
Officer Dana Whitfield becomes Accountable instead. The RACI makes
that handoff explicit before it's ever needed under pressure.

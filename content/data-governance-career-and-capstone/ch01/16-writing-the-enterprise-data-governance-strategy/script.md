# Lesson 16 — Writing the Enterprise Data Governance Strategy · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Fourteen files don't read themselves. This lesson writes the one
document that synthesizes them.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (package vs strategy)

Lesson 15's package is fourteen files, reference material, readable
in any order. This lesson's document is one narrative, read start to
finish. The test: could a new hire understand the whole program from
this alone?

## S3 · CODE CARD (document skeleton)

Six sections, in order: Executive Summary, Current State, Target
State, Roadmap, Governance Model, and Success Metrics.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (sections 1-2)

The Executive Summary is written last but read first — the six-week
DSAR, the mandate, what's been built. Current State says plainly what
existed before: no owners, no checks, no declared authority over
customer data.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (section 3)

Target State is Lesson 14's architecture: Atlas, Beacon, Comet, and
Harbor feeding a governed Summit, with Lesson 9's golden Customer
record and two AI pilots operating under the Lesson 13 checklist
instead of outside it.

## S6 · STEPS CARD (section 4 — roadmap)

The roadmap's four phases are lessons already completed, not just
planned: Foundation, Control, Operationalize, and Extend. Presenting
it as a progress report, not a proposal, is itself part of the pitch.

## S7 · STEPS CARD (sections 5-6)

Governance Model restates Lesson 12's federated hybrid in one
paragraph. Success Metrics restates Lesson 11's five KPIs unchanged —
closing with the same DSAR now carrying a ten-business-day target,
measured monthly.

# Lesson 13 — Developing the AI Governance Strategy · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Governance doesn't wait for an invitation. Two AI pilots are already
running at LTV Global, and this lesson brings both of them under the
program.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (two pilots)

A generative-AI support assistant drafts replies from Beacon and Comet
tickets. A forecasting model predicts SKU demand from Atlas orders.
Neither waited for a strategy — which is exactly why Dana Whitfield
asked Marcus Ibe to catch up now, before either reaches production.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the checklist)

Marcus applies five checks from this path's AI and Machine Learning
Governance course: risk tier, data provenance, bias and fairness,
model documentation, and a monitoring plan.

## S4 · CODE CARD (forecasting model card)

The forecasting model scores moderate risk. It trains on Orders and
SKU — CDEs that already have owners and Lesson 7's quality gate
behind them. It just needs a named monitoring owner and a written
model card.

## S5 · CODE CARD (support assistant model card)

The support assistant scores high risk. It trains on two years of
raw, unscrubbed tickets containing Confidential email addresses and
occasional DateOfBirth mentions, with no owner and no monitoring plan
defined.

## S6 · STEPS CARD (before launch)

Forecasting is close — add an owner and a model card. The assistant
is blocked until its training data is scrubbed, an owner is named,
and a human reviews every drafted reply before it's sent.

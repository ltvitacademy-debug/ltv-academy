# Lesson 11 — Developing Governance KPIs and Workflows · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Ten lessons of work only holds up if someone can measure whether it's
actually working. This lesson builds the KPIs and the workflow behind
them.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (the five KPIs)

Five KPIs, deliberately few: CDE ownership coverage, quality check
pass rate, average issue resolution time, access recertification
completion, and access request response time — a direct, measured
answer to the Lesson 1 incident.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the four-stage workflow)

Every issue moves through the same four stages: detect, when a
steward notices a failure or a request arrives. Triage, classified by
the Lesson 3 CDE criteria. Resolve, within the steward's authority or
escalated per the Lesson 4 RACI. Review, rolled into the monthly
report Dana Whitfield takes to the board.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (why it stays small)

Four stages, five KPIs, on purpose. A workflow a steward can recite
from memory gets followed; an elaborate ticketing taxonomy nobody can
explain in one sentence doesn't.

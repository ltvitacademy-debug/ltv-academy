# Lesson 2 — Reviewing the Data Landscape · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Before you can assign an owner or write a single rule, you need to know
what systems actually hold LTV Global's data. This lesson builds that
inventory.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (why it comes first)

Program lead Marcus Ibe runs a plain-language inventory first: for
every system, what kind it is, where it runs, what data domains it
holds, and what role it actually plays today.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the inventory)

Five systems, one row each: Atlas, the on-prem ERP holding customer,
product, order, and payment data for Finance. Beacon, the cloud CRM
with its own customer records for Sales. Comet, the e-commerce platform
creating a third customer copy at checkout. Harbor, the warehouse
system. And Summit, the Snowflake-and-Purview analytics layer that's
supposed to govern everything but today governs almost nothing.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (what it reveals)

Laid out side by side, three things jump out: three independent
copies of customer with no reconciliation, Summit itself being the
least mature system in the inventory, and every system being well
understood only inside its own department.

Next: Lesson 3 uses this inventory to identify which specific data
elements at LTV Global are critical enough to govern first.

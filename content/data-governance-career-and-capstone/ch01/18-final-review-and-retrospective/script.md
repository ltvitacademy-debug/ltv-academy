# Lesson 18 — Final Review and Retrospective · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Eighteen lessons ago, a six-week data access request reached the
board. This lesson closes that story and looks back on everything
built since.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what was built)

LTV Global went from an ungoverned landscape to a governed program:
five CDEs with owners, SQL quality checks, lineage and a golden
record, access and retention policy, KPIs and a workflow, a permanent
operating model, an AI governance strategy, a target architecture,
and a board-ready presentation.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (caveat: time)

First caveat: time. Eighteen lessons read as one continuous sprint. A
real version of this program plays out over twelve to twenty-four
months, across multiple budget cycles, defended more than once along
the way.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (caveat: politics)

Second caveat: politics. This capstone's decisions landed cleanly in
one meeting each. In practice, a domain owner who loses an
authoritative-source call often pushes back, and the operating model
only holds together with real executive backing when that happens.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (caveat: iteration)

Third caveat: iteration. DateOfBirth surfacing mid-program and the
drift Lesson 15's consistency check caught weren't edge cases —
they're what a real governance program looks like. Finding zero gaps
isn't maturity; it's not looking hard enough.

## S6 · STEPS CARD ("done" is the wrong word)

This program is never frozen at Lesson 18 — the operating model keeps
producing and correcting all of it going forward. And everything
built here is now a portfolio you can walk a hiring manager through,
which is exactly where this course goes next.

## S7 · OUTRO CARD

The capstone is complete. Lesson 19 opens Chapter Two: Data
Governance Career Paths and Roles.

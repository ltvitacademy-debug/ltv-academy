# Lesson 9 — Identifying Authoritative Data Sources · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

One decision has been outstanding since Lesson 1: when systems
disagree, which one wins. This lesson makes it official.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (three easy domains)

Three domains resolve in one meeting: Atlas wins for Product and
Order, because it's the only system that does that job. Beacon wins
for marketing consent, because it's the only system that actually
captures it.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the golden record)

Customer identity doesn't resolve that easily. The owners agree to
build a golden record in Summit instead: matched on Email across all
three systems, order history always from Atlas, contact details from
whichever source was most recently updated, and marketing consent
always from Beacon.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (why it matters, and how it's logged)

This is the exact mechanism that would have answered the Lesson 1
access request in minutes instead of six weeks — one golden Customer
ID, with every field traceable to the rule that produced it. And the
decision itself gets logged, not just agreed to verbally, so it
survives the next reorg.

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Now that the glossary and dictionary exist, Risk and Compliance lead
Amara Chen classifies which of these elements are actually sensitive.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (the four tiers)

Four tiers: Public, safe for anyone. Internal, safe for employees
only. Confidential, limited to the people who need it for their job.
Restricted, limited to a named, audited group, with a formal breach
response.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the classifications)

SKU and Order Total land Internal — commercially sensitive, not
personal. Customer email lands Confidential — the exact element
behind the Lesson 1 access request. Payment card token lands
Restricted — still PCI scope even as a token.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (the surfaced gap)

The pass also turns up an element nobody had flagged: date of birth,
captured during warranty registration for age-restricted gear. It
gets the same Confidential tier as email, and a note to reconsider it
as a fifth critical data element.

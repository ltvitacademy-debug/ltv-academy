# Lesson 31 — Working With Data Teams as a Governance Analyst · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

A governance analyst rarely builds the pipeline or writes the report — the role coordinates across the people who do. This lesson covers what that collaboration actually looks like with data owners, engineers, and analysts day to day.

## S2 · STEPS (data owners and stewards)

Bring owners a specific decision, not an open question. Respect that governance isn't their full-time job — a short review beats a recurring long meeting. And follow up every verbal decision in writing, so it's protected if it's questioned later.

## S3 · STEPS (data engineers)

Give engineers the "why," not just the "what" — the reason behind a rule helps them handle edge cases well. Check feasibility before finalizing a policy, and never turn a failed data quality check into a blame exercise.

## S4 · STEPS (analysts and BI developers)

Explain classification and access rules in terms of what someone can and can't actually do, not just a policy name — and ask analysts where the glossary and reality have quietly drifted apart, since they often notice first.

# Lesson 24 — The Governance Interview Landscape · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Governance interviews mix and match rounds, but the same building blocks keep showing up. Knowing them lets you prepare for categories instead of guessing questions.

## S2 · STEPS — The early rounds

A typical process starts with a recruiter screen — fit and logistics, not technical. Then a hiring manager conversation about your background and capstone. Then a technical screen testing SQL and profiling. Then a scenario-based conversation about ownership and conflicting definitions.

## S3 · STEPS — The later rounds

After the early rounds: a stakeholder or communication exercise, sometimes a short presentation. A case study or take-home, like drafting a glossary entry. Behavioral questions, often woven throughout rather than held separately. And eventually, an offer.

## S4 · STEPS — Role flavor changes the mix

Read the posting, not just the title. A steward-flavored role leans on domain knowledge and day-to-day disputes. A governance-analyst role leans on SQL and documentation. A governance-manager role leans on stakeholders and KPIs. A privacy-focused role leans on classification and access-model reasoning.

## S5 · STEPS — A three-week plan

Week one: resume, LinkedIn, portfolio, and your ninety-second capstone pitch. Week two: SQL and data quality practice daily, matched against real postings. Week three: scenario practice out loud, a mock interview, and a full review of your portfolio artifacts.

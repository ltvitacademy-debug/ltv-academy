# Lesson 33 — Your First 90 Days in a Governance Role · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

The instinct to fix every visible problem immediately is the most common mistake in a governance role's first 90 days. This lesson covers a realistic shape for the first three months instead.

## S2 · STEPS (days 1-30)

The first month is about listening and mapping: meet the people whose names you'll use constantly, read everything that already exists before suggesting anything new, and ask why things are the way they are, not just what they are.

## S3 · STEPS (days 31-60)

The second month is for scoped, low-risk contributions — one dictionary entry, one glossary definition, one data quality check someone already asked for — getting your work reviewed through the existing process before proposing anything bigger.

## S4 · STEPS (days 61-90)

By the third month, own something small end to end: propose one scoped improvement based on a pattern you noticed, run a piece of the governance process yourself, and ask your manager directly for feedback at the 90-day mark.

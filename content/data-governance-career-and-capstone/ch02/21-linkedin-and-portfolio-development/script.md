# Lesson 21 — LinkedIn and Portfolio Development · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson turns your resume into something clickable: a LinkedIn profile and a portfolio built around what you actually produced in your capstone.

## S2 · STEPS — Headline and About

Your headline gets about 220 characters — use them for searchable terms, not just a job title. Your About section works in three short paragraphs: who you are and what you're targeting, two or three specific deliverables stated honestly, and what you're looking for.

## S3 · STEPS — What goes in Featured

A governance portfolio looks different from a coding portfolio. Most of what you build is documents and diagrams, not runnable code. So your Featured section should link to a portfolio deck with real excerpts, a small SQL repository if you have one, and your lineage or operating-model diagram.

## S4 · STEPS — Assembling it from your capstone

If you've completed this course's capstone, the raw material already exists: the glossary and critical data elements, the SQL quality rules, the lineage documentation, the access policies, and the final strategy document and executive presentation.

## S5 · STEPS — Disclose the scenario honestly

The capstone's organization, LTV Global, is a fictional training scenario, built that way on purpose so you could practice the full scope of a governance program. Say so plainly. It's a strength, not a weakness — it shows you can build the whole thing, not just one piece.

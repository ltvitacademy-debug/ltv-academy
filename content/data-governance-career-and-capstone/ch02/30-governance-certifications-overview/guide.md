## Certifications support experience — they don't replace it

Every certifications lesson in this catalog says a version of the same
thing, and it's worth repeating here: a certification is most useful
*after* you have some real, hands-on governance work to point to, not
as a substitute for it. A hiring manager reading your resume will
weigh a certification differently depending on whether it's backed by
a real project (like the capstone you built in Chapter 1 of this
course) or standing alone. Treat certifications in this lesson as a
way to put a recognized credential behind skills you're already
building, not as a shortcut past building them.

Certification programs, exam names, and requirements change over
time — vendors retire and rename exams, and new credentials appear.
Treat the names below as a starting map for your own research on each
organization's current website, not a final answer.

## Vendor-neutral data management and governance certifications

| Certification | Issuing body | What it generally signals |
|---|---|---|
| **CDMP** (Certified Data Management Professional) | DAMA International | Broad data management knowledge aligned to the DMBOK framework this catalog's Data Governance Foundations course covered — DAMA offers it across multiple tiers (from an associate-level exam up through more advanced levels) |

DAMA's CDMP is the credential most directly aligned with the
vendor-neutral governance framework (the DMBOK and the DAMA Wheel)
this path's foundations course already taught — if you're looking for
one certification that maps closely to this catalog's own governance
vocabulary, this is the most direct fit.

## Privacy-focused certifications

| Certification | Issuing body | What it generally signals |
|---|---|---|
| **CIPP** (Certified Information Privacy Professional, offered in regional variants such as CIPP/E and CIPP/US) | IAPP (International Association of Privacy Professionals) | Knowledge of privacy laws and practice in a specific jurisdiction |
| **CIPM** (Certified Information Privacy Manager) | IAPP | Managing a privacy program operationally, not just knowing the law |
| **CDPSE** (Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer) | ISACA | Building privacy into systems and technical processes, a closer fit for governance roles with a technical bent |

If your governance work leans toward privacy and regulatory
compliance specifically (classification, data subject requests,
retention policy — the territory Lesson 28 covered), IAPP's
credentials are the most widely recognized name in that specific
space.

## Vendor and platform certifications — a general note

Beyond the vendor-neutral and privacy-focused credentials above,
several governance-platform vendors — including Microsoft (around
Purview and its broader compliance/security certifications),
Collibra, Informatica, and Alation — maintain their own certification
programs tied to their specific tools. These are worth knowing exist,
but this lesson deliberately doesn't name specific current exam codes
or exact requirements, because vendor exam catalogs change frequently
and a stale exam name is worse than no claim at all. If a specific
platform matters to your target role (say, a company that's
standardized on Microsoft Purview, which this path's own course
covered), check that vendor's current certification page directly
before planning around a specific exam.

## Deciding whether to pursue one

A certification is worth the time and cost when it supports a
direction you're already leaning toward — reinforcing a skill set a
target role actually asks for, or giving a credential-screening filter
something concrete to match against. It's a weaker choice when it's
pursued instead of hands-on project work, especially early in a
career, because most governance hiring managers weigh a real capstone
project (the kind built in Chapter 1 of this course) more heavily than
a certification with no project behind it.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CDMP | DAMA International's Certified Data Management Professional credential, aligned to the DMBOK |
| CIPP / CIPM | IAPP's privacy-law and privacy-program-management credentials |
| CDPSE | ISACA's Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer credential, focused on building privacy into technical systems |
| Vendor-neutral certification | A credential not tied to one company's specific software product |

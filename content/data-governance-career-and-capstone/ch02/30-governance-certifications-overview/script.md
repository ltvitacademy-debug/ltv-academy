# Lesson 30 — Governance Certifications Overview · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Certifications support real governance experience — they don't replace it. This lesson covers a small set of vendor-neutral and privacy-focused certifications worth knowing about, and how to think about vendor-specific ones without chasing a stale exam name.

## S2 · STEPS (vendor-neutral)

DAMA International's CDMP is the credential most directly aligned with this path's own governance vocabulary — the DMBOK and the DAMA Wheel this course's foundations already taught — offered across multiple tiers from associate level upward.

## S3 · STEPS (privacy-focused)

IAPP's CIPP and CIPM cover privacy law and privacy program management respectively, and ISACA's CDPSE covers building privacy into technical systems — the most recognized names if your governance work leans toward privacy and regulatory compliance specifically.

## S4 · STEPS (vendor and platform certs)

Microsoft, Collibra, Informatica, and Alation each maintain their own certification programs tied to their specific governance tools — worth knowing exist, but check each vendor's current page directly rather than planning around an exam name that might already be retired or renamed.

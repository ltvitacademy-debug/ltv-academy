# Lesson 32 — Salary and Offer Basics · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson deliberately doesn't quote a specific salary figure — numbers go stale and vary too much by market. Instead it teaches the research habit for building your own range, and the basic mechanics of evaluating a governance offer.

## S2 · STEPS (why governance pay varies)

Governance titles aren't standardized — Analyst, Steward, Quality Analyst can mean very different scopes. Regulated industries like finance and healthcare tend to fund the role more formally. And the role can sit inside IT, data, or compliance, which changes the pay band it's measured against.

## S3 · STEPS (building your range)

Use aggregated salary sites, your country's official labor data, and live job postings in your metro area — searched under several governance-adjacent titles, not just one — to build a low-to-high band from at least two independent sources.

## S4 · CODE (a sample counter)

Here's a respectful counter: thank them for the offer, state your researched range with a specific reason, ask whether there's room to move on base or elsewhere in the package, and close by confirming you're ready to move forward once aligned.

# Lesson 29 — Stakeholder Presentations and Communication · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

The same governance fact needs different explanations depending on who's in the room. This lesson covers adjusting your message for executives, business stakeholders, and technical teams, and structuring a short update so a busy audience can actually act on it.

## S2 · STEPS (audience layering)

Executives need business impact and the decision you need from them first. Business stakeholders need what this means for their team's data, in concrete terms. Technical teams need the specific rule, field, or system, precisely — each audience gets a different leading layer, without changing the underlying facts.

## S3 · STEPS (structuring an update)

Every short governance update follows the same shape: the headline stating current state, the "so what" for this specific listener, the ask if there is one, and the next checkpoint — so silence afterward doesn't read as a problem.

## S4 · CODE (a sample executive update)

Here's a sample executive update: a one-line headline that the project's on track, the business consequence that it unblocks Compliance's access review, no ask this week, and a clear next checkpoint. No tool names, no implementation detail — just the layer an executive actually needs.

# Lesson 28 — Security, Privacy and AI Governance Questions · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Governance interviews now routinely cover three topics together: security and access, privacy and regulation, and increasingly, AI governance. This lesson previews realistic questions in each, and how to answer them without overclaiming expertise you don't have.

## S2 · STEPS (security and access)

Security questions test whether you reason from least privilege and role-based access, whether you'd follow a documented approval process instead of granting access informally, and whether you understand masking and encryption solve different problems.

## S3 · STEPS (privacy and regulation)

Privacy questions test whether you can spot PII beyond the obvious fields, explain GDPR and CCPA in plain language, and describe your coordinating role in a data subject access request — not a lawyer's role, a governance role.

## S4 · STEPS (AI governance)

AI governance questions test whether you know what's different about governing a model versus a report — training data provenance, bias, model cards, drift — and whether you'd ask the right classification and vendor questions before a team plugs customer data into a generative AI tool.

## S5 · CODE (a sample answer)

Here's a sample answer to "a team wants to use generative AI on customer data" — that confirms the classification first, loops in security and legal on vendor terms, and insists on human review of outputs before anything reaches a customer. Confident on the governance angle, honest about who else needs to be involved.

# Lesson 23 — Explaining Your Capstone in Interviews · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson compresses an eighteen-lesson capstone into something you can say out loud, clearly, in under two minutes.

## S2 · STEPS — The four-part structure

Four parts, in order. Situation: the problem in one sentence — no shared definitions, no quality checks, no documented lineage. Task: what needed to be delivered — a working operating model, not just a document. Action: what you actually built. Result: what the finished program demonstrates — a capability, not an invented number.

## S3 · STEPS — What to actually name in the Action part

Be specific about what you built: critical data elements and a business glossary, named owners and stewards, SQL data quality rules, documented lineage, and the access, retention, and operating model that ties it together.

## S4 · STEPS — Fielding follow-ups

Three follow-ups come up constantly. "Walk me through one artifact" — pick the one you know best and go deep. "What would you do differently" — name one real, honest limitation. "What was the hardest decision" — name a real trade-off you actually faced.

## S5 · STEPS — Scope honesty

If part of the work wasn't yours, say so plainly: name whose focus it was, and name your own strongest contribution instead. Claiming credit for work you didn't do is the fastest way to lose credibility the moment a follow-up goes one level deeper.

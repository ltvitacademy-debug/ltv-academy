# Lesson 25 — Scenario-Based Governance Questions · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Scenario questions have no single right answer, but strong answers all share the same shape. This lesson practices four realistic ones.

## S2 · STEPS — How to answer any of them

Four moves, every time. Clarify the ambiguous parts. Name the actual governance concept this is. Walk through the decision step by step. State the outcome and what you'd watch for next.

## S3 · STEPS — Conflicting numbers and unclear ownership

"Marketing says fifty thousand customers, Finance says thirty-eight thousand." That's a missing glossary term, not a data bug — find the owner, get one authoritative definition. "Nobody can say who owns this field." Find the business function it serves, find the leader who already has authority there, confirm exactly one Accountable role.

## S4 · STEPS — An access exception and a wrong number

"A VP wants broad access outside the agreed model." Route it through the exception process — scope it, time-box it, log it, get the owner's sign-off. "The executive dashboard is eight percent off." Walk upstream from the symptom, hop by hop — the break is rarely where the number was first noticed.

## S5 · STEPS — What's underneath every scenario

Interviewers aren't grading for one correct line. They're listening for whether you reach for a named process instead of guessing, whether you know who decides versus who executes, and whether you stay structured with an unresolved, ambiguous situation.

# Lesson 20 — Resume Preparation for Governance Roles · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson turns your capstone work into a resume a recruiter will actually read, starting with the single pattern every strong bullet follows.

## S2 · STEPS — The pattern

Every strong governance bullet answers three questions. Action: what did you actually do. Artifact: what did you produce that a reviewer could ask to see. Framework: what standard, tool, or concept did you apply. "Worked on data quality" tells a reviewer nothing. "Wrote twelve SQL checks combined into one failures report" gives them something to follow up on.

## S3 · STEPS — Resume structure

Keep it to one page. Header with your contact details. An optional two-line summary. Three or four capstone deliverables, each written in the action-artifact-framework pattern. Skills grouped by category. Then experience and education.

## S4 · STEPS — Draw from the whole capstone

Pull your bullets from across the whole program: the glossary and critical data elements, the SQL quality checks, the lineage documentation, the access and retention policies, and the executive strategy document. Pick whichever match the posting in front of you.

## S5 · STEPS — Common mistakes

Four mistakes weaken a governance resume fastest. Vague verbs instead of named deliverables. Listing frameworks you can't discuss for five minutes. Blurring who decided a policy versus who implemented it. And numbers that don't match between your resume and your portfolio.

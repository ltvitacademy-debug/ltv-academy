# Script — Related Lists and Related List Filters

## Segment 1 (title)

A record rarely tells the whole story by itself. An Account's real context is everything connected to it — its Opportunities, its Contacts, its open Cases. Related lists surface that connected data directly on the record, and Dynamic Related Lists let an admin shape exactly which rows show up.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Lightning App Builder, upgrade prompt)

On a Lightning Record Page, a related list is a component like any other — dropped onto the page, pointed at a related object, configured in a panel on the right. And right there in App Builder, Salesforce surfaces the upgrade path to a Dynamic Related List.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Assets related list table)

A standard related list renders a fixed table — whatever columns were configured, every related record, no filtering of its own. Straightforward, but it shows everything.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Dynamic Related List config)

Upgrade it, and real configuration appears: its own label, its own columns, a sort field and order, a record limit, and filters. Two Dynamic Related Lists on the same object can now show entirely different slices — one open opportunities, another closed.

## Segment 5 (steps: why it matters)

A busy Account with forty closed-lost opportunities buries the three open ones a rep needs today. Filter the related list to Stage is not Closed, and that's solved directly on the record page — no separate report, no extra click.

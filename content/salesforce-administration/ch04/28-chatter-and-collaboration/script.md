# Script — Chatter and Collaboration

## Segment 1 (title)

Email templates handle outbound messages and Email-to-Case handles inbound ones, but a lot of work in Salesforce is just people talking about a record together. That's Chatter — the social layer wired directly into the platform.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Chatter publisher)

The Chatter publisher offers three post types: a plain Post, a Poll for a quick vote, or a Question users can mark an answer to. Posts can go to your followers, a group, or a specific record just by typing a slash and the record name.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: like, comment, share)

Once shared, a post behaves like any social feed: colleagues Like it, Comment on it, or Share it to their own feed or a group — familiar interaction patterns users don't need training on.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Chatter Group page)

Chatter Groups scope collaboration around a topic or team. A group page has its own feed, a member list, files, and related records — public groups anyone can find and join, private groups by invitation, unlisted groups hidden from search entirely.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: Feed Tracking on a record)

The admin-relevant piece is Feed Tracking. Turn it on per object and field, and every tracked field change — an Amount moving, a Close Date slipping — posts automatically to that record's feed, so anyone following the record sees the history without digging through field history reports.

## Segment 6 (steps: what an admin configures)

As an admin, what you actually configure is: Chatter Settings to turn features on or off org-wide, Feed Tracking per object to control what auto-posts, group visibility rules, and which publisher actions appear on a page layout to control what users can post in the first place.

# Lesson 1 — The Administrator Role · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Welcome to Salesforce Administration. Over this course you'll learn to run a Salesforce org the way a real administrator does — not writing code, but configuring, securing, and supporting everyone who touches the system. This first chapter is about the foundation under everything else: users and access. Lesson one starts with the role itself. What does a Salesforce admin actually do all day?

## S2 · IMAGE: setup-home.png (Setup home, gear menu open)

Every answer starts in the same place: Setup. Click the gear icon in the top right of any Salesforce org, and you land here — Setup Home. This is the administrator's actual workspace. Quick Find box at the top, a tree of every configuration area on the left. If a Salesforce admin spends their day doing anything, they're spending it in this screen, or two clicks away from it.

## S3 · IMAGE: setup-admin-nav.png (Administration > Users nav expanded)

Scroll that left-hand tree to Administration, and you find Users — and right next to it, Permission Set Groups, Permission Sets, Profiles, Roles, Public Groups. This whole cluster is what this chapter is about. It's the access-control toolkit: who gets into the org, and once they're in, what they're allowed to see and do.

## S4 · IMAGE: classic-administer-menu.png (Classic Administer menu)

You'll see this same toolkit under a different label in older orgs still running Salesforce Classic: Administer, then Manage Users. Same tools, same job — Salesforce has kept this structure remarkably stable for twenty years, which is part of why the skills in this chapter transfer so directly.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (Who / What / How / Safely)

So what does an admin actually control? Four things. Who gets in — users, licenses, profiles. What they can touch — objects, fields, page layouts. How work gets done — automation and process. And doing all of that safely — security settings, sandboxes, and change management. This chapter covers the first piece: who.

## S6 · OUTRO CARD

That's the role in one lesson: the person who sits in Setup and decides who gets access to what. Next lesson, we open the Users page itself and look at exactly what a user record is made of. See you there.

# Script — Apps and App Manager

## Segment 1 (title)

In Salesforce, an app isn't separate software you install — it's a named, branded set of tabs and navigation items that points different users at the objects they need. A sales rep's app leads with Opportunities; a support agent's leads with Cases. App Manager is where an admin builds and assigns these.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: App Launcher)

Users switch apps with the App Launcher — the grid icon in the top left. It shows every app they have access to, plus a searchable list of every individual tab beyond their current app. They can even drag tiles to reorder their most-used apps.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: New Lightning App - Branding)

Building a custom app starts in App Manager, with New Lightning App. The wizard walks through App Details and Branding — name, icon, highlight color — then app options, optional utility items, which tabs appear and in what order, and finally access.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: New Lightning App - User Profiles)

That last step is a dual-listbox: move profiles from Available into Selected Profiles. Only users on a selected profile ever see this app in their App Launcher — a separate, coarser layer of control from the field and object permissions you covered back in Chapter 1.

## Segment 5 (steps: the wizard in one view)

Five steps build a Lightning app: branding, app options, utility items, navigation items, and user profiles. We'll spend the next two lessons on navigation items and utility items specifically.

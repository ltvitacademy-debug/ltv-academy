# Script — Organization Settings and Company Information

## Segment 1 (title)

Every Salesforce org has one page that describes the org itself — its name, its address, its default locale and time zone, and exactly how much of everything you've bought. That page is Company Information, and it's where this chapter starts.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Company Settings menu)

From Setup, the gear icon takes you to Company Settings, where Company Information sits alongside Business Hours, Fiscal Year, Holidays, and a handful of other org-wide pages. We'll cover several of these in this chapter — this lesson is just Company Information itself.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Company Information detail)

The page itself is mostly read-only. It shows your org's identity — name, address, primary contact — plus the locale defaults every new user inherits: default time zone, default language, and default locale. Fiscal Year and Multiple Currencies live here too, with their own dedicated setup pages coming up next.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: User Licenses table)

Near the top, four links open your license detail: User Licenses, Permission Set Licenses, Feature Licenses, and Usage-based Entitlements. Before you create a new user, this is where you check whether you actually have a license seat free — total purchased, used, and remaining, for every license type in your org.

## Segment 5 (steps: why it matters)

This page isn't just informational. The time zone and locale set here are what a brand-new user inherits. The fiscal year setting drives every forecast and quarterly report org-wide. And the storage and API figures are real consumption against real limits, not estimates.

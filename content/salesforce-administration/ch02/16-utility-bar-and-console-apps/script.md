# Script — Utility Bar and Console Apps

## Segment 1 (title)

Standard navigation works well one record at a time. High-volume users — support agents especially — often need several records open at once, with productivity tools always one click away. That's console navigation and the utility bar.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Standard vs Console navigation)

Back in the App Options step of building an app, one of the first choices is Navigation Style: Standard, the familiar one-record tab experience, or Console, which opens records as subtabs inside one workspace so an agent can jump between a case, its contact, and its account without losing their place.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Utility Items step)

Either style can also carry a utility bar — a fixed footer that opens tools in docked panels without navigating away. It's configured on the Utility Items step: add a component like Notes or Recent Items, then set its label, icon, and the panel size it opens to.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Service Console app home)

Put those two together and you get the default shape of a Service Cloud-style app: a tab bar up top, records opening as subtabs, and utilities always docked at the bottom — built for exactly the kind of constant, multi-record jumping a support team does all day.

## Segment 5 (steps: the two building blocks)

Two separate choices, one app: Navigation Style decides how records open — standard or console — and the utility bar decides what tools are always one click away, regardless of which style you picked.

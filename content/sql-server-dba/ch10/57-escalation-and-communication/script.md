# Script — Escalation & Communication

## Segment 1 (title)

Triage told you what to prioritize. This lesson is about the two things around the technical work: knowing when to bring in help, and saying something useful while doing it.

## Segment 2 (code: When and who)

Escalating isn't a failure — not escalating when it's warranted is. A hard technical problem goes to a senior DBA, an app-rooted issue goes to the app team, and real business impact goes to management, regardless of whether they can help technically.

## Segment 3 (steps: A good status update)

A status update has a small, consistent shape: impact in plain terms, current action, and an ETA only if genuinely known. Saying "still assessing, next update in 15 minutes" beats guessing a number that turns out wrong — false certainty damages trust more than honest uncertainty.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Escalation and communication matter every day, not just during a crisis — including the quiet ones spent on call. Next up: on-call basics.

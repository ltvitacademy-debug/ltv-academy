# Script — Capstone: Presenting Your Work

## Segment 1 (title)

Everything in this capstone actually happened, in order, and that sequence is exactly the shape of a story a manager or an interviewer wants to hear.

## Segment 2 (steps: the five beats)

Inherited state, fixes applied, an incident handled, a change deployed safely, and measurable improvement — five beats, told in order, with the last one being the one people actually remember.

## Segment 3 (code: before / after)

Vague claims don't land, but specific numbers do. RPO went from undefined to fifteen minutes. A 210 gigabyte log file on a nearly full drive became separated and bounded. The app authenticating as sa became a least-privilege login. And a full-outage blocking chain got resolved in twelve minutes with a documented root cause.

## Segment 4 (steps: telling it well)

Start with the inherited state, told plainly, because that's what makes the after numbers mean something. Spend real time on the mechanism — which DMV, what you saw, what you did — not just "I fixed it." And close on the change deployment, because it proves the improvement is durable, not a one-time cleanup.

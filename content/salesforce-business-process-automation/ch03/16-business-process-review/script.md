# Script — Business Process Review

## Segment 1 (title)

Five lessons, five moves -- and really, one repeatable process underneath all of them. This lesson pulls Chapter 3 together before Chapter 4 adds a piece that actually comes before all of it.

## Segment 2 (steps: how chapter 3 fits together)

Lessons 11 and 12 turned vague complaints into working designs -- one driven by a dollar threshold, one by a deadline. Lesson 13 made sure that design survives the builder eventually leaving. Lesson 14 had you run the entire method yourself, on a brand-new scenario. Lesson 15 made sure "it works" actually proves something.

## Segment 3 (code: the chapter 3 method, in order)

Written out as a single method, it's five steps: translate the complaint into requirements, design the automation, document it, test it across all four categories, and only then ship it. Skipping straight from step one to step five is exactly how undocumented, untested automation happens in the first place.

## Segment 4 (steps: an automation health-check)

Run this health-check against any existing automation, yours or someone else's. Does a written requirement actually exist? Can you state the entry criteria without opening Setup? Is there a named owner with a last-reviewed date? And has it been tested against all four categories, or just run once successfully?

## Segment 5 (code: why this matters beyond this course)

Every case study in this chapter used a fictional company, but the method itself doesn't change when the company is real. Complaint, requirements, design, documentation, testing -- that's ordinary day-to-day administrator work, not some advanced special case.

## Segment 6 (outro)

Chapter 3 assumed you already had a clear requirement to design against. Chapter 4 backs up one step: how do you actually gather that requirement from a stakeholder in the first place?

# Lesson 1 — Program Overview & Portfolio Strategy · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

This capstone course closes out the AI Engineer path. Instead of
teaching a new piece, it's where everything you already know gets
shipped as three real, working systems you build end to end and put
your name on.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (three projects)

Three flagship projects: a production RAG knowledge assistant, an AI
data analyst that turns questions into safe SQL and combines it with
live API data, and a tool-using agent with human approval, security,
and logging. A career-preparation chapter closes things out.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (your own project)

There's no fictional company here — you build this under your own
name, in your own repository. Everything you've learned about LLMs,
RAG, agents, and deployment feeds directly into these three builds.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (depth over breadth)

Depth beats breadth. A GitHub profile with fifteen half-finished
notebooks says you start things. Two or three complete, measured,
deployed AI projects say you can finish them — and that's what gets
you hired.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (what complete means)

Four things make a project complete: a real end-to-end pipeline calling
an actual LLM API, a measured evaluation with real numbers, a deployed
and callable service, and a README that states your architecture and
tradeoffs honestly.

## S6 · OUTRO CARD

Next: choosing which of the three projects to polish deepest, based on
the role you're actually targeting.

# Lesson 2 — Choosing Your Project Emphasis · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

The three flagship projects share a lot of groundwork, but each leans
hardest on a different skill set — worth being deliberate about before
you start.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (three skill sets)

The RAG assistant leans on chunking, retrieval quality, and grounded
generation. The AI data analyst leans on safe natural-language-to-SQL
generation and combining structured data from more than one source.
The tool-using agent leans on orchestration, approval gates, security,
and logging.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (framework by role)

Ask yourself one question: what role am I actually applying for?
Targeting an AI or RAG engineer role, go deepest on Project 1.
Targeting an applied AI engineer working with data teams, go deepest on
Project 2. Targeting an agentic or AI platform role, go deepest on
Project 3.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (still build all three)

Choosing an emphasis doesn't mean skipping the other two. Project 1
builds first regardless, because its evaluation and deployment
patterns reuse directly into Projects 2 and 3. Emphasis just means
which one gets the extra polish pass — a sharper README, a deeper
evaluation, a recorded demo.

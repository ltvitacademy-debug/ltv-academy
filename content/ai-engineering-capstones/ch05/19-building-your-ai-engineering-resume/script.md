# Lesson 19 — Building Your AI Engineering Resume · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson turns your three capstone projects into a resume built around what AI hiring actually checks for: something deployed, something evaluated, and something documented.

## S2 · STEPS — Three proof points

A model-name buzzword list proves nothing on its own. What a reviewer actually wants is a system that's really deployed, a real number showing how well it works, and a README that explains your decisions.

## S3 · STEPS — The pattern

Every strong bullet names three things: the action, what you actually produced, and the technique or framework you used. "Worked on a chatbot" says nothing. "Built and deployed a RAG assistant, 94% answer faithfulness on a 50-question eval set" gives a reviewer something real to judge.

## S4 · STEPS — One-page structure

Header with a GitHub link. An optional two-line summary. Your three capstone projects, each with proof-backed bullets. Skills grouped by category. Then experience and education.

## S5 · STEPS — Common mistakes

A buzzword list with nothing deployed behind it. Calling notebook-only work "production." Shipping a system with no stated eval number. And metrics that don't match between your resume and your README.

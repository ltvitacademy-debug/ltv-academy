## S1 · TITLE

This is it -- the final lesson of the entire AI Engineer path. Let's close it out with salary research and a real look back at everything you've built.

## S2 · STEPS — Why AI engineering pay varies

AI engineering titles aren't standardized at all -- AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Applied AI Engineer can mean nearly the same job or very different ones. Company stage swings pay enormously. And equity adds a real variable cash pay simply doesn't have.

## S3 · STEPS — Building your range

Use the same toolkit this catalog always teaches: aggregated salary sites, government labor data, and live postings -- searched under several AI-role titles, noting whether a listing includes an equity component. Build a low-to-high band from at least two sources.

## S4 · STEPS — Evaluating a real offer

Once you have an offer, look at the whole package -- base, equity and its vesting schedule, signing bonus, flexibility, and the timeline to your first review. A respectful counter thanks them, states your researched range, and stays collaborative.

## S5 · STEPS — The path, part one

Look at everything you've built. Python, the language every later course ran on. Git and GitHub, real version control. APIs and JSON, the data format every AI call depends on. And AI/ML Foundations, where large language models fit into the bigger picture.

## S6 · STEPS — The path, part two

Then Generative AI and LLMs, and Prompt and Context Engineering -- getting reliable output by design. RAG and Vector Databases, grounding answers in real documents. And AI Agents -- moving from a system that answers questions to one that takes action, safely.

## S7 · STEPS — The path, part three

Then Azure AI and Cloud, and Docker and Deployment -- real infrastructure, not a notebook. AI Security, Evaluation and Monitoring -- keeping it safe over time. And this course -- three deployed, evaluated projects and the job-search skills to present them.

## S8 · OUTRO

That's the whole path, complete. From your first line of Python to three deployed, evaluated AI systems, with a resume, a portfolio, and a proven method for any interview you'll face. Congratulations -- go build something real.

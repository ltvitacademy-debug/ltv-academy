# Script — Accounting Configuration Manager Walkthrough

## Segment 1 (title)

You've met legal entities, primary and secondary ledgers, reporting currencies, and ledger sets as separate concepts. This lesson walks through the guided tool that actually assembles them: the Accounting Configuration Manager.

## Segment 2 (steps)

You could create a chart of accounts, calendar, legal entity, and ledger as four isolated tasks and wire them together manually. Oracle Fusion instead gives you a connected flow for the most common path, because forgetting a required link is easy when you build in isolation.

## Segment 3 (code)

The guided sequence: create the primary ledger and assign its chart of accounts, calendar, currency, and accounting method. Specify ledger options like sequencing and rounding. Assign legal entities. Then assign balancing segment values per legal entity. Business units come in chapter three.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Ledger options are the ledger's own operational settings, like journal sequencing and approval rules. Accounting options usually mean subledger accounting behavior — how Payables or Receivables transactions become journal entries for this ledger.

## Segment 5 (outro)

The step that trips up new consultants most is assigning each legal entity its own balancing segment value — that's what lets one ledger produce a separate, in-balance trial balance per legal entity. Chapter two is complete. Chapter three moves to business units and reference data.

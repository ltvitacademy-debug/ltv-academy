# Script — Legal Entities and Legal Reporting Units

## Segment 1 (title)

Legal entities are where enterprise structures stop being abstract. This lesson covers how to actually register one in Oracle Fusion, and the related concept you get almost automatically: the legal reporting unit.

## Segment 2 (code)

Registering a legal entity requires a name, a legal address, an identifying jurisdiction like United States Income Tax, and registration details for that jurisdiction, like an EIN. The legal address must already exist, created through Manage Legal Address, before you can register the entity.

## Segment 3 (steps)

The moment you register a legal entity, Oracle Fusion automatically creates a legal reporting unit with the same name. Most entities need only this one. Additional reporting units are needed when one legal entity must register separately across multiple jurisdictions, like several states.

## Segment 4 (code)

You'll find these tasks in the Enterprise Profile functional area: Manage Legal Address first, then Manage Legal Entity, then Manage Legal Entity Registrations, then Manage Legal Reporting Unit.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A legal entity is a name, an address, and jurisdiction-specific registration — not just a label in a dropdown. Get it wrong and compliance-sensitive reports are wrong too. Next up, lesson seven: ledgers, the structure that actually records a legal entity's transactions.

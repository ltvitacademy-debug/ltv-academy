# Script — Enterprise Structure Design Principles

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter one gave you the map. Before we start placing pins on it in chapter two, this lesson covers the questions a consultant answers before opening a single setup task. Design is a planning exercise first.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Every real engagement starts by answering a small set of questions: how many legally registered companies does the business operate as, in how many countries, with how many currencies, and does management need to see results grouped differently than the legal structure requires.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A common mistake is over-engineering — extra ledgers or charts of accounts added "in case we need them later." Every extra structure has to be configured, maintained, and reconciled forever. Start with the simplest structure that satisfies today's legal and reporting requirements.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Sometimes legal reporting and management reporting pull apart — a legal entity must report standalone statements, while management wants results grouped by product line across entities. Oracle Fusion has tools for exactly this, but naming the tension during design beats discovering it mid-build.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Answer how many legal entities, currencies, and reporting requirements exist, before touching a setup screen, and default to simple. Next up, lesson six: legal entities and legal reporting units, the first structure we'll actually build.

# Script — Ledger Sets

## Segment 1 (title)

Imagine five primary ledgers that share the same chart of accounts and calendar. Opening and closing periods, and running reports, five separate times every month is exactly the busywork Oracle Fusion lets you collapse into one action. That's what a ledger set is for.

## Segment 2 (code)

There's one hard requirement. Every member of a ledger set must share the same chart of accounts and the same calendar or period type. Members can be primary ledgers, secondary ledgers, or reporting currencies, but structurally mismatched ledgers simply cannot join the same set.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Ledger sets simplify two things: period management, opening or closing a period once across every member instead of one at a time, and reporting, since many reports accept a ledger set as a parameter and produce one combined result.

## Segment 4 (steps)

It's tempting to picture a ledger set as one big combined ledger, but it isn't. Each ledger keeps its own currency, legal entity assignments, and balances. The set doesn't merge data — it just lets you act on all of them with one command.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A ledger set groups structurally identical ledgers to save repetitive clicks, without merging their balances. Next up, lesson nine: a walkthrough of the Accounting Configuration Manager, which ties legal entities, ledgers, and chart of accounts together.

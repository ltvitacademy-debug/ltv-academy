# Script — Rapid Implementation Spreadsheets

## Segment 1 (title)

Everything so far has been screen by screen, inside Setup and Maintenance. For companies with straightforward requirements, Oracle Fusion offers a faster path: rapid implementation spreadsheets.

## Segment 2 (steps)

RI templates let a consultant define, in spreadsheet form, chart of accounts values, account hierarchies, the accounting calendar, legal entities, a primary ledger, and business units — generated in one coordinated upload instead of dozens of individual setup tasks.

## Segment 3 (code)

Behind the scenes: filled-in RI spreadsheets, ADFdi, the Excel add-in connecting to Oracle Fusion, FBDI for bulk data volumes, and an upload process, a scheduled process that reads the file and creates the structures.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Rapid implementation suits simple, single-chart-of-accounts, limited-entity requirements. A complex multinational with many entities and extensive hierarchies usually needs the screen-by-screen configuration this course spent five chapters covering instead.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Even a successful upload is a starting point, not a finished implementation — segment labels, balancing assignments, and cross-validation rules still need manual review. Next up, lesson twenty-eight: testing and reviewing your enterprise structure.

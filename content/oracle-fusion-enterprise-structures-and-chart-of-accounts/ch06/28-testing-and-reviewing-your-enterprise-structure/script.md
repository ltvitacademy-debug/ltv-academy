# Script — Testing and Reviewing Your Enterprise Structure

## Segment 1 (title)

Whether built screen by screen or through rapid implementation spreadsheets, an enterprise structure should never go live untested. This lesson covers the review a careful consultant runs before calling it done.

## Segment 2 (code)

A checklist spanning the whole course: legal entities and their registration, the ledger's chart of accounts, calendar, and currency, balancing segment values per entity, business unit functions and assignments, reference data sets, calendar periods, enabled currencies and daily rates, segment labels, hierarchy rollups, and cross-validation rules.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A cross-validation rule that's never actually rejected anything hasn't been tested, just assumed to work. A real test deliberately attempts a known-bad combination and confirms it's rejected, and a known-good one and confirms it's accepted.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Before trusting a hierarchy-based report, pull a known parent's total and manually verify it matches the sum of its expected children. A child attached to the wrong parent produces a quietly wrong number, not an obviously broken one.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Every item on this checklist is dramatically cheaper to fix before live transactions post than after. Next up, lesson twenty-nine: common enterprise structure mistakes, the patterns this review is designed to catch.

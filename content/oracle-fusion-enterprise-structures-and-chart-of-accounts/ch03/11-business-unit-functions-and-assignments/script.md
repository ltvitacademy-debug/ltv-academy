# Script — Business Unit Functions and Assignments

## Segment 1 (title)

A business unit by itself is just a name and a ledger assignment. What makes it useful is the business functions assigned to it — the specific jobs it's configured to perform.

## Segment 2 (code)

A business function is a specific process a business unit can carry out — Payables Invoicing, Billing and Revenue Management, Requisitioning, Project Accounting. One business unit isn't limited to one function; a mid-sized company might combine several into one business unit.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Oracle Fusion supports a shared service model. One business unit becomes the service provider for a function, and other business units become its clients for that same function — like one centralized AP team processing invoices for several operating divisions.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Function assignment isn't just descriptive. It directly affects which setup tasks and reference data options appear for a business unit. No Payables function assigned means no Payables setup options show up at all.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Business functions are the specific jobs a business unit performs, and a business unit can be a service provider for some functions while a client for others. Next up, lesson twelve: reference data sets.

# Script — Locations, Geographies and Addresses

## Segment 1 (title)

This lesson closes out chapter three with the quiet, physical-world data every other structure leans on: locations, geographies, and addresses. Skip them, and legal entities and tax calculations start behaving strangely.

## Segment 2 (code)

Geography is the hierarchical structure — country, state, city, postal code — that validates every address entered. A legal address is the specific, formal address tied to a legal entity's registration. A location is a broader-purpose address used anywhere Oracle Fusion needs "a place."

## Segment 3 (steps)

If a country's geography structure is incomplete or wrong, every address entered against it inherits the problem — validation errors, or addresses that silently fail to map to the right tax jurisdiction. Validating that data, not assuming it, is a real step.

## Segment 4 (steps)

A single location can be reused across many contexts — the office for several business units, a shipping point, and a legal entity's registered address, all without re-entering the same street address three times. That reuse keeps data consistent.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Geography validates, legal address ties to registration, location is the general-purpose reusable record. That closes chapter three. Chapter four moves to calendars and currencies, the remaining two of a ledger's four C's.

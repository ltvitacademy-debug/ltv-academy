# Script — Finding Your Way Around Setup and Maintenance

## Segment 1 (title)

Almost every lesson left in this course happens inside one work area: Setup and Maintenance. Before we touch legal entities, ledgers, or segments, let's learn how it's organized.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Oracle Fusion organizes setup work in three nested levels. An offering is a big product area, like Financials. Each offering breaks into functional areas, like Enterprise Profile or Ledger and Reporting Configuration. Each functional area contains individual setup tasks — the actual clickable steps you perform.

## Segment 3 (steps)

This course lives almost entirely inside the Financials offering, mostly in two functional areas: Enterprise Profile, which covers legal entities, business units and locations, and Ledger and Reporting Configuration, which covers ledgers, chart of accounts, calendars and currencies.

## Segment 4 (steps)

The full task list can run into hundreds of tasks. Instead of browsing the whole tree, use the search box and type part of a task name, like "chart of accounts." That's how most working consultants actually find a task day to day.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Offering, functional area, task — that's the shape of Setup and Maintenance. Next up, lesson three: a bird's-eye view of enterprise structures themselves, before we configure any piece of them individually.

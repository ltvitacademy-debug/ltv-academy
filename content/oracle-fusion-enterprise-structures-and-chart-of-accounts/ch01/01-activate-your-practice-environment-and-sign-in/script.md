# Script — Activate Your Practice Environment and Sign In

## Segment 1 (title)

Welcome to Enterprise Structures and Chart of Accounts. This is where the path stops being theory and starts being real, hands-on Oracle Fusion configuration. Before we touch a setup screen, let's get you signed in.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Oracle Fusion Cloud access isn't included with your LTV Academy subscription. It's purchased separately from a third-party provider called OracleERPGuide, for roughly eighty five dollars covering three months of access. Buy it now, at the start of this course, not earlier — you want that window lined up with the hands-on material.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Once your instance is provisioned, you'll get a sign-in URL, a username, and a temporary password. The first login forces you to set a new password. Store it somewhere safe, because you'll be signing back in constantly for the rest of this path.

## Segment 4 (steps)

After signing in, you land on the Oracle Fusion home page, sometimes called the Springboard. The Navigator menu in the top left is your gateway to every work area your user can reach — and the one you'll live in for most of this course is called Setup and Maintenance.

## Segment 5 (outro)

You now know where this environment comes from, what it costs, and what greets you after sign-in. Next up, lesson two: finding your way around the Setup and Maintenance work area.

# Activate Your Practice Environment and Sign In

Welcome to Enterprise Structures and Chart of Accounts, the course where this path stops being theory and starts being hands-on Oracle Fusion Cloud configuration. Everything from this lesson forward assumes you are clicking through a real Oracle Fusion environment, not reading about one. Before we touch a single setup screen, we need to get you signed in.

- Why this is the moment to activate your Oracle Fusion practice environment, not earlier
- Where that practice environment comes from, and what it costs
- How sign-in works once your access is provisioned

## Why now, and not before

The two courses before this one — Oracle Fusion Cloud & ERP Foundations and Accounting Fundamentals for Oracle Professionals — were deliberately built so you could complete them with no software access at all. That was intentional: there was nothing yet to configure. Starting with this lesson, every remaining course in the path has you opening real setup pages, building real (if fictional) enterprise structures, and troubleshooting real configuration problems. That is the line this course sits on.

## Where your practice environment comes from

Oracle Fusion Cloud access is **not** included with your Lifting the Veil IT Academy subscription. It is purchased separately, directly from a third-party provider called OracleERPGuide (OEG). As of the last verified pricing, an Oracle Fusion Cloud practice instance runs approximately $85 for three months of access. OracleERPGuide is independent of LTV Academy — LTV does not own, operate, resell, or control that environment, its pricing, or its availability. Treat this the way you would treat buying a textbook for a college course: it is required, but it is a separate purchase from a separate vendor.

Because access is time-boxed to roughly three months, pace yourself. Purchase it now, at the start of this course, rather than earlier — you want as much of that window as possible to land inside the hands-on material, not ticking away while you were still reading about accounting equations.

## Signing in for the first time

Once OracleERPGuide provisions your instance, you will receive a sign-in URL, a username, and a temporary password. The first time you sign in you will almost certainly be forced to set a new password that meets Oracle's complexity rules (minimum length, a mix of character types, and no reuse of recent passwords). Treat these credentials like any other production-style login: do not share them, and store the password somewhere safe — you will be signing back in constantly for the rest of this path.

```
Sign-in checklist
1. Open the URL OracleERPGuide provided (bookmark it)
2. Enter your username, then your temporary password
3. Set a new password when prompted
4. Land on the Oracle Fusion home page (the "Springboard")
```

## What you will see: the home page and the Navigator

After sign-in, you land on the Oracle Fusion **home page**, sometimes called the Springboard. It shows a row of icons for the functional areas you have access to (for this path, think Financials-related areas), plus any notifications or recent items. In the top-left corner is the **Navigator** — the menu icon that expands into the full list of every work area your user can reach, organized by application pillar (Financials, Procurement, Projects, and so on). The single work area you will live in for most of this course is called **Setup and Maintenance**, which the next lesson covers in detail.

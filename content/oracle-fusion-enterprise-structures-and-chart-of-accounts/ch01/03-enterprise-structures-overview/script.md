# Script — Enterprise Structures Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

Before you create a single legal entity or ledger, you need the map. Oracle Fusion's enterprise structures are a handful of interlocking blocks, and this lesson is that map.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Four core concepts. A legal entity is a real, registered organization — the legal "who." A ledger is the accounting book that records a legal entity's transactions, using one chart of accounts, one calendar, one currency. A business unit is the operating unit that actually processes transactions. The chart of accounts is the classification scheme everything gets coded against.

## Segment 3 (code)

These pieces are assigned to each other in a layered way. One or more legal entities are assigned to a ledger. That ledger uses one chart of accounts and one calendar. Business units are assigned to a ledger, and usually a legal entity. Get an assignment wrong, and every transaction posts to the wrong set of books.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Every later course in this path assumes these four structures already exist and are assigned correctly. You can't post an invoice without a business unit, run a business unit without a ledger, or have a ledger without a chart of accounts. Structure comes before transactions.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Legal entities, ledgers, business units, and the chart of accounts — four blocks, explicitly assigned to one another. Next up, lesson four: Functional Setup Manager, the tool that generates the task lists you'll use to actually build these structures.

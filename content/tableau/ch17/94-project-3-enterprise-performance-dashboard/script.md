# Script — Project 3: Enterprise Performance Dashboard

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the hardest and most complete project in the course — the one meant to sit at the top of your portfolio. A VP of Sales wants one comprehensive deliverable combining regional performance, product performance, and a live what-if profitability scenario.

## Segment 2 (steps: the required pieces)

You need at least five worksheets — a regional map, a category performance view, a monthly trend, a KPI header, and a discount-versus-profit analysis — combined with a what-if parameter that feeds a real calculation a viewer can adjust, at least one correctly-scoped LOD expression, and a table calculation with Compute Using deliberately configured, not left on Tableau's default.

## Segment 3 (steps: connecting it all together)

Then you connect it: filter and highlight actions between views, a navigation action linking dashboards together, a working mobile or tablet layout built with Device Designer, and at least one dynamic title that updates as parameters or filters change. Chained together, a viewer should be able to explain the click-path without being told.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Last lesson of the course: interview preparation and portfolio strategy — how to actually present these three projects, on Tableau Public, in an interview.

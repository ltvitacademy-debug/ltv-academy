# Script — Geographic Hierarchies, Groups & Custom Territories

## Segment 1 (title)

Real geography and the boundaries a business actually cares about are rarely the same thing. This lesson covers both.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: geo hierarchy)

When Tableau recognizes a field as geographic, it builds a nested hierarchy automatically — Country down to Postal Code. That's what lets you drill down on a filled map with no setup at all.

## Segment 3 (steps: where it breaks down)

But a company's real sales territories are business decisions, not geographic facts. "Northeast Region" at one company might be six states; at another, those same six states minus one county that reports elsewhere. No built-in geographic role captures a boundary like that.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: custom territories)

The fix is a Group: select the individual regions that make up your real territory and combine them. The underlying map still knows the real, small regions — the group just adds a business-defined layer on top.

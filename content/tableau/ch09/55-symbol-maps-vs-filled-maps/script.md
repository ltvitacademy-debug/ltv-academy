# Script — Symbol Maps vs. Filled Maps

## Segment 1 (title)

Tableau gives you two core map types: symbol maps and filled maps. Picking the wrong one for the question you're actually asking is one of the most common mapping mistakes.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: symbol map)

A symbol map places one mark at each individual location — a circle, usually — and encodes a quantitative value with its size and color. It's the right choice when you care about specific locations, not whole regions, and when your geographic field is precise: a city, a ZIP code, latitude and longitude.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: filled map)

A filled map, also called a choropleth map, colors in the entire polygon for each region — state, county, country — based on a value. This only tells an honest story when that value is a rate or a percentage. Shade a filled map by raw count instead, and large states start looking important just because they have more land area, whether or not the underlying number actually is.

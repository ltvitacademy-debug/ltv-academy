# Script — Geographic Roles & Creating Maps

## Segment 1 (title)

This chapter is about maps. Before you can build one, Tableau needs to know a field represents a real-world place — that's a geographic role, and this lesson covers how it works.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: geographic role menu)

If Tableau doesn't recognize a location field automatically, you assign it yourself. Right-click the field in the Data pane, open Geographic Role, and pick the match — Country/Region, State/Province, City, ZIP Code, and more.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: building the map)

Once a field has a geographic role, dragging it onto the canvas builds a map automatically. Here, Country/Region and State are on the Marks card alongside SUM of Sales — Tableau generates the Latitude and Longitude behind the scenes, from its own internal geocoding database.

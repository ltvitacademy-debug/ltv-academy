# Script — Tableau Interview Preparation & Portfolio Strategy

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the last lesson of the course. Everything before it built a skill — this lesson closes the loop on all of them, and turns that work into an interview and a job.

## Segment 2 (steps: two kinds of interview questions)

A real Tableau interview mixes conceptual questions — like when to use a relationship instead of a join or a blend — with tool-specific ones, like walking through an actual publish workflow or showing a FIXED LOD expression you've written. Prepare for them differently: explain tradeoffs out loud for the conceptual ones, and point at something real you built for the tool-specific ones. That's exactly why your three Chapter 17 projects exist.

## Segment 3 (steps: portfolio and resume)

Publish all three projects to Tableau Public — it's free and fully public, which is exactly the right home for a portfolio a hiring manager can open with no login. Rehearse a thirty-second version and a three-minute version of each pitch. And turn each one into a specific, provable resume bullet naming the actual technique you used, not a generic skill claim.

## Segment 4 (outro)

That's the whole course — ninety-five lessons, eighteen chapters, three real projects sitting in your portfolio right now. You've gone from not knowing what Tableau was to being able to design, build, optimize, publish, and present real dashboards that demonstrate exactly what a Tableau BI Developer or Data Analyst role is hiring for. Congratulations — go use it.

# Script — KPI Cards, Bullet Charts & Sparklines

## Segment 1 (title)

Three view types built for at-a-glance monitoring rather than detailed exploration: KPI cards, bullet graphs, and sparklines. All three trade precision for speed — a viewer should be able to read them in under a second.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: KPI view)

A KPI view starts with a threshold calculation — above goal or below goal — mapped onto the Shape property instead of a number. Cross it with Sub-Category on Rows and Region on Columns, and you get a grid of green checks and red X's a viewer can scan instantly.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: bullet graph)

A bullet graph is Tableau's built-in replacement for a gauge widget: one bar for the actual value, a tick mark for the target, and shaded bands behind it for qualitative ranges — poor, satisfactory, good. Show Me will build the whole thing once you've selected the right fields.

## Segment 4 (steps: sparklines)

Sparklines don't have their own chart type in Tableau. You build one from an ordinary line chart: hide both axis headers, strip the gridlines, shrink the worksheet down small, and place several side by side in a dashboard as a trend column next to a table of current values.

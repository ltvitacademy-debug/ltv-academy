# Script — Dimension, Measure & Date Filters

## Segment 1 (title)

The Filter dialog isn't one dialog — it's three, and which one you get depends entirely on the field type you drag onto Filters.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: dimension filter)

Drag a discrete dimension like Category, and you get four tabs: General for manually checking values, Wildcard for text matching, Condition for formula-based rules, and Top for keeping only the top or bottom N.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: measure filter)

Drag a measure like Sales, and Tableau asks you to pick an aggregation first — sum, average, median, and more. A measure has thousands of underlying values behind it, so "filter where Sales is over $500" is meaningless until you say which aggregation you mean.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: date filter)

Drag a date field, and you choose between Relative Date, which recalculates from today every time the view opens, and Range of Dates, which stays fixed to the exact dates you pick.

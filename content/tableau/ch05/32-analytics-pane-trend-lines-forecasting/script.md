# Script — Analytics Pane: Trend Lines, Forecasting & Reference Lines

## Segment 1 (title)

Next to the Data pane sits a second tab most beginners never open — the Analytics pane, Tableau's built-in statistical layer, all drag-and-drop.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: analytics pane)

Three groups: Summarize describes data you already have, Model fits something to it — a trend, a forecast, a cluster — and Custom lets you draw a fixed line or band yourself.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: trend line)

Drag Trend Line onto a view, and Tableau fits a line through your data automatically, one per panel. It shows direction — up, down, or flat — it doesn't predict the future by itself. Right-click it and choose Describe Trend Model to see the actual R-squared value instead of eyeballing it.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: forecast)

Drag Forecast instead, and Tableau extends the view past your last real data point, with a shaded confidence band that widens the further it projects. That widening is honesty, not decoration — a forecast whose band doesn't widen should make you suspicious.

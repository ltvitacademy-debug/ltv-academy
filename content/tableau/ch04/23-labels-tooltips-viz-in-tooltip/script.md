# Script — Labels, Tooltips & Viz in Tooltip

## Segment 1 (title)

There are three ways Tableau lets a mark explain itself, from simplest to most powerful: labels, tooltips, and Viz in Tooltip.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Edit Tooltip)

Click Tooltip on the Marks card to open the Edit Tooltip dialog. By default, Tableau auto-populates it with every field in the view, formatted as placeholders like angle-bracket Category angle-bracket. You can delete fields, rewrite the surrounding text, reformat with the toolbar, or insert new fields entirely — the tooltip is fully custom text, not just a fixed list.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Viz in Tooltip)

Viz in Tooltip goes further — instead of just text, an entire second worksheet renders inside the tooltip. Here, hovering over the Office Supplies bar reveals a full breakdown by sub-category, each with its own mini bar and dollar value, all without leaving the original chart.

## Segment 4 (steps: three ways)

Labels are always-on text baked directly onto the mark — visible without hovering at all. Tooltips are the customizable hover text you just saw in the Edit Tooltip dialog. And Viz in Tooltip is the most powerful of the three — an entire second worksheet, built separately, then inserted into the first sheet's tooltip.

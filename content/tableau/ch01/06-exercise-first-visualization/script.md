# Script — Exercise: Build Your First Tableau Visualization

## Segment 1 (title)

Time to build something real. This lesson is a hands-on exercise using everything from Lessons 1 through 5.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: finished Show Me view)

Using Sample Superstore, put continuous Year of Order Date on Columns, Profit and Sales on Rows, and Region on Color in the Marks card. Then use the Show Me panel to pick the stacked bar option. You should land on something like this — Profit and Sales by year, colored by Region.

## Segment 3 (steps: what Show Me actually does)

Show Me isn't magic — it looks at your selected fields, grays out chart types that don't fit, and arranges the same Columns, Rows, and Marks card mechanics you already know onto the shelves for you. That's exactly why the earlier lessons spent time on the mechanics first.

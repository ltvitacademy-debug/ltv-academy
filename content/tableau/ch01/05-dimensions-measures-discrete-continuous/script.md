# Script — Dimensions, Measures, Discrete & Continuous Fields

## Segment 1 (title)

This lesson covers two distinctions you'll use constantly: Dimension versus Measure, and discrete versus continuous.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: continuous axis)

Dimensions are qualitative — names, dates, geography. Measures are numeric and get aggregated automatically. But discrete versus continuous is a completely separate switch. A continuous field, colored green, forms an unbroken range and creates an axis — like Quantity here, producing a real numeric scale on Columns.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: discrete headers)

A discrete field, colored blue, has distinct separate values and creates headers instead — like Category here, producing three separate labels: Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology. No axis, just labels.

## Segment 4 (steps: why it matters)

This distinction drives almost every chart decision ahead: bar charts need discrete headers, line charts need a continuous axis, and dates can go either way depending on whether you want "2021, 2022, 2023" as labels or a smooth timeline. Right-click any field to convert it between discrete and continuous, or between Dimension and Measure — they're independent switches.

# Script — Designing Dashboards for Desktop & Mobile

## Segment 1 (title)

A dashboard built for a wide desktop canvas rarely works on a phone as-is. Today, how Tableau lets one dashboard define alternate layouts per device, and the practical rules for making a phone version actually usable.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Device Designer, Auto-Generate)

Tableau's Device Designer lets you add a Phone or Tablet layout on top of your Default desktop layout, reusing the same worksheets and objects. Auto-Generate Layout stacks everything vertically as a fast starting point — genuinely useful, but rarely finished on its own.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: real phone dashboards)

Here are two real phone layouts, side by side. Filters sit at the top, within thumb's reach. Each screen foregrounds one primary chart instead of trying to show four at once. And nothing depends on hover, because a phone doesn't have a mouse pointer.

## Segment 4 (steps: converting desktop patterns to phone)

A four-column KPI row becomes a vertical stack, or gets cut to the one or two most critical numbers. Hover-triggered actions switch to Select. Dense tables get simplified. The rule underneath all of it: a phone layout is a different information hierarchy, not a shrunk desktop layout.

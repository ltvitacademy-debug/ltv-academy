# Script — Building KPI Headers & Executive Summary Sections

## Segment 1 (title)

A Key Performance Indicator is a measurable value shown against a target. Today you'll build KPIs two ways inside Tableau — shapes and colored numbers — and assemble them into the header row every executive dashboard opens with.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: KPI shape assignment)

Here's Tableau's Edit Shape dialog, using its built-in KPI palette. A calculated field evaluates sales against a benchmark, returning "Above" or "Below" — and each outcome gets its own icon, a checkmark or an X. This is the fastest way to build a status indicator a viewer already knows how to read.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: real executive-style dashboard)

This real example dashboard combines a Profit by State map, a Profit by Segment breakdown, and a detail table — all visible without scrolling. That's the actual goal of an executive summary: a handful of headline numbers a VP reads in the first ten seconds.

## Segment 4 (steps: the 3-2-1 pattern)

A reusable structure: three headline KPIs across the top in a Horizontal container, two supporting charts in the middle, one detail area at the bottom. Pick five to ten KPIs that map to real business questions — an executive summary that shows everything shows nothing.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Next lesson, you'll make that header and every chart around it interactive, with filter and highlight actions.

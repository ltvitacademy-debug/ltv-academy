# Script — Capstone: Wrap-Up & Portfolio Presentation

## Segment 1 (title)

Four deliverables aren't a portfolio piece yet. A portfolio piece is the narrative connecting them — not "here are four documents," but a problem, how you made sure it worked, how you'd know if it stopped, and what you'd do about it.

## Segment 2 (steps: five-minute walkthrough)

The feature, one sentence. The eval — two or three cases, including a hard one. The monitoring — one chart, what it would catch. The incident plan — the failure mode and its real trigger. The model card — the one caveat you're most honest about.

## Segment 3 (code: what's not on the list)

Notice what's not there: a claim the system is perfect. The strongest signal is showing a case that failed and what you changed because of it — that's the actual skill this course has been building.

## Segment 4 (steps: talking about limitations)

There's a real difference between "this has a limitation" and "this doesn't work." Naming the specific weakness and the control that catches it is a stronger statement than hiding it or apologizing for it.

## Segment 5 (outro)

This course closes five chapters of production AI discipline — risks, evals, monitoring, governance, and one real pipeline you built yourself. The next course in this path, AI Engineering Capstones, is where that same discipline gets applied at full scale.

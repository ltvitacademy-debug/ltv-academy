# Lesson 12 — Data Quality and Adoption Metrics · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson covers the two metric categories a governance program can't skip: data quality, and the much harder one — adoption.

## S2 · STEPS — Quality: rolling rules into one number

A committee doesn't want four hundred individual rule results. It wants aggregate numbers: overall pass rate, a stricter critical-rule pass rate, and the trend direction — improving or declining quarter over quarter. Deciding which rules count as critical is a governance decision, not a dashboard-builder's call.

## S3 · STEPS — Adoption: the harder number

Quality metrics are easy — the rule results already exist. Adoption asks something fuzzier: did behavior actually change? Are new reports built on governed sources? Are stewards actually engaged? Is the glossary actually used? These numbers usually have to be pulled together by hand.

## S4 · STEPS — Reading them together

Quality and adoption tell different stories. High quality, low adoption means people haven't trusted the governed data yet — that's a change-management problem, not a data problem. Low quality, high adoption is the urgent one: people are relying on data that isn't ready.

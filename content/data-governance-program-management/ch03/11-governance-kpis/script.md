# Lesson 11 — Governance KPIs · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Welcome back to Data Governance Program Management. Chapter three is about measuring governance — and we're starting with the KPIs that prove any of it worked.

## S2 · STEPS — What makes something a governance KPI

A governance KPI isn't just any number. It's tied to a specific decision someone will make, it has a named owner accountable for moving it, and it's tracked consistently over time — not pulled together once for a slide.

## S3 · STEPS — Four categories

Most programs track four categories: program health — is the operating model itself working; data quality — is the data actually getting better; adoption — are people using what governance built; and issue resolution — is the escalation path actually working.

## S4 · STEPS — Leading vs. lagging indicators

A lagging indicator confirms an outcome after it happened — last quarter's pass rate. A leading indicator predicts a problem before it shows up — like domains still missing an assigned steward. Programs that only report lagging numbers are always explaining the past.

## S5 · STEPS — Avoiding vanity metrics

The test for any KPI: if this number moved sharply, would anyone change what they do next? "Number of policies published" looks good and drives nothing. If the answer is no, it's a vanity metric, not a KPI.

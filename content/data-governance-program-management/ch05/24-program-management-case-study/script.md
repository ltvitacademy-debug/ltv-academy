# Lesson 24 — Program Management Case Study · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Brightfield Agritech, a fictional mid-sized precision-agriculture
company, launched a governance program eighteen months ago because
agronomy field data and commodity-pricing data kept disagreeing, and
nobody could say which feed a grower-facing report should trust.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (Chapters 1-2 applied)

Chapter 1: the charter scoped exactly two domains, a stakeholder
analysis found the real root cause in a vendor's data format, and
stewards came from inside each team. Chapter 2: one real policy named
the trusted source, defined an exception workflow, and made decision
rights explicit — stewards approve new sensors, only the council
approves changes to normalization logic.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (Chapters 3-4 applied)

Chapter 3: the team tracked exactly two KPIs tied to the original
pain — disputed reports per month and time to resolve one — and the
dispute count dropped from double digits to near zero. Chapter 4 is
where it nearly stalled: field agronomists saw the new workflow as
friction, so the fix was a communication plan about growers, not
governance, training run by a respected peer, and a documented answer
to the loudest resistance.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (Chapter 5 applied)

Eighteen months in, three things converged: a maturity assessment
scored adoption outside the pilot team as only developing, the
roadmap moved that gap from Later to Now ahead of a planned expansion,
and a company-wide cost review threatened the budget — defended with
a value realization report leading with the dispute-rate drop, framed
for the CFO as rework and risk avoided.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

Two years in, the same charter, council, and stewards are still
running the program, now informed by evidence instead of guesswork,
and the adoption gap closed once training matched the people actually
doing the work. A program's hardest problems rarely show up at
launch — they show up when the first budget cycle ends, which is
exactly why this chapter exists.

# Lesson 25 — Program Management Practice Lab · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Twenty-four lessons each taught one piece of running a governance
program. This closing lesson isn't a twenty-fifth concept — it's the
exercise that makes you assemble the other twenty-four into one
packet you could hand to a real governance council.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (steps 1-3, Chapter 1)

Steps one through three come from Chapter 1: name the domain and
what's out of scope, list three affected stakeholders and who chairs
the council, and name one steward with one specific responsibility
nobody currently owns.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (steps 4-6, Chapters 2-3)

Steps four through six: write one real policy and who holds the
decision rights to change it, describe a three-step dispute and
escalation workflow, and name exactly two KPIs tied directly to the
original pain — not a long list.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (steps 7-9, Chapters 4-5)

Steps seven through nine: name the group most likely to resist and
one targeted fix for them, sort three next steps into now, next, and
later, and write one sentence defending the budget plus the single
weakest dimension today.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (reading the packet)

Read the finished packet as a whole. Solid charter but vague policy
means it isn't ready to launch yet. Solid steps one through seven but
a hard step eight means it's genuinely in the sustaining phase. And a
weak dimension that keeps repeating isn't a one-time finding — it
belongs on the roadmap every cycle until it moves.

## S6 · OUTRO CARD

Congratulations — that packet is Data Governance Program Management,
complete, charter through sustaining. The Data Governance career path
continues next with Data Governance Architecture, moving from how a
program is run to how the systems and platforms underneath it are
actually structured to support it.

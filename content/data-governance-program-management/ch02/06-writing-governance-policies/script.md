# Lesson 6 — Writing Governance Policies · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Foundations defined what a data policy is. Chapter two of this course is about what produces one
for real — the document template and the process behind it.

## S2 · STEPS — The document template

A real policy document has seven sections: a title and policy ID, purpose and rationale, scope
borrowed from the charter, the policy statement itself, a named owner and approver from the RACI
matrix, an effective date and review cycle, and links to the standards that implement it.

## S3 · STEPS — The lifecycle

Draft — the steward writes it, owner gives context. Review — legal, compliance, and IT weigh in.
Approve — the governance committee signs off, sponsor backs anything organization-wide. Publish —
somewhere central and discoverable. Review on a set cadence, not just once.

## S4 · STEPS — What weakens a policy

Naming a specific tool inside the policy ages it badly — that's a standard's job. No named owner
means no one updates or defends it. No stated scope makes it unenforceable. And no review cadence
means it quietly goes stale while the business changes underneath it.

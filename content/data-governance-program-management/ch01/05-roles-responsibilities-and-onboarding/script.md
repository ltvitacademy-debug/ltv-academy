# Lesson 5 — Roles, Responsibilities and Onboarding · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Foundations introduced owners, stewards, councils, and sponsors one at a time. This lesson puts
them in one picture, and covers how you actually get a real person productive in one of these
roles — closing out the chapter on launching the program.

## S2 · STEPS — The RACI matrix

A RACI matrix answers who does what for every activity at once. Responsible does the work.
Accountable owns the outcome and signs off. Consulted gives input beforehand. Informed is told
after. The discipline that matters: every single activity gets exactly one Accountable.

## S3 · STEPS — Why "everyone's responsible" fails

The most common mistake is assigning Accountable to more than one role, or to none, because it
feels collaborative. It's the opposite. When an issue sits unresolved for weeks, "everyone's a
little responsible" means nobody actually is, and it has nowhere specific to land.

## S4 · STEPS — Onboarding a real person

Defining a role on paper and getting someone productive in it are different problems. A practical
sequence: a written role description tied to the RACI matrix, context on the charter's scope and
the "why," the actual tools and access, a shadow period with the previous holder, and a formal,
visible announcement.

## S5 · OUTRO

Chapter one is complete — charter, stakeholders, councils, stewardship, and roles. Chapter two
moves into the actual output of all this structure: writing governance policies.

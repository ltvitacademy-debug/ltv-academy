# Lesson 18 — Communication Plans · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson is about turning last lesson's stakeholder map into an actual communication plan — not a single announcement email.

## S2 · STEPS — One email is not a plan

The most common failure is treating communication as one event — an announcement sent once, to everyone. By the time the new process actually affects someone's work weeks later, they've forgotten why it exists. A communication plan is a repeated, varied set of touches over time.

## S3 · STEPS — What a real plan needs

Multiple touches over time, not one. A message tailored to each audience. More than one channel — email, a meeting, a recorded walkthrough, office hours. A two-way path for questions, not just broadcast. And a visible owner people can actually reach.

## S4 · STEPS — Tailoring to the stakeholder map

High influence, high impact needs the business case and early input. High influence, low impact needs a concise heads-up and a specific ask. Low influence, high impact needs exactly what changes in their daily workflow. Low influence, low impact just needs a short informational note.

## S5 · STEPS — How to know it's working

A plan that was sent isn't the same as one that worked. Are people actually using the two-way channel? Is the adoption stage from lesson sixteen actually moving? Are the same basic questions still coming up weeks later — a sign the message needs repeating, not that people are slow.

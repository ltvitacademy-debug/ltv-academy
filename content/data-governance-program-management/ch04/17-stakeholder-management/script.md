# Lesson 17 — Stakeholder Management · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This lesson is about stakeholder management for a specific change — narrower and more ongoing than the broad stakeholder list from chapter one.

## S2 · STEPS — Different from Chapter 1's work

Lesson two was a one-time pass: who needs a seat at the table. This lesson asks something more specific — for one particular change, who's actually affected, and what does each of them specifically need from you to move toward adoption?

## S3 · STEPS — Mapping by influence and impact

Plot each affected group on two axes: influence — can they block or accelerate the change — and impact — how much it touches their daily work. That gives four groups: high influence high impact, high influence low impact, low influence high impact, and low influence low impact.

## S4 · STEPS — What each quadrant needs

High influence, high impact needs direct attention and early involvement. High influence, low impact needs to stay informed. Low influence, high impact — usually the biggest group — needs clear training and a channel to raise problems. Low influence, low impact just needs a general announcement.

## S5 · STEPS — Keeping the map current

A map drawn once goes stale. People change roles, a quiet skeptic turns out to have real influence, a champion moves departments. Revisit it on the same cadence as your communication plan.

## S6 · OUTRO

Next lesson: communication plans — turning this stakeholder map into an actual plan for reaching each group.

# Lesson 31 — Governance Workflows and Approvals · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

A workflow is the automation sitting underneath both policies and access requests — the thing that routes a decision to the right person and waits for their answer.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (two categories)

Purview's workflow engine covers two jobs: data product access workflows, which automate who approves a request to use data, and catalog curation workflows, which automate who signs off before something publishes.

## S3 · SCREENSHOT (workflow list)

Every configured workflow shows up in one list — its name, whether it's enabled, what it applies to, and its type.

## S4 · SCREENSHOT (name and continue)

Starting one is a short form, not a blank canvas — a name, an optional description, then Continue into the actual authoring experience.

## S5 · SCREENSHOT (canvas)

The same shape repeats across almost every workflow: a trigger, a start-and-wait-for-approval step, a condition, then an if-yes and if-no branch.

## S6 · STEPS CARD (approval types)

One setting changes how strict approval is. Pending on all means every named approver has to sign off. Pending on any means the first approval settles it.

## S7 · OUTRO CARD

Picking the wrong approval type has a real cost either way — too strict slows everyone down, too loose skips a needed second look. Next, a step back: Purview and Compliance Features Overview.

# Lesson 34 — Purview Practice Lab · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

This lesson introduces nothing new. It's six hands-on exercises, applying everything Chapters 1 through 6 already taught to one asset you'll work with start to finish.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (what you need)

All you need is a free-tier Purview account, one sample data source — never real production or customer data — and about ninety minutes worked through in order.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (exercises 1-3)

Register and scan one source. Build a collection structure matching a real or hypothetical organization. Then find one real classification hit, and apply one sensitivity label by hand.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (exercises 4-6)

Publish a glossary term that passes the four-part test, and attach it to an asset. Open that asset's lineage and explain where its data came from. Then write and run one data quality rule, and read what the score actually tells you.

## S5 · STEPS CARD (self-check)

Before moving on, you should be able to do three things without this guide open: find your asset in the catalog, trace its lineage out loud, and defend why its label and its rule are the right ones.

## S6 · OUTRO CARD

If any of those three feels shaky, that's the signal to revisit the relevant lesson now, while it's still cheap to fix. Next: this course's closing lesson, Purview Best Practices.

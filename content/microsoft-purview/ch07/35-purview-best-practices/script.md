## S1 · TITLE CARD

Five habits, pulled from across all seven chapters, separate a Purview rollout that sticks from one that quietly stalls. This closing lesson is this course, in one shape.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (habits 1-3)

Scope first — one collection, one domain, not the whole company at once. Scan before you classify, because the Data Map has to exist before anything built on top of it can. Define before you build, so a glossary term exists before three teams build three different versions.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (habits 4-5)

Split author from publisher — one person drafts a consequential change, a different person approves it, in policies and everywhere else. And make adoption real work: a perfectly governed catalog nobody searches hasn't solved anything.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (the shape)

Strip away the feature names, and this course taught one repeating shape: find it, with foundations and the Data Map. Trust it, with classification and the catalog. Govern it, with lineage and workflows.

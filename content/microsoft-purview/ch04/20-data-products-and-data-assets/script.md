# Lesson 20 — Data Products and Data Assets · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Two terms that sound similar but mean very different things: data asset, and data product. This lesson pulls them apart.

## S2 · STEPS — The table vs. the package

A data asset is one technical thing — a single table, file, or report that Data Map found. A data product is a curated bundle of assets, built around a real use case. And here's why that distinction earns its own lesson: nobody wants to request access to fifteen different tables one at a time. They want to request one data product, and get everything they need in one place.

## S3 · SCREENSHOT — Where data products live

Data products live inside governance domains. Here, inside the Fraud Services domain, business concept cards show exactly how many data products, glossary terms, OKRs, and critical data elements belong to it — four data products, nine glossary terms, at a glance.

## S4 · STEPS — What a data product contains

A well-built data product carries real content, not just a name. A description and use case, in plain language. Terms of use and contacts, so people know who owns it and how it can be used. And the actual linked assets, terms, and critical data elements that make up what's inside.

## S5 · SCREENSHOT — Owning a data product

Every data product needs an owner. Here, a contact is being assigned the role of data product owner, directly from the product's Contacts panel — a real person accountable for what's inside.

## S6 · SCREENSHOT — Measured, not just listed

And data products aren't just described — they're measured. This is a real Data product details report: a governance score per product, plus pass or fail indicators for classification, ownership, connection, and data-quality measurement, all calculated automatically, no manual reporting required.

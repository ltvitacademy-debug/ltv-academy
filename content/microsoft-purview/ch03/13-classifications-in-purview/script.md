# Lesson 13 — Classifications in Purview · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

A classification is a logical tag — Credit Card Number, Person's Name, Passport Number — based on the actual business context of what's in an asset.

## S2 · SCREENSHOT (classification example)

Here's a scanned Customer table with Email Address, Person's Name, and U.S. Phone Number already applied. That thunderbolt icon means system classification, applied automatically during the scan.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (two ways to apply)

Classifications get applied two ways. Automatically, when a scan samples your data and compares it against the scan rule set's rules. Or manually, through the asset's own detail page, any time a scan didn't catch what you need.

## S4 · SCREENSHOT (asset detail before)

Here's a file asset before any classification — the Classifications section is just empty until you select Edit.

## S5 · SCREENSHOT (select classifications)

From the dropdown, pick whichever apply — this example mixes Credit Card Number, a system classification, with CustomerAccountID, a custom one. That's completely normal.

## S6 · SCREENSHOT (confirmed)

Save it, and the Overview tab confirms what landed — both classifications now show right there.

## S7 · STEPS CARD (the table exception)

One exception worth remembering: scanning classifies files and columns automatically, but never the table asset itself — classifications live on the columns. Want one on the table? Add it by hand.

Next lesson: system versus custom classifications — what's built in, and when to build your own.

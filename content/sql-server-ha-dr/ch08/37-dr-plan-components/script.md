# Script — DR Plan Components

## Segment 1 (title)

Everything covered so far — backups, AGs, FCI, log shipping, replication — is a technology. A disaster recovery plan is the document that says which technologies protect which systems, who's responsible, and exactly what to do when things go wrong. This chapter is about that document.

## Segment 2 (steps: five real components)

A real DR plan needs a complete system and dependency inventory, RPO and RTO defined per system by the business, and named roles, a communication plan, and step-by-step recovery procedures — not just "the DBA team will handle it."

## Segment 3 (code: tools vs. plan)

Availability Groups, backups, and replication are the tools a DR plan relies on. The inventory and RPO/RTO are the targets. Roles and communication are the people. The runbook is the action. Having the tools without the rest is technology, not a plan.

## Segment 4 (outro)

That gap between having the technology and having a real plan only becomes visible during an actual disaster — the worst time to discover it. Next up: how RPO and RTO numbers actually drive technology choices in a real plan.

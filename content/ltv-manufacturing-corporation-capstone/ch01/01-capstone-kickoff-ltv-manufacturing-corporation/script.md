# Script — Capstone Kickoff: LTV Manufacturing Corporation

## Segment 1 (title)

Every course in this path handed you one piece — Enterprise Structures, General Ledger, Payables, Receivables, Cash Management, Fixed Assets, end-to-end processes, Subledger Accounting, Reporting, Security, Implementation, Troubleshooting. This capstone is where every piece becomes one job: one fictional company, start to finish, as the Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant.

## Segment 2 (steps)

This capstone runs as four chapters, not twenty-five unrelated exercises. Chapter one designs and configures the company's enterprise structure. Chapter two runs a real month of transactions through that configuration. Chapter three is the centerpiece: on January thirty-first, the CFO calls — the books don't balance and the period won't close. Chapter four wraps it into a portfolio.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Work this in your own Oracle Fusion practice environment, lesson by lesson, in order. Chapter three only makes sense if you actually built chapter one's configuration and ran chapter two's transactions yourself — the problems you'll investigate are planted inside work you did, not abstract exercises dropped in from nowhere.

## Segment 4 (steps)

By lesson twenty-five you'll have a complete enterprise structure, a month of real transactions across every module, a diagnosed and corrected month-end close, a full deliverables package — income statement, balance sheet, AP and AR aging, a reconciliation report, an issue log, implementation documentation — and a presentation you could give a real hiring panel.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Up next, lesson two: the company, its processes, and its requirements — the one canonical reference you'll use for every lesson that follows.

# Script — Subledger Accounting and General Ledger Posting

## Segment 1 (title)

This lesson runs Create Accounting across January and posts it to the General Ledger, closing Chapter 2. It also plants the last two of Chapter 3's six problems: a missing Subledger Accounting rule, and a journal that never got submitted.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Fourteen January Receivables transactions won't process — each one a freight-out charge crediting account 7850, added in December but never added to the Account Rule that tells Receivables which account a freight charge should credit. Create Accounting marks them Incomplete. Four thousand two hundred fifty dollars, stuck, not posted.

## Segment 3 (code)

Separately, Victor Okafor prepares January's intercompany overhead allocation journal, INTERCO-JAN-0131, $27,750, from the US parent's Corporate Overhead cost center to the Canadian subsidiary. He saves it as a draft, intending one more review — then a fire drill pulls him away, and it's never submitted for posting.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Six problems now exist quietly inside an otherwise clean month: an unreversed GRNI accrual, a misapplied cash receipt, a miscategorized asset, an unreconciled bank variance, fourteen incomplete Receivables transactions, and one unposted intercompany journal. Each one explainable alone. Together, they're why January 31st doesn't go smoothly.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Chapter 2 is complete. Up next, Chapter 3 begins with lesson 18: the CFO's call — the books don't balance.

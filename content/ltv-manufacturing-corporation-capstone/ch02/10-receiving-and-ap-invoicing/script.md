# Script — Receiving and AP Invoicing

## Segment 1 (title)

The bearings arrive at the Savannah dock before Meridian's invoice does. This lesson walks through the receipt, the December accrual it creates, and the January invoice that matches it — planting the first of Chapter 3's six problems along the way.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Priya Nandan receives all fifty units against PO-55210 on December 28th, no discrepancies. At December 31st close, the goods-received-not-invoiced process flags the $18,400 as an accrual. Journal JE-GRNI-1231 posts: debit inventory clearing, credit accrued liabilities.

## Segment 3 (code)

That journal is supposed to be flagged to auto-reverse on January 1st — and here's the mistake: the auto-reverse flag gets left unchecked, entered under end-of-year time pressure. The journal is correct for December. It just won't disappear when it's supposed to.

## Segment 4 (steps)

In January, Chen Liu enters Meridian's real invoice, INV-MER-88341, $18,400, matched three-way to the PO and the receipt. No price variance, no quantity variance — it validates cleanly, because the invoice itself is completely correct. The problem is one level up, in a December journal nobody's watching anymore.

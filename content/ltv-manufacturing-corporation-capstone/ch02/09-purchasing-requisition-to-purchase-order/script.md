# Script — Purchasing: Requisition to Purchase Order

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 1 built the foundation. This lesson runs the first real transaction through it: a requisition for replacement bearings at the Savannah plant, converted into a purchase order with Meridian Bearing Supply Co. Remember the amount — it comes back in Chapter 3.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Dana Whitfield, maintenance supervisor, is down to her last spare bearings for a production-line motor. She requisitions fifty units of Industrial Pump Bearing PB-4400 at $368 each — eighteen thousand four hundred dollars total — charged to Fabrication's inventory account, since it's a stocked item.

## Segment 3 (code)

Marcus Ibarra converts the approved requisition into purchase order PO-55210, with Meridian Bearing Supply Co., the same fifty units at the same total, Net 30 terms, matched at the three-way level, charged to account 1000-420-1410-0000-000.

## Segment 4 (steps)

PO-55210 is dated December 22nd — late enough that the bearings won't be fully invoiced before the year turns over. That timing gap, ordered and received in December but invoiced in January, is exactly the shape of a goods-received-not-invoiced accrual, which comes back in lesson 17 and again in Chapter 3.

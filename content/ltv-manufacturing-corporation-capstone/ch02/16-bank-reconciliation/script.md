# Script — Bank Reconciliation

## Segment 1 (title)

January's bank statement arrives. This lesson loads it into Cash Management and reconciles it against the Regions Bank account's GL activity — surfacing, but not yet resolving, the duplicate EFT from lesson eleven.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Grace Olsen loads the statement on February 1st. Almost everything clears automatically: Harborview's $55,100 deposit matches exactly, PMT-21094's $18,400 EFT matches exactly, routine smaller transactions match one for one.

## Segment 3 (code)

One line won't match. The bank shows two $9,200 EFT debits, January 10th and 11th, both referencing PMT-21087's file. The GL shows exactly one $9,200 disbursement, because that's all that actually happened on LTV's side. Unreconciled variance: $9,200, flagged but not explained.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Resolving it means contacting Regions Bank, confirming the duplicate transmission, and getting it reversed — a cross-system investigation that's exactly Chapter 3's territory. For now, it sits open on the reconciliation worksheet, carried into the January 31 close.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Up next, lesson seventeen: subledger accounting and General Ledger posting, where a missing SLA rule and an unposted intercompany journal round out this chapter's set of problems.

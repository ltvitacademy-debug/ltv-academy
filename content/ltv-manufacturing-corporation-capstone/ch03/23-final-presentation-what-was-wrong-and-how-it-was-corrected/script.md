# Script — Final Presentation: What Was Wrong and How It Was Corrected

## Segment 1 (title)

Two days after her call, Elena Marsh gathers the whole finance team for a thirty-minute readout before the board meeting. This is that presentation — every piece of Chapter 3 coming together into something a non-technical executive can use.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Lead with the headline, not the mechanics: all six issues identified, corrected, and verified, period closed and reconciled in both ledgers. Then group the six problems into three patterns — a setting that should have been checked, a step that should have been completed, and one event entirely outside LTV's control.

## Segment 3 (code)

The numbers, before and after: accrued liabilities down $18,400, Harborview's invoices showing their true status, depreciation corrected from $2,416.67 to $1,208.33, cash tying out after the bank's $9,200 reversal, $4,250 of freight now posted, $27,750 of overhead correctly allocated.

## Segment 4 (steps)

"Will this happen again?" is the question every CFO asks. The honest answer: these six specific issues won't recur because of specific process changes, but a first-ever close on new configuration will always surface something — the measure of a good implementation is catching it fast, not promising zero surprises forever.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Elena leaves with confidence in the numbers and a defensible story for her board. Up next, Chapter 4 begins with lesson twenty-four: reviewing your work against the requirements from lesson two.

# The CFO's Call: The Books Do Not Balance

**Chapter 3 · The January 31 Challenge · Lesson 18 of 25**

## The call

Elena Marsh calls at 4:30 PM on January 31, the day LTV's first-ever month-end close is supposed to complete. She's blunt: the board meets in two days, and she needs clean financials before then. Here's what she's seeing, in her own words:

1. "Our AP aging report doesn't match what the GL says we owe in Accounts Payable. I don't know which number is real."
2. "Harborview's biggest invoice this month shows up as overdue on the aging report, but I know they paid — I saw the wire. Something else looks weirdly overpaid, too."
3. "Depreciation on the new equipment looks heavier than I expected for one month. I want to know why before it rolls into February as well."
4. "Treasury flagged a bank reconciliation item that still isn't cleared. Nine thousand two hundred dollars, sitting open."
5. "Some Receivables transactions apparently never made it to the General Ledger at all. How much, and why?"
6. "And our intercompany numbers between the US and Canada don't tie. I can't close a period where intercompany doesn't eliminate."

## What "the books don't balance" actually means here

Elena's phrase is the language a CFO uses, not a technical diagnosis. In nearly every real case like this one, debits still equal credits inside every journal that actually posted — double-entry accounting doesn't silently break. What "doesn't balance" really means is: **subledgers don't reconcile to the GL, reports don't tie to each other, and known items are sitting open** — exactly the six symptoms above. Learning to hear "the books don't balance" and translate it into "which specific reconciliation is broken" is itself a core consulting skill, and it's what Chapter 3 trains.

## Mapping symptoms to causes

| Elena's symptom | The underlying problem (already planted in Chapter 2) |
|---|---|
| 1. AP aging vs. GL mismatch | The unreversed December GRNI accrual (lesson 10) |
| 2. Harborview invoice looks unpaid, something else overpaid | The misapplied cash receipt (lesson 13) |
| 3. Depreciation looks heavy | FA-10452's wrong asset category (lessons 14-15) |
| 4. $9,200.00 bank item still open | The duplicated EFT transmission (lesson 16) |
| 5. Receivables transactions missing from GL | The 14 Incomplete transactions from the SLA gap (lesson 17) |
| 6. Intercompany doesn't tie | The unposted intercompany journal (lesson 17) |

You don't yet know these root causes with certainty — Elena doesn't either. That's lessons 19 and 20's job.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Reconciling | Confirming two independent records of the same thing agree, and explaining any difference if they don't |
| Root cause | The actual originating error behind an observed symptom, as opposed to the symptom itself |

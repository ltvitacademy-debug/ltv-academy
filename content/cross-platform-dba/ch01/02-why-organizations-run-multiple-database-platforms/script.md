# Script — Why Organizations Run Multiple Database Platforms

## Segment 1 (title)

This lesson digs into the real, concrete reasons a company ends up running more than one database platform at once — not a hypothetical, but the actual business and technical events behind it.

## Segment 2 (steps: four real causes)

Four causes come up again and again. Mergers and acquisitions hand you whatever platform the other company ran. Legacy applications predate the current standard. Teams pick the best tool for a specific workload. And licensing cost pressure pushes gradual migration toward open source.

## Segment 3 (code: why standardization rarely finishes)

Given all that, why doesn't a company just standardize on one platform? Migrations are expensive and risky, some legacy systems are barely understood anymore, some vendor applications only support one platform, and the business case to migrate something that already works is often weak.

## Segment 4 (outro)

A multi-platform environment is normal, not a sign of dysfunction. Next up: a closer look at exactly which relational concepts transfer across platforms, and which don't.

# Script — Project Introduction: You're the Cross-Platform DBA

## Segment 1 (title)

This final chapter puts Oracle, MySQL, and PostgreSQL in front of you at once, the way a real job would. For the next five lessons, you're the newly hired Cross-Platform Database Administrator at a company that runs all three, and this is the work.

## Segment 2 (steps: meet the company)

Meet Harborline Retail Group, a mid-size retailer running three platforms for three ordinary business reasons. Oracle runs the finance system inherited from an acquisition. MySQL runs the customer-facing storefront built in-house years ago. PostgreSQL runs a newer analytics platform, chosen for cost and its extension ecosystem.

## Segment 3 (code: the environment)

Here's your environment for the rest of this project. Oracle instance FINPRD on host ora-fin01 holds the general ledger, AP, and AR tables. MySQL's storefront schema on mysql-web01 holds customers and orders. PostgreSQL's analytics database on pg-analytics01 holds the sales_fact reporting table.

## Segment 4 (steps: what this project builds)

Across the next four lessons, you'll stand up all three platforms in your Docker lab, secure and back up each one, walk through a real slow-performance incident on each, and turn the whole project into interview-ready answers.

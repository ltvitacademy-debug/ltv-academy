# Lesson 2 — Functional Interview Questions: General Ledger · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

These are real functional interview questions on General Ledger, with the reasoning behind each model answer — not trick questions, just the questions that confirm you've actually configured a ledger.

## S2 · STEPS — Structure questions

Asked to design a chart of accounts for two legal entities, lead with the requirement: each entity needs to stand alone and still consolidate. Name Company as the balancing segment and explain why it has to net to zero per entity. Asked about primary versus secondary ledgers, say primary is the official book, secondary restates the same activity under a different method or currency.

## S3 · STEPS — Process questions

Asked how journal approval and posting works end to end, walk it in order: entered or imported, routed through approval if the source requires it, then posted. Asked about intercompany journals, say they move balances between entities and must book a matching receivable and payable pair so each entity's own books stay balanced.

## S4 · STEPS — Judgment questions

Asked how you'd handle a period that isn't ready to close, don't say you'd force it. Identify exactly what's outstanding, get a real resolution timeline, and say so out loud to the client. Asked which reporting tool you'd use, say BI Publisher for a formatted executive report, OTBI for a fast ad hoc reconciliation check.

## S5 · OUTRO

Practice answering two of these out loud before moving on. Next lesson: the same format, applied to Payables and Receivables.

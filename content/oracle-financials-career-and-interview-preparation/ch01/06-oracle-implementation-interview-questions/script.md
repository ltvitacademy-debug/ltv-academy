# Lesson 6 — Oracle Implementation Interview Questions · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Implementation questions test whether you understand the project, not just the product. Your capstone's three chapters actually mirror this lifecycle already.

## S2 · STEPS — The phases, in practice

Plan - requirements and enterprise structure design. Configure and prototype - building it and validating with a conference room pilot. Test - system integration testing, then user acceptance testing with the client's own people. Cutover and go-live. Then hypercare, the intensive support period right after.

## S3 · STEPS — SIT, UAT, and CRP

SIT confirms the system and integrations work technically. UAT confirms the actual business users can do their real jobs in it - skipping UAT because SIT passed is a common, risky shortcut. A conference room pilot puts a working system in front of users well before go-live, surfacing gaps far more cheaply than finding them later.

## S4 · STEPS — Change control and hypercare

A late requirement doesn't get quietly squeezed in - assess the real impact and raise it through change control so the client sees the trade-off. During hypercare, your job shifts from building to rapid triage: is this a configuration gap, a training gap, or a real defect.

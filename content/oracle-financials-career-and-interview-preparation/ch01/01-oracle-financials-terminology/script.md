# Lesson 1 — Oracle Financials Terminology · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This course doesn't teach new Oracle configuration. It teaches you how to talk about everything you already know. We start with a working glossary — the vocabulary interviewers assume you already use correctly.

## S2 · STEPS — Enterprise structure and General Ledger

A legal entity is a real registered company. A ledger is the record-keeping structure it posts to — primary is the official book, secondary restates the same activity under a different convention. The chart of accounts segments every journal line, and the balancing segment is the one that must net to zero per legal entity, which is what makes intercompany elimination possible.

## S3 · STEPS — Subledger terms you'll be asked about

GRNI is an accrual for goods received before the invoice arrives, and it should auto-reverse. Matching compares an invoice to its PO, and sometimes the receipt too. AutoInvoice imports external sales data into AR. A misapplied receipt and an unreversed GRNI accrual were two of the six root causes in your capstone.

## S4 · STEPS — Subledger Accounting and reporting

Subledger Accounting, SLA, turns subledger transactions into balanced journals before they ever reach the GL — don't confuse it with "service level agreement." Create Accounting runs that engine; a transaction that fails comes back Incomplete instead of Final. OTBI is real-time ad hoc reporting; BI Publisher is pixel-perfect formatted output. FBDI and ADFdi are Oracle's two standard bulk-load methods.

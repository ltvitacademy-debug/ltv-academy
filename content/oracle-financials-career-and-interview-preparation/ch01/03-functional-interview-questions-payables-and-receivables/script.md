# Lesson 3 — Functional Interview Questions: Payables and Receivables · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Payables and Receivables questions test the full transaction lifecycle - not just entering an invoice, but what happens when something doesn't match, apply, or post cleanly.

## S2 · STEPS — Payables questions

Asked about 2-way versus 3-way matching, say 2-way compares invoice to PO, 3-way adds the receipt for anything physical. Asked about GRNI, say it's an accrual for goods received before the invoice, designed to auto-reverse - and if it doesn't, AP aging and the GL stop agreeing. Asked about holds, say you resolve the actual discrepancy, you don't just override it.

## S3 · STEPS — Receivables questions

Asked how AutoInvoice works, say it imports external transaction data into AR and most commonly fails on mismatched reference data. Asked about receipt application, say misapplying a receipt makes the wrong invoice look paid and the right one look open - exactly what the capstone's Harborview problem looked like.

## S4 · STEPS — Explaining AR aging to a client

Don't assume the report is wrong. Pull the detail, check the specific invoices and their receipt applications, and figure out if it's a timing difference or a real misapplication. Then translate what you found into one plain sentence the client can actually use.

## S5 · OUTRO

Use your own capstone work as the concrete example behind these answers whenever you can. Next lesson: scenario-based interview questions.

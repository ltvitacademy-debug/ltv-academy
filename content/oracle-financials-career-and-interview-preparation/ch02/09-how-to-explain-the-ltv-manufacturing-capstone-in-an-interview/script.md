# Lesson 9 — How to Explain the LTV Manufacturing Capstone in an Interview · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3.5 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Every resume bullet from the last lesson points back to one project. This lesson turns it into a single STAR story - Situation, Task, Action, Result - framed honestly as the training capstone it actually was.

## S2 · STEPS — Situation

LTV Manufacturing Corporation: a fictional manufacturer, a US parent in Savannah and a Canadian subsidiary in Windsor, a two-entity enterprise structure you configured yourself. On January 31, the CFO called with six specific symptoms - AP aging not matching the GL, a customer invoice and a receipt that looked wrong, depreciation running heavy, an open bank item, missing Receivables transactions, and intercompany that didn't tie.

## S3 · STEPS — Task

"The books don't balance" is a CFO's phrase, not a diagnosis. The job was to translate that into six specific, provable root causes, fix each one correctly, reconcile every subledger to the GL, and close the period before the board meeting two days later.

## S4 · STEPS — Action, part one

An unreversed GRNI accrual on a bearings purchase order, fixed with a reversing journal. A misapplied cash receipt that made one invoice look paid and another look overdue, fixed by reapplying it correctly. A newly capitalized asset in the wrong category, overstating depreciation, fixed with a reclassification.

## S5 · STEPS — Action, part two, and Result

A bank-side duplicate transmission, resolved with the bank's own written reversal - no GL correction needed. A missing Subledger Accounting rule, fixed by adding the mapping and rerunning Create Accounting. An unposted intercompany journal, reviewed and posted. Every subledger reconciled, intercompany tied out, and the period closed on time.

## S6 · OUTRO

The real skill isn't any one fix - it's translating a vague complaint into six provable problems and resolving every one. Next lesson: building a portfolio of your practice work.

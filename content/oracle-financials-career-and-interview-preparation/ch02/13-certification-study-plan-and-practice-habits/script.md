# Lesson 13 — Certification Study Plan and Practice Habits · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Lesson 12 covered why the landscape drifts. This lesson is the actual plan - how to study using what you've already built, without mistaking a passed practice exam for real readiness.

## S2 · STEPS — Build on what you already did

Map your own path work against the exam's published objectives before building a plan from zero. You spent entire courses on GL, AP, AR, Assets, Cash Management, and Subledger Accounting, plus a full capstone close. Your plan should target the specific gaps, not re-teach everything.

## S3 · STEPS — A realistic weekly structure

Two to three sessions a week, 45 to 60 minutes each, beats one long weekend cram. Alternate between reading an objective and actually rebuilding that configuration hands-on, without a guide open. Keep a running list of specific gaps, not a vague feeling.

## S4 · STEPS — The three-question readiness check

Can you rebuild the core objectives hands-on, not just recognize the right answer? Did your own path work already cover a meaningful share of these objectives? And are you using practice exams as a readiness check, not as the study plan itself?

## S5 · OUTRO

Once you can answer all three honestly, schedule the real exam only through Oracle's own current process - never a third-party shortcut. Next lesson: your first 90 days as an Oracle Financials consultant.

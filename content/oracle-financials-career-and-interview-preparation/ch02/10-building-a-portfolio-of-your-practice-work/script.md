# Lesson 10 — Building a Portfolio of Your Practice Work · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

A resume bullet and a good story go far, but a portfolio gives someone an actual artifact to look at. Oracle Fusion Cloud is licensed, login-gated software, so this is about what you realistically can share.

## S2 · STEPS — What you can't do

Don't assume it's fine to publish screenshots of a live Oracle instance publicly, even one you personally configured - check the terms that apply to your specific environment, and when in doubt, leave screenshots out of anything public.

## S3 · STEPS — Four things you can actually build

A written requirements and configuration workbook, in your own words. An issue log in the style of the capstone's close - symptom, root cause, fix, verification. Original SQL you wrote yourself for reconciliation checks. A one-page case study write-up of the capstone as a narrative.

## S4 · STEPS — Present it as evidence

Every piece should answer "what does this prove I can do" in one sentence before the detail. A requirements workbook proves you can gather real requirements. An issue log proves you can diagnose and fix, not just configure.

## S5 · OUTRO

Draft the issue log with three of your six root causes before moving on. Next lesson: behavioral interview preparation.

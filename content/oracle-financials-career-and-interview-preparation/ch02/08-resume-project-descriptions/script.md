# Lesson 8 — Resume Project Descriptions · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

You've done real, hands-on work across this entire path. None of it helps if it isn't on your resume in language a recruiter and hiring manager both understand quickly.

## S2 · STEPS — The formula

A strong bullet names what you did, in which part of the system, with what result. "Learned Oracle Fusion General Ledger" is weak. "Configured a two-entity enterprise structure supporting intercompany elimination" is strong - it's specific and provable.

## S3 · STEPS — Be honest about what this is

This is hands-on configuration and transaction practice in a structured training environment, not a paid client engagement - say so, and don't inflate it. It still reads as a real, substantive skill. Claiming client experience you don't have is the fastest way to lose credibility on the first follow-up question.

## S4 · STEPS — Sample bullets across the path

Enterprise structure, Payables matching and GRNI, Receivables AutoInvoice and receipt application, Fixed Assets categories, Cash Management reconciliation, Subledger Accounting rules, and the capstone close itself - each one becomes a specific, honest bullet naming the action, the system, and the outcome.

## S5 · OUTRO

Write three bullets of your own before moving on. Next lesson: how to explain the LTV Manufacturing capstone in an interview - the story behind all of these bullets.

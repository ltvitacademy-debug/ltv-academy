# Lesson 14 — Your First 90 Days as an Oracle Financials Consultant · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Getting the role is the goal of everything so far. This lesson is what actually happens after - a realistic 30/60/90-day framework, and the mistakes new consultants make most in exactly this window.

## S2 · STEPS — Days 1-30: learn the real environment

A real client's instance is never a blank version of what you practiced on - it has its own configuration history and quirks. The first 30 days are about learning this specific environment, shadowing, and asking more questions than you answer. The common mistake: trying to "fix" something before understanding why it's set up that way.

## S3 · STEPS — Days 31-60: contribute on bounded work

By day 60, take on real tasks within a scope someone senior reviews. Your hands-on practice pays off directly here. The mistake to avoid: taking on ambiguous work without restating it and asking a clarifying question first.

## S4 · STEPS — Days 61-90: own a workstream, build trust

By day 90, own a defined piece of work end to end. Trust comes less from technical correctness and more from communication - flagging problems early, realistic timelines, clear documentation. The mistake to avoid: going quiet when something's wrong.

## S5 · OUTRO

Be honest that the capstone gave you real reps at diagnosing problems, but not a real client's politics or scale - say so plainly, it builds confidence rather than undermining it. Next lesson: consulting career paths and next steps.

# Lesson 15 — Consulting Career Paths and Next Steps · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Final lesson of the Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant path. Target: ~2.5-3.5 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This is the last lesson in a path that started with the accounting equation and ends here, after a full enterprise structure build, every subledger, a real month-end close, and a full course of interview preparation.

## S2 · STEPS — A realistic progression

Functional Analyst, early career, well-defined tasks under supervision - roughly where your first 90 days lands you. Functional Consultant, owning workstreams independently. Senior Consultant, leading a module across a full engagement. Lead, Principal, or Practice roles, owning client relationships and solution design.

## S3 · STEPS — Two environments, same skill set

A staffing or consulting firm builds breadth faster across clients and industries, with travel and billable-hour pressure. In-house, working for one company, builds deep institutional knowledge with more stability but less variety. Neither is objectively better - they suit different priorities.

## S4 · STEPS — Specializing, and what to do next

Most people specialize over time - a vertical industry, a horizontal depth area like Subledger Accounting, or a leadership track. In the next few weeks: finish your portfolio artifacts, pick a certification target and map your study plan, start applying with your resume and capstone story, and keep building hands-on reps even after you land a role.

## S5 · OUTRO — Closing the entire path

You've completed the full Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant path - from the accounting equation through every subledger, a real month-end close, and this entire interview and career preparation course. That's real, substantial, hands-on preparation. From here, the next steps are in the real world: gaining real hands-on experience, pursuing certification if it fits your goals, and applying to roles with a resume and a story honest and specific enough to hold up under questioning. Good luck out there.

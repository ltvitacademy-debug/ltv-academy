# Lesson 11 — Behavioral Interview Preparation · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Every Oracle Financials interview eventually turns away from the software and asks about you. For consulting roles especially, these questions are often weighted as heavily as the technical ones.

## S2 · STEPS — STAR, as a general tool

Situation, Task, Action, Result works for any behavioral question, not just the capstone story. Set up the situation briefly, name what you were responsible for, describe the specific actions you took, close with a concrete result you can verify.

## S3 · STEPS — Four consulting-specific questions

Delivering difficult news - name the hard truth plainly, pair it with a credible plan. Ambiguous requirements - describe how you narrowed them, not just that they were confusing. A mistake you made - own it in one sentence, then focus on what you changed. A different communication style - name the real friction and the specific adjustment you made.

## S4 · STEPS — The capstone is behavioral material too

Lesson 9 covered the capstone as a technical story. It's also your best material for "tell me about a time you worked under pressure" - a tight deadline, six real problems, and a stakeholder who needed clear communication.

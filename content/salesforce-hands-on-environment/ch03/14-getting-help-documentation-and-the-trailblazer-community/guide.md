## The Help menu, built in

Every Salesforce org has a real Help menu — click the question mark icon in
the top navigation, and you get contextual articles for the exact page
you're currently on, plus links to Search Help and Get Support. It's part
of the product itself, not a separate site you have to remember to visit.

![The Salesforce Help and Training panel, showing contextual help articles for the current page along with Search Help and Get Support links.](/courses/salesforce-hands-on-environment/ch03/14-getting-help-documentation-and-the-trailblazer-community/help-and-training-menu.png)
*Click the question mark icon on any page, in any org — context-aware help follows you around Setup.*

## A real troubleshooting order

When you're stuck, there's a sequence worth following:

1. **help.salesforce.com** — the official documentation, and the first, most reliable stop for any "how do I" question.
2. **Trailhead's own modules** — they often cover the exact feature in more depth than a single help article can.
3. **The Trailblazer Community** — real admins, including ones who've almost certainly hit your exact problem before.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Help and Training menu | The in-product, contextual help panel available from any Setup page |
| help.salesforce.com | Salesforce's official documentation site |
| Trailblazer Community | Salesforce's public Q&A and networking community for admins, developers, and users |
| Community Groups | Topic- or role-based groups within the Trailblazer Community |

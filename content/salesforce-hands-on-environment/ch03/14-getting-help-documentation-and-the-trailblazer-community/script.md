# Script — Getting Help: Documentation and the Trailblazer Community

## Segment 1 (title)

Last lesson of this course. Everything else assumed you'd eventually get stuck — this lesson is specifically about what to do when that happens.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Help and Training menu)

Every Salesforce org has a real Help menu — click the question mark icon, and you get contextual articles for the exact page you're currently on, plus links to Search Help and Get Support. It's built into the product itself, not a separate site you have to remember.

## Segment 3 (steps: troubleshooting order)

There's a real order worth following when you're stuck. Start with help.salesforce.com, the official documentation — it's the first and most reliable stop for any "how do I" question. From there, check Trailhead's own modules; they often cover the exact feature in more depth than a single help article can. And if you're still stuck, the Trailblazer Community has real admins in it, including ones who've almost certainly hit your exact problem before.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: community question)

Here's what that actually looks like: a genuine question about building a formula field, and a genuine answer linking straight to the relevant Trailhead module. This is real give-and-take between working admins, not a forum nobody checks.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: community groups)

The community is organized into Groups built around what you're working on. Joining one relevant to Salesforce administration puts you alongside people — often tens of thousands of them — further along the exact same path you're on right now.

## Segment 6 (outro)

That closes out Hands-On Salesforce Environment. Your environment is ready, you know how to find your way around it, and you know exactly where to look when you get stuck.

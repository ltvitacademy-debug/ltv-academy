## Segment 1 (title)

You've got a permanent, organized org. This lesson is about putting it to work — using Trailhead's own modules and superbadges alongside everything LTV builds on top of it.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Trailblazer profile)

This is a real Trailblazer profile. Every badge, point, and trail you see here is tracked automatically as you complete hands-on work — it's a genuine, checkable record, not something you can fake or self-report. Employers and recruiters can verify it directly.

## Segment 3 (steps: modules vs superbadges vs LTV labs)

Trailhead gives you two different kinds of content, and it's worth knowing the difference. Modules are step-by-step units with hands-on challenges that Trailhead verifies automatically as you go. Superbadges are the opposite — no step-by-step instructions, just a real scenario you have to solve using everything you already know. And LTV's own labs, which you'll meet constantly in this path, are built the same way this course itself is structured, right inside the permanent org you set up in Lesson 11.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: LWC Recipes sample app)

A lot of Trailhead modules and projects go further than just instructions — they deploy a genuine working app directly into your org. This is LWC Recipes, a real sample app you can open and actually study the code of, not just read about in a tutorial.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: laptop Opportunity sample)

That's exactly where LTV's own labs fit in. They work the same way Trailhead's hands-on challenges do — real records, in your real org, verified by actually doing the work, not by answering multiple choice questions about it.

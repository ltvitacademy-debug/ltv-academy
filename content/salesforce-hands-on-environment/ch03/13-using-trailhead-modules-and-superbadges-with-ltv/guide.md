# Using Trailhead Modules and Superbadges With LTV

**Chapter 3 · Your Permanent Training Environment · Lesson 13 of 14**

Your permanent org isn't just for this course. This lesson covers how to use Trailhead's own
content — modules and superbadges — alongside it, and how LTV's own labs fit the same pattern.

## What you'll learn

- What your Trailblazer profile actually tracks, and why it functions as a real credential
- The difference between a Trailhead module and a superbadge
- How LTV's own hands-on labs are built on the same pattern, inside your permanent org

## Your Trailblazer profile is a real record

Every badge, point, and trail shown on your Trailblazer profile (reachable from your Trailhead
account) is tracked automatically the moment you complete hands-on work — Trailhead's own
verification checks your actual org to confirm the work was done, not just that you clicked
"complete." That's what makes it genuinely checkable: a recruiter or hiring manager can look at
your public profile and see real, verified activity, not a self-reported list.

## Modules vs. superbadges

- **Modules** are step-by-step learning units. Each unit typically ends in a hands-on challenge
  that Trailhead verifies automatically against your connected org — did you actually create the
  field, set up the flow, whatever the unit asked for.
- **Superbadges** are different on purpose: there are no step-by-step instructions. You're given a
  real business scenario and have to figure out, using everything you already know, how to solve
  it — much closer to what the actual job looks like.

Many modules and projects go a step further and deploy entire **working sample apps** directly into
your org — real code and real data you can open and study, not just read about secondhand.

## Where LTV's labs fit in

LTV's own hands-on labs, which show up constantly across this career path, are deliberately built
the same way: real records, created and verified inside your actual org (the permanent one you set
up in Lesson 11), not multiple-choice questions about what you'd theoretically do. The muscle memory
you build with Trailhead's own modules transfers directly to LTV's labs, and vice versa — it's all
the same org, the same interface, the same habits.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trailblazer profile | Your public, verified record of completed badges, points, and trails |
| Module | A step-by-step Trailhead learning unit with an auto-verified hands-on challenge |
| Superbadge | An instruction-free, scenario-based Trailhead assessment |

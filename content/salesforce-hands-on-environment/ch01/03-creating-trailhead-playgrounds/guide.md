# Creating Trailhead Playgrounds

**Chapter 1 · Your Free Practice Environment · Lesson 3 of 14**

A Trailhead account by itself doesn't give you anywhere to click. A **Trailhead Playground** does —
it's a real, full Salesforce org, created specifically for hands-on practice, and it's free.

## What you'll learn

- How to create a Trailhead Playground from Trailhead's hands-on challenge flow
- What you actually get: full Setup access, your own data, no trial expiration
- Where to manage every playground you've ever created, in one place

## Creating your first playground

The fastest way to create a playground is to open any Trailhead module's hands-on challenge and
click **Create Playground** (or, from your [Hands-On Orgs
page](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/me/hands-on-orgs), click **Create Playground** directly).
A dialog asks for a **Name** — give it something you'll actually recognize later, like "LTV
Training Org," rather than leaving the randomly generated default. Click **Create**, and Salesforce
provisions a brand-new org behind the scenes. It typically takes three to four minutes from click
to a fully working org.

## What you actually get

A Trailhead Playground is not a limited demo or a sandbox copy of someone else's data. It's a real
Salesforce org:

- **Full Setup access** — every admin feature this course walks through, with no feature gates
- **Your own data** — nothing shared with other students; it's entirely yours to populate and break
- **Free, indefinitely** — unlike a 30-day trial org, a playground doesn't expire on a timer, as
  long as you log in from time to time to keep it active

## Managing your playgrounds

Every playground (and any other org you've connected, like a Developer Edition org) appears on one
page: [trailhead.salesforce.com/me/hands-on-orgs](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/me/hands-on-orgs),
labeled **Hands-On Orgs**. From there you can:

- **Launch** — log straight into Setup for that org
- **Rename** — give it a clearer name later (covered more in Lesson 12)
- **Disconnect** — remove it from your account (covered more in Lesson 10)
- **Create Playground** or **Connect Org** — add another

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trailhead Playground | A free, full Salesforce org created for hands-on practice |
| Hands-On Orgs page | trailhead.salesforce.com/me/hands-on-orgs — manages every org you've connected |
| Launch | Logs you directly into a connected org's Setup area |

# Script — Creating Trailhead Playgrounds

## Segment 1 (title)

Your Trailhead account is set up. Now let's turn it into something you can actually click around in: a Trailhead Playground.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Create Playground dialog)

This is the real Create Playground dialog. Give it a name you'll actually recognize later — not the default random name — and click Create. Salesforce provisions a brand-new org behind the scenes, which usually takes three to four minutes.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Verify Step / Launch)

Here's the same mechanism from the other direction. Any hands-on challenge on Trailhead — including ones in this course later on — launches directly into one of your connected playgrounds. Click Launch, and you're logged straight into Setup, ready to go.

## Segment 4 (steps: what you get)

What you actually get is a real org. Full Setup access, with every admin feature this course covers and no feature gates. Your own data, not shared with other students. And it's free indefinitely — there's no trial countdown, as long as you log in every so often to keep it active.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: Hands-On Orgs page)

Every playground you've ever created lives on this one page — trailhead dot salesforce dot com slash me slash hands-on-orgs. From here you can rename a playground, disconnect one you don't need anymore, launch straight into Setup, or create another.

## Segment 6 (outro)

You now have a real Salesforce org. Next: what a Trailhead Playground actually is under the hood — a Developer Edition org — and why that distinction matters.

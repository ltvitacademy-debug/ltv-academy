# Script — Creating Your Trailhead Account

## Segment 1 (title)

Let's actually do this. Creating a Trailhead account takes about five minutes, and everything else in this course depends on having one.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Trailhead homepage)

This is the real Trailhead homepage, live right now. Trailhead is Salesforce's own free learning platform, and the Sign Up button in the top right is where every single account starts.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: sign-up screen)

Click it, and you land here — the real sign-up screen. You can sign up with Google, an existing Salesforce login, MuleSoft, Slack, or just a business email with a one-time code. There's never a credit card involved, because a Trailhead account by itself doesn't grant you an org yet.

## Segment 4 (steps: two warnings)

Two things worth knowing before you click. First, never sign up using a Salesforce trial org login — trial orgs expire, and when they do, you lose access to the Trailhead account tied to them. Second, use an email address you'll actually keep, because this account is permanent — it's the same one you'll still be using in Lesson 11. And know that your username becomes part of your public Trailblazer profile.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: account settings)

Once you're signed up, this is where your account settings live — Privacy, Connected Accounts, Email Preferences. By default your profile is public, which is completely normal and fine to leave as-is; it's how recruiters and other Trailblazers can eventually see your badges.

## Segment 6 (outro)

Account created. Next: turning that account into an actual Salesforce org you can click around in — a Trailhead Playground.

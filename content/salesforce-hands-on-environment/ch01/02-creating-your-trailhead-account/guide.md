# Creating Your Trailhead Account

**Chapter 1 · Your Free Practice Environment · Lesson 2 of 14**

This is the first concrete step in the course: creating the free Trailhead account that everything
else — your Playground, your badges, your permanent training environment — is built on top of.

## What you'll learn

- Where to sign up for Trailhead, and the sign-up options available
- Two mistakes to avoid when choosing how to sign up
- Where your account's privacy and email settings live afterward

## Signing up

Go to [trailhead.salesforce.com](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/) and click **Sign Up** in the
top right. You'll land on a screen titled "Let's set up your free Trailblazer account," with
several ways in: an existing Salesforce login, Google, MuleSoft, Slack, or a plain business email
(Trailhead emails you a one-time code — no password to remember). There's no credit card anywhere
in this flow, because a Trailhead account on its own doesn't include a Salesforce org yet — that's
Lesson 3.

## Two mistakes to avoid

1. **Don't sign up using a Salesforce trial org's login.** Trial orgs expire on a fixed schedule
   and can't be reactivated. If you sign up with one and it expires, you lose access to the
   Trailhead account tied to it — badges, trails, everything.
2. **Use an email address you'll still have access to in a year.** This account isn't disposable.
   Lesson 11 of this course is specifically about treating this as your *permanent* training
   identity across every course in this career path, not something you recreate later.

One more thing worth knowing up front: whatever username you pick becomes part of your public
Trailblazer profile URL. You don't need to use your full legal name, but pick something you
wouldn't mind a future employer seeing — Lesson 13 covers how that profile doubles as a real,
checkable portfolio.

## Your account settings

Once you're in, your account's Settings page (Privacy, Connected Accounts, Email Preferences) is
reachable from your profile icon. The default **Profile Privacy** is Public — that's normal, and
it's what lets your completed badges actually function as a portfolio later. You can switch it to
Private at any time if you'd rather.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trailhead | Salesforce's free learning platform, at trailhead.salesforce.com |
| Trailblazer account | Your personal Trailhead login — tracks badges, points, and trails |
| Trailblazer profile | The public page built from your Trailblazer account's activity |

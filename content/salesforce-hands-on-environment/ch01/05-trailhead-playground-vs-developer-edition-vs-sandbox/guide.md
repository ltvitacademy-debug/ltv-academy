# Trailhead Playground vs. Developer Edition vs. Sandbox

**Chapter 1 · Your Free Practice Environment · Lesson 5 of 14**

These three terms get used loosely, often interchangeably, which causes real confusion. This
lesson draws a clean line between them — closing out Chapter One before Chapter Two moves into
actually using Setup.

## What you'll learn

- The precise difference between a Trailhead Playground, a Developer Edition org, and a sandbox
- The one distinction that actually matters: where each one's data comes from
- Which of the three you already have, and which one you'll meet later on the job

## Three terms, three different things

| Term | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| **Trailhead Playground** | A free Developer Edition org, created and managed through Trailhead, for learning |
| **Developer Edition org** | The exact same org type, signed up for directly at developer.salesforce.com — no Trailhead badges attached |
| **Sandbox** | A copy of a company's own paid production org, used by real teams to build and test safely |

A Trailhead Playground and a standalone Developer Edition org are, under the hood, the same thing —
Lesson 4 already showed you that a Playground's Company Information page literally reads "Developer
Edition." The only difference is how you got it: through Trailhead's hands-on challenge flow, or by
signing up directly.

## The distinction that actually matters

A sandbox is fundamentally different from the other two, and the difference is about where the
data comes from:

- A **Trailhead Playground** or **Developer Edition org** always starts completely empty, and
  belongs to you personally.
- A **sandbox** always starts as a **copy of one specific, real production org**. It belongs to
  whichever company owns that production org — not to you individually.

That's why you can't just "create a sandbox" the way you create a Trailhead Playground. A sandbox
only exists in relation to a company's paid Salesforce org, and only someone with admin access to
that production org can create one.

## Which one is yours, right now

The Trailhead Playground you created in Lesson 3 is what this entire course uses. Later, once
you're working as an admin for a real company, you'll typically be given access to one of their
sandboxes to build and test changes in before anything touches production. The reassuring part:
Setup, Object Manager, Flow, and everything else this course teaches look and behave identically
across all three environments. Nothing you practice here becomes obsolete once you're working in a
real sandbox.

# Script — Your Personal Settings

## Segment 1 (title)

Setup isn't only where you configure the org for everyone else. It's also where your own personal settings live, separate from anything that affects other users.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: profile menu Settings)

Click your profile photo in the top right of any page, and choose Settings. This is deliberately separate from Setup's gear icon — Settings is scoped to you personally, not the organization.

## Segment 3 (steps: what's in there)

Inside, you'll find Personal Information — your name, email, and the time zone your records display in. Change My Password, for your own login credentials, unrelated to anyone else's. And Language and Time Zone, which controls how dates, numbers, and labels render specifically for you.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Forgot Password flow)

Locked out of your own account? This is the self-service path: Forgot Your Password. Enter your username, and Salesforce emails you a reset link — no admin needed for your own login.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: Setup Users reset password)

But if it's a teammate who's locked out, not you, this is the admin side of the same problem: Setup, then Users, select the person, and click Reset Password. That's an admin action on someone else's account, which is exactly why it lives in Setup instead of personal Settings.

## Segment 6 (outro)

Personal settings, covered. Next: zooming all the way out, from your own settings to the information and configuration that describes the entire org.

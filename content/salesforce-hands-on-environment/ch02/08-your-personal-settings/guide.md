# Your Personal Settings

**Chapter 2 · Getting Comfortable in Setup · Lesson 8 of 14**

Not everything in Salesforce's configuration areas is for configuring the org. Your own personal
settings — separate from Setup's gear icon — are scoped to you alone.

## What you'll learn

- Where your personal Settings live, and how that differs from Setup
- The three things most people adjust first: Personal Information, password, and Language/Time Zone
- The difference between resetting your own password and an admin resetting someone else's

## Finding your personal settings

Click your **profile photo** in the top right of any Salesforce page, then choose **Settings**.
This is a deliberately different menu from the gear icon that opens Setup — Settings only affects
your own account, never the organization or other users.

## What's actually in there

A few things worth knowing on day one:

- **Personal Information** — your name, email address, and the time zone your own records display
  in
- **Change My Password** — your own login credentials; this never touches anyone else's account
- **Language & Time Zone** — controls how dates, numbers, and field labels render specifically for
  you, independent of how they render for other users

## Resetting your own password vs. someone else's

If **you're** locked out, use the self-service path: on the login screen, click **Forgot Your
Password?**, enter your username, and Salesforce emails you a reset link. No admin involvement
required.

If a **teammate** is locked out, that's a different, admin-side flow: in Setup, go to **Users**,
select the person, and click **Reset Password(s)**. This is an admin action performed on someone
else's account, which is exactly why it lives in Setup rather than in personal Settings — it
requires admin permissions that an ordinary user doesn't have.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Settings (personal) | Reached from your profile photo — scoped to your own account only |
| Setup | Reached from the gear icon — org-wide configuration, requires admin access |
| Forgot Your Password? | Self-service password reset, triggered from the login screen |

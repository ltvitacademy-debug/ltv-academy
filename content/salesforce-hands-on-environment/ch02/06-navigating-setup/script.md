# Script — Navigating Setup

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter Two is about getting genuinely comfortable in the interface you'll live in as an admin: Setup. Let's start with the layout.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Setup Home numbered)

This is the real Setup Home page, numbered the way Salesforce itself documents it. One is Quick Find, the search box that filters the entire left sidebar as you type. Two is the Home and Object Manager tabs at the very top. Three is Search Setup, the global search box that can jump you to any Setup page by name. You'll use all three constantly.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: App Launcher)

One mix-up almost everyone makes early on: the grid icon opens the App Launcher, for switching between apps and records — that's not Setup. The gear icon right next to it is what actually opens Setup. They sit close together on purpose, which is exactly why it's worth seeing them side by side once.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: Quick Find filtering)

Here's Quick Find in action. Type into it, and the entire left sidebar narrows instantly to match. Typing "user" surfaces Users, Permission Sets, Profiles, and every related page — no scrolling through dozens of categories to find what you need.

## Segment 5 (steps: sidebar's two halves)

The Setup sidebar itself splits into two broad halves. Administration covers Users, Data, and Email — who has access, and what the data looks like. Platform Tools covers Apps, Objects, and Automation — how the org itself is built. And Object Manager gets its own top-level tab, separate from the sidebar entirely, because you'll open it so often.

## Segment 6 (outro)

That's the lay of the land. Next: going even faster than the sidebar, with Setup Search and Quick Find covered in real depth.

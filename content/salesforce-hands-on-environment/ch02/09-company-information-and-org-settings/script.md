# Script — Company Information and Org Settings

## Segment 1 (title)

You've covered your own personal settings. Now let's zoom all the way out, to the one Setup page that describes the entire org at once: Company Information.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Company Settings nav)

Get there by typing "Company Information" into Quick Find from Setup, or by browsing to it under Company Settings in the sidebar. Either path lands on the same page.

## Segment 3 (steps: what the page tells you)

This single page tells you the org's whole profile at a glance. Its Organization ID — a unique 15-character identifier, unique across every Salesforce org that exists. Its edition and instance — which Salesforce edition this is, and which physical data center it actually runs on. And its licenses and storage — exactly what's purchased, used, and what's left.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: User Licenses table)

Here's the real User Licenses table. It lists every license type the org has, how many are currently in use, and how many remain — the first place to check before you try creating a new user and hit a wall.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: Data Storage table)

And here's the real Data Storage table, breaking down exactly what's consuming the org's storage limit, by record type. The moment storage warnings start showing up, this is where you go to see why.

## Segment 6 (outro)

That's the org's profile page, covered. Next: what data already exists in a brand-new org, and what your actual options are if you want a clean slate.

# Script — Sample Data and Resetting Your Org

## Segment 1 (title)

You've got the org's profile page figured out. This lesson covers two practical questions: what's actually already in a brand-new org, and what to do if you want to start over.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: sample opportunity record)

A freshly created org isn't actually empty. It ships with a small set of sample records — accounts like Acme, and opportunities like this one, clearly labeled Sample right in their names. That's intentional, so there's something to look at immediately.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Coral Cloud Resorts sample app)

Sample records are just the start. Salesforce's free Sample Gallery installs entire working apps — like Coral Cloud Resorts here — complete with realistic sample data already populated. Worth exploring once the default sample records start feeling thin for whatever you're practicing.

## Segment 4 (steps: what resetting means)

Now, what about resetting? Here's the honest answer: there's no reset button. Salesforce doesn't offer a one-click way to empty out an existing org. The real path to a clean slate is disconnecting the one you have and creating a brand-new playground instead. And disconnecting doesn't delete your old org — it just removes it from your personal list.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: Hands-On Orgs Disconnect)

This happens on the same Hands-On Orgs page from Lesson 3. Disconnect removes a playground from your list. Create Playground gives you a genuinely fresh one, with only the default sample data back again — that's effectively your reset.

## Segment 6 (outro)

Sample data and resets, demystified. Chapter Two is done. Next, Chapter Three: picking the one org you'll actually keep using for the rest of this path.

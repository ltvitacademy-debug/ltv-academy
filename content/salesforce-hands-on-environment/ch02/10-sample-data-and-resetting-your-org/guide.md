# Sample Data and Resetting Your Org

**Chapter 2 · Getting Comfortable in Setup · Lesson 10 of 14**

Two practical questions close out this chapter: what's already sitting in a brand-new org before
you touch anything, and what your real options are if you want to start over.

## What you'll learn

- That new orgs ship with a small set of pre-loaded sample records
- Where to get entire sample apps with realistic data, beyond the defaults
- Why there's no literal "reset" button, and what the real equivalent is

## New orgs aren't actually empty

A freshly created Trailhead Playground or Developer Edition org isn't a blank slate. Salesforce
pre-loads a small set of **sample records** — accounts like "Acme," and opportunities such as
"Acme - 1,200 Widgets (Sample)," with "(Sample)" clearly marked right in the record name. This is
intentional: it gives you something real to look at and click into the moment the org finishes
provisioning, rather than a completely empty Home tab.

## Going further: the Sample Gallery

Once the handful of default sample records start feeling thin, Salesforce's free **Sample
Gallery** (at developer.salesforce.com) installs entire working sample apps directly into your org
— complete with realistic, pre-populated data. "Coral Cloud Resorts" and "LWC Recipes" are two real
examples: full apps with custom objects, records, and working functionality, meant for exploring
and learning from, not just reading about.

## There's no literal "reset" button

Here's the honest answer, worth knowing before you go looking for one: Salesforce doesn't offer a
one-click way to wipe an existing org back to empty. If you've built up a mess you want to walk
away from entirely, the real path is:

1. Go to your [Hands-On Orgs page](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/me/hands-on-orgs)
2. **Disconnect** the playground you want to leave behind — this removes it from *your* list, it
   doesn't delete the org itself
3. **Create Playground** to provision a genuinely fresh one, which comes back with only the default
   sample data

That's the practical equivalent of a reset: not emptying what exists, but replacing it with
something new.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sample records | The small set of pre-loaded Account/Opportunity/etc. records in a new org |
| Sample Gallery | Salesforce's free collection of installable full sample apps with real data |
| Disconnect | Removes a playground from your Hands-On Orgs list, without deleting the org |

# Script — The Accounting Equation

## Segment 1 (title)

Every accounting system ever built rests on one unbreakable rule: the accounting equation. Once you understand it, debits and credits stop feeling arbitrary and start feeling like simple arithmetic.

## Segment 2 (steps)

The equation is assets equal liabilities plus equity. Assets are everything the business owns or controls that has value. Liabilities are everything it owes to others. Equity is what's left over for the owners once liabilities are subtracted from assets. This has to be true at every single moment, for every business, no exceptions.

## Segment 3 (code)

Picture a fictional company, Meridian Fusion Consulting, started with fifty thousand dollars of the owner's cash. Assets: fifty thousand. Liabilities: zero. Equity: fifty thousand. Now it borrows twenty thousand to buy a server. Assets become seventy thousand, liabilities become twenty thousand, equity stays at fifty thousand. Still balanced.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Here's the deepest idea in accounting. Every transaction must affect the equation in at least two places, by equal amounts, so it never falls out of balance. Buy a server with a loan: an asset and a liability both go up by the same amount. This always-two-sided property is called double-entry accounting, and it's why every journal entry has at least one debit and one credit of equal value.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Here's why this matters in Oracle Fusion. General Ledger will physically reject a journal where debits don't equal credits. That's the system enforcing this exact equation. Up next, lesson three: businesses, entities, and the books.

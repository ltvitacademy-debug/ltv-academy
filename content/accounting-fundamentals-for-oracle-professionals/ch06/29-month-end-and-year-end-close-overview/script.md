# Script — Month-End and Year-End Close Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the final lesson of Accounting Fundamentals for Oracle Professionals. It zooms into closing the books, separating the routine monthly version from the more involved annual version.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A typical month-end close runs in order. Cut off new postings to the period. Complete subledger close, making sure payables and receivables have summarized to the GL. Record adjusting entries for anything without a natural paper trail yet. Reconcile key accounts against external sources like bank statements. Review the trial balance, generate financial statements, get management review, then close the period and post reversing entries for the new one.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Year-end adds a few things on top. Temporary accounts - revenue and expenses - get closed, zeroed out, with their net effect rolled into retained earnings, so the next year's income statement starts fresh. There are deeper annual reconciliations, often with external audit involvement, and older periods get permanently closed for good.

## Segment 4 (steps)

A fast, accurate close is one of the most visible signs of a well-run accounting department and a well-configured system. A slow, error-prone close usually means something upstream needs attention - which is exactly the kind of problem Oracle Fusion Financials implementations get brought in to solve.

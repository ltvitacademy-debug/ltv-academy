# Script — Deciding When to Go External

## Segment 1 (title)

Knowing the limits is half the skill. The other half is deciding what to do about them. Native reports are free, always current, and secured by your sharing rules, so leaving them has to be earned.

## Segment 2 (steps: five questions)

Write the business question and the audience first, then ask five things in order. Can one report type plus filters answer it? Does it need history nobody captured? Does it need outside data? Is volume the real constraint? And what does the audience need? Most requests stop at the first question.

## Segment 3 (steps: options)

The options fall into categories. CRM Analytics goes deeper inside Salesforce and can bring in outside data. Tableau explores many sources visually. Tableau Next is Salesforce's newer analytics experience. A warehouse with a BI tool suits organizations that already have one. Names and licensing change, so confirm current details.

## Segment 4 (code: hidden costs)

Then count the hidden costs. Licensing and pipelines. Governance, because a copy outside Salesforce needs its own security. Freshness, since a copy is only as current as its last refresh. Skills and support. And worst of all, two versions of the truth. Each of these costs real time long after the demo is over.

## Segment 5 (code: rule)

The rule of thumb: native first, escalate on evidence. Point to a specific requirement the native tool can't meet and the business will pay for. Evidence means you can name what the native version failed to answer. Escalating by taste builds expensive dashboards. Never escalating buys weeks of workarounds.

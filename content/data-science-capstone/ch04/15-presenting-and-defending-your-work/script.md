Now someone smart and slightly skeptical will try to find the hole in your work. That is how organizations protect their money. So write down the questions you dread, and answer each with a number from your own project.

Use four parts for any challenge: answer in one sentence, give the evidence, concede what is true in the objection, and name the next step. Conceding is not weakness. It shows you saw the problem first.

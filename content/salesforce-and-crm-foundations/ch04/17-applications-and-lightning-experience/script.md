# Script — Applications and Lightning Experience

## Segment 1 (title)

This lesson is about what a Salesforce "app" actually is, how you switch between apps, and the Lightning Experience interface you'll be working in for this entire course.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: seller home + app launcher icon)

This is a real Sales app Home page. Notice the tabs across the top — Home, Opportunities, Leads, Tasks, Files, Accounts, Contacts — that's one curated bundle of tabs, built for a sales job function. And in the top-left, that grid-of-dots icon is the App Launcher, used to switch to a completely different app.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: app launcher dropdown)

Clicking the App Launcher opens this quick panel — a search box plus a short list of your apps, things like Service, Community, and Sales. Switching apps doesn't change your data or your permissions. It just changes which set of tabs is in front of you.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: app launcher full view)

Clicking "View All" opens the full App Launcher page. All Apps up top shows everything you can switch into. All Items below it lists every single object you have access to — even ones that aren't in your current app's tab bar at all.

## Segment 5 (steps: lightning vs classic)

Everything you've seen is Lightning Experience — the current, modern Salesforce interface. It replaced the older Salesforce Classic interface, which is being phased out. As a student starting today, you'll do essentially all of your work in Lightning.

# Script — How Businesses Actually Use Salesforce

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the final lesson of Salesforce & CRM Foundations. It connects everything from this course's four chapters into one realistic picture of a business actually running on Salesforce.

## Segment 2 (steps: a day across departments)

Picture a mid-size company on Salesforce. Marketing runs a campaign, and everyone who clicks through becomes a Lead. Sales works that Lead into an Account, Contact, and Opportunity, living in the Sales app and using a list view to plan their week. Service picks up after the sale — a Case, worked from a completely different app with a completely different Home page, even though it's the exact same org and the exact same Account record underneath. And leadership checks a Dashboard built from live Reports, showing pipeline and case volume across everyone's work.

## Segment 3 (code: what stays the same)

Notice what's consistent underneath all of that: one multitenant org, one edition and set of licenses, the same metadata-driven objects and fields — just surfaced through different apps for different jobs.

## Segment 4 (steps: across industries)

Because Salesforce is this customizable, wildly different organizations use it in wildly different ways. A nonprofit might use Accounts for donor households. A university might use a custom Course object. A financial services firm runs an entire specialized Cloud product built on the same core platform. And a small business might run everything out of one Sales app with almost no customization at all.

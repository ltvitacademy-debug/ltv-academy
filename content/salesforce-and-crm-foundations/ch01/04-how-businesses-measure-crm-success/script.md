# Script — How Businesses Measure CRM Success

## Segment 1 (title)

A business can buy CRM licenses for everyone and still get nothing out of it, if the data inside is thin or wrong. The real measure of success is whether the numbers coming out of it are ones leadership can actually trust and act on.

## Segment 2 (steps: what each team watches)

Marketing is judged on lead volume, conversion rate, and cost per lead. Sales is judged on win rate, average deal size, sales cycle length, and pipeline — the total value of open opportunities. Service is judged on case volume, resolution time, customer satisfaction, and churn rate.

## Segment 3 (steps: bad data breaks every number)

Every one of those numbers is only as good as the data behind it. Duplicate accounts split a customer's history in two. Opportunities left sitting in a stale stage make win rate and pipeline meaningless. Leads nobody logged don't exist for a conversion-rate calculation, even though they were real.

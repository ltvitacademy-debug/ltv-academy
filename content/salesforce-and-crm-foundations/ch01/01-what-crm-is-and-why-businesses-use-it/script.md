# Script — What CRM Is and Why Businesses Use It

## Segment 1 (title)

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management — a shared system of record for every interaction a business has with a prospect or customer, plus the discipline of actually using it instead of letting information scatter across inboxes and spreadsheets.

## Segment 2 (steps: what breaks without it)

Without a shared system: leads go cold because no one follows up. Customers repeat themselves to every new person they talk to. Deals get double-worked or dropped when a rep is out. And leadership can't forecast revenue, because what's likely to close is a gut feeling, not a number.

## Segment 3 (steps: what CRM buys you)

CRM turns all of that into shared, structured data — every account, contact, deal, and case in one place, visible to whoever needs it, with a history attached. That turns individual effort into organizational memory the business keeps, even after any one person moves on.

# Script — The Salesforce Platform vs. Salesforce Applications

## Segment 1 (title)

People casually say "Salesforce" to mean several different things. The Salesforce Platform is the underlying foundation — a database, a security model, customization and development tools. A Salesforce application, like Sales Cloud or Service Cloud, is something built on that platform for a specific job.

## Segment 2 (steps: platform vs. application)

Strip away Sales Cloud's leads and opportunities, and the platform underneath is still fully functional — your own data structures, a security model, automation without code, and a full programming environment for deeper building. Every application ships on the platform. Not everything built on the platform is one of the named CRM applications.

## Segment 3 (steps: beyond CRM)

Because the platform is general-purpose, companies build things on Salesforce that have nothing to do with CRM — an equipment maintenance tracker, an event registration portal, an entirely custom application for an industry Salesforce never designed for.

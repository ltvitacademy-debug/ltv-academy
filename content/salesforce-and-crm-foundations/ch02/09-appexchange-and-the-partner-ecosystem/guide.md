## A marketplace built on the platform/application split

Lesson 8 drew a line between the Salesforce Platform and the
applications built on top of it. AppExchange is where that line
becomes a business: a marketplace, launched in 2005 (Lesson 6), where
companies and individual developers package applications they've
built on the platform and make them available — free or paid — to any
other Salesforce customer.

## What's actually listed there

AppExchange isn't only pre-built applications. It lists several kinds
of things a Salesforce customer might need:

- **Full applications** that add entirely new functionality
- **Components** that extend or customize an existing screen
- **Lightning data sets** — cleaned, ready-to-use external data
- **Consultants** — implementation partners available to hire directly
  through the marketplace, not just software

## Evaluating a listing

Every listing follows a similar format: a description, a pricing
model, a star rating with written reviews, and the publisher's
category and business need. Before installing anything into a real
org, it's worth actually reading that listing closely — what it costs,
what data access it requires, and what other customers say about it in
the reviews.

## The partner ecosystem, beyond the listings

Behind every AppExchange listing is a company in the Salesforce
**partner ecosystem** — consulting firms and independent software
vendors (ISVs) that build a business around Salesforce specifically.
This ecosystem is a major reason Salesforce careers exist outside of
companies that simply *use* Salesforce internally: partner firms hire
consultants, architects, and developers whose entire job is building
and implementing Salesforce solutions for other companies, covered
fully in Lesson 10.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AppExchange | Salesforce's marketplace for applications, components, data, and consultants built on the platform |
| ISV | Independent Software Vendor — a company that builds and sells software on the platform |
| Partner ecosystem | The network of consulting firms and ISVs whose business is built around Salesforce |

# Script — AppExchange and the Partner Ecosystem

## Segment 1 (title)

AppExchange is where the platform-and-application split from Lesson 8 becomes a business — a marketplace, launched in 2005, where companies and individual developers package applications built on the platform and make them available to any other Salesforce customer.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: AppExchange home/nav)

This is the real AppExchange home page: search, top categories like Finance, Sales, and Customer Service, and navigation to Explore, Collections, and Consultants — the last one meaning you can hire implementation help directly through the marketplace, not just install software.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: explore by category)

It's not only full applications. AppExchange lists components that extend an existing screen, Lightning data sets of ready-to-use external data, and consultants — all organized by business need, industry, and category, the way this filter panel shows.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: listing detail)

Every listing follows a similar format: description, pricing, a star rating with reviews. Here, a real listing — free, a 4.6 rating from 772 reviews — showing exactly what to check before installing anything into a production org.

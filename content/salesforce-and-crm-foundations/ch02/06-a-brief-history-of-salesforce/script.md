# Script — A Brief History of Salesforce

## Segment 1 (title)

Salesforce was founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff, along with Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff, and Frank Dominguez. At the time, CRM meant installed software — expensive licenses, long implementations. Salesforce's bet was the opposite: deliver CRM entirely through a web browser, sold as a subscription.

## Segment 2 (steps: "No Software")

The company's slogan captured the pitch directly — no installation, no hardware, no IT department required just to get started. This model is now called SaaS, Software as a Service. In 1999 it was a genuine risk, and it's the biggest reason Salesforce could undercut installed CRM vendors on cost and time-to-value.

## Segment 3 (steps: key milestones)

Salesforce didn't stay a single product for long. AppExchange launched in 2005, turning it into a marketplace. Force.com opened the platform to custom apps in 2007. Major acquisitions — ExactTarget, Demandware, Tableau, Slack — built out the clouds. And Lightning Experience eventually replaced the older Classic interface.

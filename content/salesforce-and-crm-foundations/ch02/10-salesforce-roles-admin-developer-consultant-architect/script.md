# Script — Salesforce Roles: Admin, Developer, Consultant, Architect

## Segment 1 (title)

This whole chapter built toward this lesson — the clouds, the platform and application split, AppExchange's partner ecosystem, all show up directly in how Salesforce careers are organized. Most job titles fall under four broad roles.

## Segment 2 (steps: what each role does)

The administrator configures and maintains an org day to day, mostly without code — customizing the pre-built applications from earlier in this chapter. The developer builds custom logic and entirely new applications on the platform. The consultant, usually at a partner firm, figures out what to build before anyone configures or codes anything. And the architect designs how large, complex implementations fit together.

## Segment 3 (steps: how they connect)

These aren't competing titles for the same work — they're different altitudes on one ecosystem. A typical implementation touches all four: a consultant scopes it, an architect designs how it fits together, a developer builds the custom pieces, and an administrator maintains it afterward.

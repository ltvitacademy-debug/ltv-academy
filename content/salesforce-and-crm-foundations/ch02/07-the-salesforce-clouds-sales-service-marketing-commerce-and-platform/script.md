# Script — The Salesforce Clouds: Sales, Service, Marketing, Commerce and Platform

## Segment 1 (title)

A Salesforce "cloud" is a packaged product line, not a separate piece of software — Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud, Commerce Cloud, all built on the same underlying Salesforce Platform.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Setup home, Cross Cloud)

That shared foundation is visible right inside Setup. Here, the admin home page surfaces goals that span clouds — a Cross Cloud card referencing Sales Cloud Everywhere, Agentforce, and Einstein Sales Emails, all in one console. Sales Cloud itself is the original, flagship product — leads, opportunities, pipeline, built for reps and sales managers.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Service Cloud console)

Service Cloud is built for support teams, centered on the case record — this is a real console: customer context on the left, the case in the center, and an AI Service Rep Assistant on the right suggesting replies and relevant knowledge articles.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: app branding)

Marketing Cloud handles campaigns and customer journeys — grown largely through the ExactTarget acquisition. Commerce Cloud powers online storefronts, grown through the Demandware acquisition. And under the hood, every cloud is just a configured app, like this Service Console app's branding and settings here, switchable from the App Launcher.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Next lesson: the Salesforce Platform itself — the foundation underneath every cloud you just saw, and how it differs from the applications built on top of it.

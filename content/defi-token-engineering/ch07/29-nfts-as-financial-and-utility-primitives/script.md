# Script — NFTs as Financial & Utility Primitives

## Segment 1 (title)

An NFT you've already used in this course: a concentrated-liquidity LP position is minted as an NFT precisely because every position has a different price range and fee tier -- it isn't a picture, it's proof of a specific financial position.

## Segment 2 (code: fractionalization, worked)

Lock an illiquid NFT in a vault, mint a million fungible shares against it. A five-hundred-thousand-dollar NFT becomes fifty-cent shares -- buying fifty thousand dollars of shares gets five percent ownership, without needing the full price or sole custody.

## Segment 3 (code: NFTs as loan collateral)

NFT-backed lending works like overcollateralized lending applied to a unique asset: deposit an NFT worth an estimated twenty thousand dollars, borrow eight thousand at 40 percent loan-to-value. Repay and get it back, default and it gets liquidated -- at a lower LTV than fungible collateral, because pricing a single NFT is harder than pricing a liquid token.

## Segment 4 (steps: non-financial utility)

Event ticketing with built-in resale royalties, membership and gated access checked by a simple ownership call, transferable software licenses, and verifiable credentials -- all functional keys, no trading intent required.

## Segment 5 (outro)

An NFT can be a financial position, loan collateral, or a functional key -- the picture is optional. Next up: royalties and marketplace mechanics, closing out the course.

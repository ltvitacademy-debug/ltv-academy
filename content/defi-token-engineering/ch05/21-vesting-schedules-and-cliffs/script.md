# Script — Vesting Schedules & Cliffs

## Segment 1 (title)

Team, investor, and advisor allocations almost never unlock on day one. The near-universal pattern is a cliff -- zero tokens for an initial period -- followed by linear vesting until the full allocation releases.

## Segment 2 (code: the vesting formula)

A four-year vesting schedule with a one-year cliff on 2.4 million tokens unlocks nothing through month 12, then releases the remaining 36 months in equal monthly increments -- about 66,667 tokens a month -- until fully vested at month 48.

## Segment 3 (code: the cumulative unlock curve)

Zero percent unlocked through year one, 33 percent by year two, 67 percent by year three, 100 percent at year four. Flat through the cliff, then a straight diagonal ramp -- the shape behind every vesting chart in a crypto investor deck.

## Segment 4 (steps: why cliffs matter)

Cliffs align long-term incentives by giving early leavers nothing, prevent insiders from dumping straight into launch-day demand, signal confidence since insiders accept the same illiquidity they're asking holders to accept risk under, and smooth out sell-pressure spikes by spreading unlocks over years.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A cliff plus linear vesting is the standard shape behind almost every token allocation. Next up: designing sustainable incentives -- telling real yield apart from a schedule that's quietly unsustainable.

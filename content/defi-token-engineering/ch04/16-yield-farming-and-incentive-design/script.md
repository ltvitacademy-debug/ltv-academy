# Script — Yield Farming & Incentive Design

## Segment 1 (title)

A yield farm adds a second income layer on top of an LP token: stake it into a rewards contract and the protocol emits its own token on top of the trading fees you're already earning.

## Segment 2 (code: farm APY formula)

Farm APY is annual reward value in dollars divided by total value locked. Two million dollars a year in rewards against ten million TVL is a 20 percent APY -- but that number is only as real as the reward token's price holding up.

## Segment 3 (steps: the flywheel and where it breaks)

The virtuous loop: emit rewards, attract LPs, deepen liquidity, generate more fee revenue. It breaks when farmers are only there for the reward -- they sell it immediately, the price falls, the advertised APY falls, and they move to the next farm. That's mercenary capital.

## Segment 4 (code: designing against the death spiral)

Durable designs vest reward claims instead of paying out instantly, taper emissions on a schedule instead of staying permanently high, and route a real-yield component from actual trading fees so some return survives even if the reward token's price falls to zero.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Trading fees are real revenue; emissions are a bet on the reward token's price. Next up: vaults and auto-compounding -- what happens when a strategy harvests and reinvests those rewards for you automatically.

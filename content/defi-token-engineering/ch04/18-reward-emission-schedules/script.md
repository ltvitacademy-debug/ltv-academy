# Script — Reward Emission Schedules

## Segment 1 (title)

Every staking or farming reward comes from a fixed reserve paid out according to a schedule. Two shapes dominate: linear, constant emission, and decaying emission that shrinks over time.

## Segment 2 (code: linear emission)

Linear emission releases the same number of tokens every block. Ten tokens per block at two million blocks a year is twenty million tokens a year -- a hundred-million-token reserve lasts exactly five years, then emissions stop.

## Segment 3 (code: decaying emission / halving)

Decaying emission shrinks on a schedule, most commonly by halving at fixed intervals -- the Bitcoin pattern. Fifty tokens per block halves to twenty-five, then twelve and a half, front-loading early adopters and approaching, but never quite reaching, zero.

## Segment 4 (steps: tapering toward sustainability)

Bootstrap high to attract initial liquidity, taper on a defined and announced schedule, and shift the weight toward real fee revenue over time -- so the protocol isn't permanently dependent on inflation once emissions taper to near zero.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Linear versus decaying emissions shape farmer behavior as much as the APY number does. Next up: tokenomics design -- starting with the supply models that decide how many tokens exist in the first place.

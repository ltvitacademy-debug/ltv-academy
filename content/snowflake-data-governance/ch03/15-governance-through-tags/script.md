# Lesson 15 — Governance Through Tags · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Fifteen lessons in, it's time to tie this together. Roles, masking, tags — one governance system, not three separate ones.

## S2 · SCREENSHOT — Roles still matter

Chapter One's system roles, exactly as relevant as the day they were introduced. Roles decide who can do what — that question never went away.

## S3 · SCREENSHOT — One tag, five columns

And this is the other question — what gets protected, and how. One tag, applied once, protected five columns the moment a policy was attached to it. Neither question replaces the other.

## S4 · CODE — The three-step recap

Tag it. Apply it, by hand or automatically via classification. Wire a masking policy to the tag, once. Three statements, three lessons, one continuous thread — and step three only ever has to happen once.

## S5 · STEPS — What tags bought you

One tag protects many columns, present and future. Schema-wide classification tags an entire schema in one call. Custom classifiers find formats Snowflake doesn't know by default. And Universal Search plus TAG_REFERENCES_ALL_COLUMNS turn all of it into an audit trail.

## S6 · SCREENSHOT — Discoverable by meaning

Universal Search finds objects by name across an account. Tags and classification extend that same idea to searching by meaning — PII, a semantic category, a cost center — not just a name someone typed correctly.

## S7 · OUTRO

Chapters One through Three complete: access control, data protection, and tags and classification. Auditing and enterprise governance pick up from here.

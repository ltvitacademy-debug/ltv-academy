# Script — Sharing Rules

## Segment 1 (title)

The role hierarchy only opens access upward. This lesson covers the tool for everything that doesn't fit that shape — sharing rules.

## Segment 2 (steps: two kinds)

Owner-based sharing rules share records based on who owns them — everything owned by one group, shared with another. Criteria-based sharing rules share based on field values on the record itself, regardless of who owns it — every Case where Case Origin equals Email, shared with a support group, no ownership check at all. Both types always share to a public group, a role, or a role and its subordinates — never to one individual user directly.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Reviewers group)

Here's a public group's own detail page — Owner-Based Sharing Rules and Criteria-Based Sharing Rules sit as tabs right on the group, showing both rule types a group can be the target of, in one place.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: decision tree)

Sharing rules only ever add access on top of OWD and the role hierarchy — never restrict it. That means they only matter when OWD for that object is Private or Public Read Only. An object already Public Read/Write at the OWD level has nothing left to add.

## Segment 5 (screenshot: layers diagram)

In the stack, sharing rules sit above the automatic role hierarchy — opening access sideways and across, to whatever groups an admin explicitly configures, instead of just upward through reporting lines.

## Segment 6 (outro)

Sharing rules handle structured, ongoing exceptions. Up next: manual sharing and teams — the one-off, record-by-record layer at the very top.

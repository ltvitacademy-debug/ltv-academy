# Script — Preparing for the Sharing and Visibility Architect Path

## Segment 1 (title)

Last lesson of this course. No new material — a full recap, a look at what comes later in the path, and where this course hands off.

## Segment 2 (steps: the recap)

Chapter 1 was what a user can touch — object permissions, profiles, permission sets, field-level security. Chapter 2 was which records they can touch it on — org-wide defaults, role hierarchy, sharing rules, teams and queues. Chapter 3 was applying all of it to real designs, testing them, troubleshooting them, and a full audit case study. Chapter 4 covered the operational edges — recalculation timing, the unauthenticated guest case, and session security underneath all of it.

## Segment 3 (code: what the Architect path adds)

This course is fundamentals — the full toolbox, used correctly for a typical org. The Sharing and Visibility Architect specialization, later in the path, goes deeper: orgs with millions of users and records, performance-tuning recalculation at real scale, deep territory and scoping design, architect-level review boards. None of that is needed yet — it's the next level up, mentioned so the scope of what you've learned is clear.

## Segment 4 (steps: practice before moving on)

Before moving forward, build Lesson 14's Meridian Outfitters design from scratch in a free org. Verify it with Login As from a test user's seat, not just the admin's. Then deliberately break something the way Lesson 19 describes, and use Lesson 18's troubleshooting order to find and fix it yourself.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Security & Access Fundamentals is complete — the full toolbox, how to design with it, how to test it, and the operational edges around it. Next in the Salesforce Administrator path: Salesforce Data Management — how data actually gets into, gets cleaned within, and stays maintained inside the org you now know how to secure.

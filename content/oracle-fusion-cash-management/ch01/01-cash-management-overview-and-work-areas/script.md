# Script — Cash Management Overview and Work Areas

## Segment 1 (title)

Welcome to Oracle Fusion Cash Management, the first course in the Financial Operations stage of the Oracle Fusion Financials Consultant path. You already know General Ledger, Payables and Receivables. Cash Management is the module that sits underneath all three, proving that what the books say happened actually happened at the bank.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Cash Management has three jobs. It holds the record of every bank account the business uses. It reconciles bank statements against the payments and receipts already recorded in Oracle. And it gives treasury a day to day cash position plus a longer range forecast. It does not initiate payments and it does not record what a customer owes — those stay in Payables and Receivables.

## Segment 3 (steps)

You'll work in three areas across this course. Cash Management and Banking is where statement loading, reconciliation and bank setup happen — that's most of chapters one through three. Cash Positioning is a short term, account by account view used to decide whether to invest surplus cash or cover a shortfall. Cash Forecasting looks further out, in chapter four.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Every bank account sits inside a strict three level hierarchy. A bank is the financial institution itself. A bank branch is one specific branch of that bank. A bank account is the actual account number at that branch, owned by one or more legal entities. One bank can have many branches, and one branch can have many accounts. Setup for all three lives in the Setup and Maintenance work area, and requires the Cash Management Administration duty role, usually carried by the Cash Manager job role.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Keep that bank, branch, account shape in mind — it's the foundation for everything else in this course. Up next, lesson two: setting up banks, bank branches and bank accounts.

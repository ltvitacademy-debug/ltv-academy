# Script — Manual Reconciliation

## Segment 1 (title)

Automatic reconciliation handles the routine cases. Manual reconciliation is where a human steps in — either because an account's volume doesn't justify automated rules at all, or because automatic reconciliation left exceptions behind.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Manual reconciliation is ideally suited to accounts with a small volume of monthly transactions, where maintaining matching rules isn't worth the effort — a petty cash account, a rarely used escrow account. But it's also where every account ends up for the exceptions automatic reconciliation couldn't resolve, so it's a skill every Cash Manager needs regardless of account volume.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A Cash Manager working manually sees unreconciled statement lines on one side and open system transactions on the other, and selects the pair that represents the same event. A human can apply judgment a rule can't easily encode — recognizing that a slightly different reference format is clearly the same invoice payment. A tolerance rule can also be assigned directly to the bank account for manual use, separate from the matching-rule tolerance covered in lesson nine.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Sometimes a line genuinely has no system counterpart — a bank fee, interest earned. During manual reconciliation, a Cash Manager can create the missing external transaction right there, rather than leaving the line stuck.

## Segment 5 (outro)

A fictional example: of Harborview Metals Inc's twenty exceptions, twelve get new external transactions for bank charges, six turn out to just need Receivables to catch up, and two need a judgment call on a payment that arrived two dollars short because of a bank-deducted wire fee. Up next, lesson twelve: the specifics of reconciling Payables payments and Receivables receipts.

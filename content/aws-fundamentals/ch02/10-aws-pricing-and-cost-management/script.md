# Script — AWS Pricing & Cost Management

## Segment 1 (title)

AWS's core model is pay-as-you-go — no upfront purchase, no fixed seat count. You're billed on compute, storage, and mostly outbound data transfer, based on actual usage.

## Segment 2 (code: the three dimensions)

An idle EC2 instance still costs money because it's still running, but an empty S3 bucket costs essentially nothing — that's the flip side of elastic infrastructure. The Free Tier gives bounded no-cost allowances, but exceed them and normal billing kicks in immediately.

## Segment 3 (steps: buying it cheaper)

Reserved Instances, Savings Plans, and Spot don't change what you're running, they change how much you pay by trading flexibility for discount — a one-to-three-year commitment for Reserved and Savings Plans, or accepting interruption for Spot's steepest discount.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Cost Explorer shows you where the money's going, and Budgets alerts you before a forgotten resource becomes a surprise bill. Next up: Chapter 3 — Users, Groups & Roles, IAM in real depth.

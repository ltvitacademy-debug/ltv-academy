# Script — DP-750 Exam Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

DP-750 is Microsoft's associate-level exam for Azure Databricks Data Engineer Associate. It validates exactly what this course covers past the fundamentals — environment setup, Unity Catalog governance, data preparation, and pipeline deployment.

## Segment 2 (steps: the four domains)

Microsoft's own study guide lists four skill domains: setting up and configuring the environment, securing and governing Unity Catalog objects, preparing and processing data, and deploying and maintaining pipelines and workloads. The last two carry the most weight — thirty to thirty-five percent each.

## Segment 3 (code: domain to chapter mapping)

Map those domains onto this course and the pattern is direct. Setup and governance map to Chapters 1, 5, and 6. Preparing data maps to Chapters 2 and 3. Deploying and maintaining pipelines maps to Chapters 3 through 5 — which means Chapters 2 through 5 carry most of the exam's real weight.

## Segment 4 (code: scoring)

Scoring is a scaled score from 1 to 1000, with 700 or greater required to pass — not a raw percentage of questions correct. Microsoft doesn't publish an exact question count or duration in the public study guide, and both can vary by exam form, so check the current exam page before you register.

## Segment 5 (code: the gotcha)

Here's the honest caveat: Microsoft revises its exam skill lists on its own schedule, sometimes more than once a year. Treat every percentage in this lesson as accurate as of when it was recorded, and re-verify against Microsoft's own current study guide before you actually schedule this exam.

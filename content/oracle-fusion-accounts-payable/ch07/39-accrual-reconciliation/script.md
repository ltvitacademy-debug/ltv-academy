# Lesson 39 — Accrual Reconciliation · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

What happens when goods arrive before the invoice does? That gap gets its own account, and this lesson is about reconciling it.

## S2 · STEPS

For purchase orders set to accrue at receipt, receiving goods creates an entry before any invoice exists: debit inventory or expense, credit an accrual liability account. That's distinct from the AP liability used once an invoice is validated. When the invoice finally arrives and matches, it clears the accrual account and moves the liability to AP instead.

## S3 · STEPS

In a clean world every dollar in accrual clears out the same way. In practice, timing gaps and errors happen: a receipt posted this period with the invoice expected next period, a quantity logged wrong, a cancelled order where goods were already accrued, or a return to vendor where the original accrual was never reversed.

## S4 · CODE

Here's an illustrative example. Meridian Office Supply shows a thirty-four-hundred-dollar accrual balance at month end for one receipt of office chairs. The Accrual Reconciliation Report breaks it down: twenty-one hundred dollars is a normal receipt awaiting next week's invoice. Thirteen hundred is tied to a PO that was cancelled last month — no invoice is ever coming.

## S5 · CODE

The twenty-one hundred will clear naturally. The thirteen hundred needs a manual correcting entry, because nothing will clear it on its own.

## S6 · OUTRO

An accrual balance that grows unreviewed for months is a red flag at close. Next up, lesson forty: payables aging and reports, the broader reporting toolkit this chapter builds toward.

# Lesson 38 — Payables to General Ledger Transfer · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Final accounting in Payables isn't the last step. This lesson covers how those entries actually reach the General Ledger.

## S2 · STEPS

Three distinct states exist, not one. Accounted, inside Payables, once Create Accounting runs in Final mode. Transferred, once journal batches exist in GL, still unposted. And posted, once those batches actually update account balances.

## S3 · STEPS

The transfer itself can happen two ways: Create Accounting can be run with Transfer to General Ledger selected, combining both steps, or a separate Transfer Journal Entries to GL program can pick up anything already accounted but not yet transferred.

## S4 · CODE

Here's an illustrative example. Solace Robotics runs Create Accounting in Final mode for last week's activity with the transfer option selected. The result: one journal batch for fifty-eight invoice lines, and a second for twenty-four payment lines. Both arrive in GL, unposted.

## S5 · STEPS

Arriving unposted means the entries exist, but balances haven't changed yet. A GL accountant reviews both batches, confirms the totals tie back to Payables, and posts them. Only then does the chart of accounts reflect that week's AP activity.

## S6 · OUTRO

Accounted, transferred, posted — three distinct, checkable states. Next up, lesson thirty-nine: accrual reconciliation, where that kind of checking becomes the whole point.

# Lesson 40 — Payables Aging and Reports · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

This lesson is about turning a long list of unpaid invoices into something useful for cash planning: the aging report, plus a few other key reports.

## S2 · STEPS

The aging report buckets every outstanding balance by how overdue it is: current, one to thirty days, thirty-one to sixty, sixty-one to ninety, and ninety-plus. Its real job is cash planning — how much is coming due soon versus how much is already late and needs explaining.

## S3 · CODE

Here's an illustrative example. Three fictional suppliers at month end. Cascade Industrial Parts is fully current at eighty-two hundred dollars. Harbor Point Logistics has one invoice slipping into the one-to-thirty bucket. Meridian Office Supply has that thirteen-hundred-dollar stale accrual balance from last lesson sitting in the ninety-plus bucket.

## S4 · STEPS

At a glance, that ninety-plus balance is exactly what the aging report is good at surfacing for someone to chase down — the same number we already identified as needing a correcting entry.

## S5 · STEPS

A few other reports round out the toolkit. The Invoice Register lists everything entered in a period, for audit purposes. The Payment Register lists everything paid, by method and account. And the Open Items Revaluation Report restates open foreign-currency balances at today's exchange rate.

## S6 · OUTRO

Aging answers what's overdue, the registers answer what happened, revaluation answers what it's worth today. Next up, lesson forty-one: payables period close and reconciliation, where all of this comes together.

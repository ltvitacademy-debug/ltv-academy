## The aging report: outstanding balances, sorted by how overdue

The **Payables aging report** (sometimes called the AP Aging report) lists every outstanding, unpaid invoice balance by supplier, bucketed into ranges based on how far past due each one is:

| Aging bucket | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Current** | Not yet due |
| **1–30 days past due** | Mildly overdue |
| **31–60 days past due** | Moderately overdue |
| **61–90 days past due** | Significantly overdue |
| **90+ days past due** | Seriously overdue — needs attention |

The report's real job is turning a long list of individual invoices into a shape that's immediately useful for **cash planning**: how much is coming due soon, versus how much is already late and needs explaining.

## Illustrative example

**Cascade Industrial Parts**, **Harbor Point Logistics**, and **Meridian Office Supply** (all fictional, reused from earlier lessons) have outstanding balances at month end:

| Supplier | Current | 1–30 | 31–60 | 61–90 | 90+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cascade Industrial Parts | $8,200 | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Harbor Point Logistics | $4,200 | $6,100 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Meridian Office Supply | $0 | $0 | $2,100 | $0 | $1,300 |
| **Totals** | **$12,400** | **$6,100** | **$2,100** | **$0** | **$1,300** |

At a glance: Cascade is fully current, Harbor Point has one invoice slipping slightly past due, and Meridian has the $1,300 stale accrual-related balance from Lesson 39 sitting in the 90+ bucket — exactly the kind of thing the aging report is good at surfacing for someone to chase down.

## Other key Payables reports

| Report | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Invoice Register** | A detailed listing of invoices entered in a given period — useful for audit and period activity review |
| **Payment Register** | A detailed listing of payments issued in a given period, by method and bank account |
| **Open Items Revaluation Report** | Revalues open foreign-currency invoices/payments at a current exchange rate for reporting purposes |

Each of these answers a different question: aging answers "what's overdue and how much," the Invoice Register answers "what did we enter," the Payment Register answers "what did we pay," and revaluation answers "what do our open foreign-currency balances look like in our own currency today."

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Aging bucket | A range of days past due used to group outstanding invoice balances |
| Invoice Register | A detailed report of invoices entered in a period |
| Payment Register | A detailed report of payments issued in a period |

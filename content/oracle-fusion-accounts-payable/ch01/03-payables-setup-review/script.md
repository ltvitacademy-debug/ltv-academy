# Script — Payables Setup Review

## Segment 1 (title)

Before Brightfield Office Supply's team can enter a single invoice, a fair amount of configuration has to exist. This lesson is a map, not a deep dive: every piece of required Payables setup, named once, so the next several lessons have somewhere to attach.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Almost all of this setup is scoped at the business unit level, not one global switch for the whole company. A business unit is the organizational entity that actually processes transactions, tied to a ledger that determines the chart of accounts and currency behind every invoice. Two business units in the same company can run completely different tolerances or payment terms.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Seven areas make up the full map: business unit and ledger assignment, Payables Options and System Options, payment terms, tax configuration, the supplier model, bank accounts and payment methods, and invoice approval rules. Every lesson for the rest of this course zooms into one of these seven boxes.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Here's why the map matters in practice. A price-variance hold points you to Payables Options. A missing due date points you to payment terms. A required sign-off points you to approval rules. A new vendor who can't get paid points you to the supplier record and its bank account. Knowing the map tells you where to look before you even start troubleshooting.

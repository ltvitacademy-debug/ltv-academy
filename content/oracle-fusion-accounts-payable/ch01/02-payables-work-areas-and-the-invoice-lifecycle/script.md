# Script — Payables Work Areas and the Invoice Lifecycle

## Segment 1 (title)

Now that you know where Payables sits in procure-to-pay, let's open the application itself. Fusion organizes Payables around a handful of work areas, and every invoice moves through a predictable lifecycle. Learn both together and the rest of this course gets a lot easier.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A work area is a dashboard built around a job, not a flat menu. The Invoices work area is home base for an AP specialist: infotiles across the top summarize invoices needing entry, invoices on hold, invoices awaiting approval, and invoices coming due for payment. Click a tile and a filtered table drops in below it. The Payments work area, covered later in this course, is where payment files actually get built and tracked.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Here's the part people get wrong: an invoice doesn't have one status, it has four, and they move independently. Invoice status tracks validation. Approval status tracks the workflow. Accounting status tracks whether it's posted to the ledger. Payment status tracks whether it's been paid.

## Segment 4 (steps)

That's why an invoice can be validated, approved, accounted, and still sitting unpaid - because its due date just hasn't arrived yet. That's healthy, not broken. A hold only affects invoice status and, through it, payment. It has nothing to do with whether approval happened.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Four independent fields mean you can tell a supplier exactly where their invoice stands, instead of one vague status. Up next, lesson three: the Payables setup that has to exist before any of this works at all.

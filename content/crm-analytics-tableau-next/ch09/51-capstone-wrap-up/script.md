# Script — Capstone: Wrap-Up & Portfolio Presentation

## Segment 1 (title)

You've built the app, defined the metrics, connected usage data, and secured it. This final lesson turns that into something you can show: an executive presentation and a portfolio write-up. It also closes the CRM Analytics and Tableau Next course.

## Segment 2 (code)

Start with one page. An executive won't read your recipe. Give them a handful of numbers, each with a verdict. Bookings: five point two million of eight, sixty-five percent. Pipeline: six million, about two point one times coverage. Conversion: twenty-seven percent win rate, and thirty-one stalled deals worth one point nine million. Retention: seventeen accounts at risk, two point three million.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Ten minutes, five parts. The headline: your verdict in one sentence. The evidence: two or three components, not the whole app. The risk: what the data can't tell us. The ask: the specific decision you need. And the next step: what you'll do this week regardless.

## Segment 4 (code)

Turn numbers into a verdict. Closed won is five point two million. Open pipeline at a twenty-seven percent win rate adds about one point six. That's roughly six point eight against eight, about one point two million short. Coverage of two point one times sounds comfortable, but at that win rate you'd want nearly four times. State the assumption: win rate by count, applied to dollars, is a rough rule.

## Segment 5 (steps)

Then the ask. Have the regional VPs re-engage the thirty-one stalled deals this week, and have Customer Success contact the seventeen at-risk renewals before their calls. A verdict without an ask is just commentary. The portfolio write-up mirrors this: problem, data and model, decisions, results with your own screenshots, and limits.

## Segment 6 (code)

Close with honesty. The figures are illustrative. The dashboard and metric layer needed a manual parity test. Usage data is a day behind. And product names, like Data Cloud, now Data 360, and their menus change between releases. Limits you state read as judgment. Limits an interviewer discovers read as a gap.

## Segment 7 (steps)

Look at what you can now do. Build a CRM Analytics app from a recipe. Define metrics once, with owners. Connect Data Cloud and turn usage into renewal risk. Secure it with tested predicates. And present a verdict with an ask. That's the full job, end to end.

# Script — CRM Analytics, Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

Welcome to the course that takes you past native Salesforce reporting. You already know reports and dashboards, and you know where they run out of road. CRM Analytics is Salesforce's answer to that ceiling: a separate analytics platform that lives inside your org.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: Analytics Studio Browse)

This is Analytics Studio, the home base, showing the Browse page. Every asset lives here, and you can filter by apps, dashboards, lenses, datasets, and more. Notice this is a different workspace from the Reports tab. Data here isn't queried live from your objects. It's copied into optimized datasets first, and refreshed on a schedule.

## Segment 3 (steps: four assets)

Four assets to know. A dataset is the prepared data, stored inside CRM Analytics. A lens is a quick exploration of one dataset. A dashboard is a page of interactive widgets. And an app is the container that holds them and controls who sees what. Because datasets are precomputed, they handle far more rows, and far more slicing, than a live report. That's the whole reason this platform exists.

## Segment 4 (code: naming history)

A quick word on names. This product began as Wave, became Einstein Analytics, then Tableau CRM, and today it's CRM Analytics. Old Trailhead links and blog posts still say wave. Same product family, different labels. Product names keep shifting, so check the current release notes before you rely on one.

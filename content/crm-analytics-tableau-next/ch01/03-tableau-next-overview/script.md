# Script — Tableau Next, Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

You already know Tableau. Tableau Next is not just the next version of it. Salesforce describes it as an API-first analytics platform, built on the Salesforce Platform, on top of Data 360, with Agentforce built in. Let's meet its parts.

## Segment 2 (steps: the stack)

Four layers. Data lives in Data 360, and Tableau Next reads its objects directly. On top of that sits a semantic model, defining business meaning once, instead of in every workbook. From the model you build visualizations, individual charts. Then you combine them into dashboards for business users.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: Visualization Builder)

This is the Visualization Builder. If you know Tableau, the layout is familiar: a field list on the left, filters and marks, columns and rows shelves, and a live chart. What's different is where the fields come from. They come from the shared semantic model, so every author starts from the same governed definitions.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: workspace)

And this is a workspace, the project container. It lists the dashboards, visualizations, semantic models, and tables in one place, with a details panel for the selected asset. Workspaces are how assets are organized and shared.

## Segment 5 (code: Agentforce and availability)

Agentforce is part of the experience, with built-in analytics skills that help you explore data in natural language. Salesforce's documentation names a few of them, but this area changes quickly. Licensing does too. Tableau Next is sold in several ways, so confirm what your org actually has.

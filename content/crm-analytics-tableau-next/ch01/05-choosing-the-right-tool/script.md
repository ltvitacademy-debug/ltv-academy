# Script — Choosing the Right Tool for a Given Analytics Need

## Segment 1 (title)

Knowing what each product does is only half the skill. The other half is the judgment call employers pay for: given a business question, which tool should carry it? This lesson gives you a way to make that call.

## Segment 2 (steps: four questions)

Ask four questions. How much data, and how fresh does it need to be? Where does the data live: only in Salesforce, or spread across other systems? Who builds, who consumes, and who governs? And finally, what's licensed, and what can your team actually support? A great design that needs a license nobody bought isn't a design.

## Segment 3 (code: sweet spots)

Here are the typical sweet spots. Live lists and simple KPIs: native reports. Big data, blending, and deep exploration: CRM Analytics. Shared metrics on data unified in Data 360: Tableau Next. And if you already run a Tableau estate over many non-Salesforce sources, classic Tableau. These are tendencies, not laws, and capabilities keep growing.

## Segment 4 (code: scenarios)

Try some scenarios. A weekly pipeline list for one team? A native dashboard. Blending ERP invoices with opportunities for interactive slicing? CRM Analytics. One definition of revenue shared by twenty teams? A governed semantic model in Tableau Next. A company with a mature Tableau Cloud estate? Keep building there. Don't migrate just because something newer exists.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Avoid three mistakes: choosing the newest tool by default, choosing the most familiar tool by default, and ignoring the cost of running two platforms. Start with the simplest option that works. Next chapter: CRM Analytics architecture.

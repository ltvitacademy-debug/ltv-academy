# Script — Lenses & Exploration

## Segment 1 (title)

A dashboard shows the answers someone already decided to build. A lens is how you ask a question nobody has built yet. This lesson is about lenses and exploration in CRM Analytics.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: widget Explore menu)

Exploration usually starts on a dashboard. Every widget has a small menu, and here it's open. Explore sits at the top, followed by Set Notification, Annotate, Share, Download, and Show Details. Click Explore, and the widget's underlying query opens as a lens, so you continue exactly where the dashboard stopped.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: lens editor)

This is the lens editor. On the left, the dataset, then the measure that sets bar length, here a sum of Amount, and the field that makes the bars, Product Family. Below are trellis, unused fields, and a query limit. The chart on the right is simply that query's result. Change any control, and the query reruns almost instantly.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: drill into)

To drill in, click a bar and choose Drill Into This By. A list of dimensions appears: Account Name, Account Owner, Account Type, and more. Pick one, and the lens regroups by that field. Focus narrows the chart to what you selected, and View Data Table shows the rows behind it.

## Segment 5 (steps: what a lens is for)

So a lens has four jobs. Ask a one-off question in seconds. Refine it by regrouping, filtering, and drilling. Prototype a chart before you commit it to a dashboard. And save the lens, or reuse its query. The habit to build is simple: explore in a lens first, then build the dashboard second.

## Segment 6 (outro)

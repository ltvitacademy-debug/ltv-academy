# Script — AI-Assisted Analysis in Tableau Next

## Segment 1 (title)

Tableau Next is described as an agentic analytics platform, with Agentforce built in. Names and packaging here change fast, and some features are in beta, so check current release notes. An administrator also has to enable these features before you can use them.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: assistant panel)

This is the assistant panel in Salesforce's documentation, opening beside a metric page. It offers starter prompts: show me the top insight, how has the metric trended, what drove the recent change. You type a question in your own words, and get back visualizations and an explanation. Look for the Agentforce icon on a dashboard or metric page.

## Segment 3 (steps: three skills)

Tableau describes three analytics skills. Data Pro helps build semantic models by suggesting relationships and calculated fields. Concierge answers business questions in natural language. And Inspector monitors metrics and alerts people when something changes. Marketing material also mentions root causes and suggested next steps, but check current documentation for what your org has.

## Segment 4 (code: prompts)

To use it well, be specific. A vague question gets a vague answer. Ask for the exact measure, grouping, and time window. Then check the definition behind the number, and reproduce one headline figure from a source you trust, such as a report or a SOQL query you already know is right. Treat every AI answer as a starting point, not a verdict.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Remember: the assistant is only as good as your semantic model, and it never replaces your judgment. That is why the semantic layer in Chapter 6 matters so much: clear names, correct relationships, and one definition per metric. Next up: publishing in Tableau Next.

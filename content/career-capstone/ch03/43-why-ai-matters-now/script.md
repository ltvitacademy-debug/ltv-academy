# Script — Why AI Matters for Data Engineers Now

## Segment 1 (title)

This chapter is a bonus, and it stays honest about what AI does and doesn't do for a data engineer. The right framing is a multiplier, not a replacement: AI speeds up skills you already have. It doesn't substitute for having them.

## Segment 2 (code: multiplier vs. replacement)

A data engineer who already knows what a good transformation looks like can use AI to draft one faster and catch it when it's wrong. A data engineer who doesn't know what good looks like has no way to catch a bad answer — and ships whatever the model hands back. That gap is the theme this whole chapter returns to.

## Segment 3 (steps: good at / not good at)

AI is genuinely good at boilerplate, explaining unfamiliar code, first drafts, and summarizing long logs or schemas. It is not good at knowing your business rules, knowing your data's actual shape unless you show it, or replacing judgment about whether generated logic is actually correct.

## Segment 4 (steps: chapter roadmap)

This chapter moves through three arcs: Copilot inside Microsoft Fabric, AI-assisted data quality and documentation with concrete prompting patterns, and retrieval-augmented generation built over Fabric's own metadata catalog — before closing with testing, guardrails, and the honest limits of AI in this job.

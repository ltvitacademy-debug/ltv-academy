# Script — Where AI Fits in the Production Practices From Course 3

## Segment 1 (title)

Fabric & Real-Time Analytics's production chapter established the practices that keep a pipeline reliable — testing, observability, incident response, governance. Nothing in this chapter replaces any of that. AI is a new tool inside an already-established toolkit.

## Segment 2 (code: mapping the fit)

Testing gets faster first drafts, still reviewed the same way. Root cause analysis gets a faster first hypothesis, still confirmed against logs and data. Secrets and PII handling are exactly what this chapter's guardrails exist to protect.

## Segment 3 (code: observability feeds the draft)

Observability is what actually produces the logs an RCA draft depends on in the first place — no logs, no useful draft. AI speeds up what happens after the production practice already did its job.

## Segment 4 (code: what AI cannot substitute for)

AI drafts faster within an established practice — it doesn't decide which practices apply, how strict an SLA should be, or when an incident is actually resolved. Those judgment calls still belong to the engineer.

## Segment 5 (outro)

One more tool in an already-built toolkit, not a replacement for any part of it. Next up: an honest look at what AI still can't reliably do in this field.

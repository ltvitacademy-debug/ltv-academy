# Script — Common DP-700 Traps and Gotchas

## Segment 1 (title)

This lesson teaches no new technology — just five wrong-answer patterns that repeat across every DP-700 domain, so you can recognize them under time pressure instead of meeting them cold on exam day.

## Segment 2 (code: lookalikes and overcomplication)

Trap one: similar-sounding item types get swapped — Lakehouse for Warehouse, Eventstream for Eventhouse. Re-read the noun in every option. Trap two: the exam wants the simple, native Fabric answer, not an option that bolts on an external Azure service to solve something Fabric already handles.

## Segment 3 (steps: three more patterns)

Absolute words like "always" or "never" are usually bait. The qualifier at the very end of a stem often flips the correct answer. And picking a real but broader-than-needed control — a workspace role instead of an item permission — is the single most common trap of all.

## Segment 4 (code: the pattern behind the pattern)

Every trap above has the same shape: the wrong option is real, technically correct in some other scenario, and just not what this specific scenario asked for. Read the whole stem, note the noun, and ask whether a narrower answer exists before picking the broad one.

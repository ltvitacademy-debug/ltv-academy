# Lesson 40 — Full-Length Practice Exam, Part 1

**Chapter 2 · DP-700 Certification Prep · Lesson 40 of 81**

## What you'll learn

- Nothing new — this is a timed, mixed-domain practice set, the first
  half of a full mock exam split across Lessons 40 and 41
- How it feels to answer DP-700-style questions back-to-back, under the
  time budget from Lesson 39, instead of one focused practice set at a
  time like Lessons 31–36
- Where you personally lose time or accuracy when domains are mixed
  instead of blocked

## How to actually take this lesson

Treat the quiz below as a timed block, not a casual read-through. Set a
timer for roughly 18-20 minutes (nine questions at just under two
minutes each, per Lesson 39's budget) before you open the questions.
Don't check the explanation after each question — answer all nine
first, the way the real exam works, then review every explanation in
one pass at the end.

This set intentionally mixes domains in the order a real exam would:
you won't get "all the Domain 1 questions first." Recognizing which
domain a question belongs to, on the fly, is part of what's being
tested here.

## What this set covers

Nine questions spanning all three domains, pulling from material across
this entire chapter and the courses it draws on:

- Choosing between Lakehouse and Warehouse for a given workload
  (Domain 1 — Fabric Chapter 1)
- OneLake shortcuts and when to reference data instead of copying it
  (Domain 1)
- Direct Lake Mode's performance trade-offs (Domain 1)
- Eventstream ingestion and windowing choices (Domain 2 — Fabric
  Chapter 2)
- A Delta `MERGE` pattern for an SCD Type 2 update (Domain 2 —
  Databricks & Delta Lake)
- Fabric capacities and SKU sizing under a cost constraint (Domain 1/3
  overlap)
- A security scenario testing the narrowest-control pattern from
  Lesson 35 (Domain 3)
- A monitoring-versus-deployment scenario from Lesson 36's pattern
  (Domain 3)
- One deliberately trap-laden question using more than one pattern from
  Lesson 37 at once

## After you finish

Score yourself, then sort your misses by domain. If most of your misses
cluster in one domain, that's exactly the signal Lesson 38's study plan
is built to use — go back to that domain's chapter and practice-question
lessons before Lesson 41's second half.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mixed-domain set | Questions presented in exam-realistic order, not grouped by domain |
| Miss clustering | Sorting wrong answers by domain to find where to focus review |

## Check yourself

You're ready for Lesson 41 when you've taken this set under a real
timer, scored it, and identified which domain (if any) needs another
pass before the exam.

# Script — Exam-Day Strategy and Time Management

## Segment 1 (title)

Microsoft certification exams run around two hours for fifty to sixty questions — under two minutes a question on average. That average matters more than any single question, as long as you bank extra time on the quick ones.

## Segment 2 (code: the budget)

Short, factual questions should take well under a minute. Long scenario questions get the extra time you banked. After the first fifteen to twenty questions, do a quick pace check — if you're behind, speed up on the easy ones, don't panic on the hard ones.

## Segment 3 (steps: flag and return)

If a question isn't resolving after a genuine attempt, flag it and move on — fighting one question for five minutes is a losing trade against a two-minute-per-question budget. Some exam formats include a case-study section you can't revisit once you leave it, so budget that section specifically if the interface warns you.

## Segment 4 (code: elimination first)

Apply Lesson 37's trap patterns before hunting for the right answer directly — lookalike item types, overcomplicated options, absolute wording, over-broad controls. Eliminating two wrong options turns a four-way guess into a coin flip.

## Segment 5 (outro)

State your time budget, decide your flag threshold, in advance — not mid-exam. Next up: putting all of this together in a full-length mock exam.

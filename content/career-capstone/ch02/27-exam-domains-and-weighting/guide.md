## The three domains

Microsoft groups DP-700 into three skills-measured domains, each
weighted roughly a third of the exam:

```
Domain 1: Implement and manage an analytics solution   ~30-35%
Domain 2: Ingest and transform data                    ~30-35%
Domain 3: Monitor and optimize an analytics solution    ~30-35%
```

That near-even split is deliberate on Microsoft's part, and it tells
you something about how to study: there's no single domain you can
skip or cram at the expense of the others. Missing an entire domain
costs you roughly a third of the exam, not a small fraction.

## Domain 1: Implement and manage an analytics solution

This domain covers setting up the Fabric environment itself:
workspaces, OneLake, Lakehouses, Warehouses, capacities, and the
security and Git-integration features that keep a Fabric solution
manageable. It's the material from Fabric & Real-Time Analytics
Chapter 1 (Lessons 1–17) — Lesson 28 of this chapter drills it
directly.

## Domain 2: Ingest and transform data

This domain covers moving and shaping data: Data Factory pipelines,
Dataflows Gen2, Eventstreams, notebooks, and Spark-based
transformations, plus enough of the underlying Delta Lake mechanics
from the Databricks & Delta Lake course to explain how a lakehouse
table actually gets built and updated. Lesson 29 of this chapter
drills it directly.

## Domain 3: Monitor and optimize an analytics solution

This domain covers what happens after a solution is built and
running: the Monitoring Hub, Direct Lake Mode's performance
characteristics, capacity and SKU sizing, and the production
practices from Fabric & Real-Time Analytics Chapter 3 — CI/CD,
observability, and incident response, scoped down to what a
certification question actually asks about them. Lesson 30 of this
chapter drills it directly.

```
Rough map:
Domain 1 -> Fabric Ch.1 (workspaces, OneLake, Lakehouse, Warehouse)
Domain 2 -> Fabric Ch.2 + Databricks Delta Lake mechanics
Domain 3 -> Fabric Ch.3 (monitoring, cost, CI/CD) + capacity/SKUs
```

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Skills-measured domain | One of DP-700's three official topic groupings |
| Domain weighting | The approximate percentage of the exam each domain represents |
| Near-even split | All three domains sit in the same ~30-35% range — no domain is skippable |

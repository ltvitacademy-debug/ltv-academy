# Script — Exam Domains and Weighting

## Segment 1 (title)

DP-700 groups into three skills-measured domains, each weighted roughly a third of the exam. That near-even split means there's no domain you can skip or cram at the expense of the others.

## Segment 2 (code: the three domains)

Domain one is implementing and managing an analytics solution. Domain two is ingesting and transforming data. Domain three is monitoring and optimizing a solution. Each sits in roughly the thirty to thirty-five percent range.

## Segment 3 (steps: domain 1 and 2 mapped)

Domain one maps to Fabric Chapter 1 — workspaces, OneLake, Lakehouses, Warehouses, capacities. Domain two maps to Fabric Chapter 2 plus the Delta Lake mechanics from the Databricks course — pipelines, Dataflows, Eventstreams, notebooks, Spark transformations.

## Segment 4 (code: domain 3 mapped)

Domain three maps to Fabric Chapter 3's production practices — the Monitoring Hub, Direct Lake Mode performance, capacity and SKU sizing, CI/CD, and incident response, scoped to what a certification question actually asks.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Missing an entire domain costs roughly a third of the exam, not a small fraction. Next up: a direct drill on Domain 1 — implementing and managing an analytics solution.

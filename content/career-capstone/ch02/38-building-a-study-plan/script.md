# Script — Building a Study Plan

## Segment 1 (title)

DP-700's three domains map onto material you've already taken — this course's Chapter 1, plus the Fabric and Databricks courses. A study plan here is a review schedule, not a first-pass-through-new-material schedule.

## Segment 2 (code: the domain map, again)

Domain one maps to Fabric Chapter 1. Domain two maps to Fabric Chapter 2 plus Databricks Delta Lake mechanics. Domain three maps to Fabric Chapter 3. Less "learn this for the first time," more "drill until it's fast and automatic."

## Segment 3 (steps: four weeks)

Week one: Domain 1 review plus the OneLake and Warehouse practice sets. Week two: Domain 2 review plus the Eventstream and Spark practice sets. Week three: Domain 3 review plus the security and monitoring practice sets, and a re-read of the traps lesson.

## Segment 4 (code: week four)

Week four is both full-length mock exams under real time pressure, followed by two days re-reading every missed question — going back to the original Fabric or Databricks lesson when the gap is a concept gap, not just an exam-technique gap.

## Segment 5 (outro)

If you have less time, compress weeks one through three — never skip the mock exams. Next up: exactly how to pace those exams once you sit down to take them.

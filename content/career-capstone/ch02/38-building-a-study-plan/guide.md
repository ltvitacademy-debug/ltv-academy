# Lesson 38 — Building a Study Plan

**Chapter 2 · DP-700 Certification Prep · Lesson 38 of 81**

## What you'll learn

- How to turn DP-700's three near-even domains (Lesson 27) into a
  week-by-week study schedule
- Why the study material is almost entirely material you've already
  taken — this course's Chapter 1, plus the Fabric & Real-Time Analytics
  and Databricks & Delta Lake courses
- Where the practice-question lessons and traps lesson fit into the
  schedule, not just at the end
- A concrete 4-week plan you can adapt to more or less time

## The material already exists — this is a review plan, not a new course

Lesson 27 mapped DP-700's three domains onto specific chapters you've
already been through:

```
Domain 1 -> Fabric & Real-Time Analytics Chapter 1 (workspaces, OneLake,
            Lakehouse, Warehouse, capacities)
Domain 2 -> Fabric Chapter 2 + Databricks & Delta Lake mechanics
            (pipelines, Dataflows, Eventstreams, notebooks, Spark)
Domain 3 -> Fabric Chapter 3 (Monitoring Hub, Direct Lake, CI/CD,
            incident response, governance)
```

A study plan for this exam is therefore mostly a *re-reading* schedule,
not a first-pass-through-new-material schedule. That changes how you
should spend your time: less "learn this for the first time," more
"drill until it's fast and automatic."

## A 4-week plan

**Week 1 — Domain 1 review + practice.** Re-skim Fabric Chapter 1.
Work Lesson 31 (OneLake and Lakehouses) and Lesson 32 (Data Warehouses).
Note every question you get wrong and why — that list becomes your
Week 4 review sheet.

**Week 2 — Domain 2 review + practice.** Re-skim Fabric Chapter 2 and
the relevant Databricks & Delta Lake lessons on Delta mechanics. Work
Lesson 33 (Eventstreams and KQL) and Lesson 34 (Notebooks and Spark).

**Week 3 — Domain 3 review + practice.** Re-skim Fabric Chapter 3. Work
Lesson 35 (Security and Governance) and Lesson 36 (Monitoring and
Deployment). Read Lesson 37's trap list *again* — it's short enough to
re-read every week, and the traps apply across all three domains.

**Week 4 — Full mock exams.** Take Lesson 40 and Lesson 41's full-length
practice exams under real time constraints (Lesson 39 covers exactly how
to pace them). Spend the two days after each mock exam re-reading the
guide for every question you missed, not just the explanation text —
go back to the original Fabric or Databricks lesson if the gap is a
concept gap, not just an exam-technique gap.

## Adjusting the plan

If you have less than four weeks, compress Weeks 1–3 rather than
skipping Week 4 — the mock exams under time pressure are what actually
tells you if you're ready, and skipping them means finding out on exam
day instead. If you have more than four weeks, add a Week 0 that's a
straight re-read of Lessons 26–30 before touching any practice questions.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Review plan | A study schedule built on material already taught, not new content |
| Domain-paced week | One study week dedicated to one DP-700 domain's practice questions |
| Wrong-answer log | The running list of missed questions and why, built across every practice set |

## Check yourself

You're ready for Lesson 39 when you have an actual date-blocked version
of this plan — not "study more," but "Week 2, Tuesday: Fabric Chapter 2
re-skim + Lesson 33."

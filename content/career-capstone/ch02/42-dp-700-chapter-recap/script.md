# Script — DP-700 Chapter Recap

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 2 had one job: take everything the earlier three courses already taught, and drill it from the DP-700 exam's specific angle. Nothing here was new technology — every lesson pointed back to a lesson you'd already sat through.

## Segment 2 (code: the shape of the chapter)

Lessons 26 and 27 covered what DP-700 is and its three near-even domains. Lessons 28 through 30 drilled each domain directly. Lessons 31 through 36 were six practice-question sets, and Lesson 37 named the five trap patterns behind every wrong answer in them.

## Segment 3 (steps: the plan, the pacing, the mock exam)

Lesson 38 turned the domain map into a four-week study schedule built around re-reading, not re-learning. Lesson 39 gave you exam-day mechanics — a time budget, flag-and-return, and elimination as a default habit. Lessons 40 and 41 put all of it to work in an eighteen-question mock exam.

## Segment 4 (code: the recurring lesson)

Across every practice set, the recurring pattern was the same: DP-700 rewards the narrowest correct control or tool, and punishes picking a broader one that would also technically work. That one habit carries more weight than memorizing any single fact.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Chapter 2 is complete. Next up: Chapter 3, AI for Data Engineers — a shift away from exam prep and back toward hands-on material, starting with Copilot in Fabric.

# Script — Project 2: Wrap-Up and Retrospective

## Segment 1 (title)

Project 2 took a multi-source warehouse migration from source assessment through a live cutover. None of the individual pieces were new — the value was proving they compose into something a real migration needs.

## Segment 2 (steps: what got built)

Kickoff scoped the sources and stakeholders. The migration plan mapped what moves and in what order. The target warehouse leaned on the star-schema and grain thinking from Chapter 1, plus the MERGE INTO pattern from Databricks for incremental loads. Cutover leaned on the same freshness-SLA and go/no-go discipline from earlier in the track.

## Segment 3 (steps: the honest retrospective)

A retrospective that only lists what went well is a highlight reel, not a retrospective. The real version answers three questions specifically and in writing: what worked, what you'd change, and what surprised you — while the memory is still fresh.

## Segment 4 (outro)

That written retrospective is also the raw material for the interview story two lessons from now — "what would you change" is almost exactly the question an interviewer asks. Next up: kicking off Project 3, a real-time streaming fraud detector.

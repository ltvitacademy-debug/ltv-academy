# Script — Telling Your Project Story in an Interview

## Segment 1 (title)

The README built a written version of the project. An interview needs a spoken version, because the interviewer is listening, not reading — they can't scroll back up. That needs a structure built for speech.

## Segment 2 (steps: the four-part structure)

Situation: what the system needed to do, and the constraint that mattered most. Design: the shape you chose, and why. Trade-off: what you gave up to get it. Retrospect: what you'd do differently now. The last two map directly onto the retrospective questions already written down.

## Segment 3 (steps: the why beats the what)

"I built a fraud detector with Eventstreams and KQL" is the what — true, and nearly interchangeable with any other answer. "I chose a sliding window because a tumbling window would undercount a fraud burst" is the why — only someone who actually made that decision can say it convincingly. Interviewers ask follow-ups specifically to find out if the why is really there.

## Segment 4 (outro)

Practiced out loud, not just understood in your head — that gap only closes with rehearsal. Next up: the finale, closing out this course and the entire Data Engineering track.

# Lesson 24 — Presenting a System Design: Whiteboarding Like an Interview · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2.5-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Presenting a system design — whiteboarding like an interview, where
the process matters more than landing on one right answer.

## S2 · STEPS CARD (the four-step structure)

Clarify requirements, functional and non-functional. Estimate the
scale, out loud, with rough numbers. Propose storage and a processing
model, and say why. Then discuss trade-offs — what changes if a
constraint moves.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (the most common mistake)

Jumping straight to an architecture before clarifying requirements —
designing a solution to a problem nobody actually stated. It happens
because silence feels like failure, so candidates fill it with a
diagram instead of a question.

## S4 · STEPS CARD (what's actually being evaluated)

Interviewers listen for the reasoning behind a choice, not just a
correct-looking box on a diagram. Being asked what you'd do at ten
times the scale, and having a real answer, is what separates
candidates.

## S5 · OUTRO CARD

The four case studies in this chapter are a template — rehearse
narrating each one out loud until the process transfers to a scenario
you've never seen. Next up: the System Design chapter recap.

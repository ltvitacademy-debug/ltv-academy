# Script — Reports and Dashboards Practice Lab

## Segment 1 (title)

This is the hands-on close of the course. Build a small, real dashboard from scratch, using nothing but what you've already learned across all four chapters.

## Segment 2 (steps: the brief and build)

The brief: a Sales Pipeline Overview dashboard a manager could open every Monday and act on. Start from Opportunities, build a pipeline-by-stage report, filtered to open deals, saved into a new Sales Team folder shared with the sales role at Viewer access.

## Segment 3 (steps: widgets and filter)

Add a second report — days in current stage, filtered past 45 days — into the same folder. Then the dashboard itself: a funnel widget pointed at pipeline-by-stage, a table widget pointed at the stale-deals report, and a dashboard filter on Owner so any rep can narrow it to their own deals.

## Segment 4 (steps: subscription and self-review)

Finish with a weekly Monday-morning subscription for the manager, snapshot attached. Then self-review against the design checklist — most important view above the fold, descriptive widget titles, nothing extra, shared with exactly the right audience.

## Segment 5 (steps: what's next)

Every piece here reuses a skill from an earlier lesson — nothing in this lab is new. That's the whole course, assembled once, end to end.

## Segment 6 (outro)

This closes Reports and Dashboards, and with it, the Salesforce Administration stage of the path — the checkpoint here is the Salesforce Certified Administrator credential. Next up: Salesforce Platform App Builder, where you'll start building the objects and structure that generate the data you've spent this entire course analyzing.

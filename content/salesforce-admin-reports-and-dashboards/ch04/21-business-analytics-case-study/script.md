# Script — Business Analytics Case Study

## Segment 1 (title)

Time to put the whole course together on one problem. Meridian Outdoor Supply, a fictional retailer, had two leaders with complaints their existing dashboards couldn't explain.

## Segment 2 (steps: the pipeline complaint)

The VP of Sales had a healthy-looking four-point-two million in pipeline against a one million quota — three times coverage — but kept missing the number anyway. A days-in-stage report found it: forty percent of that pipeline had been sitting in Proposal for over sixty days, way past their normal cycle. Total value was hiding staleness.

## Segment 3 (steps: the support complaint)

The Director of Support had an eighteen-hour average resolution time, comfortably under target, while satisfaction kept slipping. The average was blending fast, simple cases with a smaller number of multi-day warranty claims — exactly the cases customers remembered. One blended number was hiding two very different realities.

## Segment 4 (steps: both fixes)

Both fixes used the same move: a more specific grouping instead of a single total. A table of deals stuck past forty-five days, sitting next to the pipeline total. A Matrix split by Case Type, separating fast account-admin cases from slow warranty claims instead of blending them into one average.

## Segment 5 (steps: one dashboard, two audiences)

And both fixes needed to reach different people without doubling the work. One shared Leadership Overview folder, holding both components. One dynamic dashboard, so the VP of Sales sees their numbers and the Director of Support sees theirs, from a single build.

## Segment 6 (outro)

A vague complaint almost always means a report answering the wrong question, not bad data — more specific grouping, not more data, was the fix both times. Last lesson: a hands-on practice lab to build something like this yourself.

# Script — Journal Batches and Journal Lines

## Segment 1 (title)

When you created your first journal back in lesson six, Oracle quietly built a batch around it behind the scenes. This lesson makes that hierarchy explicit: batch, journal entry, and line.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A batch is the container you actually submit and post, and it can hold more than one journal entry — say, three related accruals prepared together at month-end. Each journal entry inside has its own header and its own lines. Batching them together means they get reviewed, approved, and posted as one unit instead of one at a time.

## Segment 3 (code)

Control totals give you a self-check. You type the total amount you expect the batch to contain before you finish entering lines — if March's accrual batch should total eighty-four hundred dollars, you enter that as the control total. If what you actually typed doesn't match, General Ledger flags it before you complete the batch, catching a mistake early instead of after posting.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Both the batch and each journal inside it carry their own status: Incomplete while still being entered or failing validation, Unposted once it's complete and balanced, Posted once it's updated balances, or an approval status if a workflow applies.

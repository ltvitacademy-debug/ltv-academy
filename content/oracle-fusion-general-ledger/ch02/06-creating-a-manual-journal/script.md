# Script — Creating a Manual Journal

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter two is where this course gets hands-on. Every journal that reaches General Ledger, no matter where it came from, carries the same six pieces of header information. Let's create our first one.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Those six fields are: ledger, journal name, accounting date, source, category, and currency. Source defaults to Manual when you're typing the journal in by hand, and category describes the business purpose — we'll cover both in depth next lesson.

## Segment 3 (code)

Here's a fictional example for Solara Fixtures: a March accrual for office supplies that haven't been invoiced yet. One line debits office supplies expense for twelve hundred fifty dollars, the other credits accrued liabilities for the same amount. Two lines, one journal, balanced.

## Segment 4 (steps)

General Ledger won't let you mark a journal complete unless debits equal credits — and it checks that for every balancing segment value in the journal, not just the grand total. If Solara has lines in two different companies, each company has to net to zero on its own.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Save stores a draft you can keep editing. Complete or submit marks it ready to move forward — either straight to posting, or into an approval workflow, which is chapter three. Next up, lesson seven: journal sources and categories.

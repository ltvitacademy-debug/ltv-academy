# Script — Consolidation Concepts

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 6 built toward this lesson one piece at a time: foreign currency journals, revaluation, translation, intercompany transactions. Consolidation is where all four come together to produce one set of statements for the whole group.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Oracle Fusion supports two methods. Reporting-only consolidation, when the subsidiary already shares the corporate chart of accounts and calendar. And balance transfer consolidation, when structures differ, which maps and transfers balances into the corporate consolidation ledger.

## Segment 3 (steps)

LTV Manufacturing UK Ltd almost certainly uses a different chart of accounts than the US parent. So the path is: translate the GBP balances to USD, map the UK structure to the corporate structure, and transfer the mapped balances in.

## Segment 4 (code)

Once every entity's balances sit in the consolidation ledger, they can't just be added together. Three elimination types remove what shouldn't be double-counted: intercompany stock ownership, intercompany debt, and intercompany revenue and expense.

## Segment 5 (steps)

That four thousand dollar IT allocation from last lesson is real to each entity, but from the group's perspective it's money moving from one pocket to another. It has to be eliminated, or consolidated expense gets overstated.

## Segment 6 (outro)

After translation, transfer, and elimination, the result is one coherent set of statements for the group, not a simple sum of each entity's reports. That closes Chapter 6. Up next, Chapter 7: Period Close, starting with lesson 33's close checklist.

# Script — Period-End Close Checklist

## Segment 1 (title)

Welcome to Chapter 7: Period Close. Everything so far has been things that happen during a period. This chapter is about the deliberate, sequenced process of actually closing one.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Subledgers don't close in random order. Inventory first, then Payables and Purchasing, then Receivables, then Fixed Assets, then Cash Management, and General Ledger last, because GL is where every subledger's activity ultimately lands.

## Segment 3 (code)

For LTV Manufacturing Corporation's March close, the checklist runs: confirm subledgers are closed, review account balances, reconcile subledgers to GL, check key system accounts for stray manual journals, post pending journals, run final reports, then close the period.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Each step depends on the one before it, or catches a category of error the automated posting process can't catch on its own. Reconciling subledgers to GL confirms the numbers actually match, not just that nothing errored out mechanically.

## Segment 5 (steps)

Key system accounts like Accounts Payable control, Accounts Receivable control, and Intercompany accounts get special attention specifically because a stray manual journal can hit them and bypass the normal subledger flow entirely.

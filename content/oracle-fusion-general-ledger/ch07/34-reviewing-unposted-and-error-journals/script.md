# Script — Reviewing Unposted and Error Journals

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 33's checklist said simply "post all pending journals." This lesson is why that single line causes most real close delays, and how to actually clear it.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A controller filters Manage Journals for the period being closed and checks status: unposted, posted, or error. The review has to cover manual journals, imported journals, and recurring or allocation journals, because any of them can be stuck for the same reasons.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A journal can be stuck because it was never submitted, because it's awaiting an approver who hasn't acted, because it failed validation on an invalid account, or because an import batch partially failed.

## Segment 4 (code)

Here's LTV Manufacturing Corporation's March close review. One journal awaiting approval for three days. One with an invalid account that errored on import. One recurring journal that generated but was never submitted. Three different problems, three different fixes.

## Segment 5 (steps)

Here's the part people miss: closing a period doesn't fix any of this. It just leaves that journal's activity out of the final numbers, which means either posting it to a still-open period, or reopening a closed one later, which is disruptive.

## Segment 6 (outro)

Clearing every unposted and error journal before close is far cheaper than discovering a missing one after. Up next, lesson 35: the actual mechanics of opening and closing a period.

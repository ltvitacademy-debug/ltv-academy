# Script — Year-End Close and Opening Balances

## Segment 1 (title)

The twelfth close of the year follows the exact same checklist as every other month, with one extra calculation that only happens now: closing income statement accounts into retained earnings.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Balance sheet accounts carry forward forever, their year-end balance is next year's beginning balance. Income statement accounts measure performance for a period, and a fiscal year is the largest period they measure, so they need to reset to zero for the new year.

## Segment 3 (code)

For LTV Manufacturing Corporation, opening January 2027 is the trigger. Net income for 2026, four hundred twelve thousand dollars, sweeps automatically into Retained Earnings, and every revenue and expense account opens January at zero.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Nobody types that number in manually. The sweep happens as a consequence of the ledger's own structure, which accounts are balance sheet versus income statement, and which account is designated Retained Earnings, triggered simply by opening the next year's first period.

## Segment 5 (steps)

That's exactly why getting the chart of accounts setup right, back in the Enterprise Structures course, matters. The retained earnings designation is configured once, and this process depends on it every single year after.

## Segment 6 (outro)

If an adjustment to the old year is found after the new year has already opened, that old period has to reopen, and retained earnings recalculates again. Up next, the final lesson of this course: General Ledger Troubleshooting Practice.

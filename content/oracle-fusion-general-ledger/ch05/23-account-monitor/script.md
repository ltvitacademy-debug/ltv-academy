# Script — Account Monitor

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 22 was about checking a balance when you decide to. This lesson flips that: Account Monitor watches accounts for you, continuously, and flags them the moment something crosses a line you defined.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Think of it as a dashboard warning light instead of glancing at a fuel gauge. You build a watchlist of accounts once, set a tolerance for each, and from then on the system does the watching, updating as journals post throughout the month.

## Segment 3 (steps)

A tolerance can be a fixed amount, a percentage versus budget, or a percentage versus the same period last year, checked against period-to-date, quarter-to-date, or year-to-date activity, depending on what you care about.

## Segment 4 (code)

Here's a tolerance for LTV Manufacturing Corporation: flag Travel and Entertainment expense if period-to-date actual exceeds one hundred ten percent of period-to-date budget. The moment enough journals post to cross that line, the account shows up flagged, without anyone running a report.

## Segment 5 (steps)

You'll find Account Monitor in two places: the General Accounting Dashboard, where it's one panel among several, and the Financial Reporting Center. It updates near real time, at any level of the chart of accounts hierarchy.

## Segment 6 (outro)

A flagged account is exactly what you'd want to click into next. That's what lesson 24 covers: Account Inspector, the drill-down tool built for exactly that moment.

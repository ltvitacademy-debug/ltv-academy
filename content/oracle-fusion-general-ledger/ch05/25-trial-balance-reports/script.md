# Script — Trial Balance Reports

## Segment 1 (title)

The last three lessons covered interactive ways to look at balances. The Trial Balance Report is different: a formal, point-in-time report, run and retained as part of the close, that proves one mechanical fact: debits equal credits.

## Segment 2 (steps)

The trial balance type parameter changes what you see. Detail prints every account with activity and ending balance. Begin Year and End Year show balances at the start or end of the fiscal year. Results shows net income-statement-style activity for the period.

## Segment 3 (code)

Here's a Detail trial balance excerpt for LTV Manufacturing Corporation, period March 2026. Total debits and total credits match exactly, across every account in the ledger, which is what lets the period move toward close.

## Segment 4 (steps)

But balancing is necessary, not sufficient. Oracle Fusion already enforces equal debits and credits at journal entry. A trial balance that balances says nothing about whether an account was coded to the wrong cost center, or whether an accrual was simply missed.

## Segment 5 (steps)

That's exactly what period-end review is for: catching things that are wrong but balanced. Once reviewed and accepted, this same trial balance becomes the source data for the income statement and balance sheet.

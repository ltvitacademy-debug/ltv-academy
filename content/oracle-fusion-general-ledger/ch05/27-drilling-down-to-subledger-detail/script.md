# Script — Drilling Down to Subledger Detail

## Segment 1 (title)

Every tool in this chapter leads to the same place: a specific question about a specific transaction. This lesson is the full path, from a summarized balance all the way down to the document that started everything.

## Segment 2 (steps)

A manual journal is its own origin, someone typed it. But most dollar volume doesn't start in GL. It starts in a subledger, and Subledger Accounting generates the debits and credits that eventually land in GL as a journal. The GL journal is often a summary, not the first record.

## Segment 3 (code)

The drill path has four stops. Account balance, where an inquiry starts. Journal lines, the individual lines that sum to it. The journal entry, or subledger journal entry if it came from a subledger. And the subledger transaction itself: the actual invoice or asset.

## Segment 4 (steps)

For LTV Manufacturing Corporation, an Accounts Payable liability that looks off drills through journal lines, into a subledger journal entry, and lands on one specific supplier invoice. That invoice is the only thing that actually explains the number.

## Segment 5 (steps)

This drill path is the single most practical day-to-day skill a General Ledger consultant uses. It's also the bridge into the next course, where you'll see how a subledger transaction becomes a GL journal from the other side.

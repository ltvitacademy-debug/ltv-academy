# Script — The General Ledger Work Area

## Segment 1 (title)

Before we create a single journal, let's get oriented. This lesson is a map of where you'll actually be clicking for the rest of this course: the General Ledger work area.

## Segment 2 (steps)

You reach General Ledger through the Navigator, under General Accounting, which lands you on the General Accounting Dashboard. That dashboard is built from infolets — small summary tiles for things like the income statement, the balance sheet, and unposted journals — each one click away from more detail.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Most of your day-to-day work happens in the Journals work area: creating manual journals, managing existing batches, checking approval status, and posting. That's also where recurring journals and allocations live, which we'll get to in chapter four.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Two more areas round things out. Period Close is where the month-end process happens — opening and closing periods, revaluation, translation, the close checklist — and we'll spend chapter seven there. And Scheduled Processes, reachable under Tools, is where the longer jobs actually run: posting, journal import, allocations — you submit them and monitor them to completion, rather than watching them finish instantly on screen.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Dashboard for the big picture, Journals for day-to-day entry, Period Close for month-end, Scheduled Processes for anything that takes real time to run. Next up, lesson four: accounting periods and period status — the calendar everything in this course posts against.

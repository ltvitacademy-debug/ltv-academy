# Lesson 3 — The General Ledger Work Area

**Chapter 1 · General Ledger Fundamentals · Lesson 3 of 37**

## What you'll learn

- How to get to General Accounting from the Oracle Fusion Navigator
- What the General Accounting Dashboard and its infolets show you
- The three work areas you'll live in for most of this course: Journals, Period Close, and Reports
- Where scheduled processes and account inquiry tools fit around them

## Getting there

In Oracle Fusion Cloud, you reach General Ledger through the **Navigator** menu, under **General Accounting**. From there you land on the **General Accounting Dashboard** — a landing page built from **infolets**, small summary tiles you click to drill into detail. A typical dashboard includes an Income Statement infolet, a Balance Sheet infolet, and an Unposted Journals infolet, each giving a one-glance read on the ledger before you go looking for detail.

## The Journals work area

Most of your day-to-day work happens in **Journals**. From here you can:

- **Create Journal** — enter a manual journal line by line
- **Manage Journals** — search for existing journal batches by period, source, category, status, or preparer
- **Manage Journal Approvals** — check where a submitted journal sits in its approval workflow (covered in Chapter 3)
- **Post** — submit validated, approved journals so they update account balances

This is also where you'll create recurring journals and launch allocations later in Chapter 4.

## The Period Close work area

**Period Close** is the work area used to run and monitor the month-end close process: opening and closing periods, running revaluation and translation, reviewing unposted journals before a period closes, and tracking a close checklist. You'll return to this work area in depth in Chapter 7, but it's worth knowing now that it exists as a separate, close-focused space from day-to-day journal entry.

## Reports, inquiry, and scheduled processes

Three more pieces round out the work area:

- **Account inquiry tools** — Account Inspector and Account Monitor let you drill from a summarized balance down to the journal lines behind it (Chapter 5).
- **Financial Reporting Center / Smart View** — where formatted trial balance and financial statement reports live.
- **Scheduled Processes** — the standard Oracle Fusion area (reachable from the same Navigator, under Tools) where you submit and monitor longer-running jobs: posting, journal import, revaluation, allocations, and the close checklist all run here as submitted processes you can monitor to completion, not instant on-screen actions.

```
Navigator
  General Accounting
    General Accounting Dashboard   (infolets: Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Unposted Journals)
    Journals                       (Create Journal, Manage Journals, Approvals, Post)
    Period Close                   (close checklist, open/close periods, revaluation, translation)
    Reports                        (Financial Reporting Center)
Tools
  Scheduled Processes               (submit and monitor long-running jobs)
```

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Infolet | A small dashboard summary tile you click to drill into detail |
| Journals work area | Where manual, recurring, and imported journals are created, reviewed, and posted |
| Period Close work area | Where the close process, revaluation, translation, and the close checklist are managed |
| Scheduled Processes | The area where longer-running jobs (posting, import, allocations) are submitted and monitored |

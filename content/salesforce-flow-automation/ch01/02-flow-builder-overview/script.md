# Script — Flow Builder Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 1 said Flow is the tool. This lesson is a tour of the screen you actually build in: Flow Builder.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: New Automation flow types)

You get here through the App Launcher — search Automation, click New in the Flows panel, and you land on this screen, where you pick which type of flow to build.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: toolbox, canvas, button bar)

Pick a type and you're in Flow Builder itself. Three parts to know by name: the toolbox on the left lists your elements and resources, the canvas in the center is where you actually build, and the button bar on top tells you the flow's status and holds the controls.

## Segment 4 (screenshot: three parts labeled)

Here they are labeled directly: toolbox, canvas, button bar. You'll hear these three words constantly for the rest of this course.

## Segment 5 (steps: button bar essentials)

Four button-bar controls matter most: Save, which doesn't activate anything by itself; Run, which only runs your last saved version; Debug, which runs the flow against sample data you choose so you can watch exactly what happens; and Activate, which makes a specific version the one users actually encounter.

# Script — Workbench, Overview

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter six adds two more tools. First, Workbench: a free, browser-based tool that many Salesforce admins and analysts keep bookmarked.

## Segment 2 (code: free and browser-based)

Workbench is a free web application, so there's nothing to install. You log in with your Salesforce org, choose Production or Sandbox, and pick an API version. One thing to know: it's a community tool, not an officially supported Salesforce product, so follow your organization's security policy before connecting a real org.

## Segment 3 (code: what's in the menus)

The menus are its map. Info shows objects, limits and session details. Queries has the SOQL Query and SOSL Search pages. Data covers insert, update, upsert, delete, undelete and purge. Migration and Utilities hold heavier developer tools, like Apex Execute and REST Explorer.

## Segment 4 (steps: workbench vs data loader)

Compared with Data Loader, Workbench needs no install and shines for quick, ad hoc work: a fast query, a look at an object's fields, a small load. Data Loader is still the tool for large volumes and for repeatable, scripted jobs.

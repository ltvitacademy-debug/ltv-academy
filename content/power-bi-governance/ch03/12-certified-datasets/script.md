# Lesson 12 — Certified Datasets · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

---

## S1 · TITLE CARD

Last lesson showed the Certified option grayed out for most users. That's deliberate — certification is off, tenant-wide, until an admin turns it on.

## S2 · SCREENSHOT (certification setup)

This admin setting answers the most important question before a single dataset gets touched: who's allowed to certify? It can open to the whole org, or narrow down to specific security groups — and most organizations land narrow, because a badge anyone can grant isn't worth much.

## S3 · STEPS CARD (what should be true)

Before certifying anything, three things should actually be true: a known, accountable owner, a documented refresh schedule, and reviewed accuracy against your org's written criteria. Skip this, and Certified just becomes a second Promoted badge with extra friction.

## S4 · SCREENSHOT (certify content)

Once someone actually holds certifier rights, applying the badge is the same simple click Lesson 11 showed. Everything before that moment — the review, the ownership check — is process the organization enforces outside the tool. Power BI itself only enforces who's allowed to click the button.

## S5 · SCREENSHOT (app settings)

And certification doesn't stop at datasets. Published apps carry their own Endorse option too — the same two questions apply: does this meet the criteria, and is the person certifying it actually allowed to.

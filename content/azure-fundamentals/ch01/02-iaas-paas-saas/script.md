# Script — IaaS, PaaS & SaaS

## Segment 1 (title)

Every resource in Azure falls into one of three service models, and they're not about price or power — they're about one question: where's the line between what Microsoft manages and what you manage?

## Segment 2 (code: the stack)

Picture a stack of layers, datacenter at the bottom up to your app and data at the top. With IaaS, you manage the OS up. With PaaS, Microsoft also manages the OS and runtime — you just bring your app and data. With SaaS, Microsoft manages everything, including the application itself.

## Segment 3 (steps: real Azure examples)

IaaS is an Azure Virtual Machine — you patch the OS yourself. PaaS is Azure App Service — you deploy your code, Microsoft runs it. SaaS is Microsoft 365 — you just open Outlook and it works.

## Segment 4 (outro)

That trade-off between control and convenience is the real decision behind every service model choice. Next up: Azure's global infrastructure — regions and availability zones.

# Script — Azure Portal Tour

## Segment 1 (title)

The Azure Portal is your web-based home base for creating, viewing, and managing almost every Azure resource — especially before you're comfortable with the CLI.

## Segment 2 (screenshot: portal-callouts)

Here's the real portal: the top command bar, the collapsible side menu, the main content area, and notifications and settings in the corner.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: azure-portal-menu)

The side menu gives you Home, Favorites for the services you use most, and All services for everything else, organized by category.

## Segment 4 (steps: search and blades)

The global search bar, Ctrl-slash, jumps straight to any service or resource. Open a specific resource and you get its blade — Overview, then Settings, in a consistent pattern across almost everything in Azure.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Once you're comfortable navigating the portal, the next building block is how resources are actually organized and billed. Next up: subscriptions and resource groups.

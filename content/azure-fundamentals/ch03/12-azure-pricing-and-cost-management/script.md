# Script — Azure Pricing & Cost Management

## Segment 1 (title)

Inside the pay-as-you-use model, Azure gives you two ways to pay for the same compute: pay-as-you-go, billed by actual usage with no commitment, or reserved instances — commit for 1 or 3 years and get a significant discount.

## Segment 2 (code: pay-as-you-go vs reserved)

A dev/test VM that gets shut off most nights stays pay-as-you-go. A production database running 24/7 for the next two years is exactly the steady load a reservation is built for — often 40 to 60 percent off.

## Segment 3 (screenshot: cost management principles)

Cost Management and Billing tracks real spending around three principles: visibility, seeing what you spend and where; accountability, knowing who owns each cost; and optimization, acting on what you see.

## Segment 4 (code: budgets and alerts)

A budget is a spending threshold you set for a subscription or resource group. An alert fires when spending crosses a percentage of that budget — catching an expensive VM left running over a weekend on day two, not day thirty.

# Script — Financial Reporting Center Tour

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 2 is about one page inside General Accounting that does a lot of work: the Financial Reporting Center. This is where seeded financial statements live, where your own reports get published, and where several other tools cross-reference. Let's tour it.

## Segment 2 (steps)

You open it from inside General Accounting, usually through a Financial Reporting Center link in the Navigator. Once it's open, it's a hub, not a single report. It organizes Financial Reporting Web Studio statements, Account Groups with Sunburst visualization, Smart View reports, OTBI analyses and dashboards, BI Publisher reports, and mobile BI content, all side by side.

## Segment 3 (steps)

So the Financial Reporting Center is less "one more report" and more a front door gathering output from every tool this course covers, specifically for the General Accounting audience.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Everything here can be opened and run by a user with the right access, usually by picking a point of view: ledger, period, scenario. Authoring new content is a separate activity in the Financial Reporting Web Studio editor, which we'll get to starting in lesson eight. The finished report gets published back into this same center.

## Segment 5 (outro)

As a consultant, this is often the first place you point a client who says "I just need my monthly financials." Up next, lesson six: actually running one of Oracle's seeded financial reports from this center.

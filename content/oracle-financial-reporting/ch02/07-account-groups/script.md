# Script — Account Groups

## Segment 1 (title)

Not every reporting need justifies a full formatted statement. Sometimes a controller just wants to watch a handful of accounts without opening a whole Income Statement. That's what Account Groups are for.

## Segment 2 (steps)

An Account Group is a user-defined watchlist: specific accounts and their current balances, grouped together, independent of any formal statement structure. Typical uses: watching cash across several bank accounts, monitoring expense accounts under scrutiny this quarter, or tracking one liability account closely.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Once created, the group shows up in the Financial Reporting Center with a distinctive radial visualization called Sunburst, nested rings sized by balance, so you can see at a glance which accounts are carrying the most weight. It's a quick visual read, not something you'd hand an auditor as a formal exhibit.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Account Groups are a General Ledger-only feature, built from GL accounts and balances. If you want to watch open Payables invoices instead, that's a different tool, an OTBI analysis, which we get to in chapter three.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Account Groups are quick to set up precisely because they skip the formal design work a full statement requires. That's their strength and their limit. Up next, lesson eight: the basics of Financial Report Studio itself, for when you do need the full statement.

# Script — Scheduling and Sharing Analyses

## Segment 1 (title)

OTBI is built for interactive, real-time viewing, but not everyone who needs a result is going to open the dashboard themselves. Let's close out this chapter with how content gets out to everyone else.

## Segment 2 (steps)

The simplest sharing is catalog permissions. Move or copy an analysis from My Folders into a Shared Folder, and anyone with access to that folder and the right duty roles sees it the next time they browse the catalog. Nothing needs to be emailed for that basic kind of sharing to work.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Beyond that, OTBI supports exporting a current result to Excel, PDF, or CSV for one-off distribution, grab a snapshot, attach it to an email. OTBI's platform has historically supported scheduled delivery agents too, but most Fusion implementations route genuinely recurring deliveries through BI Publisher instead, because its scheduling and formatting options are more mature.

## Segment 4 (steps)

That connects back to the decision framework from chapter one. OTBI's strength is interactive exploration by someone actively looking at it. BI Publisher's strength is a precisely defined, repeatable, scheduled job. When something needs to be set it and forget it, rebuild it in BI Publisher rather than stretching OTBI's delivery options.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Rule of thumb: someone checking it themselves, share via the catalog. One specific result right now, export it directly. Everyone needs it automatically every period, build it in BI Publisher. That closes Chapter 3. Up next, lesson fifteen: BI Publisher overview.

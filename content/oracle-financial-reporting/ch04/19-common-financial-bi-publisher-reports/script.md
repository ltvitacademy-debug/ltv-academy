# Script — Common Financial BI Publisher Reports

## Segment 1 (title)

Chapter 4 closes with the reports you'll actually run as a working consultant, predefined BI Publisher reports Oracle ships, built on the exact architecture you've just spent this chapter learning.

## Segment 2 (steps)

Just like Financial Reporting Studio ships seeded statements and OTBI ships curated subject areas, BI Publisher ships a large catalog of predefined reports for common, recurring needs. A consultant's job is far more often configuring and running these, or lightly adjusting a template, than building a new data model from a blank page.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Payables ships reports covering invoices, payments, reconciliation, period close, and more, including the Payables Invoice Aging Report, listing unpaid invoices by aging period. Receivables ships aging reports, customer statements, an invoice register, a receipts register. Fixed Assets ships an Asset Register. Cash Management, General Ledger, and Subledger Accounting ship their own sets too.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Most implementations start by running a predefined report exactly as Oracle ships it. When it's close but not quite right, the usual move is copying the predefined report's template and modifying the copy, not the original, preserving a fallback and keeping the change auditable. A brand-new data model is reserved for genuinely new needs.

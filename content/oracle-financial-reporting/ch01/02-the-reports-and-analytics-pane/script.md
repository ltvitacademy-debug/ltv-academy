# Script — The Reports and Analytics Pane

## Segment 1 (title)

Before you choose the right reporting tool, you need to know where Oracle Fusion actually keeps its reports. Almost everything in this course ends up in one shared catalog: Reports and Analytics. This lesson is a quick tour.

## Segment 2 (steps)

There are two doors into the same catalog. The standalone Reports and Analytics work area, reached from the Navigator under Tools, shows the full catalog as a page. And many transactional work areas — Payables, Receivables, General Accounting — have a Reports and Analytics panel tab on the side that slides out a narrower, often pre-filtered view of that same catalog.

## Segment 3 (steps)

Inside the catalog, content sits in folders. Shared Folders hold what Oracle seeds out of the box, plus anything your organization has published for everyone. My Folders holds what you've personally created or saved, visible only to you unless you share it.

## Segment 4 (steps)

Two words sound similar but mean different things here. An Analysis is OTBI content, built with drag and drop against a subject area for interactive, ad hoc use. A Report is a BI Publisher object, a data model plus a template, built for precise, repeatable, often scheduled output.

## Segment 5 (outro)

Even if you never build a single analysis yourself, you'll use this pane constantly, to find a predefined report or re-run last month's work instead of recreating it. Up next, lesson three: how to actually decide which tool to reach for on a given request.

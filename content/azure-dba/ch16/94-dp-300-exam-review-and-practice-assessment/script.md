# Script — DP-300 Complete Exam Review & Practice Assessment

## Segment 1 (title)

Lesson 1 introduced the five DP-300 domains and their real weighting. This lesson reviews what each domain actually drew on across the whole course, then puts you through a real, mixed-domain practice assessment.

## Segment 2 (code: the five domains reviewed)

Platform resources, security, monitoring and performance, automation, and HA/DR — each domain pulled from multiple chapters, and the real exam groups skills by domain, not by this course's chapter boundaries.

## Segment 3 (steps: how to think about an unsure question)

Name the constraint before the options — an RTO, RPO, or compliance requirement rules choices in or out before you even read them. And eliminate the obviously platform-wrong option first — a question about Azure SQL Database won't have an on-prem-only mechanism as the right answer.

## Segment 4 (code: practice assessment)

Eight scenario-based questions spanning deployment choice, purchasing models, query regressions, tamper-evidence, failover groups, DMVs, job troubleshooting, and data masking — each one tied back to the specific lesson that taught it.

## Segment 5 (outro)

One course, five domains, reviewed. Lesson 95 — this course's genuine final lesson — is next: real DBA interview practice, not more quiz content.

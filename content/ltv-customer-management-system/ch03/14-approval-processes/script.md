# Lesson 14 — Approval Processes · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Chapter 3's last piece of automation: a real approval process. Any Opportunity discounted past a threshold needs Monica Reyes's sign-off before it can close.

## S2 · CODE — Two new fields

Two new fields make this possible: Discount Percent, where a rep records what they're proposing, and Approval Status, which tracks where that request stands — Not Submitted, Pending Approval, Approved, or Rejected.

## S3 · STEPS — Entry criteria and submission

The approval process itself triggers when Discount Percent is greater than 15. Any owner can submit. The moment it's submitted, the record locks and Approval Status flips to Pending Approval — nobody edits Amount or Stage while Monica's deciding.

## S4 · CODE — One approval step

There's one approval step, assigned directly to Monica Reyes as a specified user, not derived from the role hierarchy — the same 15% threshold applies whether the deal belongs to Tom Baptiste or Priya Nair. Approve unlocks the record and marks it Approved; reject unlocks it, marks it Rejected, and creates a task to revise and resubmit.

## S5 · STEPS — Walking it through

Walk it through: Tom proposes a 20% discount and submits. The record locks. Monica reviews it and makes her call. Either way, the record unlocks again with Approval Status showing exactly what happened.

## S6 · OUTRO

That closes out Chapter 3 entirely. Chapter 4 starts next lesson, putting everything you've built to work with real reports and a dashboard.

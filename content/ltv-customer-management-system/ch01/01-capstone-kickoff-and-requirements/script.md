# Lesson 1 — Capstone Kickoff and Requirements · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Welcome to the capstone. Over the next twenty lessons you're going to build one real Salesforce implementation, start to finish, for one fictional company — not twenty disconnected exercises.

## S2 · STEPS — What the finished org must support

By the end, your org has to support four things. A real data model: a full lead-to-cash flow plus at least two custom objects. A security model: roles, profiles, and sharing rules. Automation: Flows, validation rules, and an approval process. And delivery: organized reports, an executive dashboard, and real documentation.

## S3 · STEPS — How this capstone unfolds

Here's how the four chapters map to that. Chapter 1, Design, is this chapter — you'll meet the company and design the data model and security model on paper before touching Setup. Chapter 2 builds the data and objects. Chapter 3 builds automation and implements security for real. Chapter 4 covers analytics, testing, documentation, and presenting the finished project.

## S4 · CODE — Definition of Done

This is the Definition of Done for the whole capstone — ten requirements, each tied to a specific chapter. Keep this checklist. Every lesson from here forward checks off one or more lines on it.

## S5 · STEPS — What "portfolio-ready" means

Portfolio-ready means more than an org that doesn't throw errors. It means one consistent scenario from Lesson 2 all the way to Lesson 20, a build you can actually defend in an interview — explaining why you made each decision — and real artifacts: a data model diagram, a security matrix, and a written retrospective, not just clicks in Setup.

## S6 · OUTRO

Next lesson, you'll meet the fictional company this entire capstone is built for — its industry, its sales process, and the people you'll be designing this org around.

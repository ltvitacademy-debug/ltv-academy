# Lesson 15 — Reports and Dashboards · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

Chapter 4 opens by putting everything you've built to work. Reports and a dashboard that answer real questions Cascade's leadership actually asks, built on real folders instead of one flat list.

## S2 · STEPS — Three folders

Three folders first, each shared to the team that actually needs it: Sales Reports for the sales team, Operations Reports for Marcus Webb's Service profile, and Customer Success Reports for Angela Wu.

## S3 · CODE — Pipeline by Stage

Pipeline by Stage lives in Sales Reports — every open Opportunity this fiscal year, grouped by Stage, summing Amount. This is the question Monica Reyes asks most: how much is sitting where, right now.

## S4 · CODE — Installation Backlog

Installation Backlog lives in Operations Reports — every Installation Project that isn't Complete yet, grouped by status and by lead installer, oldest target date first. That's Marcus Webb's report, built for exactly how his team works.

## S5 · CODE — Renewals Coming Due

Service Contract Renewals Coming Due lives in Customer Success Reports — any contract marked At Risk, or ending within ninety days, grouped by Service Tier. Angela Wu uses it to work renewals ahead of time, not after a contract has already lapsed.

## S6 · STEPS — The Executive Dashboard

All three come together on one Cascade Executive Dashboard, with a pipeline chart, a backlog donut, a renewals-at-risk bar chart, and an open pipeline total — running as Monica Reyes, so it always shows the complete picture her role hierarchy entitles her to see.

## S7 · OUTRO

Next lesson, these reports get real numbers to show — you'll load clean, realistic sample data into the org instead of testing against a handful of placeholder records.

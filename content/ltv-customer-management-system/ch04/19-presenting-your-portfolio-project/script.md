# Lesson 19 — Presenting Your Portfolio Project · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

A finished org is only half a portfolio piece. The other half is walking someone through it clearly, in their language, in the time they actually give you.

## S2 · STEPS — The walkthrough structure

Five parts, roughly ten minutes: the scenario in two sentences, the data model and why Lookup over Master-Detail, the security model and your favorite sharing rule, and one automation story told in real depth.

## S3 · CODE — Why one story beats three summaries

Pick one story, not three. An interviewer remembers one well-told problem, decision, build, and result better than three rushed feature summaries. The other two pieces of automation stay ready for a follow-up question.

## S4 · STEPS — Likely questions

Prepare for the questions this build practically invites: why Lookup instead of Master-Detail, why every object defaults to Private, why Priya Nair reports straight to the VP, how you actually tested it, and what you'd build next if this were a real client.

# Lesson 18 — Documentation · Voiceover script

Segments map 1:1 to slides. Target: ~2-3 minutes total.

## S1 · TITLE

This isn't the Lesson 1 checklist again. This is documentation written for whoever inherits this org next — a new hire, a consultant, or an interviewer deciding whether you actually understand what you built.

## S2 · STEPS — Eight sections

Eight sections, pulling straight from artifacts you already made: Overview and Data Model from Lessons 2 and 3, Security Model from Lesson 11, Automation Inventory from Lessons 12 through 14, Reports from Lesson 15, Sample Data from Lesson 16, Test Results from Lesson 17, and Known Limitations.

## S3 · CODE — The automation inventory format

Section 4 is the one a new administrator reads most carefully. Every Flow, every validation rule, and the approval process gets the same four-line treatment: its name, its type and trigger, what it actually does, and why it exists.

## S4 · STEPS — Known limitations

Section 8 is just as important — being honest about what this build deliberately doesn't cover. No Lightning Web Components, since this stayed a fully declarative build. No integration with an outside system. No Apex, by design.

## S5 · OUTRO

Next lesson, you'll take this document and turn it into something you actually say out loud — how to present this exact capstone in an interview or a portfolio review.

## The credential sits on top of four prerequisites

As covered in Lesson 1, the Salesforce Certified Application Architect credential is not a standalone exam — Salesforce requires it to be built on four prerequisite certifications: **Platform App Builder**, **Platform Developer I**, **Data Architect**, and **Sharing and Visibility Architect**. This matters for exam preparation specifically: the Application Architect exam itself is testing whether you can combine and apply those four skill sets in service of one coherent application design, not re-testing the prerequisite material in isolation. A candidate who hasn't genuinely internalized data modeling and sharing/visibility design from their prerequisite exams is going to struggle with Application Architect scenario questions no matter how well they've studied this course, because those scenarios assume that foundation rather than re-explaining it.

## Competency areas this credential assesses

Salesforce's own credential materials describe Application Architects as having a deep understanding of native Salesforce features and functionality, with particular strength in modeling a role hierarchy, data, and the appropriate sharing mechanisms for a given business scenario. Combined with the prerequisite structure, the exam's scenario questions generally draw from: data modeling and schema design choices (Lesson 16 of this course, built on the Data Architect prerequisite), sharing and visibility design applied in context (also Lesson 16, built on the Sharing and Visibility Architect prerequisite), declarative versus programmatic solution design (Lessons 5 and 17), application design patterns and reuse (Lessons 6 and 10), and the judgment calls around maintainability, scalability, and extensibility that this entire course has been building toward (Chapter 2, applied throughout Chapter 3).

## Check the official exam guide yourself, not this lesson

Specific exam mechanics — the exact number of questions, the time allowed, the passing score percentage, and the precise domain weightings — change between exam versions and are the kind of detail this course will not state as fixed numbers, because stating an outdated or unverified figure as current fact would be worse than not stating one at all. The reliable source is Salesforce's own Architect credentials page and the official exam guide Salesforce publishes for Application Architect specifically — check **trailhead.salesforce.com/credentials/applicationarchitect** and the linked official exam guide before finalizing a study plan, since those are maintained directly by Salesforce and reflect the current exam, not a snapshot that may already be stale by the time you're studying.

## Using this course as one input among several

This course has been built around the application-design judgment calls an Application Architect exercises day to day — requirements analysis, domain and data modeling applied to app design, declarative-vs-programmatic reasoning, quality attributes, and the solution design process that ties them together. That's real, exam-relevant preparation for the scenario-based judgment the credential assesses. It is not a substitute for: genuinely holding the four prerequisite certifications with real understanding, hands-on practice actually building in a Developer Edition or scratch org, and reading Salesforce's own official exam guide and study resources for the specific, current exam mechanics. A realistic study plan combines all of these, not just one.
